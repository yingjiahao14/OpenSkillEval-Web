# AI Evolution: Research, Industry, and Regulation Datasets

## Overview
Four datasets tracking the parallel development of AI across research, industry adoption, regulation, and private investment from 2010 to 2025.

## Data Source

### research_milestones
15 key research breakthroughs from AlexNet (2012) to frontier models (2025). Each entry has date, label, significance level (medium/high/critical), and a detail description.

### industry_milestones
13 industry adoption events from Siri (2011) to AI coding agents (2025). Same structure as research milestones.

### regulation_milestones
13 regulatory and policy events from the Obama AI report (2016) to EU AI Act enforcement (2025). Same structure.

### ai_investment
Annual global private AI investment in billions USD, 16 data points from 2010 to 2025.

## Data Notes
- Each milestone has a `significance` field: "medium", "high", or "critical"
- All 3 milestone datasets share the same structure (date, label, significance, detail)
- Investment data is annual aggregates from industry reports
- Dates are in YYYY-MM format for milestones, YYYY for investment
