Executive Summary

Nike Inc. (NYSE: NKE) delivered a cumulative adjusted return of 1,282% between January 3, 2000 and January 30, 2026 — equivalent to a compound annual growth rate of 10.6%. A $10,000 investment at the start of the period would have grown to approximately $138,235, before taxes and fees.

Over 26 trading years the stock compounded through five distinct macro regimes: the dot-com bust (2000–2002), a strong recovery and brand-expansion phase (2003–2007), the Global Financial Crisis (2008–2009), an exceptional decade of growth (2010–2021), and a high-rate / valuation compression period from 2022 onward. The all-time high (adjusted) was $166.25 reached on November 05, 2021.

The deepest peak-to-trough decline was -68.5%, recorded during the GFC. Annualised volatility over the full period was 31.2%, typical for a large-cap consumer discretionary name. Average daily trading volume was 10.7M shares, reflecting deep market liquidity throughout the period.

Trading Volume Patterns

Average daily volume across the full period was 10.7M shares. Volume dynamics changed markedly across eras: the 2000s averaged 13.0M shares/day, the 2010s 9.0M, and the 2020s 9.6M. The secular volume decline partly reflects the stock price appreciation — as price rose, nominal share count per transaction fell — along with a broader shift in market microstructure toward ETF and options-based activity.

Volume spikes at key crisis bottoms (GFC trough March 2009, COVID low March 2020) are visible in the chart below, consistent with "capitulation" behaviour where forced sellers meet opportunistic buyers.

Key Takeaways and Investment Implications

Exceptional Long-Run Compounder
A 10.6% CAGR over 26 years meaningfully exceeds typical large-cap benchmarks. Patient holders were richly rewarded through multiple cycles.
Resilient Through Crises
All three major drawdowns (dot-com, GFC, COVID) were fully recovered within 1–3 years, affirming brand durability and balance-sheet strength.
Volatility Is Manageable — but Real
At 31.2% annualised vol, NKE is not a low-risk holding. The max drawdown of -68.5% demands conviction; short horizons carry meaningful downside exposure.
2010s Were the Golden Decade
The 2010s delivered +604% cumulative — the DTC pivot, innovation velocity, and China tailwind are unlikely to repeat at the same magnitude.
Current Valuation Context
As of Jan 2026, NKE trades at $61.81 — -62.8% from its ATH. The 2022–2026 compression phase may represent a reset opportunity or a structural re-rating — verify fundamentals.
Time Horizon Matters
Over any rolling 10-year window in this dataset, NKE was positive. Over 1-year windows, negative returns occurred in roughly 33% of cases. Entry price and horizon are critical determinants of outcome.
