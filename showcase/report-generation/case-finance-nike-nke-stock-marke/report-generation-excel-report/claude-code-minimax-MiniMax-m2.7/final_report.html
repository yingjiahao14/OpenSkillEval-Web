Structural Bull & Bear Phases

2000–2002 (Bear): Dot-com bust and economic slowdown. Nike declined from ~$4.50 to ~$2.50, a -44.8% drawdown. This represented a painful but temporary setback in the longer-term uptrend.

2003–2007 (Bull): Recovery and expansion phase. Nike benefited from global brand building, innovation in footwear, and successful retail expansion. The stock recovered strongly and reached new all-time highs.

2008–2009 (Bear): Global Financial Crisis. Sharp drawdown but relatively quick recovery compared to many stocks. Nike's defensive brand value provided some cushion.

2010–2021 (Bull): Extended secular bull run. Nike transformed into a digital-first company, expanded direct-to-consumer channels, and saw explosive growth in Asia Pacific. This was the most consistent wealth-building period.

2022–2024 (Correction): Macro headwinds, inflation concerns, and consumer spending pressures. The stock gave back a portion of its pandemic-era gains but remained well above pre-COVID levels.

2025–2026 (Recovery): Renewed investor interest as growth initiatives showed results and market conditions stabilized.
