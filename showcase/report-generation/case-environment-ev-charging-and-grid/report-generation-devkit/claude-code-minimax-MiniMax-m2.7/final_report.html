6. Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Synthesized recommendations for load balancing, queue management, pricing strategies, and renewable energy maximization.

Priority Recommendations

1. Peak Hour Load Shifting

Problem: Peak hours show 2-3x higher waiting times and negative optimization rewards across all location types.

  • Implement time-of-use pricing incentives: 15-20% discount for Off-Peak charging
  • Deploy targeted notifications encouraging Off-Peak charging during Peak hours
  • Consider queuing priority for vehicles charging during high-renewable windows

2. Urban Station Capacity Expansion

Problem: Urban stations consistently show the highest queue lengths (avg 5.2) and waiting times (11.2 min).

  • Prioritize additional charger deployment at ST004, ST008, ST012, ST018
  • Evaluate 2-3 additional Urban station locations based on demand density
  • Implement dynamic allocation of charging posts during peak periods

3. Renewable Energy Maximization

Problem: Only 35% average renewable ratio, with significant variation by weather and time.

  • Schedule high-energy sessions during Clear weather periods (42% renewable vs 28% for Rainy)
  • Implement battery storage at Highway stations to buffer renewable energy
  • Leverage Off-Peak windows when renewable ratio is highest (42%) for large fleet charging

4. Dynamic Pricing Optimization

Observation: Current Peak pricing correlates with negative optimization rewards.

  • Introduce gradient pricing within Peak windows to smooth demand
  • Link pricing incentives to real-time renewable availability
  • Consider vehicle-type differentiated pricing (lower rates for Two-Wheelers)

5. Highway Station Service Optimization

Observation: Highway stations show moderate loads but high session duration variability.

  • Deploy higher-power chargers (50-150 kW) at Highway stations for faster turnover
  • Implement pre-conditioning recommendations for long-distance travelers
  • Consider amenities-based wait time reduction strategies
Implementation Priority
Immediate focus should be on (1) Peak load shifting through pricing incentives and (2) Urban capacity expansion. These address the most critical pain points with the highest impact on customer satisfaction and grid optimization.
