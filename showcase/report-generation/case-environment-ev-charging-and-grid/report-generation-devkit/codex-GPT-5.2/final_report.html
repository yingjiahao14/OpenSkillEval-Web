Optimization Insights & Recommendations (Actionable)

Recommendations below synthesize queue/wait, load, renewable ratio, and optimization reward signals into concrete interventions.

1) Shift flexible demand into greener windows (Off-Peak)

Off-Peak shows meaningfully higher renewables (≈0.388–0.397 across weather) and lower mean station load (34.65 kW vs 84.07 kW in Peak). Build programs that move discretionary charging to Off-Peak.

Actions

  • Introduce explicit “Green Hours” incentives during Off-Peak (discounts or loyalty multipliers tied to renewable_energy_ratio).
  • Publish station-level “green availability” guidance in-app (target stations with high mean demand + high Off-Peak renewables).

2) Target queue mitigation where long-tail waits are highest

Average waits are similar by location/time slot, but P90 waits are ~15–17 minutes in the highest-wait segments. Focus on tail reduction for customer experience.

Actions

  • Deploy “virtual queue + ETA” and appointment windows at top-wait stations to smooth arrivals and reduce on-site congestion.
  • Operational playbook during high-traffic Peak windows: temporary staffing, active bay management, and fast-turnover policies for low-energy sessions.

3) Use optimization_reward as an operational control signal

Reward is strongly linked to renewables (+0.590) and inversely linked to station_load (-0.580), charging_demand (-0.577), and waiting_time (-0.517). This makes it a useful composite KPI for multi-objective tuning.

Actions

  • Set station targets on optimization_reward with guardrails: max P90 wait and max station_load thresholds.
  • Prioritize grid-aware scheduling: cap high-power allocation during Peak when station_load is elevated, and release constraints during Off-Peak renewable-rich windows.

4) Pricing strategy: strengthen differentiation (if allowed)

Mean electricity price is nearly flat across time slots (9.97 Off-Peak vs 9.94 Peak). If pricing is intended to shape demand, larger differentials or renewables-indexed pricing may be needed.

Actions

  • Implement renewables-indexed pricing: price decreases as renewable_energy_ratio rises, encouraging “charge when green”.
  • Use Peak load adders to reduce station_load spikes and improve reward outcomes.
