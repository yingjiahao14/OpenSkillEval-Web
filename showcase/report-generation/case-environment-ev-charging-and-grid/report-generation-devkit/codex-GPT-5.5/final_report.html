Optimization Insights & Recommendations

The recommendations synthesize queue behavior, load concentration, renewable availability, dynamic pricing, and the optimization reward signal. Each action is tied directly to measured patterns in the dataset.

1
Shift discretionary charging from Peak to Off-Peak with price and booking incentives.

Peak station load averages 84.07 kW versus 34.65 kW Off-Peak, while Off-Peak renewable ratio is 39.27% versus 28.97% Peak. Offer scheduled-charging discounts, fleet reservation windows, or loyalty credits for sessions that can move to Off-Peak; measure success by reducing Peak load and improving reward from the current Peak average of -19.80.

2
Deploy queue-management pilots at the top wait station-location pairs.

Start with ST004 (Highway), where average wait is 10.35 minutes, then extend to the rest of the high-wait table. Use reservation slots, charger pre-assignment, and queue visibility to lower the network average wait of 9.53 minutes.

3
Recalibrate dynamic prices to reflect load and renewable availability.

The current price-demand correlation is -0.012, while reward is strongly tied to lower load (-0.580) and higher renewable ratio (0.590). Introduce higher Peak congestion adders and lower Off-Peak/high-renewable prices rather than keeping average prices nearly flat by time slot.

4
Protect grid assets with Peak load caps and automated throttling.

Because station load and charging demand correlate at 0.995, demand surges pass through to grid stress. Use real-time load caps, managed charging, and charger power modulation during Peak; prioritize completion for high-priority sessions while nudging flexible sessions into Off-Peak windows.

5
Make renewable-aware scheduling a customer-facing product.

Off-Peak is the clearest green-charging window in this file. Label booking windows with expected renewable availability and steer fleets toward Off-Peak slots, where renewable ratio averages 39.27% and optimization reward is materially better than Peak.

6
Track an operational scorecard by location type and time slot.

Monitor average wait, queue length, station load, renewable ratio, price, and reward every reporting cycle for Urban/Highway and Peak/Off-Peak. This keeps the management dashboard aligned with the segmentation that most clearly explains the dataset.

Methodology, Data Quality & Limitations

Calculations use /app/benchmark/data.csv as the primary source. Temporal fields were parsed from strings, and all KPIs are direct aggregations of the supplied columns. No external estimates or fabricated values are used.

Data integrity

No missing values were detected across the 8,354 records and 27 columns. Duplicate row count is 0. The dataset contains 27 columns.

Segmentation coverage
  • The source specification calls out Suburban as a possible segment, but this file contains only Urban and Highway records; Suburban comparisons are therefore not reported.
  • The source specification references Mid-Peak, but the analyzed file contains only Off-Peak and Peak time slots.

Core KPI Formulas

  • Average Waiting Time = mean of waiting_time.
  • Average Energy Consumed per Session = mean of energy_consumed_kWh.
  • Mean Station Load = mean of station_load.
  • Renewable Energy Ratio = mean of renewable_energy_ratio.
  • Mean Optimization Reward = mean of optimization_reward.
