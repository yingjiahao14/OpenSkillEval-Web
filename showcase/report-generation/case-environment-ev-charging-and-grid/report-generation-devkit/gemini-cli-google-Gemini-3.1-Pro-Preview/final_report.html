1. Station Utilization & Demand Analysis

Key Takeaway: Highway locations generally experience distinct demand patterns compared to Urban and Suburban stations. Time slots significantly influence charging demand, with specific peaks requiring capacity management.

2. Queue Length & Waiting Time Analysis

Key Takeaway: Wait times vary sharply by location type and time of day. High traffic density directly correlates with longer queues, significantly impacting the customer experience.

3. Energy Consumption & Grid Load Patterns

Key Takeaway: Grid load follows clear temporal patterns throughout the day and week. Peak time slots place the highest stress on the local grid infrastructure.

4. Renewable Energy Integration & Dynamic Pricing

Key Takeaway: Renewable energy availability is highly sensitive to weather conditions. Dynamic pricing structures successfully capture demand variations across time slots.

5. Vehicle Charging Behavior & Session Profiles

Key Takeaway: Different vehicle types exhibit vastly different charging profiles. Larger vehicles consume more energy and occupy chargers for longer durations.

6. Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Actionable Steps for Network Management

  • Load Shifting via Pricing: Incentivize off-peak charging by increasing the pricing differential between peak and off-peak times. This will help flatten the grid load curve and reduce station stress during high-demand windows.
  • Queue Mitigation at High-Traffic Hubs: Deploy dynamic queue management systems at Highway and high-traffic Urban stations. Consider implementing a reservation system to smooth out arrivals and reduce average wait times.
  • Renewable Energy Synchronization: Align charging incentives with weather forecasts. Offer lower rates when renewable energy (solar/wind) is abundant (e.g., Clear and Windy days) to boost the Renewable Energy Ratio.
  • Capacity Expansion Targeting: Focus physical infrastructure upgrades (adding chargers) at locations that consistently show high demand and queue lengths during peak hours, particularly for heavy-duty vehicle types.
