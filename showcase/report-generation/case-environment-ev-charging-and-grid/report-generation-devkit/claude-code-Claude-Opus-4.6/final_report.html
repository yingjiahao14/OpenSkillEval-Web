Actionable Recommendations

High Priority

1. Load Shifting to Off-Peak Windows

Peak station load averages 84.07 kW vs. 34.65 kW Off-Peak. Implement time-of-use incentives (discounted rates, loyalty points) to encourage Off-Peak charging. Target non-urgent vehicle segments (Two-Wheelers, low-priority sessions) with automated scheduling to shift 15–20% of Peak demand.

High Priority

2. Dynamic Queue Management

The worst-performing station (ST004) averages 10.35 min wait with queue depth of 4.58. Deploy real-time queue visibility across nearby stations with routing recommendations. Implement priority-based queue advancement for high-priority sessions and overflow routing to adjacent under-utilized stations.

High Priority

3. Renewable-Aligned Pricing Incentives

The renewable ratio varies from 35.1% (Cloudy) to 35.7% (Clear). Introduce "Green Charging" pricing tiers that offer 10–15% discounts during high-renewable periods (tracked via renewable_energy_ratio > 0.4). This aligns customer behavior with grid sustainability goals and improves optimization reward.

Medium Priority

4. Capacity Expansion at Bottleneck Stations

ST004 (Highway), ST009 (Urban), ST017 (Highway), are consistently above-average in wait times and queue depth. Prioritize adding charger units at these locations — particularly fast chargers (50 kW) that can reduce session duration. Model ROI based on current throughput and projected demand growth.

Medium Priority

5. Vehicle-Aware Charger Allocation

Buses occupy chargers for ~179 min vs. ~177 min for Two-Wheelers. Implement dedicated charger lanes by vehicle class to prevent large-battery vehicles from blocking throughput. Reserve fast-charge bays for time-sensitive vehicles while directing Two-Wheelers to standard-power stations.

Medium Priority

6. Weather-Responsive Grid Management

Integrate weather forecasting into grid management to pre-position load balancing during adverse conditions. Rainy conditions show an average renewable ratio of 35.4%, suggesting weather-driven renewable variability. Use predictive models to adjust charger power levels and pre-charge station batteries ahead of low-renewable periods.

Optimization Summary: The mean optimization reward of -11.83 indicates significant room for improvement across the network. Top-quartile sessions achieve rewards with avg price 9.94 and renewable ratio 51.8%, while bottom-quartile sessions face price 9.94 and 23.2% renewable share. Implementing load shifting, dynamic pricing aligned with renewable availability, and targeted capacity expansion at the highest-wait stations offers the clearest path to improving network-wide optimization reward.
