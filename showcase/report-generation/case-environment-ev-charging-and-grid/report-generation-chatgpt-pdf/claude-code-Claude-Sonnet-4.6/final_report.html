Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Actionable interventions synthesized from all prior sections, prioritized by impact and implementation complexity

High Priority
⚡

Implement Time-of-Use (ToU) Pricing to Shift Peak Demand

Station load during Peak hours is 2.43× higher than Off-Peak (84.07 vs 34.65 kW), yet electricity prices show no meaningful difference between the two slots (r≈0). Introduce dynamic ToU pricing with a 30–40% price premium during Peak and discounts during Off-Peak (6–8am, 10pm–midnight). Target: shift 15–20% of flexible demand (Two-Wheelers, Low-priority sessions) to Off-Peak windows to reduce peak load below 75 kW.

High Priority
🌿

Incentivize Off-Peak Charging for Renewable Maximization

Off-Peak sessions capture 39.3% renewable energy vs 29.0% during Peak — a 35.5% improvement. Since the network's mean optimization reward is entirely negative (−11.8), and renewable ratio has the strongest positive correlation with reward (r=+0.59), shifting sessions to Off-Peak is the single highest-leverage lever for improving the grid score. Offer "Green Charge" discount rates (e.g., −20%) for sessions starting after 10pm or before 7am.

High Priority
📈

Address Structural Queue Capacity Constraints

Average waiting times (9.31–9.63 min) and queue lengths (4.5–4.7 vehicles) are uniform across both time slots and locations — a hallmark of a capacity-constrained system rather than a demand-management problem. 18.4% of customers wait over 15 minutes. Recommendation: audit the 5 lowest-session stations (ST008, ST016, ST020, ST007, ST002) for underutilized charger capacity, and prioritize adding charger units at ST013 and ST001 (highest session volumes at 459 and 443 respectively).

Medium Priority
🔌

Deploy Smart Load Balancing Across Charging Power Tiers

Four charging power tiers (7, 11, 22, 50 kW) are nearly equally distributed (~2,055–2,124 sessions each). During Peak hours, algorithmically throttle 50 kW high-power chargers to 22 kW for Low/Medium-priority sessions, then re-enable at full power during Off-Peak. This directly reduces peak station load without adding hardware. The optimization reward correlation with station load is strong and negative (r=−0.58), confirming that load reduction directly improves grid score.

Medium Priority
🕑

Expand Pre-Scheduling & Reservation System

Given the uniform 3-vehicle-type fleet with consistent ~34% arrival SOC and ~178 min session durations, sessions are highly predictable. Implement a reservation slot system that pre-assigns charger units and time windows. This converts unpredictable queue formation into managed demand, directly reducing the 28-minute maximum wait outliers and improving throughput at high-volume stations (ST013, ST001, ST005).

Ongoing
🎯

Optimize Charging Priority Dispatch Logic

High-priority sessions achieve optimization rewards of −11.64 vs −11.97 for Medium priority, a modest but consistent 2.8% improvement. Review the current priority assignment logic (currently ~34% Medium, ~33% Low, ~32% High network-wide) and increase High-priority designation for sessions occurring during high renewable-ratio windows (Off-Peak) to compound the sustainability benefit. Track the correlation between priority, time slot, and reward monthly to validate dispatch improvements.
