Data Integrity Notes

All metrics and visuals are computed directly from data.csv. Where the dataset is structurally limited, the report states the limitation rather than inferring missing segments.
Detected limitations / caveats
  • Dataset does not include `Suburban` location_type values; comparisons are limited to Urban and Highway.
  • Dataset does not include `Mid-Peak` time_slot values; comparisons are limited to Peak and Off-Peak.
  • Each `station_id` appears under multiple `location_type` values in this dataset; station-level results are best interpreted within their associated location_type grouping rather than as a fixed attribute.
Dataset brief: Kaggle synthetic smart-grid EV charging operations (see source_brief.md).
