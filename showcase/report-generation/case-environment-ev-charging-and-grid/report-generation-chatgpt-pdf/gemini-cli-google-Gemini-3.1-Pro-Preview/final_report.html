Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Based on the data analysis, the following actions are recommended:

  • Load Shifting: Incentivize users (via dynamic pricing) to shift charging to Off-Peak windows, where renewable energy is often abundant and grid load is minimal.
  • Queue Management: Urban and Highway locations experience severe peak wait times. Implement dynamic routing and reservation systems for high-traffic slots.
  • Renewable Synergy: Align dynamic pricing heavily with weather conditions. Lower prices during Clear/Sunny weather to maximize solar usage.
  • Capacity Upgrades: Focus initial infrastructure expansions on Highway stations which see high-energy demands and sustained utilization.
