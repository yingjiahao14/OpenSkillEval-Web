1 · Queue

Deploy queue controls in the highest-wait segment first

Focus operational intervention on Highway / Off-Peak, where average waiting time reaches 9.6 minutes. Apply reservation slots, dynamic charger assignment, and queue-length alerts before broad network expansion.

Success metric: reduce segment waiting time toward the network average of 9.5 minutes while keeping mean station load stable.

2 · Load

Shift flexible demand away from high-load day/time windows

The highest observed mean load occurs on Tuesday / Peak at 84.8 kW. Use demand-response thresholds, delayed-start offers, and charger power caps during these windows, especially for low-priority sessions.

Success metric: narrow the peak/off-peak load gap of 49.4 kW without increasing average wait time.

3 · Pricing

Tie dynamic pricing to renewable availability, not only demand

Promote charging in the greenest observed segment, Clear / Off-Peak with a 39.7% renewable ratio, and add surcharges or lower incentives in Rainy / Peak, where the ratio is 28.8%.

Success metric: increase the network renewable ratio above the current 35.4% baseline while maintaining non-negative or improving optimization reward.

4 · Capacity

Use station-level demand plus wait time for expansion decisions

Do not expand solely based on session counts. Prioritize stations that appear in both high-demand and high-wait rankings; currently ST012 leads mean demand at 56.6, while ST004 leads average wait at 10.4 minutes.

Success metric: reduce queue length at top-wait stations without pushing station load above the segment mean load baseline.

5 · Dispatch

Segment charger power rules by vehicle profile and priority

Assign high-power chargers to vehicle types and priorities where energy needs and queue impact justify the grid load. Lower-priority or lower-energy sessions can be steered to off-peak windows or lower-power chargers when queues are active.

Success metric: improve mean optimization reward from the current -11.83 baseline while preserving average energy delivered per session.

Management action: Pilot these interventions at the top two high-wait stations for one operating cycle, compare against the KPI baseline in this report, then scale only the measures that reduce wait time and improve reward without increasing grid load.
