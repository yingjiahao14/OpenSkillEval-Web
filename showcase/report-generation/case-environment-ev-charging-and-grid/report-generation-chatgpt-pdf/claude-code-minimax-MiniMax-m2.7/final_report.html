6. Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Synthesizing findings into actionable interventions for load balancing, queue management, pricing strategy, and renewable energy maximization.

Priority Recommendations

1. Load Shifting to Off-Peak Windows
Off-Peak periods show 23% higher renewable energy ratio (35.6% vs 29.0%) with marginally lower queue lengths. Implement dynamic pricing incentives (15–20% discount) during Off-Peak windows to encourage customers to shift charging sessions. This simultaneously reduces grid stress and maximizes renewable energy utilization.
2. Address Highway Station Capacity
Highway stations show 0.87% higher station load and 1.6% longer average wait times than Urban stations. Consider deploying additional fast chargers at top-wait stations (ST004, ST009) on highway corridors to reduce bottleneck concentration.
3. Sunday Peak Management
Sunday exhibits the highest average wait time (9.61 min) despite consistent session volume across days. This suggests weekend leisure traffic creates concentration peaks. Consider implementing time-slot reservations or promotional pricing during low-demand Sunday windows to smooth demand.
4. Clear Weather Charging Promotions
Clear weather conditions correlate with the highest renewable energy ratio (0.3569). Design "Green Charging" promotional pricing during clear weather Off-Peak windows to maximize sustainable energy usage when solar generation is highest.
5. Multi-Vehicle Session Optimization
The average session delivers ~39 kWh over ~179 minutes, with vehicles typically charging from ~35% to ~98% SOC. Consider implementing intelligent charging scheduling that batches vehicles by departure urgency to maximize throughput during high-renewable windows.
