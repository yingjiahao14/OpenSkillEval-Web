Optimization Insights & Recommendations

  1. Deploy time-slot/location-specific queue controls at highest wait cells from the heatmap (dynamic assignment and overflow routing).
  2. Introduce off-peak and high-renewable price incentives where waits and load are lower, to shift discretionary sessions.
  3. Use traffic-conditioned staffing/operations playbooks: pre-activate chargers when high traffic density aligns with high waits.
  4. Prioritize top-session stations for incremental capacity and demand-response integration.
  5. Track optimization reward jointly with waiting time and renewable ratio as a balanced scorecard for policy tuning.
