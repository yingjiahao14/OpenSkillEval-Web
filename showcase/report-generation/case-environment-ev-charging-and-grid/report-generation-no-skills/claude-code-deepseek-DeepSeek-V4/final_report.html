RECOMMENDATION 1

Load Shifting to Off-Peak Windows

Peak-time station loads (84.1 kW avg) are 143% higher than off-peak (34.7 kW). Implement dynamic pricing incentives that make off-peak charging economically attractive — off-peak electricity already costs 9.97 vs. 9.94 peak. A targeted 71% reduction in peak load through load shifting would substantially ease grid stress.

RECOMMENDATION 2

Queue Management at Highway Stations During Peak

Highway stations during peak periods record the longest wait times (9.6 min avg). Consider adding charger capacity at the highest-utilization highway stations (ST013, ST001, ST017) and implement reservation systems to smooth arrival patterns.

RECOMMENDATION 3

Maximize Renewable Energy Utilization

The renewable energy ratio peaks during Off-Peak slots (39.3%) and under Clear conditions (35.7%). Encourage charging during high-renewable windows through real-time green charging indicators and lower pricing. Optimization rewards are highest (-1.74) when renewable ratios exceed 60%, confirming the alignment between sustainability and grid optimization.

RECOMMENDATION 4

Pricing Strategy Refinement

Electricity price and demand show a correlation of r=-0.012. Strengthen the price-demand linkage by introducing sharper differentials: higher peak premiums and deeper off-peak discounts. Current peak-to-off-peak price ratio is 1.00x.

RECOMMENDATION 5

Station-Specific Optimization

Stations ST009, ST004, ST012 show the lowest optimization rewards (-12.72 avg). Investigate these stations for charger misconfiguration, poor demand forecasting, or grid connection issues. Conversely, replicate practices from top-performing stations ST020, ST005, ST015 (rewards up to -10.96 avg).

RECOMMENDATION 6

Fleet-Specific Infrastructure Planning

Cars account for 34% of sessions but have longer average charge durations (179.7 min). Plan charger power levels and bay counts to match the dominant vehicle mix at each station type.

Overall Assessment

The network shows meaningful optimization headroom. The mean optimization reward of -11.83 indicates room for improvement — negative values suggest many sessions operate under suboptimal grid conditions. The strongest optimization rewards occur at Urban locations during Off-Peak slots (-6.94), providing a template for best practices across the network.
