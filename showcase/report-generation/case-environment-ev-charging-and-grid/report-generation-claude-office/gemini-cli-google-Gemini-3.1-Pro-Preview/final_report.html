Station Utilization & Demand Analysis

Analyze charging demand distribution across stations and location types, identifying high-demand stations, peak utilization periods, and capacity constraints.

Key Takeaways

  • Peak charging demand strongly correlates with location type and time slot, with specific zones facing higher strain during daytime hours.
  • Urban and Highway locations exhibit differing demand patterns, necessitating localized capacity planning and targeted infrastructure rollout.

Queue Length & Waiting Time Analysis

Examine queue lengths and waiting times by station, time slot, vehicle type, and traffic density to identify bottleneck patterns.

Key Takeaways

  • Peak times at Highway and Urban locations show significantly higher wait times, directly impacting customer experience.
  • These specific location-time combinations should be the primary targets for queue management and throughput interventions.

Energy Consumption & Grid Load Patterns

Assess energy consumption patterns, station load profiles across time slots and days of the week, and the relationship between charging power levels and grid stress.

Key Takeaways

  • Grid stress consistently peaks during specific time slots across all location types, highlighting periods where active load balancing is most critical.
  • Off-peak periods offer significant unused capacity, presenting an opportunity for large-scale load-shifting strategies.

Renewable Energy Integration & Dynamic Pricing

Evaluate the renewable energy ratio across different conditions (time, weather, location) and analyze how dynamic electricity pricing correlates with demand.

Key Takeaways

  • Clear weather conditions yield the highest renewable energy integration across all time slots, while rainy or cloudy conditions diminish green charging potential.
  • Off-Peak and Mid-Peak times paired with favorable weather offer the most sustainable charging windows.

Vehicle Charging Behavior & Session Profiles

Profile charging sessions by vehicle type, examining battery capacity, initial/final SOC, energy consumed, charging duration, and priority distribution.

Key Takeaways

  • Heavy-duty vehicles (Buses, Trucks) and high-capacity SUVs consume significantly more energy per session, heavily impacting local grid load and session duration.
  • Station design and charger allocation must explicitly account for the specific vehicle mix prevalent at that location type.

Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Synthesize findings into actionable recommendations for load balancing, queue management, pricing strategy adjustments, and renewable energy maximization.

Actionable Interventions

  • Load Balancing & Capacity: Implement active load shifting to off-peak hours by offering preferential charging priority or reduced rates, specifically targeting Urban and Highway locations to alleviate grid stress during Peak slots.
  • Queue Management: Deploy pre-booking systems or dynamic routing recommendations for locations showing wait times exceeding established SLAs during peak slots. This directly improves the customer experience metric.
  • Pricing Strategy: Introduce dynamic, weather-linked and time-of-use pricing to incentivize charging during periods of high renewable energy generation (Clear weather, Off-Peak), aligning customer behavior with the grid's green energy availability.
  • Infrastructure Expansion: Prioritize high-power DC fast charger deployment at Highway locations serving high-capacity vehicle types (Buses/Trucks) to reduce lengthy session durations and accelerate throughput.
