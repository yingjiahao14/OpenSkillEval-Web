Urban stations average 4.6 vehicles in queue during peak periods. Target top 5 stations (ST004, ST008, ST012, ST014, ST018) for 40% capacity increase through additional fast chargers. Priority should be given to stations with current wait times exceeding 12 minutes during Peak slots.

3. Renewable Energy Maximization

Weather-correlated analysis shows Clear conditions yield 1.0x higher renewable ratios than Rainy. Implement predictive scheduling that pre-positions charging during high-renewable forecast windows. Suburban locations show 23% higher renewable integration potential — prioritize infrastructure upgrades there.
