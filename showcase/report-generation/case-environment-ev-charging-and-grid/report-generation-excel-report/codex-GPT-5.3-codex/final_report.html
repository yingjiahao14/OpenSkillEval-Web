6) Optimization Insights & Recommendations

  1. Target queue hotspots first: prioritize interventions where wait time is highest, beginning with Off-Peak (avg wait 9.58 min by time-slot aggregate).
  2. Time-slot pricing for load shifting: apply stronger incentives in high-wait windows and discounts in windows with stronger renewable supply (best renewable window: Off-Peak, avg renewable ratio 39.27%).
  3. Location-type capacity balancing: combine temporary queue caps and routing prompts between Urban/Highway/Suburban sites where observed queue-length differentials persist.
  4. Reward-driven control tuning: operationalize policies that maximize optimization reward during high-load periods (best reward time-slot currently: Off-Peak, reward -7.06).
  5. Fleet-aware scheduling: reserve charger mixes for vehicle segments with consistently higher waits (top segment sample: Bus, avg wait 9.61 min).
