Objective: Synthesize findings into actionable recommendations for load balancing, queue management, pricing strategy adjustments, and renewable energy maximization, leveraging the optimization reward metric as a performance signal.

Target the worst wait-time segment first

What to do: Prioritize interventions for Highway stations during Off-Peak (highest mean waiting time: 9.63 min).

How to implement: Add temporary capacity (mobile chargers), adjust charger assignment rules, and direct drivers via in-app routing to nearby underutilized stations during this segment.

Success metric: Track mean/95th percentile waiting_time for that segment weekly.

Fix bottleneck stations with highest average waits

What to do: Conduct capacity and queuing review for top stations by average waiting time: ST004 (10.07 min), ST009 (9.86 min), ST011 (9.77 min), ST012 (9.71 min), ST014 (9.66 min).

How to implement: Check charger availability/uptime, evaluate adding stalls, and implement virtual queue + reservation windows at these locations.

Success metric: Queue_length and waiting_time improvements at those station_ids.

Use peak-period load shaping to lift optimization reward

What to do: Peak sessions have lower mean optimization_reward than Off-Peak (Peak: -19.80, Off-Peak: -7.06).

How to implement: Offer price or loyalty incentives to shift discretionary charging from Peak to Off-Peak; cap charging_power_kW dynamically when station_load is high.

Success metric: Mean optimization_reward and station_load during Peak.

Align incentives with greenest windows

What to do: Highest mean renewable_energy_ratio occurs during Off-Peak under Clear conditions (0.397).

How to implement: Trigger 'green charging' discounts or credits during these windows and promote them in the customer app; coordinate with grid operators for renewable-rich dispatch periods.

Success metric: Renewable_energy_ratio and shifted session counts during promoted windows.

Key takeaways

  • Recommendations are derived from observed segment maxima/minima in the dataset.
