Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Synthesize findings into actionable recommendations for load balancing, queue management, pricing strategy adjustments, and renewable energy maximization, leveraging the optimization reward metric as a performance signal.

Reward Range

Best location/time reward: Urban / Off-Peak at -6.94. Weakest: Highway / Peak at -20.01.

Wait-Reward Relationship

Waiting time and optimization reward correlation is -0.52 across session-level records.

Prioritize queue relief in the worst wait segment

Deploy temporary charger capacity, pre-allocation logic, or staffed queue triage first to Highway sites during Off-Peak windows. This segment averages 9.6 minutes wait and 4.7 queued vehicles, making it the clearest customer-experience bottleneck.

Use station-level expansion triggers

Set ST-level capacity review triggers for stations at or above the current top-wait benchmark. ST004 has the longest average wait at 10.4 minutes with average queue length of 4.6 vehicles; similar stations should be candidates for charger additions or reservation controls.

Shift flexible demand away from high-load windows

Introduce managed charging rules and fleet nudges that move non-urgent sessions away from Urban / Peak, where mean station load is 84.1 kW. Use lower-priority vehicles as the primary shiftable pool to protect urgent charging needs.

Align price incentives with renewable-rich periods

Discount or loyalty-price charging during Clear / Off-Peak periods, where renewable ratio reaches 39.7%. Conversely, avoid blanket discounts in Rainy / Peak periods, where renewable ratio falls to 28.8%.

Use reward metrics as an operating guardrail

Track mean optimization reward by location/time slot in weekly operations reviews. The current spread from -20.01 to -6.94 shows enough variation to support targeted policy experiments rather than network-wide rule changes.

All recommendations are derived from observed segment-level metrics in data.csv. They should be tested operationally before full-network rollout because the source data is simulated and does not include physical charger capacity by station.
