Queue Length & Waiting Time Analysis queue-wait-analysis
Critical Insight

Peak hours show significantly higher station loads (84 kW vs 34.7 kW off-peak) but surprisingly similar waiting times across time slots. This suggests grid capacity, not queue management, is the primary constraint during peak periods. Consider load shifting incentives to reduce peak grid stress.

Energy Consumption & Grid Load Patterns energy-grid-load
Grid Stress Alert

Station load during Peak hours (84 kW) is 2.4x higher than Off-Peak (34.7 kW). The optimization reward drops from -7.06 during Off-Peak to -19.80 during Peak, indicating substantial grid stress. Load shifting strategies could improve grid efficiency by up to 60%.

Renewable Energy Integration & Dynamic Pricing renewable-pricing
Renewable Opportunity

Off-Peak periods achieve 39.2% renewable ratio vs only 28.9% during Peak hours. Clear weather slightly outperforms other conditions (35.7% vs 35.1-35.4%). Implementing time-of-use incentives to shift 15-20% of Peak demand to Off-Peak could increase renewable utilization by ~25%.

Optimization Insights & Recommendations optimization-recommendations

Actionable Recommendations

1. Implement Dynamic Load Shifting

Target: Reduce Peak hour station load by 20-25% | Method: Offer 15-20% electricity price reduction during Off-Peak windows (currently 62.5% of sessions are Off-Peak). This aligns with the 39.2% renewable ratio achievable during Off-Peak vs 28.9% during Peak.

2. Address Station-Specific Bottlenecks

Priority Stations: ST004 (10.07 min avg wait), ST009, ST011 | Action: Deploy additional fast chargers (50kW+) at these high-wait locations. ST017 and ST002 show highest queue lengths (4.78) and should be considered for capacity expansion.

3. Maximize Renewable Energy Utilization

Strategy: Time 40% of charging sessions to coincide with Off-Peak + Clear weather conditions | Expected Outcome: Increase overall renewable ratio from 35.4% to 42%+ by leveraging the 39.2% Off-Peak renewable rate and favorable weather patterns.

4. Improve Grid Optimization Performance

Current State: Mean optimization reward is -11.83 (negative indicates suboptimal conditions) | Gap: Peak hours score -19.80 vs Off-Peak -7.06 | Fix: Deploy predictive demand scheduling and real-time grid load balancing to close the 64% performance gap between time slots.
