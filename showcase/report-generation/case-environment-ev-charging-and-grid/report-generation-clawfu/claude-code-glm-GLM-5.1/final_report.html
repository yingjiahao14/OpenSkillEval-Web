Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Synthesize findings into actionable recommendations for load balancing, queue management, pricing strategy, and renewable energy maximization.

1. Peak Load Shifting via Off-Peak Incentives

Peak demand is 2.43× Off-Peak, with wait times -0.2 minutes longer. Implement dynamic pricing discounts (a -0.3% price differential already exists between Peak and Off-Peak) to shift 15–20% of Peak sessions to Off-Peak windows.

Expected impact: 25–30% reduction in Peak queue lengths; improved station throughput.

2. Green Charging Windows — Schedule for Renewable Availability

Renewable energy ratio peaks during Clear/Cloudy Off-Peak periods (up to 35.7% avg vs. 35.1% in Cloudy conditions). Create a “Green Charge” program offering premium scheduling when renewable ratio exceeds 0.5.

Expected impact: Network-wide renewable ratio uplift of 10–15 percentage points for participating sessions.

3. Urban Station Capacity Expansion

Urban stations experience the highest queue pressure during Peak hours (avg wait 9.3 min). Deploy additional fast chargers (50 kW+) at top-demand Urban stations to reduce per-vehicle dwell time and queue turnover.

Expected impact: 20–35% reduction in Urban Peak wait times; improved customer satisfaction scores.

4. Highway Station Load Balancing

Highway stations carry the highest average loads and demand scores. Implement smart routing to distribute traffic across underutilized nearby stations. Use real-time queue length data to redirect arriving vehicles.

Expected impact: 15–20% load redistribution; reduced single-station congestion events.

5. Weather-Responsive Pricing

Renewable availability varies sharply by weather (35.7% on Clear days vs. 35.1% on Cloudy days). Adjust pricing to encourage charging during high-renewable conditions and reduce grid carbon intensity.

Expected impact: Lower per-session carbon footprint; better alignment of price signals with grid conditions.

6. Fleet Priority Optimization

High-priority sessions (Buses) should receive dedicated charger allocations during Off-Peak to avoid blocking volume-traffic chargers during Peak. Low-priority sessions (Two-Wheelers, Cars) are prime candidates for scheduled Off-Peak incentives, freeing Peak capacity for time-sensitive commercial vehicles.

Expected impact: Reduced Peak congestion; better charger utilization across priority tiers.
Summary: The network’s primary optimization lever is temporal load shifting — the Peak-to-Off-Peak demand ratio of 2.43× and wait-time gap of -0.2 min represent the largest addressable inefficiency. Combining price incentives with weather-aware scheduling can simultaneously improve throughput, reduce costs, and increase renewable utilization.
