Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Actionable interventions synthesized across KPIs

The dataset indicates that Peak windows have: (1) much higher grid load (~84 kW vs ~35 kW), (2) lower renewable share (~0.29 vs ~0.39), and (3) substantially worse optimization reward (~-20 vs ~-7). Recommendations below target these drivers directly.

1) Load Shifting (Time-Slot Targeting)

  • Introduce explicit off-peak incentives that move flexible charging from Peak to Off-Peak (where renewable ratio is higher and grid load is lower).
  • Operational trigger: when station load patterns indicate Peak conditions (mean load ~84 kW segments), apply stepped discounts or credits for delayed start.
  • Success metric: increase Off-Peak session share (currently 5,222 of 8,354) while maintaining average wait time at or below ~9.5 minutes.

2) Queue Management at Bottleneck Stations

  • Prioritize targeted capacity or operational fixes at stations with highest mean waits: ST004, ST009, ST011, ST012, ST014, ST003.
  • Implement appointment/slotting during high-wait hours (e.g., Highway around 06:00; Urban around 11:00) to reduce arrival clustering.
  • Use queue-length thresholds to trigger “soft gating” (redirect to nearby sites / delayed start offers).

3) Pricing Strategy: Make Price Actually Responsive

  • Strengthen pricing coupling to congestion and renewables: current correlations with demand, wait time, and renewables are ~0, implying limited behavior-shaping.
  • Recommended rule: price = baseline + congestion premium − renewable credit, where congestion uses real-time queue length/wait estimates.
  • Validate: re-measure correlations post-rollout; target a measurable relationship between price and demand shifting (without harming throughput).

4) Renewable Maximization: “Green Windows”

  • Advertise and automate “green charging” windows aligned with higher renewable ratio (primarily Off-Peak in this dataset).
  • Offer opt-in delayed charging for drivers who can wait, coupled with a renewable-based discount/credit.
  • Track: renewable ratio uplift for sessions shifted into Off-Peak and the change in mean optimization reward.

Performance Signal: Optimization Reward

Because reward is materially worse during Peak across both location types, use it as an ongoing composite KPI to evaluate whether interventions are improving overall grid-and-cost outcomes. A practical goal is to narrow the Peak vs Off-Peak reward gap while keeping wait times stable.

Key Takeaways

  • Most leverage sits in time-slot management: move flexible load into Off-Peak for both grid relief and higher renewable share.
  • Address station-specific bottlenecks (not just system-wide averages) via targeted capacity and scheduling policies.
  • Strengthen pricing so it reflects renewables + congestion in a measurable way.

Data Integrity Notes & Limitations

  • No missing values were detected in the provided CSV columns (8,354 rows, 27 columns).
  • The dataset includes only two location_type values (Urban, Highway). The specification mentions Suburban; Suburban is not present in the data and is therefore not reported.
  • The dataset includes only two time_slot values (Off-Peak, Peak). Mid-Peak is not present in the data and is therefore not reported.
  • Weather conditions observed are: Clear, Cloudy, Rainy. Snowy is not present.
  • “Utilization” is inferred via throughput (session count), charging_demand, and station_load because charger counts/capacity per site are not provided.
