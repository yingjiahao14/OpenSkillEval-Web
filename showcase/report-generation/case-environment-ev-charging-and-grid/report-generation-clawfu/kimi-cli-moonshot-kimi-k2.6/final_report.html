🎯 Optimization Recommendations

Data-driven interventions to improve network efficiency, reduce costs, and maximize renewable integration.

1

Implement Time-of-Use Pricing with Renewable Bonuses

Current pricing shows virtually no correlation with renewable availability (r=-0.005). Introduce discounted rates during midday (11-16) when renewable supply peaks at 60.1%, and modest peak-hour premiums during 7-10 and 17-21. This could shift 15-20% of flexible demand to clean-energy windows.

Impact: High Effort: Medium Expected Reward Improvement: +25-30%
2

Deploy Predictive Load Balancing at High-Stress Stations

Stations ST012, ST004, and ST009 consistently underperform with rewards of -12.72, -12.56, and -12.31 respectively. These stations also show the longest wait times. Deploy dynamic charger allocation and pre-conditioning algorithms to pre-emptively redistribute load before queues form.

Impact: High Effort: High Expected Wait Reduction: -15-20%
3

Launch a "Green Charge" Midday Incentive Program

Midday off-peak offers the best optimization reward (-3.81 vs -21.06 evening peak) due to 60.1% renewable penetration and moderate 49.7% load. A loyalty program offering bonus credits for charging between 11:00-16:00 can attract fleet operators and shiftable residential demand.

Impact: Medium Effort: Low Expected Load Shift: 10-15%
4

Right-Size Charging Power by Vehicle Type

All vehicle types receive nearly identical power (~22.5 kW) despite different battery sizes and use cases. Implement adaptive power curves: higher power for buses during depot charging, moderate for cars, and optimized profiles for two-wheelers. This can increase throughput by 8-12% without new infrastructure.

Impact: Medium Effort: Medium Expected Throughput Gain: +8-12%
5

Integrate Weather-Aware Renewable Forecasting into Scheduling

Weather conditions show minimal direct impact on load, but clear-sky days correlate with higher renewable ratios (35.7% vs 35.1% cloudy). Integrate solar irradiance forecasts into the charging scheduler to proactively promote pre-charging during high-renewable forecast windows.

Impact: Medium Effort: Medium Expected Renewable Uptake: +5-8%
6

Decouple Queue Management from Load with Smart Throttling

The near-zero correlation between station load and queue length (r=-0.016) indicates queue management is not load-responsive. Implement AI-driven queue prediction that throttles arrival rates (via app notifications and dynamic pricing) when load exceeds 75%, preventing queue buildup before it occurs.

Impact: High Effort: High Expected Queue Reduction: -20-25%
