Actionable Recommendations

High Priority

Load Shifting to Off-Peak Windows

Peak-time station load (84.1 kW) exceeds off-peak (34.7 kW) by 142.6%. Implement time-of-use pricing incentives to shift 15–20% of peak demand to off-peak periods. This reduces grid stress during peak hours and improves the optimization reward metric, which averages -7.1 during off-peak vs -19.8 during peak.

Potential Impact: Reduce peak load by ~12.6 kW | Improve avg reward by targeting off-peak scheduling
High Priority

Queue Management at High-Traffic Stations

Top stations by volume show wait times up to 9.9 min. Deploy dynamic queue routing that redirects incoming vehicles to nearby stations with lower queue lengths. Add 1–2 charger units at high-wait stations. Implement reservation windows for commercial fleet vehicles to smooth arrival patterns.

Potential Impact: Reduce average wait times by 25–40% at bottleneck stations
Medium Priority

Renewable-Aligned Pricing Incentives

Renewable ratio peaks during Clear weather (35.7%) and Off-Peak periods (39.3%). Introduce dynamic discounts (5–10%) during high-renewable windows to encourage green charging. This simultaneously improves the sustainability KPI and leverages lower-cost renewable energy.

Potential Impact: Boost renewable utilization by 10–15% | Improve sustainability scorecard
Medium Priority

High-Capacity Vehicle Scheduling

Buses and trucks consume the most energy per session and have the longest charger dwell times. Create dedicated high-power charging bays for large vehicles and schedule their charging during off-peak hours to reduce queue buildup during high-traffic periods.

Potential Impact: Reduce queue formation by 15–20% during peak windows
Long-Term

Weather-Responsive Grid Management

Renewable energy availability varies significantly by weather condition (range: 35.1% – 35.7%). Deploy predictive models that integrate weather forecasts with charging demand predictions to proactively manage grid load and renewable utilization 24–48 hours ahead.

Potential Impact: Improve renewable energy ratio by 5–10% through proactive scheduling
Long-Term

Station Capacity Expansion Planning

Highway stations show the highest demand concentration. Prioritize capacity expansion in Highway areas, targeting stations in the top quartile by session volume. Consider adding battery storage at key stations to buffer renewable energy for peak-period dispatching.

Potential Impact: Support 835+ additional sessions with improved service levels

Synthesis

The data reveals a clear pattern: peak-period congestion is the primary operational challenge, driving higher wait times, elevated grid loads, and less favorable optimization outcomes. The most impactful interventions combine time-of-use pricing, dynamic queue routing, and renewable-aligned scheduling. These measures address multiple KPIs simultaneously — reducing wait times, balancing grid load, improving renewable utilization, and increasing the optimization reward metric across the network.
