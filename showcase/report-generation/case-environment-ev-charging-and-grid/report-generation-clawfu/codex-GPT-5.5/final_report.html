Executive Summary

Equip infrastructure managers and grid planners with data-driven insights into station utilization, queue bottlenecks, renewable energy integration, pricing impacts, and actionable recommendations to improve charging throughput, reduce wait times, and optimize grid load balancing.

What leadership should notice

  • Queue experience is the operational flashpoint: average waiting time is 9.5 min, with the highest observed location/time segment at 9.6 min.
  • Sustainability performance is measurable: network renewable energy ratio averages 35.4%, and the best weather/time combination reaches 39.7%.
  • Optimization reward is still negative on average: mean reward is -11.83, so pricing, load, and queue interventions should be tested against reward lift, not only utilization.
  • Segmentation matters: this dataset contains Urban, Highway locations and Off-Peak, Peak time slots, enabling consistent operational comparisons without inferred categories.

Data Integrity Notes

  • The requirements reference Suburban location records, but no such rows are present in data.csv; location comparisons therefore cover Urban, Highway only.
  • The requirements reference Mid-Peak time-slot records, but no such rows are present; time-slot comparisons cover Off-Peak, Peak only.
  • Weather records are limited to Clear, Cloudy, Rainy; absent categories such as Snowy are not inferred.
  • All KPIs, charts, correlations, and recommendations are calculated directly from data.csv; no external benchmarks or fabricated targets are used.
