Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Actionable interventions synthesized from cross-sectional analysis.

1. Load Shifting to Off-Peak Windows
Peak hours (84 kW avg load) show 2.4x grid stress vs Off-Peak (35 kW). Simultaneously, renewable energy drops from 39% to 29% during Peak. Recommendation: Implement time-based pricing incentives (15-20% discount) during Off-Peak hours (00:00-06:00) to shift 25% of Peak demand, reducing peak load by ~21 kW and increasing renewable utilization by 10 percentage points.
2. midday Congestion Management at Bottleneck Stations
ST004, ST009, and ST011 show 10-15% higher waiting times than network average. These stations handle concentrated demand during 11:00-13:00 windows. Recommendation: Deploy dynamic queue management at ST004 and ST009 with real-time arrival predictions. Consider temporary capacity augmentation (mobile charging units) during 11:00-14:00 peak windows.
3. Renewable Energy Maximization Strategy
Off-Peak hours achieve 39%+ renewable penetration vs 29% during Peak. Weather conditions show minimal variation (35-36% renewable ratio). Recommendation: Introduce "Green Charging" incentives during Off-Peak + Clear weather windows when renewable ratios exceed 40%. Market this as a premium sustainability option to attract ESG-conscious fleet operators.
4. Fleet-Specific Charging Protocols
Buses show 2% higher energy consumption and 2% longer waits vs Two-Wheelers. Given buses represent 33% of traffic, this creates disproportionate grid impact. Recommendation: Implement bus-dedicated charging windows (02:00-05:00) with reduced power rates to capture off-peak demand from high-capacity vehicles, improving both utilization balance and grid stability.
5. Optimization Reward Improvement
Current mean optimization reward is -11.83, indicating systemic suboptimal grid conditions. Peak hours average -19.8 vs -7.1 Off-Peak. Recommendation: Focus optimization efforts on Peak-hour demand shaping. The 12.7-point gap between Peak and Off-Peak rewards suggests significant headroom for improvement through demand response programs targeting the 15:00-20:00 window.
Priority Matrix: Focus immediate interventions on (1) Off-Peak load incentives given dual benefit of reduced grid stress + increased renewable utilization, (2) midday bottleneck management at ST004/ST009 to address visible customer experience pain points.
