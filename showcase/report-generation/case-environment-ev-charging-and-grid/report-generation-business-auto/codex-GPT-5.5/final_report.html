Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Synthesize operational, customer-experience, grid-load, sustainability, and pricing findings into specific interventions for infrastructure managers and grid planners.

1
Queue intervention: Prioritize queue controls in Highway / Off-Peak, the highest observed wait segment at 9.6 minutes, by reserving flexible chargers, adding real-time slot guidance, and routing overflow to lower-wait segments such as Urban / Peak (9.3 minutes).
2
Load shifting: Use load shifting for Tuesday Peak operations, where mean station load reaches 84.8 kW; target non-urgent sessions for delayed starts when queue length is moderate and priority is Low or Medium.
3
Renewable integration: Align price incentives with high-renewable windows: Clear / Off-Peak has the strongest renewable ratio at 39.7%, while Rainy / Peak is lowest at 28.8%.
4
Pricing adjustment: Tune dynamic prices by segment rather than globally: average price-to-demand correlation is -0.01, so pricing appears weakly coupled to demand in this data; use discounts where renewable ratio is high and surcharge only where queues and station load are both elevated.
5
Reward-based control: Use optimization reward as a control metric: the best location/time segment is Urban / Off-Peak (-6.9), while the weakest is Highway / Peak (-20.0).
Implementation guardrail: Because Suburban and Mid-Peak categories are absent from the supplied data, expansion or tariff decisions for those segments should not be inferred from this report. Collect or append those records before making segment-specific commitments.
