Optimization Insights & Recommendations
Actionable recommendations for load balancing, queue management, pricing strategy, and renewable energy maximization — grounded in the data findings above.
Overall Assessment: The network is in a chronic suboptimal state — 90.3% of sessions produce negative optimization rewards (median −11.83). The root causes are identifiable and addressable: a 2.43× peak/off-peak load imbalance, a non-functional dynamic pricing signal, an ineffective priority dispatch system, and no incentive structure to shift charging toward high-renewable Off-Peak windows.
⚡
Deploy Time-Differentiated Pricing
Critical Priority
Current pricing is effectively flat (Off-Peak: 9.97 vs Peak: 9.94 avg) despite a 2.43× difference in grid load. Introduce a pricing tier that charges 30–50% more during Peak sessions and correspondingly discounts Off-Peak sessions. The price band (5–15 units) already exists — activate it as a true signal rather than random variation.
Target: Shift ≥20% of Peak sessions to Off-Peak, reducing peak station load from 84.07 kW toward the Off-Peak baseline of 34.65 kW.
🌱
Renewable-Linked Incentive Program
Critical Priority
Off-Peak sessions deliver 39% renewable mix vs 29% at Peak — a 10pp premium already available at no infrastructure cost. Market Off-Peak charging as "green charging" with a price discount or carbon credit for fleet operators (Buses, Cars, Two-Wheelers arrive equally depleted). Target the 5,222 sessions already occurring Off-Peak to deepen this behavior.
Benchmark: Off-Peak opt-reward is −7.06 vs −19.80 at Peak — shifting sessions Off-Peak could nearly triple optimization score performance.
🔧
Fix the Priority Dispatch System
High Priority
High, Medium, and Low priority sessions wait nearly identically (9.51, 9.55, 9.51 min). The priority system is not functioning as a true dispatch differentiator. Implement preemptive queue insertion for High-priority sessions, ensuring they bypass standard queue logic. This unlocks premium service tiers for fleet contracts and emergency response vehicles.
Baseline: Current wait spread across priority tiers is <0.04 min — any functioning priority system would show ≥2–3 min differential.
🚌
Staggered Charging Schedules for Fleet Vehicles
High Priority
All vehicle types arrive at ~34–35% SoC and depart at ~98.5% — vehicles are dispatched when depleted. Work with Bus and fleet Car operators to implement staggered departure/return schedules so not all vehicles return simultaneously. Buses hold chargers 179 min on average; even a 20% desynchronization would reduce concurrent queue depth.
Impact: Avg queue length is 4.59 vehicles. Reducing by 1 vehicle (−22%) through staggering would meaningfully reduce wait times at high-throughput stations.
⚖️
Rebalance Load Across Stations
Medium Priority
ST012 (55.85 kW avg load) runs 9% heavier than ST011 (51.14 kW) within the top-10. Implement dynamic charger allocation that routes incoming vehicles to lower-load stations when load differential exceeds 15%. The balanced Highway/Urban split (50/50) suggests geographic coverage is already even — this is an intra-network load balancing opportunity.
Target: Bring all top-10 stations within ±5% of the 53.18 kW network average load.
📊
Adopt Optimization Reward as Management KPI
Medium Priority
The optimization reward metric (currently median −11.27) is a composite signal not yet being used operationally. Establish a target of moving median reward above −5.0 within 6 months by tracking it alongside the pricing and load-shifting interventions above. Off-Peak sessions already achieve −7.06 — the achievable near-term target is moving the overall median to match this.
Current gap: Off-Peak reward (−7.06) vs Peak reward (−19.80). Shifting 20% of Peak sessions Off-Peak would move the network median by ~2.5 points.
Synthesis: The two highest-leverage interventions are time-differentiated pricing and priority dispatch repair — both are software/policy changes requiring no new infrastructure. Renewable-linked incentives amplify the pricing intervention with a sustainability narrative. The underlying infrastructure is well-balanced (50/50 Urban/Highway, flat weekly demand) — the performance gap is behavioral and algorithmic, not physical. A coordinated 90-day implementation of Recommendations 1–3 could realistically move the network from 90.3% negative optimization reward sessions to below 70%.
