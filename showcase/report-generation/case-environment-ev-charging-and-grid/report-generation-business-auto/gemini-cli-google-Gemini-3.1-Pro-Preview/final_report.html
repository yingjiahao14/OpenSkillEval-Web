This section examines how charging demand is distributed across various stations and location types. It highlights high-demand nodes in our network.

2. Queue Length & Waiting Time Analysis

Customer experience is directly impacted by wait times. This heatmap visualizes average wait times across different time slots and location types.

Key Takeaway: Peak time slots at Urban and Highway locations represent the most severe bottlenecks. Implementing dynamic queuing or incentivizing off-peak charging is crucial here.

3. Energy Consumption & Grid Load Patterns

Understanding station load profiles across the week helps in anticipating grid stress and planning load balancing operations.

Key Takeaway: Peak loads consistently occur during specific days and time slots, indicating predictable patterns that can be managed through proactive load shifting and battery storage deployment.

4. Renewable Energy Integration & Dynamic Pricing

This section evaluates our sustainability metric (Renewable Energy Ratio) against weather conditions, and compares dynamic pricing with optimization rewards across time slots.

Key Takeaway: Renewable generation drops significantly during adverse weather (e.g., cloudy, snowy). Optimization rewards and electricity prices indicate that shifting demand to periods of high renewable output yields both economic and grid stability benefits.

5. Vehicle Charging Behavior & Session Profiles

Profiling charging sessions by vehicle type reveals how fleet composition affects energy consumption and charging duration.

Key Takeaway: Larger vehicles (Buses, Trucks) demand significantly more energy and time, representing "lumpy" load events. Fast chargers should be prioritized for these vehicle classes to reduce overall station congestion.

6. Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Based on the analysis above, we propose the following actionable interventions:

  • Load Shifting via Pricing: Implement steeper dynamic pricing during 'Peak' hours to shift demand toward 'Off-Peak' hours where waiting times are lower and renewable ratio is often higher.
  • Targeted Capacity Expansion: Deploy additional high-power (50kW+) chargers primarily at the top stations identified in the Utilization Analysis to alleviate the most severe queue bottlenecks.
  • Renewable-Linked Incentives: Offer charging discounts during 'Clear' weather periods when renewable energy ratios are highest, improving overall sustainability KPIs and capturing positive optimization rewards.
  • Vehicle-Specific Queueing: Introduce dedicated queuing lanes or reservation systems for high-capacity vehicles to prevent them from causing cascade delays for fast-charging standard cars.
