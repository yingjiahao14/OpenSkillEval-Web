6 Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Synthesized findings into actionable recommendations for load balancing, queue management, pricing strategy adjustments, and renewable energy maximization.

1. Implement Time-of-Use Pricing to Shift Load

Current pricing (~$9.96) is flat across time slots despite a 2.4× load differential. Introduce a 20–30% peak surcharge and a 15–20% off-peak discount to incentivize demand shifting. Off-peak windows currently handle 62.5% of sessions — pricing incentives could push this to 70%+, reducing peak station loads from 84 kW toward the 60–70 kW range.

High Impact • Load Balancing

2. Create “Green Charging” Windows

Renewable ratio is 39.3% during off-peak vs 29.0% during peak — and this is the strongest driver of optimization reward (r = 0.59). Introduce branded “Green Charging” periods with discounted rates during high-renewable off-peak windows. Promote via app notifications when renewable ratio exceeds 40%. This aligns cost savings with sustainability goals.

High Impact • Sustainability

3. Introduce Priority-Based Queue Management

Currently, priority assignment (High/Medium/Low) has no measurable effect on waiting times (9.51 min across all tiers). Implement actual priority queuing for public transit buses and high-priority commercial vehicles. Target: reduce bus wait times to <7 min while keeping overall average under 10 min. This requires software changes to the queue assignment algorithm.

Medium Impact • Queue Optimization

4. Address Peak Grid Stress at Capacity Ceiling

Peak station loads hit 100 kW maximum across all 20 stations — the grid ceiling. With demand growing, this leaves zero headroom. Options: (a) negotiate higher grid allocations at high-demand urban stations (ST012, ST003, ST007), (b) deploy on-site battery storage (200–400 kWh) to buffer peak loads, (c) implement smart charging that throttles low-priority sessions during grid stress events.

Critical • Grid Capacity

5. Optimize Charger Power Allocation

Four power tiers (7, 11, 22, 50 kW) are used in equal proportion regardless of vehicle type or urgency. Implement smart power assignment: direct 50 kW chargers to high-priority and time-sensitive sessions, reserve 7–11 kW for overnight/long-duration off-peak sessions. This reduces peak concurrency stress without adding hardware.

Medium Impact • Throughput

6. Weekend Queue Staffing & Capacity

Saturday shows the highest average queue length (4.81 vehicles) across the week. Consider deploying mobile charging units at high-traffic highway stations (ST013, ST002) on weekends, or enabling overflow routing to nearby lower-demand urban stations (ST007: lowest queue at 4.26). App-based wait time predictions can redistribute demand in real time.

Medium Impact • Customer Experience
Implementation Priority Matrix
Immediate (0–3 months): Deploy time-of-use pricing (#1) and activate priority queuing (#3) — these are software-only changes with the fastest ROI.
Near-term (3–6 months): Launch Green Charging program (#2) and smart power allocation (#5) — require app integration and charger firmware updates.
