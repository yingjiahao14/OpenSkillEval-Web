    1. Load Shifting to Off-Peak Windows

    The network sees 84.1 avg demand during Peak vs 34.6 during Off-Peak. Station load during Peak reaches 84.1 kW vs 34.6 kW Off-Peak. Recommendation: Implement time-of-use pricing incentives that shift at least 15% of Peak demand to Off-Peak / Mid-Peak windows. Target Highway locations where the Peak/Off-Peak spread is widest.

    2. Queue Management at High-Traffic Stations

    Highway stations during Off-Peak hours average 9.6 min wait. During "High" traffic conditions, queue lengths reach 4.5 vehicles with wait times of 9.4 min. Recommendation: Deploy dynamic queue routing — when queue_length exceeds 7, redirect arriving vehicles to nearby underutilized stations. Add fast-charger capacity at the top-5 bottlenecked stations.

    3. Green Charging Incentives During High-Renewable Periods

    Renewable energy ratio peaks at 39.3% during Off-Peak hours and under Clear conditions (35.7%). Recommendation: Offer discounted rates (5–10%) during high-renewable windows to maximize green charging. Urban locations are natural starting points given their 35.7% average renewable mix.

    4. Dynamic Pricing Calibration

    Electricity price spread between Peak (9.97) and Off-Peak (9.94) is 0.03 units. Price-reward correlation is r=0.005. Recommendation: Widen the Peak/Off-Peak price spread by 10–15% to strengthen the demand-shifting signal. Target: reduce Peak station load from 84.1 kW toward the network average of 53.18 kW.

    5. Fleet-Specific Charger Allocation

    Bus vehicles draw 39.4 kWh/session and occupy chargers for 180.0 min avg. Recommendation: Dedicate high-power chargers (50kW+) to high-energy-demand vehicle types, and route Two-Wheeler vehicles to lower-power (7–11kW) units to improve overall charger turnover.

    6. Day-of-Week Capacity Planning

    Grid load peaks on Friday at 53.4 kW (0% above mean). Recommendation: Pre-schedule maintenance for low-demand days (Thursday, 52.8 kW) and ensure full station availability on Friday.
