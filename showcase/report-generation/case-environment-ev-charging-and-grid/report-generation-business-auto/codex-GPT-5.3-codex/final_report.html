Station Utilization & Demand Analysis

Takeaway: Demand concentration is uneven across stations; top-demand sites should be prioritized for charger expansion and operational monitoring. Location-level demand differences should inform deployment strategy for future capacity.

Queue Length & Waiting Time Analysis

Takeaway: Waiting-time exposure is strongly tied to time slot and operational context. Queue mitigation should combine slot-level demand shaping and fast-turnaround charger allocation for high-delay segments.

Energy Consumption & Grid Load Patterns

Takeaway: Grid stress should be managed with slot-aware balancing policies and charger power orchestration. Correlation structure highlights where operational levers can most effectively reduce stress and waiting.

Renewable Energy Integration & Dynamic Pricing

Takeaway: Renewable penetration varies materially by weather and time slot. Dynamic pricing can be tuned to shift flexible charging into higher-renewable windows while reducing expensive peak-pressure periods.

Vehicle Charging Behavior & Session Profiles

Takeaway: Vehicle classes exhibit distinct session intensity and duration profiles. Segment-specific queue rules and targeted charger assignment can improve throughput without uniform infrastructure expansion.

Optimization Insights & Recommendations

Actionable Recommendations:
1) Implement time-slot demand shaping: offer stronger off-peak incentives where waiting and load are elevated, and cap peak-period discounts.
2) Deploy station-tiered capacity upgrades: prioritize highest-demand stations first, with modular charger additions and queue-threshold triggers.
3) Use renewable-aware pricing: publish green windows (high renewable ratio) and apply lower tariffs to shift deferrable sessions.
4) Introduce adaptive queue control: route high-wait vehicle segments to high-power chargers during congestion windows.
5) Track optimization reward as a management KPI: set weekly targets by location type and time slot; escalate interventions where reward remains persistently negative.
