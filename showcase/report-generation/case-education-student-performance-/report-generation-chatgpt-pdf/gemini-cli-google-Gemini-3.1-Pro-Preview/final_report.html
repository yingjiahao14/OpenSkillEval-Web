Key Findings and Policy Recommendations

  • Invest in technology and internet access for lower-income students, as the digital divide significantly impacts HSC outcomes.
  • Implement attendance monitoring programs; students with <75% attendance show marked decreases in performance.
  • Address regional disparities by targeting additional resources to underperforming districts.
  • Strengthen public school infrastructure to close the gap with private institutions in key districts.
  • Provide supplemental tutoring programs to substitute for private tuition among lower-income families.
