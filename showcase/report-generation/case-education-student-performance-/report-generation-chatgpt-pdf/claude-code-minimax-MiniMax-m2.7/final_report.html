Key Findings and Policy Recommendations

Evidence-based recommendations for educators, administrators, and policymakers

Summary of Key Findings

  • Socio-economic factors show minimal impact: Family income (r = -0.007), internet access (0.02 difference), and private tuition (0.01 difference) all show negligible correlation with HSC results.
  • Strong overall performance: 100% pass rate with 26.8% achieving Excellent (≥4.5) and 0% At-Risk (<3.0).
  • Marginal GPA progression: Students show a slight average improvement (+0.018) from Previous GPA to HSC.
  • Regional disparities exist but are modest: Top district (Barisal, 4.11) vs bottom (Rangpur, 3.91) represents ~0.2 GPA difference.
  • Gender parity: Female (4.02) and Male (4.00) students perform virtually identically.

Policy Recommendations

1. Reassess Resource Allocation

Given minimal correlation between socio-economic factors and outcomes, consider shifting focus from remedial interventions based on income to universal support mechanisms.

2. Target Underperforming Regions

Rajshahi and Barisal show higher performance; investigate successful practices there. Rangpur and Khulna show lower averages and may benefit from targeted support.

3. Investigate Study Effectiveness

The near-zero correlation between study hours and outcomes suggests that study quality, not quantity, may be the differentiating factor.

4. Leverage Gender Success

Gender parity is a success story. Document and share pedagogical approaches that work equally well for male and female students.
