6) Key Findings and Policy Recommendations

  • Target low-income and low-parent-education groups with scholarship, remedial tutoring, and school support packages.
  • Prioritize internet/device support in low-performing areas to mitigate digital-access disadvantage.
  • Implement attendance early-warning dashboards and district-level absentee intervention protocols.
  • Use district-differentiated budgeting based on observed GPA gaps and progression deltas.
  • Track the five KPI metrics quarterly for intervention monitoring and accountability.
