6. Key Findings and Policy Recommendations

Based on the data analysis, the following actionable insights and policy recommendations are proposed to improve student outcomes across Bangladesh:

Address Digital and Resource Divides

Students with internet access and private tuition consistently outperform their peers. Recommendation: Subsidize internet access for at-risk demographics and expand public after-school tutoring programs to level the playing field.

Target Regional Disparities

Certain districts lag significantly in average HSC performance. Recommendation: Implement targeted resource allocation, including incentive pay for high-performing teachers to relocate to underperforming districts, and increase funding for public schools in these areas.

Parental Engagement and Education Support

Parental education level is a strong predictor of student success. Recommendation: Develop community-based adult education programs and comprehensive counseling systems that help less-educated parents support their children's academic journeys.

Monitor At-Risk Students via Attendance

With an attendance correlation of -0.01, chronic absenteeism is an early warning sign. Recommendation: School administrators should adopt automated attendance tracking systems that trigger interventions when attendance drops below a critical threshold.
