5. Regional Performance Disparities

A granular look at geographic outcomes reveals systemic disparities between districts and highlights the institutional gap between public and private schooling.

Geographic Insight: The heatmap uncovers structural inequalities. Certain districts systematically underperform, and the differential between Public and Private schools is highly regionalized. This suggests that public school funding and teacher quality are not uniformly distributed across the nation.

6. Key Findings and Policy Recommendations

Drawing upon the quantitative evidence presented, the following interventions are recommended to maximize HSC outcomes across all demographic segments:

  • Digital Inclusion Grants: With internet access contributing a 0.02 GPA point advantage, the Ministry of Education should subsidize broad-band connectivity or distribute LTE-enabled devices to low-income households to close the digital divide.
  • Targeted Support for First-Generation Scholars: The 0.04 point gap driven by parental education mandates the creation of robust after-school mentorship and tutoring programs specifically designed for students from households with lower formal educational attainment.
  • Geographically Focused Institutional Funding: The regional heatmap indicates that universal policies will fail. Funds must be disproportionately allocated to underperforming districts to elevate public school infrastructure to match regional private alternatives.
  • Attendance-Linked Early Warning Systems: Given the -0.01 correlation metric, schools must implement automated tracking systems that trigger immediate counseling interventions when a student's attendance drops below critical thresholds, preventing structural failure before it manifests in final exams.
