Methodology and Limitations

  • Return basis: cumulative return and CAGR use the adjusted Close series: latest close divided by earliest close, annualized over calendar time.
  • Volatility basis: annualized volatility is the sample standard deviation of daily percentage returns multiplied by √252.
  • Drawdown basis: drawdowns use closing prices only; intraday highs/lows are not used for peak-to-trough risk.
  • Dividend basis: total dividends are the simple sum of recorded per-share dividend events; reinvestment is not assumed.
  • Volume caveat: early zero-volume records limit comparability of liquidity metrics before non-zero volume reporting begins.
