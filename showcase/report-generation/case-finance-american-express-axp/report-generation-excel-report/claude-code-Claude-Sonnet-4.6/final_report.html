1. Executive Overview

American Express (NYSE: AXP) has delivered exceptional long-term returns to buy-and-hold shareholders over its 50-year publicly traded history. From an adjusted closing price of $1.2242 on June 01, 1972, the stock reached $305.99 by March 11, 2026 — a cumulative capital appreciation that reflects AXP's transformation from a travel-and-entertainment card issuer into a global premium financial services brand.

The stock has navigated multiple severe market cycles — including the 1987 crash, the 2001 dot-com bust, the 2008 Global Financial Crisis (where AXP required Berkshire Hathaway's backing and TARP assistance), and the 2020 COVID-19 shock — and recovered to new highs each time. This resilience underpins its appeal as a long-duration equity holding.
