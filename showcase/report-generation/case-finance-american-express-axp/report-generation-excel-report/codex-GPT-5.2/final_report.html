Executive Overview

Over the full dataset window, AXP’s adjusted closing price increased from $1.22 (1972-06-01) to $305.99 (2026-03-11), a cumulative price return of 24894.9% and a long-run CAGR of 10.81%.

The stock’s risk profile is characterized by high equity volatility (annualized volatility 34.7%) and severe historical drawdowns. The worst peak-to-trough drawdown in the dataset was -83.9% from 2007-07-19 (close $49.30) to 2009-03-06 (close $7.93).

Total recorded dividends sum to $34.27 per share across the dataset, supporting an income component alongside capital appreciation.
