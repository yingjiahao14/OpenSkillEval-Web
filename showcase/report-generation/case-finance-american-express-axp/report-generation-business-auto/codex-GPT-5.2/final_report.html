Executive Overview

From 1972-06-01 through 2026-03-11, AXP’s adjusted closing price rose from 1.224 to 305.99, a cumulative price return of 24894.9% and a price CAGR of 10.81% over ~53.8 years.

The defining downside event in the dataset is the Global Financial Crisis drawdown: peak 2007-07-19 → trough 2009-03-06 with a maximum peak-to-trough decline of -83.9%.

Volatility is material: annualized volatility of daily returns over the full sample is 34.7%, with clear regime shifts during crisis periods.

Method Notes

  • All analytics are computed from benchmark/data.csv only. No external estimates are used.
  • Returns use Close price (adjusted series per dataset notes). Dividend reinvestment total return cannot be computed without a reinvestment index; we therefore report price-based return plus “Total Dividends Paid Per Share”.
  • Volatility uses trading-day annualization (√252). Sub-period CAGR uses a day-count year fraction (days/365.25).
