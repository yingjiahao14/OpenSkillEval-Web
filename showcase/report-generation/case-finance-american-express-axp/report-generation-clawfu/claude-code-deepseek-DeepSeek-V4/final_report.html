This equates to a compounded annual growth rate (CAGR) of +10.81%, significantly outpacing long-term inflation and delivering meaningful real wealth creation for multi-decade shareholders.

However, this journey was far from smooth. The stock endured a maximum drawdown of -83.91% during the 2007-07-19 to 2009-03-06 period (the 2008 Global Financial Crisis), and posted negative total returns during the 1970s (-40.25%) and the 2000s (-0.27%). Annualized volatility of 34.69% reflects the inherent risk in holding a financial services equity through multiple economic cycles.

Income-oriented investors benefited from $34.27 in cumulative dividends per share, distributed across 197 payment events. The dividend program strengthened markedly from the 2000s onward, with over 73% of all dividends paid since 2010. As of 2026-03-11, AXP trades at $305.99, approximately 79.7% of its all-time high of $384.04 reached on 2025-12-11.
