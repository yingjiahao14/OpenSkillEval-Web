Risk Observations

The 2008 Global Financial Crisis was by far the most damaging period, with AXP dropping -83.9% from peak to trough. The company faced existential challenges as charge-off rates spiked and capital markets froze. Recovery took until 2013.

The 2000-2002 dot-com bust also impacted AXP significantly, though it recovered faster than financials.

Positive: The 2020 COVID crash saw a swift -27.8% decline but V-shaped recovery by November 2020, demonstrating improved crisis management.

Overall, AXP's volatility is elevated vs. the S&P 500 (~20% historical), but has improved notably since 2010 with more stable earnings and better capital management.
