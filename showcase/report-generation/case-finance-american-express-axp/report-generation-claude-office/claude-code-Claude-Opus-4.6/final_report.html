Executive Overview

Over more than five decades of trading, American Express has transformed from a $1.22 adjusted share price in June 1972 to $305.99 in March 2026 — delivering a cumulative return of 24,895% and a compounded annual growth rate of 10.81%. The stock reached its all-time high of $384.04 on 2025-12-11 and its adjusted all-time low of $0.3761 on 1974-10-03.

AXP has navigated multiple economic cycles — from the oil crises of the 1970s, through the financial deregulation boom of the 1980s–90s, the devastating 2008 financial crisis (which inflicted a maximum drawdown of -83.9%), and the COVID-19 pandemic. Despite these challenges, the stock has consistently recovered to new highs, rewarding long-term holders with both capital appreciation and $34.27 in cumulative adjusted dividends per share.
