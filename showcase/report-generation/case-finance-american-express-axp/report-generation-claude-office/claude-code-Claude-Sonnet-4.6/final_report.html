Executive Overview

American Express has delivered exceptional long-term shareholder value over its 50+ year publicly traded history. From an adjusted closing price of $1.22 in June 1972 to $305.99 in March 2026, AXP has compounded at 10.81% annually — transforming a $10,000 investment into over $2.49 million. The journey spans six distinct market cycles, including the catastrophic 2008 Financial Crisis (-83.9% drawdown) and the rapid COVID-19 recovery. Throughout all periods, AXP has consistently rewarded patient, long-term shareholders through both capital appreciation and growing dividends.
