Regime Characterization

  • Low-vol regime (<13.5% ann. vol): Typical of trending bull markets — 2002–2004, 2005–2007, 2016–2017, 2022–2023. Favorable risk-adjusted entry windows.

Statistical Findings

  • January Effect (t=3.21): Strongest seasonal signal. Avg +3.0%/month with t-stat crossing 95% significance. Consistent across 22 of 26 years in the dataset.
  • August Effect (t=2.45): Second-strongest month. +2.0% average. Often driven by pre-monsoon demand in India and late-summer hedging flows.
  • June Weakness (t=-0.46): Only month with negative average return (-0.45%). Statistically weak — not a reliable short signal.
  • September (t=0.70): High variance month (σ=5.5%). Known for geopolitical shocks but no systematic directional edge.
  • Day-of-week: Monday (t=2.04) and Friday (t=2.83) show the strongest directional signals, with marginally positive mean returns. Effect size is economically small.

Moving Average Signal Analysis

  • Golden Cross (50MA > 200MA): Historically preceded extended bull runs: 2002–2007, 2019–2020, 2023–2026. Each cross marked trend continuation, not reversal.
  • Death Cross (50MA < 200MA): 2012–2013 and 2014–2015 bear phases confirmed by sustained crossovers. Trend-following investors avoided significant losses by respecting these signals.
  • Current regime (as of Mar 2026): Deep golden-cross territory with 50MA well above 200MA — momentum strongly bullish.

Volume–Price Dynamics

  • Volume data reliable only from 2004 onward. Avg volume grew from ~1,000 contracts/day (2004) to 5,000+ (2020+).
  • Volume spikes coincide with volatility regimes: GFC 2008, April 2013 crash, COVID 2020.
  • Recent (2024–2026) volume acceleration alongside price breakout is consistent with broad institutional demand — not retail-driven froth.
