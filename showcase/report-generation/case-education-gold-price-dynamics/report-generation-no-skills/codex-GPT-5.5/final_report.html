Key Takeaways & Forward-Looking Considerations

  • Strategic trend: Gold compounded at 11.51% annually over the dataset, but returns arrived through multi-year trend phases rather than stable carry.
  • Risk is regime-dependent: Full-sample annualized volatility is 17.41%, while crisis-vol regimes realize 28.3% volatility; allocation sizing should adapt to rolling volatility.
  • Drawdowns are material: The largest close-to-close drawdown was -44.36%, demonstrating that gold is not a drawdown-free hedge despite long-run appreciation.
  • Seasonality is weak: Monthly and weekday eta-squared values of 0.0012 and 0.0006 indicate minimal explanatory power; use calendar effects only as secondary context.
  • Tail risk matters: Excess kurtosis of 5.17 and 1% CVaR of -4.04% support stress-tested position limits and non-normal risk assumptions.
  • Current regime: As of 2025-12-31, the dataset ends with close $4,325.60, 63D annualized volatility 25.5%, and a classified regime of Crisis-vol.

Portfolio Construction Implications

  • Use rolling-volatility targeting or regime caps when gold shifts into high/crisis volatility.
  • Pair strategic gold exposure with trend filters when drawdown control is prioritized.
  • Evaluate gold hedging benefits conditional on macro regime; historical price strength does not eliminate clustered downside risk.
  • Treat volume signals as more reliable after 2003 due to documented early data-quality limitations.
