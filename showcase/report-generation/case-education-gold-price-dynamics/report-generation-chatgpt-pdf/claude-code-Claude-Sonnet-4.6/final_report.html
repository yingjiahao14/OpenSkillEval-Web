02 Volatility Analysis & Regime Detection
Three distinct volatility regimes are identified using a rolling 90-day annualized volatility measure, segmented at the 33rd and 67th percentiles (Low < 13.8%, High > 17.2%). Crisis episodes — 2008–2009 GFC, 2011 ATH reversal, 2020 COVID shock, and 2024–2026 macro stress — dominate the High-Vol regime.

03 Seasonal & Calendar-Based Patterns
Monthly seasonality is assessed using average daily log returns per calendar month, tested for statistical significance via a t-test against zero. January, February, and August show the most consistently positive average returns. Statistical significance (p < 0.10) is limited, consistent with gold being a macro-driven, not calendar-driven, asset.
