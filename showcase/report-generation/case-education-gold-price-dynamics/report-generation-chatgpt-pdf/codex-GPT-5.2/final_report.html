Key Takeaways & Forward-Looking Considerations

  • Full-sample CAGR is 11.51% with annualized volatility 17.41% (Sharpe-like 0.66).
  • Maximum drawdown over the sample is -44.36%; drawdowns are structurally tied to regime shifts and volatility clustering.
  • Volatility regimes are defined mechanically from rolling 20D annualized volatility quantiles; ‘Crisis’ corresponds to the top decile of 20D vol.
  • The most recent (end-of-2025) detected regime is: High.
  • Monthly and day-of-week seasonality tests show high p-values, suggesting weak evidence of persistent calendar edges in mean returns for this sample.
  • Return distribution is fat-tailed (high excess kurtosis) with modest negative skew, emphasizing tail-risk-aware sizing and stress testing.
  • Volume is sparse/zero in early years per dataset documentation; liquidity conclusions should weight later years more heavily.
