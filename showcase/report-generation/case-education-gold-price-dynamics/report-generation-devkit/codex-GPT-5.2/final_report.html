Key Takeaways & Forward-Looking Considerations

  • Volatility clusters meaningfully. Rolling-vol quantiles separate extended Low/Mid regimes from episodic High/Crisis bursts; regime overlays show clear persistence rather than i.i.d. noise.
  • Tail risk is material. Daily returns exhibit negative skew ({skew:.2f}) and high kurtosis ({kurt:.2f}); ~{frac_3sig*100:.2f}% of days exceed |μ±3σ| (normal would imply ~0.27%).
  • Seasonal effects are subtle. Best-vs-worst month spread is statistically detectable under permutation (p≈{month_perm['p']:.4f}) but economically small on a daily basis; treat as a weak prior, not a standalone signal.
  • Volume is weakly related to absolute returns (post-2004). Corr(|r|,Volume)≈{corr_post:.3f}; down days show higher average volume than up days ({mean_vol_down:,.0f} vs {mean_vol_up:,.0f}).
  • Simple trend-following is not a free lunch in-sample. MA(20/100) underperforms buy-&-hold on risk-adjusted metrics over the same window (Sharpe-like {sh_strat:.2f} vs {sh_bh:.2f}).
