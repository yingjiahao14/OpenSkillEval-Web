Momentum Dynamics & Liquidity Behavior

Trend persistence is assessed via moving averages and rate-of-change measures. Gold is in a 50D>200D state for 70.8% of valid observations. Volume–volatility linkage (|return| vs volume) is modest: full-sample correlation -0.021, post-2003 correlation -0.025 (more reliable volume regime).

Key Takeaways & Forward-Looking Considerations

  • Gold’s long-run return profile is positive, but path dependency is material due to deep drawdowns and clustered volatility.
  • Regime shifts are frequent; risk controls should condition position sizing on realized volatility state rather than static allocations.
  • Seasonal effects should be treated as probabilistic overlays; statistical test outcomes should gate deployment of calendar-based tilts.
  • Tail diagnostics and non-normality suggest stress testing with fat-tail assumptions and drawdown-aware risk budgets.
  • Volume information is more informative in post-2003 data; liquidity-sensitive signals should down-weight early-period volume observations.
