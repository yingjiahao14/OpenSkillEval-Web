6. Key Takeaways & Forward-Looking Considerations

Summary of Findings

  • Strong long-term compounding: Gold has delivered a 12.4% CAGR over 26 years, with a Sharpe-like ratio of 0.70 — respectable for a real asset with zero yield.
  • Asymmetric risk profile: The maximum drawdown of -44.4% underscores that gold is not a low-risk safe haven in all timeframes. Position sizing must account for the possibility of deep, prolonged drawdowns.
  • Volatility clustering is pronounced: Gold spends roughly 33.0% of time in a low-vol regime and 34.0% in a high-vol regime. Regime-aware risk management — widening stops in high-vol periods, tightening in low-vol — is essential.
  • Fat tails matter: Excess kurtosis of 7.0 means extreme moves occur far more frequently than a normal distribution would predict. VaR and CVaR are more appropriate risk metrics than standard deviation alone.
  • Seasonal effects are weak: Most monthly and day-of-week return patterns are not statistically significant at the 5% level. Any calendar-based trading edge in gold is marginal at best and should not be the foundation of a strategy.
  • Momentum has been effective: The SMA crossover strategy has historically provided downside protection during major bear phases, though at the cost of whipsaw losses in range-bound markets.
  • Volume confirms conviction: The volume-|return| correlation of -0.03 indicates that high-volume days carry more information — trend following is more reliable when confirmed by volume.

Implications for Portfolio Construction

  • Strategic allocation: Gold's low-to-moderate correlation with equities and positive skew during crisis periods make it a valuable diversifier, but the high volatility (17.7% annualized) argues for a measured allocation (typically 5–15% in a multi-asset portfolio).
  • Regime-aware sizing: Reduce position risk in high-vol regimes and increase in low-vol trending environments. The 63-day rolling vol provides a practical real-time regime indicator.
  • Tail risk hedging: The 99% CVaR of -4.3% daily implies that a 1% worst-case day is approximately 4.3%. Options strategies or stop-losses are warranted for risk-sensitive mandates.
  • Momentum as a filter: Rather than using moving average crossovers as standalone signals, use them as regime filters — be long gold only when the trend is confirmed, and reduce exposure during death-cross periods.
