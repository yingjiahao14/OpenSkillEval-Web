Key Takeaways & Forward-Looking Considerations

  1. Long-term positive drift: Gold has delivered 12.38% annualized returns over the analysis period, with a Sharpe-like ratio of 0.700. Positive risk-adjusted performance is a key rationale for strategic allocation.
  2. Volatility is regime-dependent: Crisis regimes (25.0% of days) are highly clustered in macro stress periods. Dynamic position sizing should account for current vol regime.
  3. Tail risk is real: The maximum drawdown of 44.4% and CVaR of 2.64% underscore that gold, while often a safe haven, experiences significant drawdowns during certain regimes.
  4. Seasonal edges are weak: Statistical tests show no strong monthly or day-of-week effects with high confidence. Any seasonal strategies would require careful backtesting before implementation.
  5. Volume is not a price driver: Volume shows limited correlation with price changes, confirming gold is primarily macro-driven rather than technically driven by flow.
  6. Current regime context: Recent price action above key moving averages suggests continued bullish momentum.
