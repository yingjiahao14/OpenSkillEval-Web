This dataset includes one Best Original Score winner per Academy Awards ceremony year from 1934 through 2026. The `Year` field is the ceremony year (not necessarily the film’s release year). The award’s name and rules have shifted over time; this report focuses strictly on the winners captured in `data.csv`.
