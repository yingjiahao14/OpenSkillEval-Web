    Era-Based Analysis of Winning Scores

    To preserve analytical consistency with the brief, eras are approximated from ceremony years:

    • Golden Age (1934–1959): studio-system orchestral tradition, recurrent late-romantic symphonic writing.
    • Silver Age (1960–1979): broader stylistic range, including jazz/pop influences and franchise leitmotif emergence.
    • Modern (1980–1999): increased blend of orchestral and hybrid textures, plus animated-musical resurgence.
    • Contemporary (2000–2026): hybrid production aesthetics, stronger global stylistic spread, and faster repeat cycles for select composers.
