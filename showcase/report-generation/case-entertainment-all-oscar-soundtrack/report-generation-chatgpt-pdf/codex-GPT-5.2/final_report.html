Dataset Overview & Historical Context

This analysis covers every Academy Award winner for Best Original Score in the provided dataset, spanning ceremony years 1934–2026 (93 rows). The dataset is deliberately compact—only Year, Film, and Composer—which makes it well-suited to studying dominance (repeat wins), diversity (how broad the winner pool is), and era shifts using time as the primary lens.
