Question Type Composition & Trends

Across the available years, MCQs and Long questions are completely stable year-to-year (15 MCQs and 3 Long per year). Short questions are the only component that changes, increasing from 9 (2015–2016) to 18 (2017–2019) and then settling at 15 (2021–2025).

Strategic Insights for Exam Preparation

  • Plan around a stable structure: In all recorded years, the paper includes exactly 15 MCQs and 3 Long questions; preparation can consistently allocate time to these formats.
  • Short questions drive most structural variation: The only year-to-year changes in question counts come from Short questions (9 → 18 → 15). Teachers should ensure coverage depth for short-answer prompts.
  • Prioritize recurring content anchors: High-frequency keywords suggest persistent emphasis on databases (database, table, key, DBMS, relational, access) and programming fundamentals (program, function, loop, statement, operator, printf, data types).
  • Balance theory and practical syntax knowledge: Frequent tokens like printf, int, float, bytes, and control/loop terms indicate that even theory papers repeatedly test language constructs.
  • Use keyword lists to build revision checklists: Convert the top keywords into a “must-revise” checklist, then map each to the corresponding chapter/topic outcomes for curriculum alignment.
Recommendations above are derived from observed frequency patterns in data.csv. If your local syllabus includes topics not appearing here, treat this report as complementary rather than exhaustive.

Conclusions & Limitations

Conclusion: Based on the dataset, the exam structure appears highly stable in MCQs and Long questions across all represented years, with variation concentrated in the number of Short questions. Topic signals repeatedly highlight database concepts and foundational programming constructs, suggesting these remain core assessment targets across the 2015–2025 window.

Limitations

  • The dataset has no entries for year(s): 2020. Trends for missing years cannot be inferred.
  • The dataset does not distinguish annual vs supplementary examinations; if both are present, they are merged in the same year bucket.
  • Within-question '$' delimiters separate options/sub-parts and were treated as word separators for text analysis.
