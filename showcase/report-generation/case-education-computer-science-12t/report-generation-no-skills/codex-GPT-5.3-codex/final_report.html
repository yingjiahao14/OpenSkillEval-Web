Strategic Insights for Exam Preparation

  • Highest total question volume appears in 2017 (36 questions), while the lowest appears in 2015 (27 questions).
  • Question type share variability (population std. dev.) suggests relative stability for: MCQs, Short, Long.
  • Keyword frequency shows recurring emphasis on programming fundamentals (loop, function, variable), database concepts (database, table, key, query), and algorithmic logic/representation.
  • Year-over-year changes are uneven, consistent with possible annual vs supplementary mix effects not separately encoded in the dataset.
  • Adopt a balanced prep design: regular MCQ drilling for recall speed, plus short/long-answer writing cycles for conceptual articulation.
  • Prioritize recurrent high-frequency themes in teaching plans: control structures, functions, operators/data types, and database schema/query concepts.

Conclusions & Limitations

The 2015–2025 data indicates a recurring overall exam pattern with visible annual fluctuation in total volume and type composition. This suggests partial structural stability with meaningful year-level shifts.

  • No missing years were found within 2015–2025 in `data.csv`.
  • The dataset does not separate annual and supplementary papers, which can influence year-level counts.
  • As a community-compiled source, minor transcription/format inconsistencies may exist.
  • Keyword analysis captures lexical recurrence, but not full semantic equivalence across differently worded questions.
