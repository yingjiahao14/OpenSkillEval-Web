Conclusions & Limitations

  • Stability vs shift: The dataset suggests a largely stable structure with one clear shift in Short-question volume beginning in 2017 (and a later adjustment in 2021). MCQs and Long questions remain constant in this dataset across all included years.
  • Topic recurrence: Database concepts and foundational programming constructs recur frequently and consistently, indicating they are reliable focus areas for curriculum alignment and exam preparation.
  • Missing year: 2020 is absent from data.csv; any claims about 2019→2021 changes should be interpreted cautiously because the time series has a gap.
