Format-Specific Strategies

MCQs (15 questions every year — 100% predictable count)

MCQs test factual recall and concept identification. The most tested MCQ categories are:

  • Operator identification (logical AND &&, OR ||, NOT !, modulus %, increment ++, etc.) — appears virtually every year
  • Format specifier matching (%d, %f, %c, %s, %lf, %ld) — appears every year
  • Database terminology (tuple, attribute, primary key, foreign key, DBMS) — appears every year
  • Data type sizes (int 2 bytes, float 4 bytes, char 1 byte, double 8 bytes) — appears most years
  • MS Access object identification (Table, Query, Form, Report) — appears most years

Short Questions (15 questions since 2021 — most variable section)

Short Questions test both recall and application. Expect:

  • 1–2 program-writing tasks (area calculations, number series, loops)
  • 1 output prediction question (trace a loop or conditional)
  • 1–2 definition/comparison questions (while vs. do-while, primary vs. foreign key)
  • 1 database concept (keys, relationships, DBA role)

Long Questions (3 questions every year — 100% predictable count)

Each Long Question contains 2–3 sub-parts spanning C programming and database topics. The pattern is remarkably consistent:

  • Question 3: Typically covers control structures/loops + a C concept + a database concept
  • Question 4: Typically covers a C program + operators/I/O/functions + a DBMS/RDBMS concept
  • Question 5: Typically covers functions/recursion + control structures + MS Access/normalization
Curriculum Planners Take Note: The 2024–2025 papers introduced several new advanced topics (pointers, structures, bitwise operators, ACID properties, ER diagrams, storage classes) that were entirely absent from 2015–2023 papers. This signals a potential curriculum revision that makes earlier past papers insufficient for full preparation. Students should be made aware that the exam is expanding in scope.

6. Conclusions & Limitations

Key Findings

  1. The exam has undergone one major structural shift — Short Questions doubled from 9 to 18 in 2017, then settled at 15 from 2021 onward. MCQs (15) and Long Questions (3) have never changed.
  2. Three structural eras exist: Era 1 (2015–2016, 27 questions), Era 2 (2017–2019, 36 questions), Era 3 (2021–2025, 33 questions). The current format has been stable for five consecutive years.
  3. C Programming and DBMS are the two dominant domains, with roughly equal representation. MS Access is a consistent but smaller component.
  4. Operators, loops, and database keys are the single most-tested concepts, appearing in every year without exception.
  5. Program output tracing and simple program writing are the most persistent Short Question themes, appearing in 8 of 10 years.
  6. Advanced topics are emerging: Pointers, structures, bitwise operators, ACID properties, ER diagrams, and normalization appeared only in 2022–2025, suggesting a curriculum evolution that educators must track.
  7. Some traditional topics are declining: Mathematical expression conversion to C, acronym full forms, and identifier validation have not appeared since 2019.

Data Limitations

  • Missing year 2020: No examination data is available for 2020, likely due to COVID-19 disruptions. This creates a gap in the trend line and prevents comparison of 2019 vs. 2020 vs. 2021 structural changes.
  • No annual/supplementary distinction: The dataset does not separate annual and supplementary examination papers. If some years include both, the question pool may be conflated.
  • Community-compiled data: This is not an official BIEK dataset. Minor transcription errors may exist, though the overall structure appears consistent.
  • Small sample size: With only 327 questions across 10 years, fine-grained sub-topic trend analysis has limited statistical power. Individual year anomalies should be interpreted cautiously.
  • Long Question sub-part counting: Some Long Questions contain 2 sub-parts (2025 Q5) while others contain 3. This report counts each Long Question as one unit regardless of sub-part count, matching the dataset structure.
  • Question text delimiter: The $ delimiter used for MCQ options and Long Question sub-parts was handled during analysis but may affect automated keyword extraction if not properly preprocessed.
  • Scope limited to Paper-II: This analysis covers only Computer Science Paper-II (theory). Paper-I and practical examination content are not included.
