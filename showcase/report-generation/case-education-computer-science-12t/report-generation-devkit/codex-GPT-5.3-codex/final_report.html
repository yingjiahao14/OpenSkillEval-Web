Strategic Insights for Exam Preparation

  • Allocate preparation by long-run weight: MCQs first, then Short, then Long practice depth.
  • Train topics in three modes (recall, short explanation, long-form solution) to match persistent mixed-format assessment.
  • Maintain recurring spiral revision on DBMS and C-programming fundamentals due to repeated appearance in question text.
  • Use yearly mock variations in question volume to reduce sensitivity to paper-length fluctuations.

Conclusions & Limitations

  • Exam structure is stable in format categories (MCQ/Short/Long persist), but not fully static in counts and yearly mix.
  • All figures are derived directly from data.csv; no fabricated values were used.
  • Keyword analysis is text-based and can be influenced by source spelling/encoding artifacts.
  • If annual/supplementary paper distinctions are not fully captured in source extraction, yearly totals represent available records only.
