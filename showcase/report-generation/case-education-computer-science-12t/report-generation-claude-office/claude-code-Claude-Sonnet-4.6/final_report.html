Exam Evolution Timeline
Key structural and content shifts from 2015 to 2025
2015 – 2016 • Baseline Period
Compact Format Established
Exam settled at 27 questions: 15 MCQs + 9 Short + 3 Long. Core curriculum focused on C programming fundamentals and MS-Access database. Short section had only 9 questions — relatively light testing load.
2017 – 2019 • Expansion Phase
Short Questions Double to 18 (+100%)
A major structural change: the Short question section expanded from 9 to 18 questions, raising total paper length to 36 questions. File handling topics introduced. Broader coverage of C programs — tracing, writing, and debugging. This was the peak-difficulty period in terms of volume.
2020 • Data Gap
Year Absent from Dataset
No 2020 data is present in the dataset. This likely reflects the COVID-19 pandemic disruption to board examinations across Pakistan, where many boards cancelled or deferred papers.
2021 – 2022 • Stabilisation
Short Questions Settle at 15
Post-pandemic, Short questions adjusted to 15 — a middle-ground between the 2015–16 low (9) and 2017–19 high (18). New topics appeared: arrays became a regular feature, and normalization was introduced in 2022. Paper total stabilised at 33 questions.
2023 – 2025 • Curriculum Modernisation
Advanced C Topics Enter the Paper
The 2024–2025 papers introduced pointers, structures and unions, storage classes, ACID properties, ER diagrams, and binary search — signalling a curriculum shift towards more advanced programming and database theory. File handling returned in 2025. Core topics remain unchanged but the difficulty ceiling has risen.

MCQ Section: Format and Data Types Are Predictable
All 15 MCQs per year follow consistent patterns: format specifiers (%d, %f, %c, %s), data types (int/float/char/double), library functions, operator precedence, and output prediction questions. Practice these for guaranteed marks.

Long Questions: 3 Questions, High Weight — Prepare Deeply
With 3 long questions every year at consistent difficulty, these carry the highest per-question marks. They typically test: writing complete C programs (with loops/functions), database schema design, and relationships/ER diagrams (recent years).
Complete programs Schema design ER diagrams (recent)
Emerging Topics
Pointers, Structures & Unions — New from 2024–2025
These advanced C topics appeared first in 2024 and returned in 2025. Given the two-year pattern, they are very likely to appear again. Students targeting 2026 exams should prepare pointer declaration, dereferencing, and struct member access.

Strategic Note
Short Questions Have Settled — Use 2021–2025 as Baseline
The 2017–2019 expansion (18 short questions) has been rolled back. From 2021 onwards, 15 short questions per year has been the steady-state. Students should prepare for 15 short questions and not over-prepare for the older 18-question format.

Conclusions
Key findings synthesised from the full 10-year dataset
1

Structural Stability With One Major Shift

MCQs (15/year) and Long Questions (3/year) have been perfectly stable for a decade. The only structural change was in Short Questions, which expanded from 9 (2015–16) to 18 (2017–19), then settled at 15 (2021–present).

2

C Programming and DBMS Are the Twin Pillars

Every single year, the paper is built around two domains: C language programming and database management. Together they account for the vast majority of marks across all three question types.

3

The Exam Is Becoming More Advanced

From 2022 onwards, new advanced topics entered: arrays, normalization, then pointers, structures, unions, storage classes, ACID properties, and ER diagrams in 2024–2025. The curriculum is clearly evolving towards deeper CS content.

4

2020 Data Is Absent — Likely COVID Related

The 327-question dataset spans 10 years but skips 2020 entirely. This is consistent with the widespread disruption to board examinations in Pakistan during the COVID-19 pandemic.

5

MCQ Patterns Are Highly Predictable

Format specifiers, data type sizes, operator precedence, and output prediction MCQs repeat with near-identical structure year after year. Practicing 5 years of MCQs gives very high coverage of the testable MCQ space.

6

Use 2021–2025 as the Primary Study Corpus

The current paper format (15+15+3 = 33 questions) has been consistent since 2021. For exam preparation, the five most recent papers best represent what future candidates will face — while 2015–2019 papers remain valuable for topic coverage.
