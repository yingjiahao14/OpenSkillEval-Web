Key Findings and Strategic Takeaways

  • White edge (decisive games): White wins 52.34% of decisive games (95% CI 51.63%–53.05%).
  • Rating gaps matter: The correlation between abs(Δrating) and higher-rated win indicator is 0.229; binned curves show win probability rising sharply past ~150–200 Elo.
  • How games end: Checkmates are 31.53% of all games; resignations dominate the outcome mix.
  • Time trouble signature: Among the most common time controls, 8+0 has the highest timeout share (17.86%).
  • Opening cautionary flag: In the top-15 openings by frequency (decisive games), black performs best vs white in A00 — Van't Kruijs Opening (white win rate 35.80%).
