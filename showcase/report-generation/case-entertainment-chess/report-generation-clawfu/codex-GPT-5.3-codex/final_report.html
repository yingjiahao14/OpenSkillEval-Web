Lichess Chess Game Analytics: Patterns of Victory, Openings, and Player Dynamics

Comprehensive analysis of 20,058 Lichess games from data.csv.

Key Findings

  • White vs. Black (centerpiece): White wins 52.34% of decisive games vs Black 47.66% (N=19,108); z=6.47, p=9.97e-11, indicating a statistically meaningful first-move edge.
  • Rating differential is highly predictive: Correlation between absolute rating gap and higher-rated player win probability is 0.225; win probability rises monotonically across gap bins.
  • Outcome mix: Checkmate rate is 31.53%, resignation 55.57%, timeout 8.38%, draw 4.52%.
  • Opening concentration: Top 15 openings account for 18.28% of all games; several popular lines show near parity while a subset slightly favors Black.
  • Time controls shift end mechanisms: Faster pools show more timeout endings; longer controls shift share toward resignation/checkmate with longer average games.

Method Notes & Limitations

  • All statistics derive directly from /app/benchmark/data.csv; no external enrichment used.
  • White/Black win-rate comparisons use decisive games unless stated otherwise.
  • Rating differential analysis uses absolute gap and excludes exactly equal-rating games for higher-rated-win probability.
  • Time-control class is inferred from increment_code base minutes: Bullet (<3), Blitz (3-9), Rapid (10-24), Classical (25+).
