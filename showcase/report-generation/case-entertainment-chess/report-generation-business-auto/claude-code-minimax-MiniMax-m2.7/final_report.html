Key Findings

White Advantage is Real but Moderate: Across all decisive games, White wins 52.34% of the time — a statistically significant 2.3 percentage point advantage over Black. However, this advantage varies considerably by rating level and time control.
Rating Differentials Drive Outcomes: The correlation between absolute rating difference and higher-rated player victories is 0.234, indicating that skill gaps systematically predict game outcomes. In games where the rating gap exceeds 300 points, the higher-rated player wins over 80% of decisive games.
Openings Show Limited Color Bias: Among the top 15 most-played openings, only a handful show meaningful deviation from the overall White win rate. Some openings (like the Sicilian Defense) actually favor Black slightly in decisive games.
Time Controls Affect How Games End: Bullet games are far more likely to end in timeouts (12.8% for Bullet vs 4.5% for Classical), while Classical games see the highest checkmate rates (30.0%).
