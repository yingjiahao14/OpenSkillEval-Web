Key Findings and Strategic Takeaways

  • White has a measurable edge: 52.3% White wins in decisive games (95% CI 51.6%–53.0%), i.e., 2.34 pp over 50%.
  • Rating gaps dominate outcomes: White’s expected score is 35.4% when White is −200 to −150 Elo, versus 67.8% when White is +150 to +200 Elo (draw-inclusive score).
  • Color advantage is not uniform: the rating×time heatmap ranges from near-even segments to 59.1% (e.g., 1800-1999 × Classical (>=30m)).
  • Openings show large empirical spread: within the top 15, several openings are sub-50% for White; the lowest is A00 Van't Kruijs Opening at 36.4% White score.
  • Time pressure shifts termination modes: timeouts are concentrated in faster controls; resignations still dominate across buckets.
