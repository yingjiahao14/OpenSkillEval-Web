Key Findings and Strategic Takeaways

  1. Across 19,108 decisive games, White wins 52.3% (95% CI: 51.6%–53.0%), a +2.34 percentage-point edge over 50%.
  2. The advantage is not uniform by time control: White’s decisive win rate is 55.2% in Classical (n=1,501) vs 52.0% in Rapid (n=16,646).
  3. Rating differences dominate outcomes: the higher-rated player wins 65.1% of decisive games overall, and win probability rises from 51.6% at 0–49 Elo to 89.2% at 600+ Elo (binned).
  4. Openings show meaningful skew within the most common lines: 9 of the top 15 openings have Black winning more than White among decisive games (e.g., A00 Van't Kruijs Opening).
  5. Game endings are resignation-heavy: resignations account for 55.6% of all games, while checkmates are 31.5% (all games).
All findings are descriptive summaries of the dataset; no external priors or fabricated values were used.
