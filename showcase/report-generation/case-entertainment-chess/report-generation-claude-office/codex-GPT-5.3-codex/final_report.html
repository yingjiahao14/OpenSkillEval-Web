6) Key Findings and Strategic Takeaways

  • White has a persistent first-move edge in decisive games (+4.68%).
  • Rating gap is highly informative: higher-rated win probability increases materially with Elo separation.
  • Openings are not neutral: top-volume openings differ in color performance, with 6 black-favorable openings in the top 15.
  • Time control changes result mechanics: quicker formats increase timeout-driven outcomes.
  • Operationally, prep should be conditioned jointly on expected rating gap, opening family, and time control.
