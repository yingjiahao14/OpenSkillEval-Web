Key Findings and Strategic Takeaways

  • White wins 52.34% of decisive games (n=19,108); estimated 95% CI 51.63%–53.05%.
  • Color edge is not constant: white win rate varies meaningfully by rating bracket and time control (see heatmap).
  • Rating gaps dominate outcomes: correlation between |ΔElo| and higher-rated win indicator is 0.230; higher-rated win probability rises sharply past ~200 Elo.
  • End conditions skew strongly to resignation: 11,147 resignations vs 6,325 mates; overall checkmate rate is 31.53%.
  • Among the 15 most-played openings, several show black outperforming white in decisive results (notably some unorthodox/irregular lines).

Methodology & Data Integrity Notes

  • All statistics are computed strictly from /app/benchmark/data.csv; no external enrichment.
  • Win-rate analyses typically exclude draws when explicitly labeled as ‘decisive’.
  • Time control buckets are derived from the base minutes of increment_code (e.g., 15+2 → 15 minutes).
  • Confidence interval for white win rate uses a normal approximation for a Bernoulli proportion (decisive games).
  • Small-sample segments (e.g., bullet, 2200+) can show noisy rates; interpret with caution.
