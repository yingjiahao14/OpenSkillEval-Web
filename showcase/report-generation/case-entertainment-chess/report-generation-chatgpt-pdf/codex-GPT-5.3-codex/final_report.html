Lichess Chess Game Analytics: Patterns of Victory, Openings, and Player Dynamics

Comprehensive analysis of 20,058 Lichess games with emphasis on first-move advantage, rating dynamics, opening outcomes, and time-control effects.

Key Findings and Strategic Takeaways

  • First-move advantage is real and statistically significant: white wins 52.34% of decisive games (black: 47.66%), a +2.34pp edge; chi-square p = 9.97e-11.
  • Rating Differential Impact is strong and monotonic in bins: correlation between absolute rating gap and higher-rated win indicator is 0.225, and higher-rated conversion rises with larger gaps.
  • Ending mechanics: checkmate rate is 31.53%, so most games end via resignation/timeout/other mechanisms rather than mate.
  • Openings matter in practice: top openings show distinct outcome profiles; black outperforms white in Van't Kruijs Opening, Sicilian Defense, Sicilian Defense: Bowdler Attack.
  • Time control shapes behavior: faster controls change both average turns and victory-status composition, especially timeout share.

Methods, Data Integrity, and Limits

  • Every metric and chart is computed directly from /app/benchmark/data.csv; no fabricated or externally sourced values.
  • White Win Rate KPI uses decisive games only (draws excluded), per specification.
  • Rating Differential Impact uses Pearson correlation between |white_rating-black_rating| and a binary higher-rated-win flag.
  • Opening section restricts scope to top-frequency openings to avoid unstable small-sample estimates.
  • Timestamp fields are documented but not needed for required KPI computations in this report.
