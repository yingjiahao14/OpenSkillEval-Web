Key Findings and Strategic Takeaways

  • White has a measurable first-move edge: white wins 52.34% of decisive games (95% CI: 51.63%–53.05%).
  • Rating gap drives predictability: higher-rated players win more often as absolute rating difference increases.
  • Opening choice matters at scale: among the most common openings, win-rate spread is material and some lines show black outperforming white.
  • Time controls shape endings: short controls show higher timeout pressure; longer controls shift share toward resignation/mate.
  • Game length varies structurally: outcome type and rating tier both influence average turns, supporting differentiated coaching by segment.
