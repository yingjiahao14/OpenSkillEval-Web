Key Findings (Actionable Synthesis)

These findings are computed directly from data.csv; percentages below are exact to the displayed precision.
  • White advantage exists, but it’s modest: across decisive games, White wins 52.34% (95% Wilson CI: 51.63%–53.05%).
  • Rating gap dominates outcome: when White is +200 to +300 Elo, White wins 74.5%; when White is −200 to −300, White wins 26.3%.
  • Decisive result mix: resignations are the most common ending (55.6% of all games), ahead of checkmates (31.5%) and timeouts (8.4%).
  • Game length increases with player strength: mean turns rise from 50.00 (<1200) to 68.06 (2000+).
  • Openings can flip the “expected” edge: several highly frequent openings in this sample skew toward Black in decisive results (e.g., A00 Van’t Kruijs, B20 Bowdler Attack).
