💡 Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

1. Prioritize Deadstock inventory — condition is a profitability gate
Deadstock guarantees above-retail clearance (100% rate) with a $247 average margin. VNDS is viable but introduces a ~17% loss-rate risk. Used inventory should be treated as a loss-leader or avoided unless sourced at deep discounts well below retail.
2. Favor GOAT and StockX for capital-efficient turnover
Both platforms turn inventory in ~7.5 days with margin-per-day figures ($10.89 on GOAT, $9.82 on StockX) that far exceed slower channels. If capital turnover is the constraint, these are the default outlets. Walk-in retail and Stadium Goods are better suited for rare, high-premium pairs where absolute margin justifies the 33-day hold.
3. Target Air Jordan 11 Retro and 4 Retro for absolute margin
These silhouettes lead in average margin and above-retail probability. The Air Jordan 1 Low, despite high volume, offers the thinnest margins and should only be sourced at or below retail with a clear path to quick liquidation.
4. Colorway diversification is lower priority than model selection
The spread between the best (Pine Green, $89.23) and worst (Black Cat, $69.34) colorway margins is only ~$20. Model selection creates a ~$40 spread. Resellers should prioritize silhouette over colorway when allocating sourcing capital.
5. Timing should follow release calendars, not seasons
Monthly margin variance is driven by specific drops rather than seasonal demand. There is no reliable “holiday bump” or “summer slump” in the data. Monitor Nike SNKRS and retailer restock schedules rather than calendar quarters.
6. Manage downside: 38.7% of transactions sell below retail
With a median margin of $35.27 but a mean of $82.06, the distribution is right-skewed. Resellers should size positions to survive the ~39% probability of a below-retail exit, especially in VNDS and Used tiers.
