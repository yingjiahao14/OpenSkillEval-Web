Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

  1. Channel strategy should balance yield and speed: pair high-margin channels with fast-turn outlets to optimize blended ROI and working-capital turnover.
  2. Condition discipline is a primary lever: allocate more budget to condition tiers with stronger premium rates and tighter inventory days; raise buy-price strictness for weaker tiers.
  3. Model/colorway selection should be margin-volume filtered: prioritize products that rank high on both average margin and transaction count.
  4. Use seasonality for timing: increase acquisition/listing intensity into months with strengthening margin and volume trajectories.
  5. Set channel-specific aging rules: deploy markdowns or channel migration when items exceed channel baseline days in inventory.
