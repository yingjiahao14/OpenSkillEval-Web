Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

The core strategic tension is not whether Air Jordan resale can be profitable in this dataset, but how much capital can be tied up to capture higher margins. Fast marketplace channels accelerate turnover; local and boutique channels tend to earn higher margins with materially longer hold times.

1
Prioritize Deadstock where acquisition cost permits. Deadstock produces $264.01 average margin and a 100.0% above-retail rate. VNDS is viable but should be bought with tighter entry-price discipline because average margin falls to $37.62.
2
Use GOAT and StockX for velocity-sensitive inventory. GOAT and StockX clear in roughly 7.5 days on average across the two channels, versus more than 32 days for the slower channels. This supports cash-cycle strategies and markdown-risk reduction.
3
Reserve slow channels for higher-margin targets. Walk-in Retail leads channel margin at $89.55, but the 33.3-day average hold means sellers should reserve it for pairs with enough expected spread to justify capital lockup.
4
Source around proven model-colorway pockets. The best observed combination with at least 50 transactions is Air Jordan 11 Retro / Pine Green, averaging $134.16 margin and a 69.2% premium rate.
5
Time listings around observed seasonal strength. May shows the highest calendar-month average margin at $95.22, while October is weakest at $60.36. Treat this as directional because the dataset is synthetic and transaction mix may vary by month.
