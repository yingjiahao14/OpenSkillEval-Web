Right-Skewed Margin Distribution

The mean profit margin ($82.06) is significantly higher than the median ($35.27), indicating a long right tail driven by Deadstock transactions at premium prices. Analysts should not treat the mean as "typical" — median better reflects the most likely outcome.

Minority Achieve True Premium

Only 61.3% of transactions clear above retail. Nearly 4-in-10 transactions result in a loss vs. MSRP, concentrated almost entirely in Used-condition inventory — a critical sourcing risk to quantify.

Fast-Moving Market

At 22.5 days average, inventory cycles are relatively rapid. However, channel selection has a 4× impact on hold time — GOAT and StockX average ~7.5 days vs. 33+ days on local channels.

Deadstock Is Categorically Different

Deadstock transactions average $264.01 margin — 7× VNDS and a $321.67 spread over Used. Every Deadstock unit sold at 2.38× MSRP. This is the only condition where 100% of transactions are profitable.

VNDS: Moderate Upside, Real Risk

VNDS clears above retail 83.3% of the time at +$37.62 avg margin. But 16.7% of VNDS units sell below retail — meaning sourcing quality inspection is non-optional at this tier.

Used Always Loses to Retail

Zero Used transactions clear above retail price. At –$57.66 avg margin (0.70× resale-to-retail), sourcing Used inventory is structurally unprofitable unless acquisition cost is substantially below MSRP.

Inventory Speed Is Similar Across Tiers

All three conditions sell in 21–23 days on average. Condition does not accelerate or slow inventory velocity — channel selection (Section 4) is the dominant driver of turnover speed.

GOAT is the dominant channel. It delivers near-best margin ($82.42) alongside the joint-fastest velocity (7.6 days avg). This is the only channel where high margin and rapid turnover co-exist. StockX matches on speed but trails on margin by $8.50/unit. Walk-in Retail leads on margin (+$7.13 over GOAT) but takes 4.4× longer to move inventory.

GOAT: The Optimal Default Channel

GOAT combines $82.42 avg margin with 7.6-day avg turnover. On a capital-efficiency basis (margin ÷ days held), GOAT outperforms every other channel. For high-velocity operations, GOAT should be the primary listing destination.

Walk-in Retail: Premium for Patient Capital

Walk-in Retail leads on avg margin at $89.55, but at 33.3-day avg hold. If carrying cost is low and product is in demand, Walk-in Retail extracts the highest per-unit return — at the cost of capital cycle time.

StockX: Speed Without the Margin

StockX matches GOAT on velocity (7.5 days) but trails on avg margin ($73.92 vs. $82.42) — a $8.50/unit penalty for the same turnover rate. Unless liquidity is critical, GOAT is preferred over StockX.

Local Channels Are Slow, Marginally Higher

Stadium Goods, kick hub 2026, and Walk-in Retail all average 32–33 day holds. Their marginal margin advantage over GOAT ($0–$7) rarely justifies the 4.4× capital cycle extension for active operators.

May Is the Peak Selling Month

May averages $95.22 margin — the highest of any calendar month, 57.8% above October's $60.36. Listing inventory to close in May maximizes per-unit return. Q1–Q2 (Mar–May) is the strongest sustained window.

October Is the Market Trough

October averages only $60.36 margin — the weakest month by a wide margin. Analysts should avoid major sells in October and use the period for sourcing instead, when prices are softer across the board.

2025 Showing Upward Momentum

Jan–Sep 2025 avg margin of $84.47 and a 63.1% premium rate both exceed full-year 2023 and 2024 values. The market is appreciating; early 2025 data suggests accelerating demand.

November–December Recovery Is Reliable

After the October dip, margins recover sharply in Nov ($87.99) and Dec ($84.61). Holiday demand provides a reliable secondary peak window — source in October, list in November.

Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Data-derived recommendations for resale market analysts and sourcing strategists — covering model selection, condition quality, channel routing, and timing strategy.

  • 1

    Prioritize Air Jordan 11 Retro and AJ4 Retro for Sourcing

    These two silhouettes average $99.84 and $95.76 margin respectively — the only models consistently clearing near or above +$100 per unit. AJ1 Low should be treated as volume filler at best, with its $50.52 margin rarely justifying the same sourcing effort as premium silhouettes.

  • 2

    Source Deadstock Only — or Deeply Discounted Below Retail for Used

    Condition is the single biggest driver of profitability. Deadstock is the only tier with a 100% above-retail success rate at $264.01 avg margin. VNDS is workable at $37.62 avg margin with 83.3% probability of profit. Used inventory (–$57.66 avg margin, 0% above-retail rate) should only be acquired at acquisition costs below $130–$135 to achieve any margin — which is at or below MSRP for most models.

  • 3

    Default to GOAT for Primary Listings; Use StockX as Overflow

    GOAT delivers the optimal margin-velocity combination: $82.42 avg margin at 7.6-day avg turnover. StockX matches velocity (7.5 days) but costs $8.50/unit in margin. For high-volume operators, that delta compounds to thousands per month. Walk-in Retail's $89.55 margin is the highest, but its 33.3-day avg hold makes it capital-inefficient for active operations — reserve it for high-demand Deadstock units where patience is tolerable.

  • 4

    Target Pine Green and Military Blue Colorways Within Any Silhouette

    Pine Green ($89.23 avg margin) and Military Blue ($87.55) lead all colorways by a meaningful gap. Black Cat ($69.34) should be deprioritized — it trails the top colorway by $19.89/unit. Sail's 64.2% above-retail rate makes it the most reliable colorway even if its avg margin ranks 4th — useful when minimizing variance matters more than maximizing expected value.

  • 5

    Time Sells for March–May and November; Avoid October

    May averages $95.22 margin — the strongest selling month. March ($89.90) and February ($85.55) form a strong Q1 window. November ($87.99) offers a reliable holiday peak. October is the market trough at $60.36 — 37% below the May peak. Use October as a sourcing window when resale prices are depressed, and rotate inventory out in Q1–Q2 or November.

  • 6

    Monitor 2025 Momentum — Market Appears to Be Appreciating

    Jan–Sep 2025 avg margin ($84.47) and premium rate (63.1%) both exceed full-year 2023 and 2024. If the trend holds through Q4 2025, it signals a structurally tightening supply/demand environment — an argument for holding larger Deadstock inventory positions and being less aggressive on price reductions.
