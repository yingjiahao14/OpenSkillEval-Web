6. Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Sourcing Strategy

Prioritize Deadstock inventory. With a 100% premium rate and $264.01 avg margin, Deadstock is the only condition tier with consistently positive economics. VNDS is viable (83.3% premium rate, $37.62 margin) but requires higher volume to match Deadstock returns. Avoid sourcing Used inventory for resale — every Used transaction in this dataset lost money.

Model Selection

Focus on Jordan 11 Retro and Jordan 4 Retro. These two models average $99.84 and $95.76 margins respectively. Top combos to target: AJ 11 Pine Green ($134.16), AJ 11 Military Blue ($126.06), AJ 4 Military Blue ($116.61). The Jordan 1 Low, despite high liquidity, yields only $50.52 per unit — half the margin of premium models.

Channel Strategy

GOAT is the optimal default channel. It matches brick-and-mortar margins ($82.42) at 4× the turnover speed (7.6 days vs. ~33 days). For a capital-constrained reseller, this velocity advantage compounds — the same capital can turn ~4 times more often on GOAT than on Walk-in Retail. Reserve Walk-in Retail for high-value Deadstock pairs where the $7/unit margin premium justifies the holding period.

Timing

Sell in March and May; source in August–October. Spring months (March: $89.90, May: $95.22) deliver peak margins, likely driven by new release cycles. The August–October trough ($60–$74) represents the best window for acquiring inventory at favorable prices ahead of the Q4 recovery.
