Core Strategic Tension

The central finding: margins and velocity are inversely related across channels. Traditional retail (Walk-in, Stadium Goods, kick hub) yields ~$20 more margin per transaction but requires 4x longer inventory holding. For resellers with limited capital, platform marketplaces (GOAT, StockX) enable higher annual return on capital through velocity — even at lower per-unit margins.
