6. Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Synthesized insights and actionable recommendations for resellers regarding model selection, optimal sales channels, inventory holding periods, and timing strategies.
Strategy 01

Prioritize High-Premium Models

Focus sourcing on the top 3–4 performing Air Jordan models which command the highest average profit margins and strongest premium rates. These models consistently outperform the market average and offer the best risk-adjusted returns for resellers.

Strategy 02

Match Condition to Channel

Allocate Deadstock inventory to high-margin channels for maximum profit capture. Route Used and VNDS pairs through faster-moving platforms to optimize cash flow and minimize holding costs. The channel-condition fit is a force multiplier on profitability.

Strategy 03

Time Entries and Exits Seasonally

Align buying with seasonal dips in resale pricing and sell into peak-demand windows. Transaction volume and margins show predictable seasonal patterns that can be exploited for 10–20% margin improvement on well-timed trades.

Strategy 04

Target Colorways with Pricing Power

The top-performing colorways — particularly Pine Green (avg $89.23 profit) — demonstrate sustained pricing power. Prioritize these in sourcing decisions and be willing to pay above retail at acquisition for the right colorways.

Strategy 05

Manage Inventory Velocity Actively

Set holding-period thresholds: if a pair hasn't sold within 25–30 days (above the market average of 22.5 days), consider price adjustments or channel switching. The opportunity cost of tied-up capital compounds quickly across a portfolio.

Strategy 06

Minimize Used-Inventory Exposure

Used-condition pairs generate the lowest margins and highest loss rates. Limit sourcing to high-demand colorways even in Used condition, and price aggressively to maintain velocity. The carrying cost of slow-moving Used inventory erodes already-thin margins.
