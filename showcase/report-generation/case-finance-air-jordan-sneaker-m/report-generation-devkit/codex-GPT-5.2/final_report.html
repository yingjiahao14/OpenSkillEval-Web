Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

What to source (product selection)

  • Highest average-margin model: Air Jordan 11 Retro at $99.84.
  • Highest average-margin colorway: Pine Green at $89.23.
  • Condition is the biggest lever: Deadstock avg margin $264.01 vs VNDS $37.62 vs Used $-57.66.

Where to sell (channel strategy)

  • Speed leaders: StockX and GOAT average ~7.5 days; 75% of their sales close within 11 days.
  • Margin leader (but slower): Walk-in Retail shows the highest avg margin but ~33 days average holding time.
  • Efficiency lens: Use Margin/Day to balance profit vs time; GOAT leads in this dataset.

Timing (seasonality + momentum)

  • Seasonality signal: Avg margins peak in month 5 and trough in month 10 (month-of-year view).
  • Recent-period softness: The dataset ends in 2025-09; the last months show lower avg margins than the 2023–2025 average (see monthly chart/table).

Limitations

  • Dataset is synthetic (per source brief); treat outputs as scenario guidance rather than ground truth.
  • Title indicates 2023–2026, but available transactions end on 2025-09-27.
  • No buy/acquisition cost, fees, shipping, or return rates are included; margins are retail-to-resale only.
