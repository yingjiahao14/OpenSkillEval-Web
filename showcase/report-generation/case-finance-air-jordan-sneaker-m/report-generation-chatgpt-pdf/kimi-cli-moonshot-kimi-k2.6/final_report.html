1. Market Overview & Transaction Summary

This report analyzes 5,000 transactions spanning 2023-01-01 to 2025-09-27. The dataset covers five Air Jordan silhouettes, nine colorways, three condition tiers, and five sales channels. Overall, the secondary market demonstrates strong pricing power: the average resale price of $273.23 sits well above the average retail price of $191.17, yielding a market-wide average profit margin of $82.06.

However, profitability is not uniform. The median profit margin is $35.27—substantially lower than the mean—indicating a right-skewed distribution driven by a subset of high-premium transactions. The standard deviation of $159.07 underscores significant dispersion. Of the 5,000 sales, 3,066 (61.3%) cleared above retail, while 1,934 (38.7%) sold at or below retail.

Key takeaway: While the majority of transactions (61.3%) are profitable, the long left tail of below-retail sales means sourcing discipline matters. Roughly 10% of transactions lose $72.54 or more relative to retail.

6. Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Findings

  • Condition dominates pricing: Deadstock items average $455.51 resale versus $132.13 for Used—a $323.38 gap. No Used transaction cleared above retail in this dataset.
  • Model hierarchy is clear: AJ11 and AJ4 lead margins; AJ1 Low lags. All models show positive average margins, but the spread is meaningful ($59.87 to $84.03).
  • Colorway concentration is moderate: Pine Green tops at $89.23 average margin, but the spread across colorways is narrower than across conditions, indicating condition is the bigger lever.
  • Channel bifurcation: StockX/GOAT deliver ~7.5-day velocity; Walk-in Retail/Stadium Goods deliver ~33-day holds with higher per-unit margins.
  • Market stability: Annual margins have held within a ~$4 band (2023–2025), suggesting predictable, if not explosive, market dynamics.

Recommendations

  • Prioritize Deadstock inventory. The 100% sell-above-retail rate and $323 premium over Used make condition the highest-ROI sourcing filter.
  • Target AJ11 and AJ4 silhouettes. These models combine high margins with strong transaction volume, reducing liquidity risk.
  • Use GOAT as the default channel. It offers the best balance of margin ($82.35) and velocity (7.6 days), optimizing cash turnover.
  • Reserve Walk-in Retail for rare/hype releases. The 33-day hold is justified only when per-unit margin is substantially above the $89.55 average.
  • Monitor release calendars, not seasons. The absence of a clear Q4 spike suggests timing strategies should focus on drop dates rather than holiday windows.
Bottom line: In the Air Jordan resale market, the winning formula is Deadstock AJ11/AJ4 via GOAT or StockX for velocity, with selective Walk-in Retail placement for ultra-premium pairs. Condition and channel choice matter more than colorway or seasonal timing.
