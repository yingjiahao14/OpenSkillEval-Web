Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

1. Source around condition economics

Prioritize Deadstock when acquisition cost preserves at least the observed $nan margin advantage over VNDS. Treat Used inventory as discount-driven: its average margin is -$57.66 and above-retail probability is 0.0%.

2. Balance channel margin and turnover

Route high-confidence premium inventory to Walk-in Retail when margin maximization is the goal. For faster cash conversion, benchmark against StockX at 7.5 days average inventory time.

3. Use margin-per-day for allocation

GOAT has the strongest observed inventory-efficiency score at $10.89/day. This metric is useful for capital-constrained sellers because it connects gross profit with holding period.

4. Concentrate model/colorway buys

Favor Air Jordan 11 Retro and high-margin colorways such as Pine Green. Deprioritize low-margin colorways like Black Cat unless buy prices are below retail enough to offset observed markdowns.

5. Time listings by seasonal margin

Use May as a benchmark for stronger historical margin and monitor lower-margin periods such as Oct for defensive pricing or selective buying.

6. Keep inventory thresholds explicit

The market average is 22.5 days in inventory. Items exceeding the channel-specific average should trigger markdown review, channel transfer, or bundling to protect capital velocity.
