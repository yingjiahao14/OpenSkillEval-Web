Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Actionable insights synthesizing model, colorway, condition, channel, and timing analysis into sourcing and selling strategies.

Finding 1

Air Jordan 11 Retro is the premier resale silhouette

With a $99.84 average profit, 63.4% premium rate, and 973 transactions, the AJ11 Retro delivers the best combination of margin and liquidity across the dataset. Its higher MSRP ($227) creates a larger absolute profit base. Pine Green, Military Blue, and Lost & Found colorways on this model in Deadstock condition routinely clear $500+ in resale.

Finding 2

Condition is the dominant profit lever — Deadstock is non-negotiable for premium returns

Deadstock pairs average $264.01 profit at 2.38x retail. VNDS drops to $37.62 (1.20x), and Used pairs lose $57.66 on average (0.70x). The Deadstock→VNDS transition destroys $226 in value — more than any other single factor. Sourcing strategy should prioritize Deadstock exclusively for investment-grade inventory.

Finding 3

Digital platforms (GOAT, StockX) win on velocity; physical retail wins on margin

GOAT and StockX clear inventory in ~7.5 days versus ~33 days for Walk-in Retail, Stadium Goods, and kick hub 2026 — a 4.3x turnover advantage. The per-unit margin sacrifice is modest ($7–$15). For any reseller holding more than a few pairs, velocity compounding outweighs per-unit margin: faster capital recycling drives higher annualized returns.

Finding 4

Colorway differentiation matters, but less than condition and model selection

The spread between top colorway (Pine Green, $89.23) and bottom (Black Cat, $69.34) is only $19.89 — far smaller than condition gaps. Colorway is a refinement lever, not a primary strategy. Sail offers the best risk-adjusted return with a 64.2% premium rate despite lower absolute margin.

Finding 5

Spring and early fall are seasonal profit peaks

May 2025 ($126.46 avg profit, 72.1% premium) and September 2024 ($106.92, 69.0%) represent seasonal highs. December 2023 (65.8% premium) confirms holiday demand. October 2024 was the trough ($52.39). A buy-in-August, sell-in-May strategy captures the spring surge; a buy-in-July, sell-in-September play captures back-to-school demand.

Finding 6

2025 shows margin improvement despite volume decline

Partial 2025 data (1,271 transactions through September) shows the highest premium rate at 63.1% and the highest average profit at $84.47 — up $3.63 from 2023 — despite lower transaction volume. This suggests a market that is becoming more selective but more profitable per transaction: fewer, better-curated listings may be the emerging dynamic.

Strategic Playbook: Source Deadstock AJ11 Retro and AJ4 Retro pairs in Pine Green, Military Blue, and Sail colorways. List on GOAT for optimal margin-velocity balance (~$275 Deadstock profit, 7.3-day turnover). Time acquisitions in late summer (August) and liquidations in spring (May) and early fall (September). For Used pairs, accept the ~$57 loss as cost of inventory cycling and liquidate on StockX for fastest turnaround (7.3 days). Avoid holding Used pairs on physical retail channels where 33-day waits compound the loss.
