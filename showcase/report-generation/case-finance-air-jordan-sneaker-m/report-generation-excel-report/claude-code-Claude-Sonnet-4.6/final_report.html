05 Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Synthesized insights into actionable recommendations for resellers across model selection, channel strategy, inventory management, and timing.

1 Prioritize Air Jordan 11 Retro & 4 Retro

The Air Jordan 11 Retro ($99.84 avg profit, 63.4% premium rate) and Air Jordan 4 Retro ($95.76, 1,044 transactions) consistently outperform. Allocate the majority of sourcing budget to these two silhouettes. Air Jordan 1 Low ($50.52) should be deprioritized unless acquired significantly below MSRP.

2 Deadstock-or-VNDS Sourcing Policy

Deadstock achieves 100% above-retail sell-through at a 2.38x multiple. Used inventory universally sells at a loss (avg -$57.66). A strict DS-or-VNDS policy eliminates the primary driver of negative-margin transactions. VNDS is viable at 83.3% hit rate but commands a meaningful risk discount vs. DS.

3 Balance Digital Speed vs. Physical Margins

For capital velocity: GOAT or StockX (~7.5-day avg turnover) maximizes inventory turns. For per-unit margin: Walk-in Retail yields $89.55 avg profit ($15.63 above StockX) but holds for 33 days. High-volume operators should default to digital; low-volume/high-margin operators may prefer physical channels.

4 Buy in Fall, Sell in Spring

March and September consistently peak across years. July–August 2024 were the weakest months (both ~$68). Sourcing in October–November and listing in February–March may capture the seasonal spread. May 2025 ($126.46 avg profit) reinforces spring demand strength.

5 Target Pine Green & Military Blue Colorways

Pine Green ($89.23 avg profit) and Military Blue ($87.55) lead all colorways by margin. Chicago ($85.60) and Sail ($84.44) also outperform. Black Cat ($69.34) trails the leader by nearly $20. When model–colorway pairing is available, prioritize these combinations for maximum margin capture.

6 Scale Allocation on 2025 Momentum

The market shows YoY margin growth: $80.84 (2023) → $81.61 (2024) → $84.47 (2025 YTD). Q2 2025 delivered $97.49 avg quarterly profit — the highest on record. This trajectory warrants increasing allocation to high-confidence models as market conditions continue improving.
