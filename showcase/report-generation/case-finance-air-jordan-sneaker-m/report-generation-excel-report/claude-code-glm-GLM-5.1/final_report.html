Actionable takeaways for sourcing, pricing, channel selection, and timing strategies.

$321.67

Deadstock outperforms Used by $321.67 per transaction on average. Prioritize Deadstock inventory acquisition — even at higher buy-in cost, the margin recovery is substantial. VNDS offers a middle ground at $37.62 avg profit.

Model Selection: Air Jordan 11 Retro

$99.84

Air Jordan 11 Retro delivers the highest average profit margin across all models at $99.84, with a 63.4% premium rate. Pair with top colorways (Pine Green, Military Blue) for maximum returns.

Channel Strategy: Margin vs. Velocity

$89.55 vs. 8d

Walk-in Retail maximizes margin ($89.55 avg profit) while StockX moves inventory fastest (8 avg days). For high-demand Deadstock, favor the high-margin channel. For slow-moving Used inventory, prioritize velocity to minimize holding costs.

Timing: Seasonal Sweet Spots

May, Mar

List during peak months (May, Mar) when margins are highest. Avoid listing in Oct, Aug when prices compress. Build inventory in low-price months to sell into seasonal peaks.

Colorway Concentration

Pine Green: $89.23

Pine Green is the top-performing colorway with $89.23 average margin. Focus sourcing on Pine Green, Military Blue, Chicago. Avoid heavy positions in Bred, Black Cat which average below or near breakeven.

Below-Retail Risk Management

38.7%

38.7% of all transactions sell below retail. This risk is concentrated in Used-condition inventory and lower-tier models. Set stop-loss thresholds: if a Used pair hasn't sold within 48 days, consider repricing or channel-switching to faster-turnover platforms.
