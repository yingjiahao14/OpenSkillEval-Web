Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

  • Prioritize condition discipline: Deadstock delivered $264.01 average profit and a 100.0% premium rate, while Used averaged $-57.66; sourcing standards should price in that condition gap upfront.
  • Match channel to capital strategy: Use GOAT or StockX when rapid liquidation is the priority; use Walk-in Retail selectively when maximizing per-unit margin is worth a longer hold.
  • Favor proven model/colorway demand: Air Jordan 11 Retro and Pine Green led their respective rankings, making them stronger sourcing candidates when purchase cost is near retail.
  • Watch timing windows: May showed the strongest calendar-month profitability at $95.22, while October was weakest at $60.36; buying and listing calendars should account for these observed seasonal differences.
  • Segment risk rather than average it: Market-wide average profit was $82.06, but Used-condition and slower-turning inventory profiles can erode cash efficiency even when headline averages are positive.

Bottom line: the best operating strategy in this dataset is not simply “sell where margins are highest.” It is to reserve slower high-margin channels for high-confidence inventory, while routing standard volume through faster platforms to protect turnover and cash recycling.
