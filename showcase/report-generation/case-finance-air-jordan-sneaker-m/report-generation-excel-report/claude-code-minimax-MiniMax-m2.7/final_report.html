6. Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

1. Model Selection Strategy

Air Jordan 3 Retro and Air Jordan 11 Retro lead in average profit margins. Prioritize these models for sourcing, especially in Deadstock or VNDS condition. Air Jordan 1 Low shows lower margins — consider for volume-based strategies rather than margin maximization.

2. Colorway Focus

"Pine Green" delivers the highest average margin at $89.23. Other high-performing colorways include Military Blue and Chicago. These should be primary targets for acquisition.

3. Condition Sourcing

Deadstock items yield 100.0% above-retail rates with margins averaging $264.01. While VNDS offers a balanced trade-off, Used items frequently sell below retail — avoid unless acquiring at significant discounts.

6. Risk Mitigation

With 38.7% of transactions selling below retail, strategic pricing and condition-aware sourcing are essential. Set floor prices based on condition-adjusted market data rather than emotion-driven pricing.
