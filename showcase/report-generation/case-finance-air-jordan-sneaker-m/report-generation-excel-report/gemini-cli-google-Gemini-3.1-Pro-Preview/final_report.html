6. Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

  • Sourcing Focus: Prioritize models and colorways like Air Jordan 11 Retro and Pine Green that consistently yield top-tier profit margins. Deadstock condition remains paramount for maximizing the Resale Premium Rate.
  • Channel Optimization: Allocate inventory based on the margin vs. velocity trade-off. Channels demonstrating lower Days in Inventory (e.g., StockX) are ideal for moving volume quickly, whereas platforms with higher margins (e.g., Walk-in Retail) should be reserved for high-value Deadstock items.
  • Condition Strategy: Given the steep drop in profitability for Used items (as seen in the Condition Impact analysis), sourcing strategies should heavily discount Used inventory unless acquiring at a significant markdown from retail.
  • Timing and Seasonality: Align buying and selling strategies with observed momentum shifts. High-volume periods identified in the temporal trends suggest optimal times to liquidate stagnant inventory, while margin peaks indicate the best windows to list premium items.
