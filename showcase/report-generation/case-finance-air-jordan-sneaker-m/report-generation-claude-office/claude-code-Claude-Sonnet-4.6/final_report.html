Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Data-driven sourcing, channel, timing, and condition strategies synthesized from the full 5,000-transaction dataset.

🎯

Model Selection Strategy

  • Prioritize Air Jordan 11 Retro and 4 Retro — they deliver the highest average margins ($99.84 and $95.76) while the 4 Retro adds volume insurance at 1,044 transactions.
  • Avoid Air Jordan 1 Low as a primary sourcing target — $50.52 avg margin with 1,020 transactions shows thin returns at scale.
  • Air Jordan 3 Retro and 1 High offer mid-tier margins (~$82) as portfolio diversification plays.
📦

Condition Sourcing Guidelines

  • Deadstock is the only condition guaranteed above retail — 100% above-retail rate at $264 avg margin. Source Deadstock exclusively when budget allows.
  • VNDS offers value-tier upside — $37.62 avg margin, 83% above-retail rate. Ideal when Deadstock is unavailable or premium-priced.
  • Avoid Used inventory — 0% above-retail rate with -$57.66 avg margin. Used sourcing only viable for bulk liquidation at deep discounts.
🏪

Channel Optimization

  • Use GOAT as the primary channel — best margin-velocity trade-off: $82.42 avg margin + 7.6 avg days. Maximizes capital turn.
  • Walk-in Retail and Stadium Goods for high-heat product where 33-day holds are acceptable — both clear $82–90 in margin.
  • StockX for rapid liquidation: 7.5 avg days but lowest avg margin ($73.92) among channels — best for time-sensitive exits.
📅

Timing & Seasonality

  • List in March, May, July, and November — these are the highest-margin months averaging $88–95+. Q1 spring demand and Q4 holiday season are prime windows.
  • Avoid listing in August–October — these months average $60–74 in margin, suggesting post-summer saturation and pre-holiday inventory buildup.
  • The market is in a confirmed uptrend (+4.5% YoY margin growth from 2023→2025) — a favorable macro environment for holding inventory into 2026.

Executive Summary

The Air Jordan resale market across 5,000 transactions (Jan 2023–Sep 2025) demonstrates a structurally profitable ecosystem with an average margin of $82.06 and a 61.3% above-retail transaction rate. The central strategic insight is that condition is the single most predictive variable: Deadstock delivers a 100% above-retail guarantee at $264 avg margin, while Used inventory uniformly sells below cost. Channel selection introduces a second-order tension: digital platforms (GOAT, StockX) move product 4× faster than physical retail at a modest ~8–16% margin discount — making GOAT the dominant choice for capital-efficient operators. Seasonal timing amplifies returns by 10–35%: Q1 spring and the November holiday window consistently outperform August–October. With the 1.427× average resale-to-retail multiplier trending upward year-over-year, the market fundamentals remain favorable for well-sourced Deadstock of high-demand silhouettes like the Air Jordan 11 Retro and 4 Retro.
