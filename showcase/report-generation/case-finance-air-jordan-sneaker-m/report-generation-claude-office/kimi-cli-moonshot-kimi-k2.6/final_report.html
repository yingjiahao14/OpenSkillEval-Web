Market Overview & Transaction Summary

This report analyzes 5,000 Air Jordan secondary-market transactions spanning 2023-01-01 to 2025-09-27. The dataset captures sales across multiple platforms, conditions, models, and colorways, providing a comprehensive view of resale dynamics.

Key Snapshot: The average retail price across all transactions is $191.17, while the average resale price is $273.23 — a mean markup of 1.427x. However, the median profit margin is only $35.27 with a standard deviation of $159.07, indicating a wide dispersion in outcomes. Notably, 38.7% of transactions (1,933 sales) cleared below retail, underscoring the risk profile of resale sourcing.

The profit margin distribution is bimodal: Deadstock items virtually always sell above retail, while Used items almost never do. VNDS sits in the middle with an ~83% premium rate. This condition-driven split is the single largest determinant of profitability in the dataset.

Avoid Used inventory unless sourcing at deep discounts; the average Used transaction loses $58 relative to retail.
  • Use GOAT and StockX for velocity-driven strategies. With ~7.5 days average inventory hold, these platforms maximize capital turnover. The margin differential versus slower channels is narrow enough that the compounded annual return favors the fast marketplaces for most inventory profiles.
