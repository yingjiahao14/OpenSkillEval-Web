6. Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Actionable recommendations for resellers based on the data-driven insights above.

1. Prioritize Deadstock Sourcing

Deadstock condition is the single biggest margin driver at $264.01 avg profit — 7× higher than VNDS and always profitable. Every sourcing dollar should target brand-new inventory.

  • 100% above-retail sell-through on Deadstock
  • VNDS is viable at $37.62 avg margin, 83.3% premium rate
  • Avoid Used entirely — 0% above-retail, avg loss of $57.66

2. Focus on High-Margin Models

Concentrate sourcing on Air Jordan 11 Retro ($99.84 avg margin) and Air Jordan 4 Retro ($95.76). Together they represent 40% of transactions and deliver 17–21% higher margins than the dataset average.

  • Top combo: AJ 11 Retro × Pine Green ($134.16 margin)
  • AJ 1 Low has lowest ROI despite high volume
  • Model selection matters ~2.5× more than colorway selection

3. Use GOAT for Capital Efficiency

GOAT delivers the optimal margin-velocity balance: $82.42 avg margin with 7.6-day turnover = $10.85 margin per day of inventory. StockX is equally fast but yields $8.55 less per transaction.

  • Fast channels: ~$10–11/day return on capital
  • Slow channels: ~$2.50–2.69/day despite higher per-unit margins
  • Use Walk-in Retail only for highest-premium Deadstock pairs

4. Time Purchases for H1 Sales

Q1 and Q2 show stronger margins ($85–$86) vs. Q3–Q4 ($77–$79). Acquire inventory in late Q4 when margins compress and sell through H1 when premiums are higher.

  • ~$8–9 margin advantage in H1 vs. H2
  • Watch for month-to-month volatility (Sep vs Oct 2024 swing: $54)
  • Year-over-year trend is mildly positive (+$3.63 from 2023 to 2025)

5. Manage Inventory Holding Period

The 22.5-day avg holding period masks a massive channel gap. On fast platforms, product moves in ~1 week. Capital locked in slow channels for 33+ days has significant opportunity cost.

  • List on GOAT/StockX first for fast liquidation
  • Reserve Stadium Goods/Walk-in for premium-priced DS pairs
  • Consider multi-channel listing to reduce time-on-market

6. Optimal Sourcing Portfolio

An ideal sourcing strategy based on the data:

  • Tier 1: AJ 11 Retro × Pine Green/Military Blue (DS) → GOAT
  • Tier 2: AJ 4 Retro × Military Blue/Lost & Found (DS) → GOAT or StockX
