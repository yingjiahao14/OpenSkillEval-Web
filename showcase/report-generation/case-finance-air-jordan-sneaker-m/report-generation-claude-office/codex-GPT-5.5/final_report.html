Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Prioritize condition discipline. Deadstock generated $264.01 average profit and a 100.0% above-retail rate. Used inventory averaged $-57.66, so sourcing used pairs requires exceptional entry-price discounts.
Use GOAT for fast profitable liquidity. GOAT averaged $82.42 profit with 7.6 days in inventory, a stronger speed-adjusted profile than StockX in this file.
Reserve slow channels for premium asks. Walk-in Retail led margin at $89.55 but took 33.3 days; use it when capital is less constrained or price realization matters more than turnover.
  • Model selection: Air Jordan 11 Retro and Air Jordan 4 Retro were the highest-margin silhouettes at $99.84 and $95.76, respectively.
  • Colorway sourcing: Pine Green, Military Blue, and Chicago led average colorway margins; Black Cat was the weakest colorway by average profit in this dataset.
  • Timing: May and March showed the highest pooled calendar-month margins ($95.22 and $89.90), while October was the lowest at $60.36.
  • Inventory policy: Treat channel choice as a capital allocation decision: fast marketplaces free cash in roughly a week, while slower local/consignment channels can add margin but tie up stock for about a month.
