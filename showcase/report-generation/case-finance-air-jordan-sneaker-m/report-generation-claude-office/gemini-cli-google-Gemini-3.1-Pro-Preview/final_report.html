6. Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

  • Sourcing Priority: Focus acquisition efforts on top-performing colorways like Pine Green, which yield the highest average margins.
  • Condition Constraints: Prioritize Deadstock and VNDS inventory. Used pairs pose a higher risk of negative margins and should be acquired only at significant discounts to retail.
  • Channel Optimization: Allocate inventory based on channel velocity and margin tradeoffs. High-margin platforms with slower velocity are better for highly sought-after pairs, whereas faster channels can be used for liquidating lower-margin inventory.
