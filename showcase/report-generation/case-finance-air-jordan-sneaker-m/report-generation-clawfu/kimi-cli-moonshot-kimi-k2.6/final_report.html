Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

1. Prioritize Deadstock, High-Demand Colorways
Deadstock condition delivers the largest and most reliable premiums. Within Deadstock, target colorways like Pine Green and models like Air Jordan 11 Retro that historically post above-average margins. Avoid generic Used inventory unless acquired at deep discounts.
2. Match Channel to Capital Strategy
Use Walk-in Retail when optimizing for per-unit margin and StockX when liquidity and turnover speed matter more. The 33.3-day average hold at Walk-in Retail ties up working capital — factor this into ROI calculations.
3. Time the Market Seasonally
Monthly margin seasonality suggests predictable demand windows. Build inventory in low-margin months and liquidate in high-margin months to maximize annualized returns. Monitor year-over-year shifts to detect structural market momentum changes.
4. Diversify Across Models, Not Just Colorways
Model-level dispersion is wide — Air Jordan 11 Retro averages $99.84 while Air Jordan 1 Low averages $50.52. Diversifying across silhouettes reduces concentration risk if a single model falls out of favor.
5. Monitor Premium Rate as a Market Health Signal
With only 61.32% of transactions currently profitable, the market is selective. A sustained drop in this rate would signal broad softening and warrant tighter inventory controls and lower acquisition prices.
