Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Actionable recommendations for resellers on model selection, channel strategy, condition sourcing, and timing.

1

Prioritize Deadstock Sourcing Above All Else

Deadstock is the only condition tier with a 100% premium rate, averaging $264.01 profit per pair vs. $37.62 for VNDS. The condition gap is the single largest driver of profitability in this dataset — larger than model, colorway, or channel selection. Allocate maximum sourcing budget toward Deadstock pairs.

2

Focus on Air Jordan 11 Retro & 4 Retro

These two models combine the highest margins ($99.84 and $95.76 respectively) with strong turnover. The 11 Retro × Pine Green ($134.16 avg profit) and 11 Retro × Military Blue ($126.06) are the top two model-colorway combinations. Deprioritize the 1 Low unless sourcing at discount.

3

Use GOAT as Your Primary Sales Channel

GOAT offers the best margin-to-velocity combination: $82.42 average profit with just 7.6 days in inventory. It also delivers the highest Deadstock margin ($274.73) among fast channels. StockX is comparable on speed (7.5 days) but yields ~$8.50 less per transaction.

4

Avoid Used Inventory as a Sourcing Strategy

Zero percent of Used transactions sell above retail. The −$57.66 average loss is structural across all channels (−$56 to −$60 range). Used inventory should only be acquired for personal inventory or when priced at 50% or more below retail to create a margin window.

5

Target Q1–Q2 for Selling, Q3–Q4 for Buying

Margins tend to peak in Q1–Q2 (2024-Q2: $87.49; 2025-Q2: $97.49) and compress in Q3–Q4 (2024-Q4: $73.38). While the seasonal effect is modest (~$14 swing), timing acquisitions into the softer Q3–Q4 market and selling into Q1–Q2 can improve per-unit returns by 10–15%.

6

Use Walk-in Retail for High-Value Consignment

Walk-in Retail yields the highest per-unit margin ($89.55) but with 33.3-day turnover. Reserve this channel for highest-margin SKUs (11 Retro, 4 Retro Deadstock) where the additional ~$7/pair over GOAT justifies the extra 26 days of capital lock-up. List velocity-sensitive inventory on GOAT/StockX.
