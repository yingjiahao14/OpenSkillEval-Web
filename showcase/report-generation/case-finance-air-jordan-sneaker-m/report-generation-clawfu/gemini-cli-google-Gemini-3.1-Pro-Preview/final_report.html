6. Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Based on the quantitative analysis of the resale market, we recommend the following strategies for sourcing and inventory management:

  • Prioritize sourcing Pine Green as it yields the highest profit margin of $89.23.
  • Conditions greatly affect margin. Deadstock shoes are heavily advised for strong returns.
  • Channels like StockX show optimal balance of quick turnover and high margin.
  • Channel Optimization: Balance high-margin channels with platforms that offer faster inventory turnover. Platforms that sit in the top-left quadrant of the Channel Performance chart are ideal for maximizing cash flow.
  • Condition Filtering: Used products carry significant risk of selling below retail. Sourcing efforts should heavily prioritize Deadstock items, or secure VNDS items at a strict discount to retail to protect margins.
  • Colorway Tracking: Top-tier colorways drive the vast majority of outsized returns. Continuously monitor trends in "hype" colorways to capture maximum upside.
