Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Prioritize Pine Green colorways where available: it leads colorways with $89.23 average profit and a 60.8% premium-sale rate.
Use StockX for velocity-sensitive inventory: it has the fastest average turn at 7.5 days, versus the market average of 22.5 days.
Use Walk-in Retail when margin is the primary objective: it produces the highest channel average profit at $89.55, with 33.3 days average inventory age.
Protect condition quality in sourcing: Deadstock clears $323.38 above Used on average, a $321.67 profit-margin gap.
Avoid treating high margin as sufficient on its own: compare channel profit against inventory age because capital recycles faster on channels with shorter days-in-inventory.
For timing, monitor months where both transaction volume and margin rise together; in this sample, the monthly peak-profit period is 2025-05.
