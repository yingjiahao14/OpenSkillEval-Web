Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Prioritize channel selection by strategy: If throughput is the constraint, focus on GOAT and StockX (≈7.5 days average). If maximizing dollars-per-pair is the constraint, Walk-in Retail leads in average profit ($89.55) but requires longer holding (33.29 days).
Condition is your primary sourcing lever: Deadstock averages $264.01 profit and clears above retail in 100% of transactions; Used averages -$57.66 profit and clears above retail in 0%. VNDS is a middle band (avg profit $37.62, premium rate 83.26%).
Target high-performing colorways with stable volume: The top colorway by average profit is Pine Green at $89.23 (566 transactions). Several other colorways cluster tightly behind (e.g., Military Blue at $87.55).
Timing signal (within this dataset): Average monthly profit is strongest in 2025-07 ($100.24) and then softens into 2025-08 ($63.90) and 2025-09 ($58.29). Treat 2025 results as partial-year coverage (through 2025-09-27).
