Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Synthesized action items for resale market analysts and sourcing strategists, grounded in the quantitative findings above.

🎯

Model Selection

Prioritize Air Jordan 11 Retro and AJ4 Retro for maximum margin efficiency. Both post 1.44x resale ratios and lead all silhouettes on per-unit profit.

  • AJ11 Retro: $99.84 avg margin
  • AJ4 Retro: $95.76 avg margin
  • Avoid AJ1 Low for margin; use for volume/cash flow
📦

Condition Sourcing

Deadstock is the only condition tier that guarantees above-retail outcomes (100% success rate). Never source Used at or near retail — it loses money without exception.

  • Target Deadstock at ≤MSRP acquisition
  • VNDS viable at 15–20% discount to MSRP
  • Used only at 40%+ discount to MSRP
🎯

Colorway Priority

Pine Green, Military Blue, and Chicago lead all colorways in average margin. Sail has the highest above-retail rate (64.2%), making it the most consistent performer.

  • Pine Green: $89.23 avg margin
  • Military Blue: $87.55 avg margin
  • Sail: 64.2% above-retail success rate
⚡

Channel Strategy

Use GOAT for premium Deadstock disposal — it achieves the highest DS margin ($274.73) while clearing in ~7.6 days. Reserve physical channels for VNDS where margin justifies the 33-day hold.

  • GOAT: best DS margin, 7.6-day clearance
  • Walk-in Retail: highest overall avg margin
  • StockX: fastest velocity (7.5 days), lowest margin
📅

Timing Strategy

Enter market in Q1 (Feb–Mar) and May for peak margins. Accelerate inventory clearance before October — the weakest month in the dataset at $60.36 avg margin.

  • Buy window: Oct–Nov (softening prices)
  • Sell window: Feb–Mar, May, Nov
  • Avoid holding into October
📊

Inventory Holding

Condition is a 20× stronger determinant of outcome than platform choice. Grade aggressively at acquisition — a Deadstock/Used misclassification is a $321.67 per-unit error.

  • DS → VNDS condition drift = -$226 avg
  • VNDS → Used condition drift = -$95 avg
  • Minimize hold time to preserve grade
