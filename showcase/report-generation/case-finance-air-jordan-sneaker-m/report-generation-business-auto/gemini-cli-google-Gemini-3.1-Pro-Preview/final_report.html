6. Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

  • Model Selection: Focus sourcing efforts on models showing the highest average profit margins, specifically prioritizing deadstock conditions for maximum yield.
  • Channel Strategy: Balance platforms based on inventory velocity and margin. The channel performance scatter plot illustrates platforms that turn inventory quickly versus those that yield higher margins for patient sellers.
  • Condition Thresholds: Used inventory primarily sells below retail. Limit sourcing of used models unless acquired at steep discounts, as their resale premium rate is significantly lower than Deadstock or VNDS items.
  • Timing: Align sales with identified seasonal peaks in volume and margin, holding inventory during low-margin months if cash flow permits.
