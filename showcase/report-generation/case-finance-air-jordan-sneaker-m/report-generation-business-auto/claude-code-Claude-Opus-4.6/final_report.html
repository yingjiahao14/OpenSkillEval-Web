Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Key Findings

  • Condition is the dominant profit driver. Deadstock items average $264.01 profit with a 100.0% profitability rate, while Used items average -$57.66 with only 0.0% profitable. VNDS sits between at $37.62 avg profit and 83.0% profitability.
  • Premium colorways outperform regardless of model. Pine Green leads all colorways with an average margin of $89.23. The top-performing model-colorway combinations are concentrated among hype-driven and limited-release colorways.
  • Channel selection creates meaningful margin differentiation. The spread between the highest and lowest-margin channels is $15.63, meaning channel selection alone can swing a transaction from profitable to loss-making.
  • Inventory velocity varies significantly across platforms. The fastest channels average ~8 days while the slowest reach ~33 days. This 2-3x spread in holding time affects capital efficiency and risk exposure.
  • The market shows overall positive economics. With a 61.32% premium rate and 1.43x average resale-to-retail ratio, the Air Jordan resale market remains profitable at scale, though individual transaction outcomes vary widely (std. dev: $159.07).

Strategic Recommendations

  • Prioritize Deadstock sourcing above all else. The $323.38 resale premium and 100pp profitability advantage over Used pairs makes Deadstock the only condition tier worth sourcing at scale. VNDS is acceptable for high-demand colorways only.
  • Focus sourcing on high-margin model-colorway combinations. Concentrate capital on the top-15 combinations identified in Section 2. Avoid bottom-10 combinations entirely — these consistently underperform regardless of channel or holding period.
  • Match channel to product and urgency. For high-value Deadstock pairs where margin is the priority, use higher-margin channels even at the cost of longer hold times. For VNDS or time-sensitive inventory, prioritize faster-turnover platforms to reduce capital lock-up and depreciation risk.
  • Implement seasonal timing strategies. Use the monthly seasonality patterns in Section 5 to time purchases (during lower-margin months) and sales (during peak-margin periods). Even small timing advantages compound across a portfolio.
  • Monitor the margin-velocity quadrant. Continuously track the scatter relationship between channel margin and days-in-inventory. Optimal portfolio allocation shifts inventory toward the upper-left quadrant (high margin, fast turnover) and away from the lower-right (low margin, slow turnover).
