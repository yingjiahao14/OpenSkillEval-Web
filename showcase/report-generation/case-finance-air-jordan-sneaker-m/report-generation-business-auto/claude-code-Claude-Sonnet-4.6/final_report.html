Profit Margin Distribution — All Transactions ($50 buckets)

Positive Margin Concentration

The median margin of $35.30 sits well below the mean of $82.06, indicating a right-skewed distribution. A minority of high-premium Deadstock transactions (avg $264 margin) substantially lift the mean, while the modal outcome for Used merchandise is a loss of ~$50–$100.

Below-Retail Exposure

38.7% of all transactions (1,933 of 5,000) cleared below the original MSRP. This concentration in used-condition inventory represents real capital risk: 847 transactions lost $50–$100 and 96 transactions lost more than $100, confirming that condition misjudgement at sourcing is the primary loss driver.

Price Range & Volatility

Resale prices ranged from extreme lows near $65 (Used Jordan 1 Low) to peaks above $750 (Deadstock Jordan 11 Retro). Retail prices were relatively tight at $110–$230. This asymmetry means condition and colorway selection, not retail cost, determine final P&L.

Market Scale & Coverage

The dataset spans 33 months (Jan 2023 – Sep 2025) across 5 silhouettes, 9 colorways, 3 condition tiers, and 5 sales channels — providing full-cycle coverage through market entry (2023), growth (2024), and continued expansion (2025). Transaction volumes are consistent, averaging ~153 records/month.

Condition Premium Gap — Avg Resale Price Comparison
Deadstock Premium: $226 Above VNDS

Deadstock items command an average resale price $225.67 higher than VNDS and $323.38 higher than Used. The condition premium gap is the single largest margin lever in this dataset — dwarfing model and colorway effects by a 3×–10× multiplier.

Used: Systemic Loss at Every Model

Zero Used transactions cleared above retail price. Every used-condition sale resulted in a loss relative to MSRP, ranging from -$37.18 (AJ1 Low) to -$70.90 (AJ11 Retro). Used inventory should only be sourced significantly below retail to generate positive margin.

VNDS: Moderate but Reliable

VNDS averages a positive $37.62 margin with an 83.3% above-retail hit rate — a defensible middle tier. The AJ11 Retro VNDS ($43.84) and AJ4 Retro VNDS ($42.70) offer the best VNDS margins if sourcing at less than full Deadstock prices.

Inventory Speed Parity Across Conditions

Days in inventory is remarkably consistent across conditions: Deadstock 23.0, Used 22.7, VNDS 21.7 days. Condition does not meaningfully affect sell-through speed — VNDS moves marginally faster but the gap is negligible. The decision calculus is purely margin-driven.

The Velocity–Margin Tradeoff

The data reveals a clear two-cluster split: StockX and GOAT clear in ~7.5 days (4.4× faster than offline channels), while Walk-in Retail, Stadium Goods, and kick hub 2026 average 32–33 days. The offline premium is worth ~$7–$16 per transaction — equivalent to 1–2 weeks of additional capital lockup cost.

GOAT: Best Risk-Adjusted Channel

GOAT achieves the highest premium rate (62.4%), the highest Deadstock margin among digital platforms ($274.73), and 7.6-day turnover. It sits in the optimal position on the margin-velocity frontier — strong throughput with above-average margin capture.

StockX: Volume Efficiency, Margin Discount

StockX is the fastest channel (7.5 days average) but delivers the lowest average margin ($73.92) and lowest Deadstock margin ($246.01 vs GOAT's $274.73). It works best for high-turnover, lower-premium inventory where capital velocity matters more than per-unit margin.

Offline Channels: Margin Held, Velocity Sacrificed

Walk-in Retail delivers the highest margin ($89.55) but the slowest velocity (33.3 days). With ~4.4× longer hold periods, the annualized return advantage shrinks considerably. Offline channels make sense only for highly illiquid, high-premium Deadstock where time sensitivity is low.

May 2025 recorded the highest monthly average margin in the dataset at $126.46, followed by July 2025 at $100.24. Q2 2025 averaged $97.49 — the best quarter across the entire analysis window, suggesting an accelerating market in early-mid 2025.

Gradual Upward Trend

Year-over-year margin has increased modestly: 2023 ($80.84) → 2024 ($81.61) → 2025 ($84.47, annualized pace). Premium rate also improved from 60.7% to 63.1% in 2025. The market is slowly but consistently expanding margin headroom.

Seasonal Pattern: Q4 Weakness, Q1/Q2 Strength

Q4 2024 (Oct–Dec) was the weakest quarter at $73.38 avg margin, while Q1 and Q2 consistently outperform. October appears to be a recurring soft month (2023: $68.90; 2024: $52.39). Strategically, holding inventory through October and listing in Q1/Q2 may capture 15–20% higher margins.

October: Persistent Dip

October is the weakest single month in both 2023 ($68.90) and 2024 ($52.39). This may reflect post-back-to-school demand saturation or platform-specific pricing dynamics. Sourcing in October may offer below-market acquisition opportunities — inverting the dip into a buying signal.

Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations Data-derived playbook for sourcing, channel selection, timing, and condition-based portfolio strategy

01

Prioritize Deadstock — It's Not Discretionary

Deadstock is the only condition tier with a 100% above-retail hit rate and $264 average margin. VNDS provides a reliable but reduced $37.62 margin. Used generates guaranteed losses at every price point. Condition at sourcing determines P&L outcome more than any other variable — source only DS unless acquiring Used at a minimum 30–40% discount to retail.

02

Lead with AJ11 Retro & AJ4 Retro

The AJ11 Retro ($99.84/unit avg margin) and AJ4 Retro ($95.76) generate the highest absolute margin per transaction. The AJ1 High offers the best resale-to-retail ratio (1.454×) for lower-capital deployments. The AJ1 Low should be treated as a high-volume, low-margin liquidity vehicle, not a margin vehicle.

03

Target Pine Green, Military Blue & Chicago

The top three colorways by avg margin are Pine Green ($89.23), Military Blue ($87.55), and Chicago ($85.60) — each outperforming the dataset mean by $3–$7. Black Cat trails at $69.34. When sourcing within the same model, colorway selection adds a measurable edge at portfolio scale.

04

Use GOAT as Primary Digital Channel

GOAT delivers the best combination of margin ($82.42) and speed (7.6 days) among digital platforms. It outperforms StockX on Deadstock margin by $28.72/transaction while matching its sell-through velocity. Reserve StockX for rapid liquidation of price-insensitive inventory where capital recovery speed outweighs per-unit margin.

05

Time Listings for Q1–Q2 Strength

Q2 2025 averaged $97.49/unit — the highest quarter in the dataset. Historically, March–June and August–September are the strongest months. October is the single weakest month (as low as $52.39 avg margin in Oct 2024). Avoid major liquidation events in October; use that period for sourcing instead.

06

Offline Only for Illiquid Premiums

Walk-in Retail and Stadium Goods deliver the highest raw margins ($89.55, $82.61) but hold inventory 4.4× longer than digital platforms. At 33-day average hold times, the annualized return advantage may not justify capital lockup. Route high-premium DS to offline only when digital platforms show thin market depth for the specific SKU.
