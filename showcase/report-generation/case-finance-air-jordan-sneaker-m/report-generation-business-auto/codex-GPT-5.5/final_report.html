Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Prioritize Deadstock sourcing when capital allows.Deadstock generated $264.01 average profit and a 100.0% above-retail rate. VNDS is a viable secondary tier at $37.62, while Used inventory should be priced as discount stock.
Separate margin and velocity channels.Use GOAT/StockX-like fast-turn channels when working-capital velocity matters; use higher-margin but slower channels selectively for inventory where holding cost is acceptable.
Source premium silhouettes first.Air Jordan 11 Retro and Air Jordan 4 Retro led model-level average profit, while Air Jordan 1 Low trailed the group at $50.52.
Screen colorways with empirical ranking.Pine Green, Military Blue, and Chicago led colorway margins; Black Cat had the lowest average margin and should require stricter buy-price discipline.
Time listings around observed momentum.May had the strongest pooled calendar-month margin ($95.22). October was weakest ($60.36), suggesting caution on aggressive buy prices ahead of slower seasonal windows.
Track turnover as an explicit KPI.The gap between fastest and slowest channel velocity was 25.8 days. Margin-only decisions can overstate channel attractiveness if inventory capital is constrained.
