Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

  • Prioritize condition discipline: Deadstock delivers $226.38 more margin than VNDS and $321.67 more than Used; Used sourcing should require deep discounts because average resale is below retail.
  • Segment channel strategy: Use GOAT for high-velocity exits where margin still matters ($82.42, 7.6 days); use Walk-in Retail when the strategy can absorb 33.3 days for higher margin.
  • Source strongest silhouettes first: Air Jordan 11 Retro and Air Jordan 4 Retro lead model economics at $99.84 and $95.76 average margin, respectively.
  • Target colorways with proven premium: Pine Green, Military Blue, and Chicago are the top three colorways by average margin; Black Cat is the lowest in this file.
  • Time inventory around stronger months cautiously: May shows the highest month-of-year average margin, but 2025 coverage ends in September and 2026 is absent, so use this as a historical signal rather than a forward forecast.
  • Manage downside at transaction level: Despite positive aggregate margins, only 61.3% of all transactions sell above retail; buying decisions should price in channel fees, storage costs, and the chance of below-retail clearing.
