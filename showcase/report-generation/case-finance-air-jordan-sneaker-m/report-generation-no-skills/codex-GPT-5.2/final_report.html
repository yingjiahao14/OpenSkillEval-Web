Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Channel strategy (margin ↔ velocity)
  • GOAT and StockX are the velocity leaders (~7.5 avg days) with similar avg margins (~$82.42 and $73.92).
  • Walk-in Retail shows the highest average margin ($89.55) but at ~33.29 avg days; use it when capital is less constrained or for higher-ticket pairs.
  • Capital efficiency: profit/day ranks the fast channels materially higher (e.g., GOAT ≈ $10.89/day, StockX ≈ $9.82/day) vs. slower channels (~$2.6–$2.8/day).
Sourcing strategy (condition + SKU focus)
  • Condition is the dominant profitability driver: Deadstock avg profit $264.01 vs VNDS $37.62 vs Used -$57.66.
  • Deadstock is “structurally above retail” here (100% above retail). Used is “structurally below retail” (0% above retail). Treat these as separate sourcing programs, not a single pool.
  • Top SKU clusters (avg profit): Air Jordan 11 Retro + Pine Green ($134.16), and Air Jordan 11 Retro + Military Blue ($126.06).
Timing strategy (seasonality)
  • Month-of-year avg profit peaks in May ($95.22) and remains strong in March ($89.90) and November ($87.99).
  • Avg profit trough in October ($60.36); consider more conservative buys or faster channels to reduce holding time.
Operating guidance (rules of thumb)
  • If you optimize for speed: prioritize GOAT/StockX and Deadstock/VNDS intake.
  • If you optimize for $/pair: Walk-in Retail edges margin, but plan for ~+25 extra holding days vs GOAT/StockX.
  • Used inventory is loss-making across every channel in this dataset; only acquire Used if basis is far below retail (not observable here), or if other value-add strategies exist.
