Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

  • Sourcing Focus: Prioritize Deadstock pairs of the highest-margin models and colorways (e.g., Pine Green). Avoid "Used" conditions unless sourcing costs are exceptionally low, as they generally clear below retail MSRP.
  • Channel Allocation: Leverage channels that exhibit both strong margins and rapid turnover. Use high-velocity platforms to liquidate lower-tier models quickly, mitigating capital lock-up, while reserving specialized or independent channels for premium items where patience yields higher returns.
  • Inventory Velocity: With an average hold time of 22.5 days, sellers must actively manage their capital cycle. If an item surpasses this threshold, consider aggressive repricing or migrating the listing to a higher-velocity channel to free up capital.
  • Market Timing: Analyze the temporal trends to identify peak selling seasons. Align major inventory acquisitions with historical dips and list during momentum upswings to maximize margin capture.
