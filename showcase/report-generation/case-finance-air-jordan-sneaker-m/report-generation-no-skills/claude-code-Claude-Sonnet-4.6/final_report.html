Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

🎯
Prioritize AJ11 Retro & AJ4 Retro for Sourcing
These two models generate $99.84 and $95.76 average margins respectively — roughly 2× the margin of AJ1 Low. They represent the highest-ROI sourcing targets while maintaining comparable inventory velocity to the rest of the lineup. AJ1 Low should only be sourced at aggressive discounts to retail.
📦
Condition is the Dominant Margin Driver
Deadstock inventory averages $264.01 profit margin — 7× the VNDS margin ($37.62) and the only condition that guarantees above-retail clearance (100% rate). Avoid sourcing Used inventory unless acquisition cost is at least 30% below retail to offset the −$57.66 average margin.
⚡
Use GOAT as the Default Channel
GOAT achieves 100% turnover under 30 days (avg 7.56 days) while maintaining near-top margins ($82.42). For capital-constrained sellers, this velocity advantage outweighs the $7.13 margin premium of Walk-in Retail. GOAT is the best risk-adjusted channel in this dataset.
🏪
Walk-in Retail for Premium Deadstock
Walk-in Retail commands the highest resale/retail ratio (1.47×) and top overall margin ($89.55), but 55.5% of inventory exceeds 30 days. Reserve this channel for highly liquid Deadstock product where patience on timing is feasible, or where buyer relationships support faster turnover.
📅
Time Acquisitions for Q1 Selling Windows
Q1 consistently delivers the highest or near-highest quarterly margins. Acquire inventory in Q4 (when margins are softest, particularly Oct–Nov at $52–$69 avg margin) to sell into the Q1 premium window. The 22.5-day average hold period means October buys can be listed by early November.
🎨
Colorway Selection: Pine Green & Military Blue
Pine Green ($89.23) and Military Blue ($87.55) lead colorway margins by ~$17 over Black Cat. However, since all colorways are positive-margin and the spread is modest relative to condition effects, colorway selection is a secondary optimization. Model and condition decisions have 5–10× more impact on margin outcomes.
