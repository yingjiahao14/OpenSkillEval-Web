6. Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Key Findings

  1. Condition is the dominant profit driver. Deadstock pairs command a 100.0% premium rate with avg margin of $264.01, compared to just 0.0% premium rate and $-57.66 for Used. The Deadstock premium is structural, not cyclical.
  2. Model selection creates a $49.31 margin swing. Air Jordan 11 Retro delivers the highest average margin at $99.84, while Air Jordan 1 Low averages $50.52. Sourcing strategy should tilt heavily toward the top-performing models.
  3. Channel choice is a margin × velocity tradeoff. Walk-in Retail delivers the highest margin ($89.55), while StockX turns over inventory fastest (7.5 days avg). Resellers must choose between maximizing per-unit profit and accelerating cash conversion.
  4. Deadstock + right channel = compounding advantage. Deadstock pairs on top-performing channels achieve both the highest margins and fastest turnover — the market's closest thing to a free lunch.
  5. Colorway matters within every model. Even top models have losing colorways, and even weaker models have winning ones. Granular colorway-level analysis is essential for sourcing decisions.

Strategic Recommendations
