Key Insights & Recommendations

Insights

  • Workout Engagement & Calories: Workout participation directly correlates with calorie burn, but a large portion of users record no specific workout. There is significant variation across genders in terms of steps and calories burned.
  • Stress and Sleep Interplay: Higher reported stress levels demonstrate a clear negative impact on average sleep duration. Conversely, a 'Happy' mood is associated with better sleep and higher activity levels.
  • Activity Level Impact on Stress: Stress levels vary based on activity intensity and mood, indicating that while physical activity is beneficial, its impact may be mediated by an individual's current mental state.
  • Seasonal and Day-of-Week Variation: Step counts exhibit a seasonal pattern (with variations corresponding to specific months) and differ depending on the day of the week, indicating differing routines during weekends vs. weekdays.
  • Year-over-Year Stability: The year-over-year participation and step metrics provide a baseline indicating structural stability but highlight periods where targeted engagement could increase output.

Recommendations for Product & Engagement

  • Personalized Seasonal Campaigns: Introduce targeted monthly challenges, particularly in lower-activity months, utilizing gamification to boost step counts when users naturally slow down.
  • Wellness Interventions: Integrate mental well-being prompts. For users showing high stress or poor mood, suggest low-intensity restorative workouts (like Yoga) or meditation to improve sleep quality.
  • Weekend Boosting Strategies: Push tailored notifications on Friday evenings or Saturday mornings to increase activity during times when step counts typically dip or alter pattern compared to the workweek.
  • Workout Categorization: Encourage users who default to 'None' to log micro-activities or shorter walks by making tracking more seamless, directly increasing the 'Workout Participation Rate' KPI.
