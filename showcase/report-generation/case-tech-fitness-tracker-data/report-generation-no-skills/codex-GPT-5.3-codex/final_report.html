Key Insights & Recommendations

Insight: Workout participation is 83.12% overall, with males at 83.56% and females at 82.68%.
Insight: Average daily steps are 10,574; by mood, Tired users are highest at 10,749 vs Happy at 10,358.
Insight: Yearly steps peak in 2016 (11,105) and are lowest in 2018 (10,003).
Insight: Monthly seasonality shows highest average steps in month 12 (11,113) and lowest in month 1 (10,176).
Insight: Steps and calories are strongly correlated (r=0.973); sleep and stress are near-uncorrelated (r=-0.008).
Recommendation: Build adaptive engagement nudges around periods with lower participation (e.g., yearly trough years and lower-participation months), prioritizing step-goal prompts and workout reminders tied to user mood segment baselines.
Recommendation: Use KPI segmentation dashboards that track Workout Participation Rate and Average Daily Steps by gender, workout type, and mood as default views for feature experiment evaluation.
Recommendation: Since steps strongly align with calories (r=0.973), use step-based progression systems as primary engagement mechanics; avoid relying on stress-sleep coupling signals alone (r=-0.008).
