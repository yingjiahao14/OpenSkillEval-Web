Top Findings

Workout participation is 83.1% across the cohort, meaning roughly 17% of tracked days involve no structured workout. This represents a large addressable engagement gap.
Step counts average 10,574/day, which is above the commonly cited 7,500-step health threshold. Segmentation by mood shows that users in a "Tired" mood average the highest steps (10,749).
Sleep averages 6.99 hours, with minimal gender variance. Sleep shows a -0.008 correlation with stress — a weak relationship.
The most popular workout is Gym, comprising 20.7% of all active days. Product features that optimize for gym tracking may yield the highest engagement.
Year-over-year step trends show a downward trajectory from 10,963 (2015) to 10,271 (2025). Workout participation moved from 84.4% to 85.6%.

Product & Engagement Recommendations

Target the non-workout majority
With 17% of days showing no workout, introduce lightweight activity prompts (e.g., 10-min guided walks) during typical low-activity windows. Even converting 5% of inactive days would meaningfully lift engagement KPIs.
Optimize for the dominant workout type
Gym is the most popular activity. Prioritize gym-specific features (route tracking, pace coaching, gear reminders) in the next product cycle.
Leverage mood-activity correlation for nudges
Users with "Neutral" mood show the highest workout participation (83.9%). Use mood check-ins as a trigger: when users log a positive mood, prompt them with a workout suggestion while motivation is high.
Seasonal campaign timing
Steps peak in Dec and dip in Jan. Launch re-engagement campaigns and challenges in the trough months to counteract seasonal drop-off. Front-load new feature releases before peak months to maximize adoption.
Stress-aware wellness features
Stress averages 5.45 and correlates -0.008 with sleep. Consider integrating stress-reduction content (breathing exercises, meditation prompts) that triggers when high stress + low sleep patterns are detected.
