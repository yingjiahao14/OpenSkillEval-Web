6) Key Insights & Recommendations

  • Segment KPI dashboards by gender, workout type, and mood because both steps and workout participation vary materially across these cohorts.
  • Prioritize adaptive workout prompts on days/users with low participation probability to improve Workout Participation Rate.
  • Deploy stress-aware sleep/recovery nudges because sleep decreases as stress rises in the stress-level segmentation.
  • Use monthly seasonal troughs for campaign timing and feature launches to stabilize engagement across the year.
  • Treat Average Daily Steps and Workout Participation Rate as primary product health KPIs in weekly experiment reviews.
