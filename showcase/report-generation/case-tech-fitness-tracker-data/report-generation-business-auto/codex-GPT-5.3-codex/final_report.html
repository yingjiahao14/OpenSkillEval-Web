Key Insights & Recommendations

  • Engagement baseline: Average daily steps are 10,574 and workout participation is 100.0%, providing clear baseline KPIs for feature experiments.
  • Segmented engagement opportunity: Highest step segment is mood Tired (10,749 steps). Design mood-adaptive nudges and messaging to lift lower-step mood segments.
  • Workout-type behavior: Running users show the highest average steps (10,721). Prioritize recommendations and challenges around top-performing workout modes.
  • Stress-sleep coupling: Sleep duration varies by stress level (see section), supporting stress-aware recovery features (sleep reminders, decompression prompts) when stress rises.
  • Temporal planning: Monthly and yearly charts show measurable fluctuations in steps and participation. Time major campaigns around stronger months and deploy retention interventions ahead of weaker periods.
  • Data limitation note: Socioeconomic, medical history, and device adherence variables are not present in data.csv; those effects cannot be quantified in this report.
