Strategic Recommendations

  • Prioritize sourcing plays around geographic dispersion in Samsung: its country-median spread is $24,978.50, the widest among the three brands.
  • Use Garmin as the baseline pricing anchor: it has the highest median listing price ($566.98).
  • Treat Samsung as the most promotion-sensitive inventory: its median is lowest ($530.00), implying tighter ceiling for resale pricing without differentiation.
  • Bake condition into pricing rules: Samsung shows the largest average New-over-Used premium at 18.2% (mean New $638.84 vs mean Used $540.34).
  • Operationalize power-seller partnerships carefully: the top 5% of sellers contribute 48.3% of listings, meaning access to a small cohort can move supply—but pricing dynamics differ at the seller level (see Power vs Non-power table).
