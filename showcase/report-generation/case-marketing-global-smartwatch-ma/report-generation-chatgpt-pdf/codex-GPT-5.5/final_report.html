For Apple, prioritize sourcing tests in Ireland where the median is -36.4% ($350) versus the global Apple median, and use Belgium as a price-ceiling market at +45.5% ($800).
For Garmin, prioritize sourcing tests in Israel where the median is -22.4% ($440) versus the global Garmin median, and use Australia as a price-ceiling market at +77.3% ($1,005).
For Samsung, prioritize sourcing tests in Germany where the median is -30.0% ($371) versus the global Samsung median, and use Estados Unidos as a price-ceiling market at +4682.9% ($25,350).
Use power sellers as premium-market signals: the top 5% of sellers control 48.5% of listings and price at a median +4.5% versus matched brand-country medians, above other sellers at -2.1%.
Do not ignore case size in pricing rules: listings with extracted sizes show a 0.021 price correlation and an all-market slope of $8.31 per millimeter, so larger-face SKUs warrant explicit price checks before markdowns.
Apply condition premiums by brand, not globally: the new-over-used average premium is highest for Samsung at 18.2%, compared with the brand-specific premiums shown in the pricing table.
Methodology & Guardrails
  • All figures are calculated directly from /app/benchmark/data.csv; no external market data or fabricated estimates are used.
  • Country arbitrage uses country-level median price by brand minus global median price for the same brand.
  • Power sellers are defined mechanically as the top 5% of sellers by listing count, matching the required concentration KPI.
  • Case-size analysis excludes 878 listings with missing Case_Size_mm; results should not be generalized to rows where sellers omitted dimensions.
  • Prices are listing prices in USD and do not include shipping, fees, taxes, or sell-through probability.
