Strategic Recommendations

Based on Q1 2026 data, the following strategic actions are recommended for secondary-market operators:

  1. Exploit Geographic Arbitrage: Target sourcing in regions identified with low median prices and reposition inventory to high-premium regions.
  2. Prioritize Premium/Large-Format Models: The strong positive correlation between case size and price indicates that securing inventory of 45mm+ models yields higher per-unit margins.
  3. Brand Focus for Value Retention: Allocate higher capital reserves for Garmin inventory due to its superior median listing price and stronger value retention compared to standard Apple or Samsung models.
  4. Monitor Power Seller Pricing: Use Power Seller medians as the baseline market clearing price. If Power Sellers are undercutting the market on specific SKUs, avoid over-investing in those exact conditions.
