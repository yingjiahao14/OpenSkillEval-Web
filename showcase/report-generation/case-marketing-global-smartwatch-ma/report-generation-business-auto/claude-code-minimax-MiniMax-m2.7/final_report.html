Source Garmin from Japan; Sell into UK

Japan Garmin listings median at $506 vs. UK at $775—a 53% arbitrage spread. Given Japan's high-volume Garmin presence (161 listings), establish sourcing relationships with Japanese sellers (Seller_0001 alone has 65 Garmin listings). Factor in shipping and duties, but the margin potential justifies the cross-border complexity.

Target Samsung from Japan and Hong Kong

Samsung shows a $388 spread between UK ($825) and Japan ($437). Japan and Hong Kong combined offer 23 Samsung listings at sub-$500 median prices. These markets are undersupplied in the secondary channel relative to demand in the UK and Australia.

📱

Focus Inventory on Garmin's 51mm+ Tier

Garmin's 51mm+ category (Fenix 7X Pro) commands a $750 median—35% above smaller models. This tier has the strongest size-premium relationship of any brand. Prioritize acquiring these large-format Garmin watches as they hold value better and attract buyers willing to pay for the premium specs.

⌚

Apple Watch Ultra 49mm is the Price Anchor

The 47-50mm Apple category (Ultra models) sells at a $590 median—22% above the 36-40mm category ($485). Apple's Ultra line retains value better than standard Series models. Stocking Apple Watch Ultra units provides a defensive position in volatile markets.

🤝

Partner with Power Sellers for Market Access

The top 5% of sellers (91 entities) control 48.4% of inventory AND charge 14.5% premiums—indicating buyers value their reliability. Rather than competing, consider wholesale partnerships with top-10 sellers like Seller_0042 (206 listings) or Seller_0033 (175 Garmin listings). Their worldwide shipping capability (47% offer it) solves cross-border logistics.

💰

Price Apple Competitively in the US; Premium in Australia

Apple pricing shows geographic convergence, but Australia commands a $242 premium over the US ($792 vs $550). For US sales, match the competitive median. For Australian-bound inventory, price with the premium already factored in. This applies even more for Garmin, where Australia's $1,005 median far exceeds the US $560.

Key Risk: Market Concentration
Three sellers account for 15.4% of listings. Any disruption from these sellers—whether policy changes, inventory depletion, or exit—would significantly impact market availability. Maintain relationships with at least 5-10 mid-tier sellers (10-50 listings) to diversify supply risk.
