Section 06

Strategic Recommendations

Each recommendation is directly grounded in a specific finding from the dataset.

Rec 01 — Sourcing
Source Apple in UK & Hong Kong; Sell into Australia

Apple median prices in the UK ($393) and Hong Kong ($442) are 29–20% below the global median. Australia's Apple median ($792) is 44% above. On a per-unit basis, the UK→Australia route offers a $399 gross spread before fees and shipping. At 27% worldwide shipping availability, cross-border fulfillment is viable for volume operators.

Evidence: Section 03 — Apple geographic spread = $399; UK median $393, Australia median $792
Rec 02 — Brand Focus
Prioritize Garmin for Margin Consistency

Garmin's standard deviation ($198) is 4× lower than Apple's ($834) and 11× lower than Samsung's ($2,286). Lower price variance means more predictable margin modeling. The brand also has the highest median ($567) with the tightest inter-quartile spread. Garmin inventory is less prone to catastrophic devaluation from outlier listings skewing comps.

Evidence: Section 02 — Garmin Std Dev $198 vs Apple $834 vs Samsung $2,286
Rec 03 — Pricing Strategy
Apply Condition Premium Only to Apple Listings

Apple "New" condition commands a +17.2% median premium over "Used" ($590 vs $503). Garmin shows −1.8% and Samsung only +1.4% — effectively zero condition sensitivity. For Apple, invest in grading, cleaning, and "New" or "Open Box" listing upgrades. For Garmin and Samsung, condition labor yields no return; prioritize speed-to-list over presentation upgrades.

Evidence: Section 02 — Apple New/Used premium +17.2%; Garmin −1.8%; Samsung +1.4%
Rec 04 — Garmin Arbitrage
Source Garmin from Japan; Sell into Canada or UK

Garmin median in Japan is $506 vs $890 in Canada (76% spread) and $775 in the UK (53% spread). With 47.5% of Garmin listings offering worldwide shipping, cross-border fulfillment infrastructure is already present in the market. This is the single highest absolute arbitrage spread in the dataset ($384 Japan→Canada).

Evidence: Section 03 — Garmin Japan $506, Canada $890, UK $775; 47.5% worldwide shipping
Rec 05 — Inventory Selection
Focus Garmin Buys on 47mm & 51mm Models

Among Garmin listings with case size data, 47mm median ($669) is $102 above brand median and 51mm ($764) is $197 above. Smaller models (41mm, 45mm) trade at or below median. Given Garmin's tight price distribution, model selection — specifically targeting larger expedition/multi-sport models — is the primary lever to capture above-median resale value.

Evidence: Section 04 — Garmin 51mm $764 vs 41mm $555; +$209 size premium
Rec 06 — Seller Partnerships
Avoid Top-5 Power Sellers for Sourcing; Target Seller_0142

Power sellers (top 5%) price 15% above low-volume sellers at the median. Sourcing from them erodes margin. The notable exception is Seller_0142 — a 31-listing Apple seller with a $349 median, 35% below market. This seller either specializes in lower-grade inventory or systematically underprices. Monitor their catalog for sourcing opportunities. Also note: 86.3% of sellers are single-listing casual sellers, who price closer to or below market median.

Evidence: Section 05 — Power seller median $599 vs low-vol $520; Seller_0142 median $349 (−35%)
