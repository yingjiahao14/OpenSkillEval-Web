6) Strategic Recommendations (Directly From Findings)

Sourcing & inventory allocation

  • Prioritize cross-border sourcing tests for Apple from the United Kingdom: median $394.99 vs Apple’s global median $550.00 (n=215), indicating a substantial price gap.
  • For Garmin, treat Japan as a lower-priced supply region (median $506.06, n=161) and United Kingdom as a premium demand region (median $775.00, n=140).
  • For Samsung, the United Kingdom commands a much higher median ($825.00, n=106) than the United States ($500.00, n=196), supporting region-aware pricing.

Pricing rules & catalog strategy

  • Use median-based brand anchors for pricing guardrails (Garmin $566.98, Apple $550.00, Samsung $530.00) and treat means cautiously due to outliers.
  • Apply a stronger condition premium only where supported: Apple’s median New premium is +17.2%; Garmin and Samsung show near-parity at the median.
  • Treat case size as a secondary feature: overall correlation is 0.021 (weak). Use size buckets mainly as a merchandising filter (e.g., Apple/Garmin ≥47mm higher medians).

Seller partnerships & marketplace operations

  • Because the top 5% of sellers control 48.3% of listings, build a “power seller” program (preferred intake, negotiated fees, bulk buyback) to secure supply.
  • Do not assume power sellers are discounting: their median prices exceed the long tail by +$61.99 to +$120.82 depending on brand. Treat them as premium supply sources and benchmark their mix and pricing against your target margin.
  • Use seller-level brand specialization (e.g., Garmin-heavy sellers) to staff category managers and tailor acquisition playbooks.

Implementation note: For live deployment, replicate this analysis weekly with fresh listings and enforce minimum sample thresholds (n) before making regional sourcing decisions.

Appendix: Data Handling & Definitions

All figures are derived from data.csv. This report restricts the analysis to Apple, Garmin, and Samsung (n=3,586) because the brief targets those brands; the raw dataset includes 21 additional listings across other brands.

  • Country normalization: A small number of Spanish/Korean country labels were mapped to English equivalents for aggregation (e.g., “Estados Unidos” → “United States”).
  • Power sellers: Defined as the top 5% of sellers by listing volume (top 88 sellers out of 1,750).
  • Case size bins: <=38mm, 39–42mm, 43–46mm, >=47mm. Rows with missing Case_Size_mm are excluded from size analysis.
  • Outliers: Extremely high prices exist (e.g., bundle listings). Medians and percentiles are used to reduce distortion.
