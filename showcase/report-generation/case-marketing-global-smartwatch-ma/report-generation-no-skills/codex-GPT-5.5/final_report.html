Strategic Recommendations

  • Use brand-specific acquisition ceilings. Set initial buy ceilings around core-brand medians: Garmin $567, Apple $550, Samsung $530. This prevents lower-retention brands from being priced using the market-wide average of $618.
  • Pilot the largest supported country spread first. Test Apple sourcing from United Kingdom against resale benchmarks in Australia, where reliable medians differ by $397. Treat n<5 cells as watchlist signals, not immediate scale routes.
  • Separate new and used repricing rules. The largest new-vs-used average premium among core brands is Samsung at 48.8%; discounting new inventory by a pooled condition factor would understate that brand’s resale lift.
  • Normalize listings by case size before margin calls. Size-tagged listings show r=0.02 overall, and 48mm+ units carry a 19.6% median premium over ≤40mm units. Use case-size buckets in comp matching, especially for Garmin.
  • Approach power sellers selectively. The top 5% control 48.5% of supply, but their mix-adjusted median price index is +2.0%. Prioritize power sellers with high worldwide-shipping coverage and below-market indexes rather than volume alone.
  • Exploit variance where assortment is broadest. Samsung has the widest observed core-brand price range ($49,655), making it the best candidate for model-level mispricing screens and title-quality improvements.
