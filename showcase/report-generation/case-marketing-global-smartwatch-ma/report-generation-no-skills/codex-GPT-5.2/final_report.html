6) Strategic Recommendations (Directly Tied to Findings)

What to do next (actionable playbook)

  • Exploit UK “two-sided” arbitrage: Apple median in the UK is $392.74 (−28.6% vs Apple’s global median), while Garmin and Samsung medians in the UK are $775 (+36.7%) and $825 (+55.7%). Action: prioritize UK sourcing for Apple inventory; treat UK as a premium sell-through destination for Garmin/Samsung.
  • Samsung is condition-sensitive: New listings price +18.2% above Used (mean-based), larger than Apple (+8.2%) and Garmin (+4.7%). Action: when acquiring Samsung, pay up for “New” only if your channel can preserve that condition signal; otherwise bias toward Apple/Garmin for used-heavy sourcing.
  • Use brand medians for price anchoring (not means): Samsung and Apple means are pulled upward by rare extreme outliers. Action: build pricing rules around median + percentile bands (10th–90th) to avoid overpaying on model-mix noise.
  • Do not assume power sellers undercut: Top 5% of sellers account for 48.3% of listings and price higher (median +15.2% overall; +26.2% for Samsung). Action: treat power-seller partnerships as a supply stability / refurb quality play, not a low-cost sourcing channel. Negotiate on volume reliability and returns, not “cheap inventory.”
  • Case size is a secondary lever: overall size/price correlation is weak (r ≈ 0.02). Action: include case size as a tie-breaker within model families, but don’t over-index on it when deciding what to source.
