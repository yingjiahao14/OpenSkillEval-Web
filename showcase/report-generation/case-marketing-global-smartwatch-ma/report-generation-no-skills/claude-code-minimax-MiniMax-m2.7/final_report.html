Market Composition
Apple dominates the secondary market with 65.7% of all listings (2,370), followed by Garmin at 20.7% (746) and Samsung at 13.0% (470). The market skews toward used inventory — only 42.5% of listings are new, with used items comprising 34.6% and open-box/refurbished items accounting for the remainder.

New vs. Used Price Premium
Apple commands a 17.7% premium for new listings over used ($589 vs $501 median). Garmin and Samsung show negligible premiums (–0.3% and +0.6% respectively), suggesting their resale value is less condition-dependent. Actionable: Prioritize Apple inventory in new condition for maximum margin capture.
