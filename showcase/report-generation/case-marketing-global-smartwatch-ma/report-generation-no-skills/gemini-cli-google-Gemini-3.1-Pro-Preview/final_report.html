Strategic Recommendations

1. Exploit Garmin Geographic Arbitrage: Focus cross-border sourcing on markets where Garmin median prices are lowest, liquidating them in high-value markets. With a price spread of $384 globally, this is a clear high-margin opportunity.
2. De-emphasize Case Size as a Core Pricing Metric: The overall data indicates a very weak correlation (0.02) between case size and resale value. Sourcing strategies should prioritize brand, generation, and condition over physical dimensions, though Garmin models show a slight size premium worth noting for their ultra-premium outdoor lines.
3. Partner with Power Sellers for Wholesale Acquisition: With top sellers controlling 48.2% of listings and generally pricing more competitively than casual sellers, operators should build direct wholesale or lot-purchase relationships with these power sellers to acquire high-turnover inventory (like Apple) at scale.
4. Condition Arbitrage on Apple Devices: Apple devices show a strong New vs. Used premium (17.2%). Sourcing pristine "Used" or "Refurbished" units and repackaging/certifying them presents a clear path to capturing this premium gap.
