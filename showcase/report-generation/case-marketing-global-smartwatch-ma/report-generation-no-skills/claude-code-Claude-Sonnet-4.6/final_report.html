Key Insight — Condition Premium

Apple is the only brand where condition meaningfully affects resale price: New Apple watches command a 17.7% premium ($589 vs $501 median). Garmin and Samsung show near-zero condition premium, suggesting the resale buyer base treats condition as irrelevant for those brands — or that secondary-market listings don't reliably differentiate genuine "new" stock.

Outlier Alert — Samsung & Apple

Samsung's mean price ($725) is $195 above its median ($530), inflated by extreme outliers reaching $49,900. Apple's max of $40,500 similarly skews the mean. When making inventory decisions, use median price as the operative benchmark — the mean is unreliable for these brands.

Key Insight — The 50mm+ Effect

The ≥50mm category is exclusively Garmin (74 listings — dive computers and rugged outdoor watches). Apple has no listings above 49mm in the case-size data; Samsung tops out at 49mm. Sourcing strategy should target these Garmin large-format models (Descent, Epix Pro 51mm) as premium inventory items.

Section 06

Strategic Recommendations

Each recommendation flows directly from a measured finding above. No generic advice.

01
Sourcing
Source Apple from UK and Hong Kong — target the 28.6% discount
UK Apple listings median at $393 — 28.6% below the global median of $550. Hong Kong lands at $442 (−19.6%). These are the two deepest Apple discounts in the dataset. Combined with Australia's premium ($792, +44%) and Japan's ($688, +25.1%), a UK→Australia or UK→Japan arbitrage route generates ~$399 gross spread per unit — the widest single-brand geographic spread in the dataset. Prioritize UK listings with worldwide shipping enabled (27.3% of all listings offer it).
02
Sourcing
Source Garmin from Japan — exploit the 57% Canada premium
Japan Garmin median is $506 (−10.7% vs global), while Canada prices at $890 (+57%). That is a $384 spread. Japan has 161 Garmin listings — the second-largest country pool after the US — providing meaningful supply depth. The top power seller (Seller_0042) already appears to operate this channel at scale ($864 avg, Garmin-dominant). Entering this lane is viable but faces incumbent competition from well-capitalized power sellers.
03
Inventory
Stock Apple "New" condition — its 17.7% condition premium is unique
Apple is the only brand with a meaningful new-vs-used condition premium: New Apple ($589 median) outprices Used Apple ($501 median) by 17.7%. Garmin and Samsung show near-zero condition premiums (−0.3% and +0.6% respectively). For Apple inventory, condition grading directly affects pricing power. Prioritize "New" and "Open box" Apple stock over used. For Garmin and Samsung, condition is not a meaningful pricing lever — sourcing should focus on model and case size instead.
04
Inventory
Target Garmin ≥50mm (Descent, Epix Pro 51mm) for premium inventory
The ≥50mm case size bucket — exclusively Garmin dive/rugged models — posts a $630 median vs. $530 for the overall market. These 74 listings represent a low-volume, high-ASP niche. Garmin's Descent Mk series and Epix Pro 51mm command the highest prices in the dataset (up to $1,319). Maintaining even a small SKU depth in these models provides outsized revenue contribution per unit stored.
05
Seller Strategy
Partner with mid-tier power sellers — not the top 2
91 power sellers (≥4 listings) control 48.8% of supply. The top two sellers (Seller_0042 at 206 listings, Seller_0035 at 136 listings) price at $864 and $834 avg respectively — these are premium-positioned and unlikely to offer below-market supply. Sellers ranked 5–15 (Seller_0029 at $578, Seller_0084 at $581, Seller_0004 at $543) price closer to or below market median and represent better partnership targets for supply at competitive costs. Look for Garmin-dominant mid-tier sellers given the brand's arbitrage potential.
06
Pricing
Use median — not mean — for all brand pricing benchmarks
Samsung's mean ($725) overstates the actual market price by $195 vs. its median ($530) due to extreme outliers reaching $49,900. Apple's mean ($595) is similarly inflated by Hermès bundles. Using mean-based pricing models will lead to systematic overpricing of standard Apple and Samsung units. Set replenishment and resale price thresholds using median + interquartile range, not mean ± std dev. The median is the reliable anchor for all three brands.
