06Strategic Recommendations

Five data-grounded actions for secondary-market sourcing, pricing, and partnership strategy.
1
High Priority

Source Apple from UK & Hong Kong; Sell into Australia

The UK median Apple price ($393) is 29% below the global brand median ($550) and 50% below Australia ($792). Hong Kong offers a similar source price ($442). With 214 UK Apple listings available, supply is sufficient. This is the single largest documented arbitrage — $399 gross spread per unit.

Evidence: §3 Geographic Arbitrage — Apple UK median $392.74, Australia $792.00, n=214 UK listings.
2
High Priority

Target Japan for Garmin and Samsung Sourcing

Japan is the cheapest source market for both Garmin ($506, −11% vs. global) and Samsung ($437, −18% vs. global). Garmin can be re-sold into Canada ($890, +57%) or the UK ($775, +37%). Samsung can be re-sold into the UK ($825, +56%). Japan carries 185 listings — meaningful but not deep, so act before supply tightens.

Evidence: §3 — Garmin Japan $506 vs. Canada $890 ($384 spread); Samsung Japan $437 vs. UK $825 ($388 spread).
3
High Priority

Default to Apple for Inventory — Garmin for Predictability

Apple commands the highest liquidity (65.7% of all listings) at a stable $550 median. Garmin offers the most predictable pricing: std dev of only $198 vs. $834 for Apple and $2,286 for Samsung. For operators who need inventory turn certainty, Garmin is the lower-risk brand. Avoid heavy Samsung positioning — its extreme variance ($49,900 max) distorts benchmarks and creates valuation uncertainty.

Evidence: §2 — Apple stdev $834, Garmin $198, Samsung $2,286. Garmin IQR $282 vs Samsung $289.
4
Medium Priority

Partner with Garmin-Specialist Power Sellers for Consistent Supply

Sellers Seller_0033, Seller_0001, Seller_0031, and Seller_0004 collectively hold 344 Garmin-exclusive listings at median prices of $550–$675. These sellers have deep brand knowledge and consistent supply. Partnership (volume purchasing or consignment arrangements) could lock in a supply channel before the broader market does. Power sellers price 11.7% above regular sellers on Garmin — their quality justifies negotiating wholesale discounts.

Evidence: §5 — Top 10 sellers table; Garmin power seller median $591 vs. regular $529 (+11.7%).
5
Medium Priority

Price "New" Apple & Garmin at a 10% Markup; Avoid New Samsung Premium

Apple New listings command a 10.2% median premium over Used ($589 vs. $535). Garmin New adds only 3.4%. Samsung New actually trades at a 3.3% discount to Used — pricing "New" Samsung above used at market median is a mistake. When listing New Samsung inventory, benchmark against used comparables, not new-only averages.

Evidence: §2 New vs. Used table — Samsung new_med $520, used_med $538, premium −3.3%.
6
Medium Priority

Offer Worldwide Shipping to Command a 27% Median Uplift

Listings with worldwide shipping enabled carry a median price of $667 vs. $526 for domestic-only listings — a $141 spread (26.8%). This is partly self-selecting (expensive items are more likely to ship worldwide), but enabling worldwide shipping on premium inventory positions it for the highest-paying buyer pools (Canada, Australia) rather than local demand curves.

Evidence: §1 data — worldwide shipping median $667 (n=986) vs. domestic $526 (n=2,621).
