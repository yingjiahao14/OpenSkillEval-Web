6. Strategic Recommendations

🔴 High Priority: Exploit Geographic Arbitrage in Garmin Devices
The data shows a substantial geographic price spread for Garmin. Sourcing from regions with the lowest median price and drop-shipping or reselling into premium markets (like the US or Japan) represents the highest-margin opportunity.
🟡 Medium Priority: Condition-Based Sourcing for Apple
With a 10.3% premium for "New" condition over "Used", sourcing "Open Box" or lightly used Apple devices, professionally refurbishing them to "Excellent - Refurbished", and closing the price gap to "New" offers reliable, volume-based margin.
🟢 Structural Priority: Partner with or Bypass Power Sellers
The top 5% of sellers control nearly half the market. Depending on the brand pricing visualized above, strategy analysts should decide whether to bulk-purchase from these power sellers (who may underprice to move volume) or avoid competing with them on their anchor brands (where they may have a cost-basis advantage).
🟢 Product Targeting: Larger Case Sizes Yield Higher Returns
Particularly in Garmin and Apple lines, larger case sizes strongly correlate with higher resale values. Inventory acquisition algorithms should weight (≥44mm) models higher due to stronger value retention.
