Strategic Recommendations

Each recommendation ties directly to computed listing medians, spreads, correlations, or seller concentration metrics above.

The practical playbook is geography-first sourcing, median-led pricing, and selective seller partnerships rather than blanket brand or condition rules.
  1. Prioritize Garmin cross-border sourcing from Japan and Germany for UK resale tests.Garmin qualifying-country medians range from $506.06 in Japan to $775.00 in the United Kingdom, a $268.94 spread on 161+ low-country listings.
  2. Use the United Kingdom as a premium-demand listing market for Samsung and Garmin, not as the primary buy market.UK medians sit at $825.00 for Samsung and $775.00 for Garmin, the highest qualifying medians for both brands.
  3. Treat Apple UK inventory as a sourcing opportunity, with Australia as the premium-price benchmark.Apple's UK median is $394.99 versus $792.00 in Australia, the largest Apple country spread at $397.01.
  4. Do not price solely from case size; use model, condition, and geography first.The overall case-size/price correlation is only 0.021; even the strongest primary-brand relationship is Garmin at 0.107, despite visible 47+ mm bucket premiums.
  5. Partner selectively with power sellers for breadth and logistics, but audit price floors before bulk buys.The top 88 sellers control 48.5% of all listings and ship worldwide at 46.6%, but their median listing is 6.6% above comparable brand-condition baselines.
