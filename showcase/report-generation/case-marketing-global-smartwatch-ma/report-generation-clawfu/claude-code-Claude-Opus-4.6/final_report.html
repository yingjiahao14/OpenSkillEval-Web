Apple: UK listings price 28.2% below global median ($395 vs $550). Sourcing from the UK and reselling in Australia (+44.0%, $792) or Japan (+25.1%, $688) represents a potential $200–$400 per unit margin before shipping.

6. Strategic Recommendations

1. Prioritize UK→Australia and UK→Japan Apple Sourcing
Apple UK listings are 28.2% below global median ($395), while Australia (+44.0%, $792) and Japan (+25.1%, $688) are premium markets. This creates a $200–$400/unit gross margin opportunity. Focus on listings with worldwide shipping enabled (27.0% of all listings).
2. Exploit the Japan→UK Garmin Pipeline
Garmin in Japan trades at a 10.7% discount ($506), while UK buyers pay a 36.7% premium ($775). With 161 Japan-based Garmin listings and strong worldwide shipping from Japanese sellers, this is a high-volume arbitrage play worth ~$269/unit.
3. Source Samsung from Japan and Israel for UK Resale
Samsung's UK premium (+55.7%, $825) against Japan sourcing (−17.5%, $437) represents the largest country-pair spread in the dataset at ~$388/unit. Israel offers similar sourcing economics at $439 median.
4. Focus on Used Garmin and Samsung — Skip the "New" Premium
Garmin and Samsung show near-zero new/used price gaps (-0.5% and +1.4% respectively). Source used units at lower cost without sacrificing resale price. Apple is the exception — its 17.2% new premium justifies sourcing new inventory where possible.
5. Undercut Power Sellers on Samsung
Power sellers price Samsung 26.2% above casual sellers ($618 vs $490). Source at casual-seller prices and list at or slightly below power-seller prices to capture the spread. The market's 48.2% concentration among top 5% sellers means buyers have limited alternatives.
6. Stock Apple Ultra and Garmin Compact Premium Models
Apple 48mm+ (Ultra line) commands a $590 median — 11.5% above standard sizes. Garmin ≤40mm models (compact premium) hit $735 median. These size segments outperform their brand averages and should be prioritized in inventory planning.
