06 — Strategic Recommendations
Strategic Recommendations
Actionable decisions derived directly from the data above. Each recommendation references a specific quantitative finding.
🎯
Sourcing Strategy
Source Apple from the UK — 28.6% discount to global median

The United Kingdom lists Apple at a median of $392.74 versus the global median of $550 — a 28.6% discount backed by 214 listings. This is the deepest sustained discount in the dataset for any major brand in a high-volume market. Prioritize UK-based Apple sourcing for resale into AU, JP, or IT channels where Apple trades at 9–44% above the global median.

🌏
Geographic Arbitrage
Japan is a consistent buy-market across all three brands

Japan prices all three brands below the global median: Apple −25.1%, Garmin −10.7%, Samsung −17.5%. With 185 Japan-based listings in the dataset, there is sufficient supply to build a systematic sourcing pipeline. Route Samsung and Apple sourced in Japan into the UK (Samsung +55.7%) and Australia (Apple +44%) for maximum margin capture — the spread exceeds $400 in both cases.

📦
Brand Focus
Garmin is the highest-consistency brand — prioritize for stable margin

Garmin carries the highest median resale price ($567) and the lowest price variance (std dev $198 vs $834 for Apple, $2,286 for Samsung). Unlike Apple, Garmin shows no condition premium — new and used list at nearly identical prices — eliminating condition grading risk. For operators who want predictable margins without model-level expertise, Garmin is the lowest-risk inventory choice.

📐
Pricing Intelligence
Do not use case size as a pricing signal — use brand and condition instead

Case size correlates at just r=0.021 with price across the full dataset. For Apple, case size explains essentially nothing (r=0.033). The only partial exception is Garmin (r=0.107), where the 47–55mm bucket commands a $75 premium. Pricing algorithms should weight brand, condition, and geography heavily over physical dimensions. Investing in model-line identification (Series vs Ultra vs SE for Apple) will yield far greater pricing accuracy than measuring case diameter.

🤝
Seller Partnerships
Partner selectively with power sellers — expect to pay 15% above market

The top 5% of sellers (88 accounts) control 48.3% of listings and price 15% above casual sellers. They are not competing on price — they compete on breadth and reliability. The value of a power seller partnership is consistent access to Garmin supply (35% of power seller listings vs 7% for casual). If sourcing Garmin at scale, budget for the 11.7% premium as a supply reliability cost. For Samsung, the 24.7% power seller premium makes casual sourcing far more attractive.

💡
Condition Grading
Prioritize "New" condition for Apple; condition is irrelevant for Garmin

Apple shows a 17.2% new-vs-used premium ($590 vs $503) — the largest condition premium of the three brands. For Apple inventory, condition grading directly affects realized margin. Garmin and Samsung both show near-zero condition premiums (−0.5% and +1.4% respectively), meaning condition certification costs for those brands will not be recovered in higher prices. Grade Apple aggressively; treat Garmin and Samsung condition as operationally irrelevant for pricing.
