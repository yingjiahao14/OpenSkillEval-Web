Strategic Recommendations

Based on data analysis of 3,607 listings, the following recommendations address sourcing, pricing, and partnership decisions for secondary-market operators.

1. Target Garmin for Consistent Margins

Garmin's low price variance (std: $198) and strong median ($567) make it ideal for predictable margin extraction. Source from Japan (median: $506) and sell into Canada/Australia where premiums exceed $400.

2. Exploit UK-to-Australia Arbitrage for Apple

Apple Watch pricing in UK ($393 median) vs Australia ($792) creates a $399 spread. Acquire from UK sellers, leverage Australia's elevated demand, and account for shipping costs in margin calculations.

3. Prioritize New Condition Apple Inventory

Apple's New vs Used premium of 17.7% is the highest across all brands. Stocking New condition Apple Watch units offers 3× the premium uplift versus Garmin (0%) and Samsung (0.6%).

4. Partner with Power Sellers for Volume

The top 87 sellers control 48.4% of inventory at 15% price premium. Negotiate bulk acquisition agreements or become a power seller yourself through consistent listing quality and inventory depth.

5. Ignore Case Size in Pricing Decisions

Case size shows r² < 1.2% across all brands. Physical dimension data in listings is unreliable and not a price driver. Focus on model generation and condition grade instead.

6. Leverage Worldwide Shipping Sellers

Listings with worldwide shipping command a median premium of $146 for Apple ($676 vs $530). Cross-border fulfillment capability justifies higher prices and expands buyer pool.
