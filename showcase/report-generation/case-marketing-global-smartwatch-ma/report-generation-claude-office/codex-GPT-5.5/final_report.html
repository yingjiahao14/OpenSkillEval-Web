Strategic Recommendations

1

Prioritize geography-led sourcing before brand-only buying.

Country spreads exceed brand median differences: Samsung’s usable country spread is $388 and Apple’s is $397. Build sourcing screens around low-median markets such as Japan for Garmin/Samsung and the United Kingdom for Apple.

2

Route inventory toward premium destination markets.

The United Kingdom carries premiums for Garmin (36.7%) and Samsung (55.7%), while Australia is the highest usable Apple median at $792. Use these markets as pricing references for export or marketplace-listing decisions.

3

Use Garmin as the steadier value-retention anchor.

Garmin has the highest core median at $566.98 and much lower price dispersion than Apple or Samsung by standard deviation. Favor Garmin when predictable resale realization matters more than upside from rare high-priced listings.

4

Do not pay blanket premiums for larger case sizes.

The overall case-size correlation is only 0.021. Require model- or country-specific support before bidding up larger cases; otherwise treat size as merchandising metadata rather than a primary pricing lever.

5

Partner with power sellers for access, not markdowns.

Power sellers control 48.3% of listings and ship worldwide at 46.6%, but their median price is $598, above other sellers. Negotiate volume terms, early access, or logistics reliability rather than assuming listed-price discounts.

6

Apply condition premiums selectively.

Samsung New listings show a 48.8% average premium over Used, but median premiums are only 1.4%. Use condition premiums where they survive model and country checks; avoid applying one global New markup.
