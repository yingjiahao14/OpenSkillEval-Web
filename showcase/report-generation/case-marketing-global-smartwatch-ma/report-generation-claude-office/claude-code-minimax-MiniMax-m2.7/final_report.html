Finding: Case size has minimal predictive value for price across all brands. The correlation coefficients (r < 0.11) indicate that larger watch faces do not command systematically higher resale prices. Price is driven more by model, condition, and included accessories than physical dimensions.

For Garmin, there is a weak positive correlation (r = 0.107) suggesting that larger Fenix/XL models tend to price slightly higher, but this effect is modest. Apple's near-zero correlation confirms that Series 9 vs Series 10 size variations don't impact resale value—the premium Series 9 Hermès models command higher prices regardless of 41mm vs 45mm sizing.

Strategic Implication

Sellers should not premium-price larger case sizes based on size alone. Focus pricing strategy on model tier (Standard, Edition, Hermès) and included accessories rather than case dimensions.

6. Strategic Recommendations

1. Prioritize Garmin for Value Retention

  • Garmin commands the highest median resale price ($567) among major brands
  • Garmin's specialized use cases (diving, aviation, outdoor) create resilient secondary demand
  • New vs. used premium is lowest (4.5%), suggesting stable value across condition tiers

2. Exploit Japan-to-Western Market Arbitrage

  • Japan offers Garmin at $506 median (vs. $890 in Canada, $775 in UK)
  • Samsung is priced at $437 in Japan vs. $825 in UK—a 89% markup opportunity
  • Establish sourcing relationships with Japanese sellers or use Japan-based fulfillment partners

3. Target UK Market for Apple & Samsung Premium Capture

  • UK Apple Watch prices ($393 median) are below global average despite being a premium market
  • Samsung commands $825 median in UK vs. $437 in Japan—the highest brand-country premium
  • UK buyers show willingness to pay premium for authentic, well-described listings

4. Partner with Power Sellers Rather Than Compete

  • Top 5% of sellers control 48.5% of listings—their pricing power is structural
  • Power sellers command a price premium over regular sellers (~$26 median)
  • Consider wholesale/partnership arrangements with top-10 volume sellers

5. Condition-Based Pricing: Samsung Offers Highest Margin on New

  • Samsung new vs. used premium: 18.4%—highest among all brands
  • Apple: 8.1% premium; Garmin: 4.5% premium
  • Prioritize sourcing Samsung "New" condition inventory for maximum margin capture

6. Don't Premium Large Case Sizes—Focus on Model Tier

  • Case size shows negligible correlation with price (r < 0.11 for all brands)
  • Apple Hermès/Edition models command premiums regardless of 41mm vs. 45mm
  • Marketing larger watches as "premium" due to size alone will not resonate with buyers
