Strategic Recommendations

Data-grounded actions for sourcing, pricing, and partnerships.

1Geographic Arbitrage

Source Apple from UK, Sell to US/Australia

UK Apple median is $393 vs US $550 — a $157 gross per unit before shipping. With 214 UK Apple listings and worldwide shipping common (Apple r=high), this is the highest-confidence arbitrage play in the dataset.

2Sourcing Strategy

Source Garmin from Japan/Germany for Western Resale

Garmin in Japan ($506 median) and Germany ($516) trades at roughly half the Australian price ($1,005) and significantly below UK ($775) and Canada ($890). The Japan Garmin discount appears structural (Seller_0001 alone has 65 listings). Establish a direct relationship with high-volume Japan sellers.

3Inventory Mix

Focus Apple Inventory on New Condition — Premium Justifies Stock Cost

Apple New listings command a 17.2% premium over Used ($590 vs $503). Given that Garmin and Samsung show near-zero condition premiums (-1.8%, 1.4%), capital should be concentrated in New Apple inventory rather than New Garmin/Samsung.

4Seller Partnership

Partner With Power Sellers — They Hold Premium SKUs

Power sellers price 13.9%–24.7% above market median, confirming they list higher-tier models, not commodities. The top 88 sellers control 48% of supply. Establishing reseller relationships or bulk-buy agreements with Seller_0042 (196 listings), Seller_0033 (175), and Seller_0005 (173) would secure consistent premium inventory.

5Pricing Intelligence

Do Not Over-Index on Case Size as a Buying Signal

Case size correlates weakly with price (r=0.033 Apple, r=0.107 Garmin). Sourcing decisions based on size alone will misvalue inventory. Use model tier (Ultra, Pro, Sapphire, Hermès) as the primary pricing signal, not dimensions.

6Sourcing Filter

Prioritize Worldwide-Shipping Listings for Premium Margin

Worldwide-shipping Apple listings have a $146 median premium over domestic. This reflects higher-tier model mix. When sourcing, filter for worldwide-shipping sellers to pre-screen for premium inventory — the signal is noisy but directionally valid across all three brands.
