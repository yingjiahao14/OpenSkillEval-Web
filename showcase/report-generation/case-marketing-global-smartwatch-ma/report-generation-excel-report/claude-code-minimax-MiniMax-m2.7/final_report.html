6. Strategic Recommendations

1. Prioritize Garmin for Margin-Focused Sourcing

Finding: Garmin commands the highest median price ($566.98) with the widest geographic spread ($565). Power sellers disproportionately favor Garmin (+27.9 pp vs regular sellers).

Action: Build Garmin-focused sourcing pipelines. Target acquisition in Japan and Hong Kong where Garmin prices run 13-15% below global mean. Resell into Australia or South Korea where premiums reach 25-40%.

2. Exploit Geographic Arbitrage — Focus on Apple

Finding: Apple shows a $450 geographic spread with the US market offering the lowest median entry point ($550) and Australia the highest ($792).

Action: Source Apple Watch units from the US market en masse and resell internationally. The 44% price differential between US and Australian Apple listings represents pure arbitrage opportunity.

3. Condition is King — Invest in New/Open Box Inventory

Finding: Samsung commands an 18.2% new-vs-used premium, Apple 8.2%, and Garmin 4.7%. Power sellers maintain 53% new inventory and achieve 4.4% higher average prices.

Action: Prioritize sourcing new and open-box items, particularly Samsung. The condition premium significantly outweighs case size or color variations. Quality-certification partnerships with refurbishers can unlock consistent margins.

4. Partner with Power Sellers for Inventory Scale

Finding: The top 5% of sellers control 48.5% of listings and price 4.4% higher. They prefer Garmin and Samsung, and focus on new/open-box condition.

Action: Establish reseller partnerships with high-volume sellers. Their preference for Garmin/Samsung suggests inventory access to these brands at favorable terms. A consignment or volume-purchase agreement could provide consistent stock at predictable costs.

5. Don't Overweight Case Size — Focus on Model Tier

Finding: Case size correlation with price is essentially zero (r=0.021). Standard deviation in case size within brands is ~3mm, meaning most listings cluster around 45-46mm regardless of price.

Action: De-prioritize case size in procurement decisions. Instead, focus on model tier (Ultra vs Series), included accessories, and condition grade — these factors drive 80%+ of price variance.

Summary: Top 3 Action Items

  1. Buy Garmin in Japan/Hong Kong → Sell in Australia/South Korea (25-40% margin potential)
  2. Source Apple from US → Resell internationally (44% price differential)
  3. Prioritize New/Open Box Samsung and Garmin (highest condition premium)
