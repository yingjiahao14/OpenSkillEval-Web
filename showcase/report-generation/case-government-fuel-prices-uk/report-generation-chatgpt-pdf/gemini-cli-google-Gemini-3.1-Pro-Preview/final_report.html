Key Findings & Policy Implications

Policy Recommendation: Regulators should target the fuel types exhibiting the highest price spreads for market transparency investigations, while implementing stricter open-data reporting SLAs to minimize reporting lag.

  1. Price Disparities: E5 shows the highest variance with a spread of 1889.0 PPL.
  2. Data Freshness: There is a notable delay in reporting updates for HVO, which averaged 1.02 hours of lag.
  3. Market Transparency: A significant Interquartile Range (IQR) across specific fuels suggests consumers could save substantially by price-shopping, validating the need for open-data fuel apps.
