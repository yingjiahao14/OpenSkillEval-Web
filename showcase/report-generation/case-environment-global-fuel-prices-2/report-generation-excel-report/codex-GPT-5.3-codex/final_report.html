6) Key Findings & Policy Implications

  1. Brent pass-through is strong globally (petrol 1.000, diesel 1.000), confirming crude benchmark dominance.
  2. Retail prices moved materially during major shocks; 2020 and 2022 windows show benchmark-linked changes.
  3. High-subsidy regimes are associated with lower average retail prices than low-subsidy regimes.
  4. Regional price inequality remains persistent year-to-year.
  5. Tax burden differs by income tier, supporting differentiated tax-smoothing policy design.

Policy implications: calibrate subsidies to be targeted and temporary; deploy counter-cyclical tax bands; monitor pass-through coefficients by income/subsidy segment; combine price controls with direct transfers when fiscal leakage risk is high.
