6) Strategic Recommendations

  • Center launch pricing in dense bestseller bands ($6–15), where title concentration is highest.
  • Do not depend on premium price to improve ratings; observed correlation (-0.133) is weak and negative.
  • Run genre-specific pricing playbooks: sharper price competitiveness in Fiction; selective premium positioning in Non-Fiction.
  • Use yearly trend monitoring and inflection tracking for annual pricing recalibration, not static rules.
  • Prioritize repeat-bestseller authors to improve catalog hit-rate and portfolio stability.
