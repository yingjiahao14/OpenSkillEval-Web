Strategic Recommendations

1) Don’t expect price increases to improve ratings. Pearson r = -0.133 indicates a weak negative relationship between price and rating on the bestseller list.

2) Anchor standard pricing within the dominant bestseller bands. $6–10 and $11–15 account for 321 of 550 entries.

3) In Non Fiction, defend premium pricing with explicit value. Non Fiction has higher median price ($12) but lower average rating (4.595) than Fiction.

4) Invest in repeat-author engines. Top authors by appearances (e.g., Jeff Kinney with 12) indicate durable franchises that can sustain bestseller visibility.
