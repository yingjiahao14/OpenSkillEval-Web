Strategic Recommendations

  • Avoid price-only rating strategies: The correlation (r=-0.1331) is too weak to support rating gains through pricing changes alone.
  • Run genre-specific pricing playbooks: Fiction and Non Fiction show different average price/rating/review profiles and should not share one price architecture.
  • Institutionalize annual inflection reviews: Use YoY turning points in price, rating, and review volume as formal triggers for catalog and promotion resets.
  • Back repeat-winning authors: Authors with persistent appearances represent lower execution risk for bestseller targeting.
  • Concentrate spend in resilient price segments: Segment performance indicates specific bands sustain engagement and rating quality more consistently.
