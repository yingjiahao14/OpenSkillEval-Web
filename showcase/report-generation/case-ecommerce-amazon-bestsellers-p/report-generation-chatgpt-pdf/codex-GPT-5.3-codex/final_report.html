This analysis covers 550 annual bestseller entries across 351 unique titles and 248 authors. Ratings are structurally high (mean 4.62/5), while prices vary widely (mean $13.10), enabling robust price-rating testing.

Price vs. User Rating: Correlation Analysis

Pearson correlation coefficient (Price, Rating): -0.133 — very weak relationship.

Ratings cluster at high levels across low and high prices, indicating perceived quality among bestsellers is not strongly price-dependent.

Strategic Recommendations

  • Anchor strategy on content value, not premium pricing: Pearson -0.133 shows price increases alone are unlikely to lift ratings.
  • Set genre-differentiated price corridors: Manage Fiction and Non-Fiction with separate pricing playbooks based on observed average price/rating/review profiles.
  • Prioritize proven price segments: Concentrate launch and promotion activity in segments with strongest bestseller incidence and stable ratings.
  • Institutionalize inflection monitoring: Use annual shifts in price/rating/reviews as governance triggers (notable years: 2015, 2019, 2012).
  • Scale repeat-author franchises: Expand investment in high-frequency authors with strong rating and engagement consistency.
