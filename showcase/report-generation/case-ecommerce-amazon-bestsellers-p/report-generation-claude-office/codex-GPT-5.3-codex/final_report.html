Strategic Recommendations

  • Prioritize bestseller-focused pricing in the $0–$15 range, which represents 74.2% of bestseller entries.
  • Avoid premium-price assumptions for satisfaction uplift; with r = -0.133, higher price does not systematically yield higher ratings.
  • Use genre-specific pricing guardrails: Fiction leads on average rating, while Fiction carries the lower average price baseline.
  • Treat 2015, 2019, and 2012 as benchmark years for annual pricing and promotion retrospectives.
