Strategic Recommendations

Grounded in computed findings
  1. Price is not a satisfaction lever: Pearson r = -0.133 indicates pricing up doesn’t reliably move ratings up; protect rating via value cues and QA on premium-positioned titles.
  2. Segment pricing by genre: Non-Fiction sustains higher prices; Fiction drives higher review volume. Use genre-specific targets for price bands and promo depth.
  3. Standardize on price bands: Bestseller appearances cluster heavily in lower/mid segments; treat premium segments as selective, proof-driven plays.
  4. Use inflection years as checkpoints: Largest price shift (2015), rating shift (2019), and review shift (2012) are ideal anchors for retrospective strategy reviews.
  5. Prioritize repeat-winner authors: High-frequency authors provide durable demand; build multi-title pipelines and coordinated releases around them.
