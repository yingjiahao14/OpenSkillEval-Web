6. Strategic Recommendations

  • Do Not Rely on Price to Signal Quality: The weak negative correlation (-0.133) shows that readers do not automatically rate premium-priced books higher. Ensure the content justifies any premium pricing, as readers are highly price-sensitive and perfectly willing to rate cheaper titles exceptionally well.
  • Leverage Fiction for Volume, Non-Fiction for Margin: Fiction drives higher engagement (avg. 15,684 reviews) and slightly better ratings but commands lower prices. Non-Fiction maintains a higher average price ($14.84). Balance the catalog to capitalize on Fiction's volume and Non-Fiction's margin.
  • Adapt to the Deflationary Pricing Trend: The downward trend in average bestseller prices since 2014 dictates that competing in the top 50 requires competitive pricing. Consider adopting strategic discount promotions to break into the bestseller list, which historically correlates with climbing user satisfaction.
  • Invest in Perennial Franchises: Authors like Jeff Kinney and Rick Riordan appear repeatedly on the lists, often carrying robust average ratings. Long-term investment in reliable franchises provides a more stable revenue base than betting on one-off hits.
