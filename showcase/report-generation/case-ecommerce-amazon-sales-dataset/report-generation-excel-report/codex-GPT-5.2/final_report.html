Strategic Recommendations

  1. Rebalance promo depth toward targeted use-cases (not blanket discounting). Discount%→rating correlation is -0.155 (negative) and Discount%→rating_count is 0.012 (near zero), so deeper discounts are not reliably converting to higher satisfaction or markedly higher engagement in this dataset.
  2. Protect catalog quality by prioritizing high-rating coverage. 75.8% of products are rated ≥4.0; maintain (or increase) this share by pruning low-rating sub-categories and tightening listing quality controls.
  3. Attack the weakest sub-category first for fast quality lift. The lowest average-rating sub-category among groups with n≥20 is Electronics → Headphones,Earbuds&Accessories (avg 3.93, n=66); audit top SKUs there for recurring defects, mismatched expectations, and content gaps.
  4. Use price-band patterns to guide assortment and margin strategy. The highest average-rating price band is 10,000+ (avg 4.20, n=122); lean into quality-led positioning there, and avoid over-discounting purely to move volume.
