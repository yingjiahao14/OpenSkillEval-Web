What to do with these themes

  • Use “quality”, “value”, “easy”, “battery”, “sound” as review-driven merchandising angles for relevant categories where they naturally apply.
  • Investigate “defective/broken/return” flags in lower-rated products as potential quality-control and listing-accuracy issues.
  • Promotional messaging should avoid leaning purely on discount depth; emphasize product experience drivers instead.
Note: Since reviews are stored as comma-separated strings per product row, counts here reflect token presence in those strings rather than per-review counts.
