Strategic Recommendations

  1. Prioritize category-level quality fixes before increasing discounts: discount→rating correlation is only -0.155, so satisfaction gains from deeper discounts are limited.
  2. Use discounts tactically to drive traffic, not as primary quality lever: discount→rating_count correlation of 0.012 indicates stronger engagement impact than rating uplift.
  3. Rebalance assortment toward high-rated categories and remediate low-rated segments: best/worst category ranking shows persistent quality gaps despite similar promotional intensity.
  4. Standardize playbooks from highly engaged SKUs: top products combine strong ratings and large rating_count, suggesting repeatable patterns in positioning and price-value messaging.
  5. Address recurring complaint themes in PDP and QA processes: frequent negative terms (issue/problem/poor/return/defect) indicate friction points suitable for root-cause elimination.
