Pricing & Discount Strategy Analysis

This section examines whether deeper discounts drive better ratings or higher volume. The overall correlation between discount percentage and product rating is -0.16, and between discount percentage and rating count is 0.01.

These near-zero correlation coefficients strongly suggest that deeper discounts do not correlate with higher ratings or exponentially higher review volumes. Customers evaluate product value based on intrinsic quality rather than the perceived "deal" size.

Customer Engagement & Rating Distribution

The majority of products in the catalog are well-received, with 75.8% achieving a rating of 4.0 or higher. High-engagement products (those with exceptionally large rating counts) often share characteristics of being essential electronics accessories or deeply discounted core household items.

Strategic Recommendations

1. Optimize Promotional Spend: With a Price-to-Discount Efficiency correlation of -0.16, aggressive discounting is eroding margins without driving corresponding increases in customer satisfaction or review volume. Shift strategy from deep continuous discounts to targeted, time-bound promotions.
2. Intervene in "Home&Kitchen": As the lowest-rated major category (Avg 4.04), this segment requires immediate catalog curation. Conduct quality audits and delist chronically defective SKUs to protect brand reputation.
3. Protect High-Engagement SKUs: The "hero" items driving the median 5,179 ratings per product are the lifeblood of catalog visibility. Ensure these products maintain high in-stock rates and pristine fulfillment performance to capitalize on organic traffic.
