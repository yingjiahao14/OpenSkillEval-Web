Executive Overview

Review Sentiment Snapshot

The dataset is product-level rather than review-level; review fields contain comma-separated review excerpts. The snapshot below counts products whose review titles or content mention defined theme keywords, so it should be read as directional customer-language evidence rather than a full NLP sentiment score.

Strategic Recommendations

Each recommendation below ties directly to measured catalog performance, discount efficiency, category ratings, or engagement concentration in the dataset.

Stop using deep discounts as a satisfaction lever

Discount percentage has a Pearson correlation of -0.155 with ratings and 0.012 with rating count; the 81–100% band averages 3.98 stars.

Protect high-rated, low-discount office assortment

OfficeProducts leads sizeable top-level categories at 4.31 stars with only 12.4% average discount, indicating margin-friendly quality perception.

Prioritize Home & Kitchen quality fixes

Home&Kitchen is the lowest-rated sizeable top-level category at 4.04 stars and has the lowest median engagement among sizeable categories at 2,306 ratings.

Use electronics as engagement engine, not blanket promo target

Electronics contributes 15,778,848 total ratings, but its average discount is 50.8% and rating is 4.08; refine promotions to proven subcategories and hero SKUs.

Curate premium price tiers for satisfaction

The >₹5,000 segment has the highest average rating at 4.17 while carrying the lowest average discount at 33.5%.
