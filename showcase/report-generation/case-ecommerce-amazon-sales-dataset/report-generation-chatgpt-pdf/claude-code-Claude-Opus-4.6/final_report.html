5. Review Sentiment Snapshot

A keyword-frequency analysis of review titles and content reveals the dominant themes in customer feedback. This is a high-level qualitative overview based on keyword occurrence in the review corpus.

Positive Themes: Customers most frequently mention "good" (10,520 mentions), "quality" (3,214), and "nice" (1,925). Quality and value perception dominate positive sentiment.
Negative Themes: The most common concern keywords are "issue" (766 mentions), "problem" (434), and "bad" (405). Reliability and product defects are the primary pain points.

6. Strategic Recommendations

The following recommendations are derived directly from the data analysis above. Each is paired with its supporting evidence.

  1. Reassess Blanket Discounting Strategy The correlation between discount depth and customer rating is near zero (r = -0.155). Deeper discounts are not yielding proportionally higher satisfaction. Action: Shift from uniform deep discounting toward targeted, category-specific promotions. Reallocate discount budget to categories where promotional lift is measurable.
  2. Curate Underperforming Categories Home&Kitchen averages only 4.04/5.0, significantly below the catalog mean. Action: Audit product quality and seller standards in bottom-performing categories. Consider tightening listing requirements or establishing quality score thresholds for catalog inclusion.
  3. Double Down on Affordable, High-Quality SKUs The highest-engagement products (top 10% by reviews) average ₹2,334 in price and 4.17 in rating — they are affordable, well-rated items with moderate discounts. Action: Prioritize sourcing and promoting quality products in the ₹200–₹2,000 range, which represents the catalog's engagement sweet spot.
  4. Address Product Reliability Concerns Negative review keywords center on "issue" and "problem" — recurring themes of product defects and functional issues. Action: Implement post-purchase quality monitoring using review keyword alerts. Flag products with rising defect mentions for supplier review.
  5. Leverage Price-Segment Insights for Assortment Planning Higher-priced segments show modestly better ratings (r = +0.120), suggesting room to expand premium offerings without sacrificing satisfaction. Action: Use price-segment rating data to inform category expansion decisions. Invest in quality assurance across all segments rather than assuming premium price signals quality.
