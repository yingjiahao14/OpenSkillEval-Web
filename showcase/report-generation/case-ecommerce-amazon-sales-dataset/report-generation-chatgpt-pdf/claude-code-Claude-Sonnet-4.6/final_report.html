Strategic Recommendations

Five data-driven recommendations for category management, pricing optimization, and customer experience improvement. Each recommendation is directly supported by findings from the analysis above.

1

Restructure Deep-Discount Promotions — Cap Aggressive Discounts for Quality-Sensitive SKUs

The data shows a clear inverse relationship (r = −0.155) between discount depth and customer rating. Products discounted 81–100% average just 3.98 stars, versus 4.15 for those under 20% off. Deep discounts are eroding the perception of product quality without driving meaningful engagement gains (discount–volume corr. = +0.012).

Data: 81–100% bucket avg rating 3.98; 0–20% bucket avg rating 4.15; 439 products currently exceed 60% discount
2

Invest in Home&Kitchen Category Quality — Close the Gap vs. Peers

Home&Kitchen (448 products, 30.6% of catalog) trails both Electronics and Computers&Accessories in average rating (4.04 vs. 4.08–4.15) and engagement (avg 6,676 reviews vs. 17,137–29,998). The Heating, Cooling & Air Quality sub-category is the weakest performer at 3.99. A targeted assortment audit to remove or improve low-rated SKUs would lift the category average and reduce return-driven costs.

Data: Home&Kitchen avg rating 4.04 vs. Electronics 4.08, C&A 4.15; Heating sub-cat avg 3.99; avg rating count 6,676 vs. category peers
3

Address Product Reliability as the #1 Customer Complaint

Review content analysis shows 261 combined mentions of "not working," "defective," and "broken" — the single largest cluster of negative feedback. Combined with 265 damage/return mentions, product failure accounts for the majority of negative customer experience. Implementing stricter pre-listing quality testing and enforcing seller warranty compliance would directly address the pain driving the catalog's 4.7% sub-3.5 rating tail.

Data: "not working" 149x, "stopped" 108x, "defective" 63x in review content; 68 products rated below 3.5
4

Implement Catalog Hygiene Controls for Counterfeit & Low-Quality Listings

The word "fake" appears 21 times in customer reviews — a small but significant signal of counterfeit product listings. Combined with 288 "bad" mentions and 47 "worst" mentions, there is a subset of catalog listings actively harming brand trust. A systematic review of products rated below 3.5 (68 products) with cross-referencing of counterfeit-flagged reviews would identify the highest-priority removals.

Data: "fake" 21x in reviews; "bad" 288x; 68 products rated <3.5; 1 product rated below 2.0
5

Expand the OfficeProducts Catalog — Best Rating, Lowest Discount Pressure

OfficeProducts is the highest-rated category at 4.31 stars and operates at the lowest average discount of just 12.4% — meaning it delivers superior customer satisfaction without margin erosion. With only 31 products today it is significantly underrepresented relative to its quality profile. Expanding this category through curated assortment additions represents the best risk-adjusted growth opportunity in the catalog.

Data: OfficeProducts avg rating 4.31 (#1), avg discount 12.4% (lowest), product count 31 (vs. 448–526 for core categories)
