Pricing & Discount Strategy Analysis

Core finding for pricing teams: deeper markdowns are associated with slightly lower ratings, not higher ones (r=-0.155). Ratings are modestly higher for higher-priced items (actual price vs rating r=0.122), and discount depth does not drive more review volume (r=0.012).

Customer Engagement & Rating Distribution

The most-engaged SKUs are concentrated in Electronics, led by HDMI cables and entry smartphones, each with 300k–426k ratings and stable ~4.1–4.4 scores. High engagement clusters in utility, high-frequency purchase products.

Review Sentiment Snapshot

Positive language is dominated by product utility/value terms (good, quality, nice, best), while negatives are concentrated around reliability/friction (issue, bad, problem).

Strategic Recommendations

1) Shift from blanket markdowns to targeted promotions. Discount depth is not improving satisfaction (r=-0.155) and is neutral on engagement (r=0.012). Prioritize selective promos on price-elastic SKUs rather than broad deep discounts.
2) Protect and scale high-performing assortments in Computers&Accessories. This large category (453 products) combines strong ratings (4.15) with high discount responsiveness and healthy engagement (median 7,732 ratings).
3) Raise quality floor in Home&Kitchen. It has substantial assortment weight (448 products) but lower satisfaction (4.04) and weak engagement (median 2,306). Focus on quality control and content optimization before incremental discounting.
4) Replicate Electronics engagement playbook across adjacent categories. Electronics leads engagement (median 10,689 ratings). Emulate high-volume SKU patterns: clearer value messaging, proven utility products, and strong review-generation loops.
5) Prioritize premium-value positioning over aggressive discounting in higher price tiers. ₹10,000+ products show highest average rating (4.18) despite lower average discounting (38.70%), suggesting perceived quality/support may matter more than markdown depth.
