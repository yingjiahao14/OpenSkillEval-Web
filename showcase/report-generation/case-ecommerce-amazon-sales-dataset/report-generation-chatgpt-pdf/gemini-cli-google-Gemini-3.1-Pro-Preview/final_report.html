Strategic Recommendations

  1. Optimize Discount Strategy: With nearly zero correlation between deep discounts (>60%) and improved customer satisfaction, scale back extreme discounting to preserve margins.
  2. Investigate 'Car&Motorbike': This category has the lowest average rating. Conduct a deep dive into product quality and returns for these items.
  3. Promote High-Engagement Anchors: The top 5% most reviewed products drive massive traffic. Ensure consistent stock and utilize them as cross-selling anchors.
  4. Address Defect Sentiment: Establish automated monitoring for keywords like 'broken' and 'defective' to rapidly pull poorly-performing SKUs before they accumulate negative reviews.
