6. Strategic Recommendations

Data-driven actions synthesized from the analysis above.

1. Rationalize Deep Discounting (>60%)

Products with 60%+ discounts show declining ratings (4.08-4.02 avg) and the 80%+ tier has the lowest review volume. Pull back from margin-eroding promotions on products where discounts aren't generating satisfaction or engagement lift.

Supporting data: r = -0.16 discount-rating correlation; 80%+ discount products average only 9,954 reviews vs. 16,813 for the 10-19% bracket.

2. Prioritize Home & Kitchen Catalog Quality

Home & Kitchen is the lowest-rated major category (4.04) with the weakest engagement (median 2,306 reviews). Conduct vendor quality audits and consider culling underperforming SKUs to lift category health.

Supporting data: 448 products, avg rating 4.04 (vs. 4.10 catalog avg), median review count less than half of Electronics.

3. Invest in Premium Segment Expansion

Products priced ₹10,000+ show the highest average rating (4.20) and require the least discounting (32.4%). Expand assortment in high-performing premium sub-categories to improve overall catalog quality and margin.

Supporting data: ₹10,000+ segment: avg rating 4.20, avg discount 32.4%, 122 products.

4. Replicate Office Products Strategy

Office Products achieves the highest category rating (4.31) with the lowest average discount (12.4%), demonstrating that curated, needs-based categories thrive without heavy promotion. Apply this low-discount, high-quality curation model to adjacent categories.

Supporting data: 31 products, avg rating 4.31, avg discount 12.4% — highest satisfaction with lowest promotional cost.

5. Address the Headphones & Earbuds Quality Gap

This high-volume sub-category (66 products) has the lowest rating among major sub-categories (3.93) despite aggressive 59.5% average discounts. Partner with vendors to improve quality standards or delist chronic underperformers.

Supporting data: avg rating 3.93 (0.17 below catalog avg), avg discount 59.5% — worst satisfaction-to-discount ratio in the catalog.
