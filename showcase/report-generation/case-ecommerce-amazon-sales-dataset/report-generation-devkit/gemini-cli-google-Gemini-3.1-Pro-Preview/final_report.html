6. Strategic Recommendations

  1. Re-evaluate Discount Depth: With a correlation of near zero between discount percentage and customer rating, deep discounting is likely eroding margin without significantly improving customer satisfaction. Optimize promotional depth based on category elasticity rather than blanket discounts.
  2. Address Underperforming Categories: Categories like Home&Kitchen show significant variance in quality. Implement stricter catalog quality controls and review lower-rated ASINs in these segments.
  3. Leverage the "Value for Money" Sentiment: High engagement products cluster around practical items (cables, accessories) where reviews frequently mention "fast" and "value". Emphasize clear functional benefits and durable specifications in product descriptions.
  4. Protect the High-Rating Core: Over 70% of the catalog is rated 4.0 or above. Shift focus towards upselling and bundling top-tier products rather than heavily discounting lower-tier ones to move volume.
