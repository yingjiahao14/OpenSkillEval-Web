Executive Overview

This report analyzes 1,465 Amazon India products spanning 9 top-level categories. The catalog demonstrates strong overall quality: 75.8% of products carry ratings of 4.0 or above, with an average rating of 4.10/5.0.

The promotional environment is aggressive — the average discount is 47.7% (median 50%), indicating that nearly half the catalog is discounted by 50% or more. However, deeper discounts show a weak negative correlation with ratings (r = -0.155), suggesting that heavy discounting is not translating into higher customer satisfaction and may even signal lower-quality products.

Key Finding: The correlation between discount percentage and review volume is near zero (r = 0.0117), meaning discounts are not meaningfully driving more customer engagement. Management should re-evaluate whether deep discounting is delivering ROI beyond volume, as it appears to neither increase ratings nor significantly boost review counts.

Strategic Recommendations

1. Recalibrate Discount Strategy

Reduce reliance on deep discounts (60%+) which correlate with lower ratings. Shift promotional spend toward value-added bundles or loyalty rewards instead of price-only promotions.

Products at 80–100% discount average only 3.98 stars vs 4.17 at 0–20% discount (r = -0.155)

2. Prioritize Home & Kitchen Quality

Home&Kitchen — the catalog's third-largest category (448 products) — has the lowest average rating among major categories. Conduct supplier quality audits and curate underperformers.

Home&Kitchen avg rating: 4.04 vs catalog avg: 4.10

3. Address "Not Working" / Defective Complaints

Product functionality failures ("not working": 72 mentions, "defective": 21) are the most damaging review themes. Implement a defect-flagging system to auto-escalate products crossing a complaint threshold.

93 combined mentions of functionality failures across review titles

4. Invest in the Premium Segment

Products priced above ₹10K show the highest average rating (4.20). Expand and better merchandise the premium tier — these products drive satisfaction and likely higher margins with lower discount intensity.

Over ₹10K: 4.20 avg rating with only 122 products — underrepresented high-performer

5. Leverage Office Products as a Best-Practice Model

Office Products achieves the highest average rating (4.31) with the lowest average discount (12.4%). Study this category's vendor curation, product listing standards, and customer communication practices as a model for underperforming categories — proof that quality-first merchandising can outperform discount-heavy strategies.

Office Products: 4.31 rating at 12.4% avg discount — highest satisfaction, lowest promotional intensity
