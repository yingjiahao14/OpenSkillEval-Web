Performance by Price Segment

Key finding: The correlation between discount percentage and customer rating is −0.155 (weak negative). The correlation with review volume (log-transformed) is also negative at −0.106. Products discounted >60% average 4.06 stars and 21,347 reviews; products discounted <30% average 4.16 stars and 18,492 reviews. The extra engagement from deep discounting is marginal and comes at the cost of lower perceived quality.
Price segment insight: Luxury products (>₹10K) achieve the highest average rating (4.20) with the lowest discount intensity (32.4%). Budget products (<₹500) carry the deepest discounts (55.8%) and the lowest rating (4.07). Higher price points appear to signal quality that sustains satisfaction without aggressive promotions.

Strategic Recommendations

1
Reduce reliance on deep discounting in low-rating sub-categories. Electronics > Headphones (3.92 avg rating, 57.9% discount) and Electronics > Smart Watches (4.02, 69.8% discount) show that steep cuts do not salvage satisfaction. Reallocate promo spend toward quality assurance and listing optimization (better images, specs clarity) instead.
2
Invest in Home & Kitchen quality and marketing. Home & Kitchen has the lowest average rating (4.04) and the weakest engagement (6,689 avg reviews vs. 29,998 in Electronics). Room Heaters score just 3.83 — a priority for supplier audits and specification upgrades. Boosting this category’s content and review solicitation could close the engagement gap.
3
Protect the Office Products “quality premium” positioning. Office Products leads in rating (4.31) with minimal discounting (12.4%). This is a margin-friendly, high-satisfaction segment. Avoid promotional races here; instead, expand assortment depth and cross-sell into adjacent workspace categories.
4
Double down on budget “hero” SKUs that drive engagement. Top-reviewed products are sub-₹500 cables and accessories with broad compatibility. These SKUs generate massive review volume and organic visibility. Prioritize inventory depth, competitive pricing (not deeper discounts), and bundling for these high-frequency purchases.
5
Address heating and compatibility complaints in electronics. Heating (165 mentions) and compatibility (186 mentions) are the most actionable negative themes. Work with suppliers to improve thermal design and publish clearer compatibility matrices. Proactive FAQ updates can reduce negative review incidence and improve star ratings over time.
