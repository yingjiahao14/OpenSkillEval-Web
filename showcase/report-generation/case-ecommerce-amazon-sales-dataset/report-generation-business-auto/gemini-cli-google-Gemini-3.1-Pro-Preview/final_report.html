Pricing & Discount Strategy Analysis

Management requested insight on whether deeper discounts correlate with better ratings. The analysis shows a weak correlation (-0.155) between discount percentage and customer rating, and a weak correlation (0.011) between discount and review volume.

This indicates that heavy discounting does not automatically guarantee higher customer satisfaction or engagement.

Customer Engagement & Rating Distribution

The catalog leans heavily positive, with 75.8% of products scoring 4.0 or above. However, engagement levels (rating counts) vary across a massive logarithmic scale. High engagement products tend to cluster between 3.8 and 4.4 ratings.

Review Sentiment Snapshot

A qualitative snapshot of review titles highlights recurring themes. Positive reviews frequently focus on "value", "quality", and "speed" (fast charging/delivery). Negative themes center on products being "bad", "poor" quality, or a "waste" of money.

Strategic Recommendations

  • 1. Re-evaluate Heavy Discounting for Margin Recovery With a correlation of -0.155 between discounts and ratings, deeper discounts are not buying higher customer satisfaction. Consider reducing promotional intensity (currently averaging 47.7%) on high-rating products to recover margin.
  • 2. Cleanse Bottom-Performing Sub-Categories Sub-categories highlighted in the "Worst Performing" chart consistently drag down catalog quality. Initiate a review of these segments to delist defective items or mandate quality improvements from suppliers.
  • 3. Capitalize on the "Value" Sentiment "Good" and "Value" are the top positive drivers. Marketing and product titles should emphasize value-for-money and durability ("sturdy", "fast"), as these resonate most strongly with the Amazon India customer base.
  • 4. Leverage High-Engagement Mid-Tier Products Products clustering around a 3.8-4.2 rating often have the highest engagement volume. These are prime candidates for incremental improvements—small fixes to address recurring "poor quality" complaints could push them into the 4.3+ tier, drastically improving conversion.
