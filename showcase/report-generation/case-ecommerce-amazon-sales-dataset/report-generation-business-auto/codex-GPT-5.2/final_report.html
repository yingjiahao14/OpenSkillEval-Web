Strategic Recommendations

  1. Rebalance discount strategy away from “deep discount = higher satisfaction.” discount% ↔ rating correlation is -0.156 (negative), so discounts should be treated as a conversion lever—not a rating lever.
  2. Audit extreme discount bands for quality or expectation gaps. The 81–90% band averages 3.95 rating vs 4.24 in 0% discount; investigate product quality, fit/finish, and listing accuracy among deep-discount SKUs.
  3. Prioritize engagement-heavy categories for CX and content improvements. 35.9% of products are in Electronics and this category has the highest typical engagement (median rating_count among major categories).
  4. Use price-band benchmarks to guide assortment positioning. ₹5k+ is the highest-rated price band (avg rating 4.17) and operates with lower average discounts (33.5%)—suggesting value can come from quality/features, not only promotions.
  5. Target “problem sub-categories” with focused curation. Lowest-rated sub-category (min 15 products) is Electronics · Headphones,Earbuds&Accessories (avg rating 3.93); review assortment and strengthen QA gates and product content.
Notes: Prices are in Indian Rupees (₹). Correlations are Pearson. All numbers are computed directly from benchmark/data.csv with string-cleaning for currency, percentages, and counts.
