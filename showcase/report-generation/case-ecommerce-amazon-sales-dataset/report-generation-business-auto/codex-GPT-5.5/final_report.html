Executive Overview

The catalog is broadly healthy, with an average product rating of 4.10 and 75.8% of products rated at least 4.0. However, promotional intensity is high: the average discount is 47.7%, and the data does not show evidence that deeper discounts improve satisfaction.

Best major category: OfficeProducts leads on satisfaction at 4.31 average rating across 31 products.
Highest engagement: Electronics has the highest median rating count among major categories at 10,689.
Watch area: Home&Kitchen is the lowest-rated major category at 4.04, with median engagement of 2,306.
Pricing takeaway: discount percentage has a weak negative correlation with rating (Pearson r = -0.155; Spearman ρ = -0.146) and essentially no positive relationship with review volume (Pearson r = 0.012; Spearman ρ = -0.097). Deep discounts appear more likely to erode margin than lift satisfaction.

Review Sentiment Snapshot

The dataset stores multiple review titles and contents inside product-level fields, so this snapshot uses transparent keyword counts rather than claiming review-level sentiment classification. Positive language is led by value and quality terms; negative language clusters around issues, working condition, returns, and product failure.

Positive themes: “good” (10,520), “quality” (3,214), “value” (1,048), and “worth” (839) reinforce value-for-money positioning.
Negative themes: “issue” (766), “problem” (434), “not working” (221), and “return” (160) point to reliability and fulfillment experience.
Interpretation guardrail: keyword frequencies are directional because product rows can contain multiple comma-separated reviews and repeated terms.

Strategic Recommendations

1
Reduce blanket deep discounting; shift to targeted promotions.
Discount depth correlates negatively with rating (Pearson r = -0.155), and the 81–100% discount band has the lowest average rating at 3.98. Preserve margin unless a promotion has a clear traffic or inventory objective.
2
Audit Electronics audio and wearable sub-categories before scaling promotions.
Electronics Headphones/Earbuds & Accessories averages 3.93 with 40,296 median rating count and 59.5% average discount; Wearable Technology averages 4.02 with 69.8% average discount. High visibility plus lower ratings makes these priority quality-improvement areas.
3
Use Office Products and battery/accessory patterns as quality benchmarks.
Office Products leads major categories at 4.31 average rating with only 12.4% average discount. Electronics General Purpose Batteries & Battery Chargers average 4.35, showing strong satisfaction without extreme discounting.
4
Improve Home & Kitchen catalog curation and review generation.
Home & Kitchen is the lowest-rated major category at 4.04 and has the lowest median rating count among major categories at 2,306. Prioritize low-rated SKUs, clearer specifications, and post-purchase review capture.
5
Separate engagement winners from satisfaction winners in category planning.
Electronics has the highest major-category median rating count (10,689) but lower average rating (4.08) than Computers & Accessories (4.15). Use both rating and rating-count thresholds when selecting hero products.
