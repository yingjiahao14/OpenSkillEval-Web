Executive Overview

Catalog quality is broadly healthy: 75.8% of products are rated 4.0+, with an average rating of 4.10 across 1,464 valid ratings.
Discount depth is not buying satisfaction: discount percentage has a weak negative Pearson correlation with rating (r=-0.155, n=1,464).

Among major categories with at least 30 products, OfficeProducts leads on customer rating at 4.31, while Home&Kitchen trails at 4.04. Electronics leads engagement with a median 10,689 ratings per product.

Review Sentiment Snapshot

This snapshot uses direct keyword counts across product-level review titles and contents. It is not a full NLP sentiment model, but it highlights repeated customer language without inventing sentiment scores.

Positive language is broad: 99.9% of products include at least one tracked positive term; “good” appears in 1,452 product review fields and “quality” in 1,059.
Friction is still visible: 73.5% of products include at least one tracked negative term; “issue” appears in 444, “problem” in 294, and “not working” in 158 product review fields.

Common positive themes center on value, quality, ease of use, and speed. Negative themes point to reliability and defect experiences: issues/problems, not working, damage, heating, returns, and slow performance.

Strategic Recommendations

1
Do not use deeper blanket discounts as a satisfaction lever. Discount percentage correlates weakly negatively with rating (r=-0.155) and barely with rating count (r=0.012); shift promotion planning toward targeted SKUs where discounts defend conversion rather than assuming satisfaction uplift.
2
Prioritize category quality interventions in Home&Kitchen. It is the weakest major category by average rating (4.04) and has only 67.6% of products rated 4.0+, versus 75.8% catalog-wide.
3
Protect and scale winning playbooks in OfficeProducts and Electronics. OfficeProducts leads major-category rating at 4.31; Electronics leads engagement with a median 10,689 ratings per product.
4
Audit reliability and fulfillment signals in review-heavy SKUs. Negative terms appear in 73.5% of product review fields, led by “issue” (444), “problem” (294), “heat” (249), and “not working” (158); these are operational quality cues, not just marketing concerns.
5
Use high-engagement products as merchandising anchors. Products above 39,555 ratings average 4.17 stars and are concentrated in Electronics and Computers&Accessories; feature these as traffic drivers while monitoring duplicate variants that may fragment catalog reporting.
