Review Sentiment Snapshot
Common themes extracted from 1,465 review title sets across the catalog

The ratio of positive-to-negative term mentions in review titles is approximately 9:1, consistent with the high-quality catalog composition (75.8% products rated ≥4.0★). The most prevalent positive themes revolve around value, product quality, and reliability; negative themes cluster around performance issues and product quality concerns.

✓ Top Positive Themes in Review Titles

good (3,936) best (508) nice (902) quality (761) worth (362) great (261) perfect (152) fast (154) awesome (134) amazing (114) recommend (55) durable (53) love (48)

"Good" alone accounts for 3,936 review titles. Value-for-money ("worth"), charging speed ("fast"), and build ("quality", "durable") are recurrent satisfaction drivers — especially relevant for the cable and accessories sub-categories.

✗ Top Negative Themes in Review Titles

bad (117) not working (76) issue (76) poor (46) cheap (42) problem (39) worst (36) slow (32) damage (31) waste (21) defective (21) fake (13)

"Not working" (72 exact-phrase matches) and "defective" (21) point to functional failures — most acute in heater and remote control sub-categories. "Fake" (13) signals counterfeit concerns that carry brand-trust risk.

Key Positive Driver
Value-for-money is the primary satisfaction signal. Customers rate products positively when perceived quality meets or exceeds price expectations — not when discounts are highest.
Key Negative Driver
Functional failures (not working, defective, damage) dominate complaints. These align with the lowest-rated sub-categories (Remote Controls, Fan Heaters) and suggest assortment quality issues, not pricing friction.
Strategic Recommendations
Five data-grounded actions for category managers — each anchored to a specific finding
1

Audit and triage the bottom-rated sub-categories before the next promotional cycle

Remote Controls (avg 3.80★, 49 SKUs), Fan Heaters (3.81★), and Electric Heaters (3.89★) are dragging catalog quality. Both the low ratings and the low review counts (median: 151–425 per product) indicate buyer dissatisfaction combined with thin demand. Evaluate each SKU for replacement with higher-rated alternatives or delist entirely if supplier quality cannot be improved.

Data: Remote Controls avg 3.80★; Fan Heaters avg 3.81★ · median review count: 151
2

Reframe promotional strategy: use discounts selectively on proven high-quality products only

The discount–rating correlation is −0.155, and the 80–100% discount bucket averages 3.98★ — the worst of any tier. Blanket deep discounts do not improve satisfaction and may signal quality concerns to buyers. Reserve promotions for products already rated ≥4.2★ to protect brand perception. Consider redirecting promotional budget in Computers & Accessories (54% avg discount) toward content and review quality programs instead.

Data: 80–100% discount tier: avg 3.98★ vs. 4.15★ for 0–20% tier · corr = −0.155
3

Double down on the Office Products category — the highest-quality, lowest-promoted segment

Office Products achieves the highest avg rating (4.31★) with only 12.4% average discount and 22.6% of SKUs rated ≥4.5★ — indicating genuine product-market fit without price promotion. Expanding SKU count and deepening this category is likely to improve overall catalog quality metrics with minimal promotional cost.

Data: Office Products avg 4.31★, avg discount 12.4%, 22.6% SKUs ≥4.5★
4

Invest in engagement infrastructure for Home & Kitchen — it is underperforming on reviews relative to its size

Home & Kitchen is the second-largest category (448 SKUs) but has the lowest median review count (2,306 per product) — less than a quarter of the Electronics median (10,689). Low review volume reduces buyer confidence and search ranking. A targeted post-purchase review solicitation program for this category could significantly improve visibility and conversion, given the category's reasonable avg rating of 4.04★.

Data: Home & Kitchen median reviews: 2,306 vs. Electronics: 10,689 — 4.6× gap
5

Address counterfeit and functional-failure signals in review content before they scale

Review titles contain 13 "fake" mentions and 72 "not working" mentions — a small but reputationally significant signal. These issues, combined with "defective" (21) and "damage" (31) terms, are disproportionately concentrated in lower-rated categories. Implement enhanced supplier quality checks and returns-data monitoring for any sub-category with avg rating <4.0★ and more than 20 SKUs. Proactive intervention prevents review score deterioration and potential Amazon policy risk.

Data: 72 "not working" · 21 "defective" · 13 "fake" in review titles
