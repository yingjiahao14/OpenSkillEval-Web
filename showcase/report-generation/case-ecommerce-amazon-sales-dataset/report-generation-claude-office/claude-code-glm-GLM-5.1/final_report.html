1. Executive Overview

This report analyzes 1,465 Amazon India products across 9 top-level categories. After data cleaning (1,462 products with complete metrics), the catalog demonstrates a generally positive customer response with an overall average rating of 4.10/5.0.

6. Strategic Recommendations

1. Reassess Deep Discounting Strategy — Discounts Don't Drive Satisfaction
The correlation between discount depth and customer rating is r = -0.156, effectively flat to negative. Deep discounts increase purchase volume (rating count) but do not improve satisfaction. Reduce blanket discounting and redirect promotional spend toward product quality improvements and targeted loyalty programs.
Data: Discount-Rating correlation r = -0.156 | Discount-Rating Count correlation r = 0.011 | Average discount: 47.7%
2. Audit Underperforming Categories for Catalog Quality
Car&Motorbike products average only 3.80 stars. Conduct a catalog audit to identify and delist consistently low-rated SKUs. Tighten vendor quality requirements and enforce minimum rating thresholds for continued listing.
Data: Car&Motorbike avg rating = 3.80 vs. catalog average 4.10 | Gap = 0.30 stars
3. Optimize Category-Level Discount Allocation
Health&PersonalCare carries 53.0% average discounts yet only achieves 4.00 ratings — a clear case of margin erosion without satisfaction gains. Meanwhile, Toys&Games achieves 4.30 ratings with only 0.0% discounts. Reallocate discount budgets from low-efficiency to high-efficiency categories.
Data: Health&PersonalCare discount=53.0%, rating=4.00 | Toys&Games discount=0.0%, rating=4.30
4. Leverage High-Engagement Products for Cross-Selling
The top 10 products by review volume demonstrate that affordable accessories (cables, chargers) generate outsized engagement. Use these high-traffic products as entry points for cross-selling higher-margin items within the same category.
Data: Top 10 products by engagement average 374,053 ratings vs. catalog median 5,179
5. Address Durability & Quality Concerns in Negative Reviews
Negative sentiment is dominated by complaints about products "stopped" working, being "waste" money, and "poor" quality. These durability failures erode trust and increase return costs. Implement stricter incoming quality checks and work with vendors on reliability testing, especially in categories with the highest negative sentiment rates.
Data: 1.8% of products have negative-leaning reviews | Top negative keywords: "stopped," "waste," "poor"
