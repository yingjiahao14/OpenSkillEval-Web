    Data point: Categories such as Electronics, Home&Kitchen, Health&PersonalCare score below the catalog average rating of 4.10.
    Action: Initiate assortment reviews, tighten supplier quality gates, and increase inspection sampling in these categories.

    Data point: Review titles most frequently praise 'good' and 'quality' (7601 positive mentions vs 492 negative), but complaints around 'poor', 'defective', and 'waste' signal recurring quality risks.
