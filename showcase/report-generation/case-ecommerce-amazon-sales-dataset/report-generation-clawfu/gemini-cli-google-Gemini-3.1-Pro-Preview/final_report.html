Pricing & Discount Strategy Analysis

Management requested an analysis on whether deeper discounts correlate with better ratings. Our findings show a correlation coefficient of -0.16, indicating a negligible relationship between the magnitude of the discount and the customer rating.

Insight: Deeper discounts do not automatically guarantee higher customer satisfaction or better reviews. Other factors, such as product quality and brand perception, likely play a much stronger role.

Strategic Recommendations

  1. Re-evaluate Discount Strategies: Given the low correlation (-0.16) between discount percentage and ratings, we recommend testing smaller discount thresholds to preserve margin without sacrificing customer satisfaction. Focus discounts on driving volume for highly-rated products rather than trying to rescue poorly-rated ones.
  2. Audit the Lowest Performing Categories: The Home&Kitchen category has the lowest average rating. Implement a strict quality audit for these products. Consider delisting items consistently rated below 3.5 to protect brand reputation.
  3. Capitalize on High-Engagement Products: The top 5 engaged products drive significant traffic. Ensure adequate inventory levels for these items and use them as anchor products for cross-selling related accessories.
  4. Focus on Product Quality over Price Tactics: The data shows that "High-Rating Share" is strong (75.8%). Marketing campaigns should emphasize product quality, features, and durability rather than relying primarily on discount messaging.
