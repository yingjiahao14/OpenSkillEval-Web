Section 6
Strategic Recommendations
Five data-backed recommendations for category management, pricing optimization, and customer experience. Each cites a specific finding.
1

Reassess Home&Kitchen Assortment

With 448 SKUs but the catalog's lowest high-rating share (67.8%) and lowest average rating (4.04), Home&Kitchen has a structural quality problem. Audit the bottom-rated products in this category and apply a minimum rating threshold (e.g., ≥3.8) for continued listing.

Data: 67.8% high-rating share vs. 83.4% for Computers&Accessories. 32% of H&K products are below 4.0 rating.
2

Reduce Extreme Discounting on Low-Quality Products

Deep discounts (81–100% off) are associated with the worst average ratings (3.98) and represent 59 products. These promotions are likely eroding margin without delivering customer satisfaction. Review this cohort and consider delisting or improving sourcing before re-promoting.

Data: 81–100% discount tier avg rating = 3.98 vs. catalog avg 4.1. Correlation of discount % and rating = −0.155.
3

Invest in Premium Price Tier Expansion

Products priced ₹10,001+ achieve the highest average rating (4.20) with the lowest average discount (32.4%) — indicating they command full-price purchases and satisfy customers. Expanding SKU depth in this segment could improve catalog quality metrics while protecting margins.

Data: ₹10,001+ tier avg rating = 4.20 vs. ₹0–500 tier = 4.07. Discount gap: 55.8% vs. 32.4%.
4

Scale OfficeProducts Category Depth

OfficeProducts achieves a 100% high-rating share and avg rating of 4.31 — the strongest in the catalog — but represents only 31 products (2.1% of catalog). This is the clearest category opportunity: expand SKU count while rating quality is high.

Data: OfficeProducts 100% products rated ≥4.0. Avg rating 4.31 with lowest avg discount (12.4%), suggesting full-price viability.
5

Address Durability & Damage Complaints to Lift Tail-End Ratings

Sentiment analysis surfaces "stopped", "damaged", "issue", and "return" as recurring negative signals — pointing to product durability failures and logistics damage as primary detractors. Implementing improved packaging standards and sourcing quality controls for flagged categories (primarily Home&Kitchen and deep-discount tier) could shift the 24.2% of below-4.0-rated products into the high-quality tier and improve repeat purchase rates.

Data: 24.2% of products rated below 4.0 (355 products). Dominant negative review terms: "issue", "damaged", "stopped", "return". Deep-discount tier (81–100%) has disproportionate quality issues.
