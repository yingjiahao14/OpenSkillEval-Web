Customer Engagement & Rating Distribution

Engagement is concentrated in a limited subset of products/categories, while the median rating count remains much lower, indicating a long-tail catalog with uneven visibility.

Review Sentiment Snapshot

Keyword pattern scan over review titles/content shows more positive than negative expressions: 6731 positive-theme hits vs 1719 negative-theme hits.

Strategic Recommendations

  1. Discount depth vs rating correlation is -0.155; avoid blanket markdowns and target discounts where rating and engagement are lagging.
  2. Discount depth vs review-volume correlation is 0.012; use promotions primarily to drive visibility and review accumulation.
  3. Scale assortment and inventory support in top-rated, high-engagement categories.
  4. Launch corrective plans for lowest-rated categories (listing clarity, quality checks, and price-positioning fixes).
  5. Use price-band governance to protect margin in premium bands and stay competitive in high-volume lower bands.
