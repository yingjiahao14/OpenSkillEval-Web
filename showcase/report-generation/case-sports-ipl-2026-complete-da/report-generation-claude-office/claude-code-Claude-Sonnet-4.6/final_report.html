Analyst Notes & Caveats

  • Dataset covers 15 matches (3,284 deliveries) — a partial-season snapshot. Trends may shift as more matches are played.
  • Economy rates are computed off legal deliveries only (wides excluded from over count).
  • Balls faced by batters exclude wides (which do not count as a ball faced by the batsman).
  • Strike rate = (runs / balls faced) × 100. Players with fewer than ~30 balls faced should be interpreted with caution.
  • Dot ball definition: deliveries where both runs_of_bat = 0 and extras = 0.
  • Phase definitions: Powerplay = overs 1–6, Middle Overs = overs 7–15, Death Overs = overs 16–20.
  • All figures derived directly from data.csv — no imputation or external data used.
