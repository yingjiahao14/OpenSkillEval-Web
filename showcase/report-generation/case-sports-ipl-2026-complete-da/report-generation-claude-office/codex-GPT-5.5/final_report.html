Analyst Notes & Methodology

No fabrication Every number is computed from /app/benchmark/data.csv. Legal balls exclude wides and no-balls. RPO/economy = total runs divided by legal balls × 6. Boundary percentage counts deliveries where runs_of_bat is 4 or 6. Dot balls require runs_of_bat=0 and extras=0. Wickets use non-empty player_dismissed. Phases are Powerplay overs 0–5, Middle overs 6–14, and Death overs 15–19 based on the 0-indexed over field.
