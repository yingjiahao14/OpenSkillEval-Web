Methodology and definitions

Calculation notes

  • Total runs = runs_of_bat + extras; bowler runs = runs_of_bat + wide + noballs.
  • Legal balls exclude wides and no-balls; over-based rates use legal balls divided by six.
  • Powerplay = overs 0–5, middle overs = 6–14, death overs = 15–19 based on the integer part of over.
  • Bowler wickets exclude run-outs from wicket credit; team wickets use every non-empty player_dismissed.
  • Boundary percentage KPI follows the brief exactly: boundaries as a percentage of all rows/deliveries, not legal balls only.
