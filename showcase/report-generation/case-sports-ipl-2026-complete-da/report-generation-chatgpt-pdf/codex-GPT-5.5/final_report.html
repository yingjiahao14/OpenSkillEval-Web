Method Notes

All figures are derived from /app/benchmark/data.csv. Runs use runs_of_bat + extras. Legal balls exclude wides and no-balls. Strike rate is batter runs per 100 legal balls faced. Economy uses total runs conceded per legal over. Dot balls are deliveries where runs_of_bat = 0 and extras = 0. Bowler wicket tables exclude run-outs; dismissal charts include all recorded wicket types. The data covers an early partial-season sample of 15 matches, so venue and team signals should be refreshed as new matches are added.

Generated as a self-contained HTML analytics report for cricket analysts and team strategists. No statistics are fabricated; all rankings and values are computed from the provided CSV.
