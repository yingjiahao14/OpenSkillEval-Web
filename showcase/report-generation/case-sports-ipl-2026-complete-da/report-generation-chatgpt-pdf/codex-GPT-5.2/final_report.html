Appendix

Methodology & Definitions

  • Runs: runs_total = runs_of_bat + extras.
  • Dot ball: runs_of_bat = 0 and extras = 0.
  • Boundary: runs_of_bat is 4 or 6.
  • Wicket: a delivery where player_dismissed is non-empty.
  • Phases: Powerplay = overs 0–5, Middle = 6–14, Death = 15–20 (0-indexed overs).
  • Bowler economy: runs_conceded / (legal_balls/6), where legal balls exclude wides and no-balls.
