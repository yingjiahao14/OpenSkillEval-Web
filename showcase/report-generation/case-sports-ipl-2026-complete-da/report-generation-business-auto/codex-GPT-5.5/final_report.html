Batting Performance Analysis

Batting value is concentrated in players combining volume with boundary frequency. The leaderboard shows that the most useful fantasy and tactical profiles are not just run accumulators: several leading players clear 20% boundary-ball rates while maintaining strike rates above 155.

  • Fastest high-volume batting: Vaibhav Sooryavanshi scores 249.0 per 100 balls with a 42.9% boundary rate, the most explosive profile among the top run-getters.
  • Run volume: Yashasvi Jaiswal leads the batting table with 170 runs from 104 legal balls.
  • Wicket pressure: Ravi Bishnoi tops the bowling wicket list with 7 wickets in 9.0 overs, striking every 7.7 legal balls.
  • Control benchmark: Rashid Khan concedes 5.33 RPO across 12.0 overs, the best economy among bowlers with at least six overs.

Methodology Notes

  • All calculations derive directly from /app/benchmark/data.csv; no external results, names or match outcomes were added.
  • Legal balls exclude wides and no-balls; economy and strike rate use legal balls.
  • Match phases are Powerplay overs 0-5, Middle overs 6-14 and Death overs 15-19.
  • Dot balls require runs_of_bat = 0 and extras = 0.
  • Boundary percentage counts deliveries where runs_of_bat is 4 or 6.
