Appendix: Method Notes & Limitations

  • The dataset is synthetic (trend-based simulation). Treat 2025–2035 as scenario projections, not measured outcomes.
  • “Job survival rate” is computed as the share of records with job_survival_class ≥ 1.
  • Salary is expressed in USD and not adjusted for PPP or inflation; interpret cross-country comparisons accordingly.
  • Correlations (Pearson/Spearman) describe association, not causation.
