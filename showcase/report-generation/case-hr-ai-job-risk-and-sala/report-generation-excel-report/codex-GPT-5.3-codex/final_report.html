Strategic Recommendations

  • Prioritize premium retention and internal mobility paths for lower-risk, higher-paid technical tracks.
  • Accelerate reskilling from high-risk pathways (notably Business Analyst) into adjacent low-risk skill families.
  • Use country-level salary-risk-openings profiles to rebalance talent placement and near-term hiring budgets.
  • Institutionalize annual risk-openings reviews to trigger proactive redeployment before displacement risk compounds.

Method & Limitations

  • All metrics come directly from data.csv; no fabricated values were used.
  • Analysis window is 2015–2035 as present in data.
  • Findings are descriptive trend analysis for strategy support, not deterministic forecasts.
