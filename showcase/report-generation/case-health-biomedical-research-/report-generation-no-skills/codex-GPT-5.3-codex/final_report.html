Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

  • 2024 dominance reflects indexing lag. Raw declines in 2025–2026 are expected partial-window effects, not necessarily reduced research activity.
  • Open access is policy material. Overall OA is 65.1%, with year and month variation relevant for mandates and compliance tracking.
  • Geographic concentration remains high. Top 10 countries contribute 48.1% of outputs, supporting targeted capacity investments.
  • Collaboration is team-based. Median author count of 6.0 indicates sustained multi-author research structures.
  • Acceleration metrics outperform static popularity. MoM growth in major topics identifies momentum areas for funding rebalancing.

Method Notes & Limitations

  • All metrics are derived directly from /app/benchmark/data.csv; no imputation or external normalization was applied.
  • Comparisons across 2024, 2025, and early 2026 are affected by indexing lag and partial-year coverage.
  • Missing major-topic values are labeled Unknown/Not Indexed; newer publications may be under-indexed for MeSH.
  • research_type is multi-label and is exploded on semicolon delimiters for distribution counts.
