Data Integrity Notes

  • Row count mismatch The provided benchmark/data.csv contains 4,886 rows (not 126,832). All figures in this report are computed from the provided file only.
  • Indexing lag 2024 dominates volume (expected per dataset brief). Comparisons across years should prioritize rates/normalized indicators.
  • Unknown months 754 records have unknown publication month and are excluded from monthly time-series charts (but included in yearly totals).
  • MeSH completeness 2,303 records have missing major_topic and/or keywords, limiting topic trend analysis, especially for 2025–2026.

Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

  1. Time-series interpretation must account for indexing and metadata completeness. 2024 contains 3,924/4,886 records (80.3%). Monthly charts also exclude 754 unknown-month records; MeSH indexing is missing for 2,303 records, disproportionately affecting 2025–2026.
  2. Open access is substantial and trackable monthly. Overall OA rate is 65.1% (via PMC flag). For policy monitoring, year-level OA rates are more robust than month-level rates in low-volume months.
  3. Geographic concentration is meaningful. The top 10 countries contribute 49.7% of all publications in this dataset; this concentration can guide partnership and capacity-building strategies.
  4. Topic intelligence is currently constrained by sparse late-window MeSH. While top major topics include COVID-19, Type 2 diabetes, and major cancers, month-over-month “emerging topic” KPIs cannot be estimated reliably for 2025–2026 from this file. Consider supplementing with title/abstract NLP topic modeling to reduce reliance on MeSH lag.
  5. Collaboration norms appear stable across years. Median authors per paper is 6 in 2024, 2025, and 2026 within this dataset, suggesting collaboration intensity is not shifting strongly in this sample.

Methods (high-level)

  • All computations derive exclusively from benchmark/data.csv.
  • Monthly time series use month_year where month is known (e.g., Jan-2024); Unknown-YYYY rows are excluded from monthly charts.
  • MeSH topic analysis uses major_topic and keywords when present; missing values are treated as “not indexed”.
  • Research-type counts are exploded on ; and |, so totals can exceed the number of publications.
