Executive Summary (Key Takeaways)

Data integrity: all figures below are computed directly from /app/benchmark/data.csv. No external estimates were used.
  • Strong indexing skew toward 2024: 2024 accounts for 80.3% of records (2025: 15.1%, 2026: 4.6%). Treat 2025–2026 counts as incomplete due to indexing lag.
  • Open access is high overall (overall 65.1%), with year-level rates of 64.1% (2024), 74.1% (2025), and 53.8% (2026). The 2026 value is based on fewer indexed records and should be interpreted cautiously.
  • MeSH completeness is a major limitation for newer records: 47.1% of publications have no major_topic (likely not yet indexed).
  • Geographic concentration: China contributes 21.0% of publications (first-author affiliation), and the United States contributes 10.7%. The top-10 countries account for 52.4% of output (after basic country normalization).
  • Collaboration proxy: median authors per paper is 6; the 75th percentile is 9, indicating a long tail of larger teams.
