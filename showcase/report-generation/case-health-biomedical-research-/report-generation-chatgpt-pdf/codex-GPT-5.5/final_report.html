Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

The strongest policy signal is not a simple rise or fall in output; it is a partial-window dataset shaped by PubMed indexing lag, concentrated country contributions, majority open-access availability, and incomplete MeSH indexing for newer records.

Temporal skew dominates interpretation
The local file contains 4,886 records, while the dataset documentation describes 126,832 abstracts; all calculations in this report use only `/app/benchmark/data.csv`. The observed year mix is heavily weighted to 2024 (80.3% of records), so 2025–2026 should be interpreted as partial/indexing-lagged windows rather than full-year declines.
Open access is a majority publishing mode
65.1% of records are flagged open access overall, with annual rates of 2024: 64.1%, 2025: 74.1%, 2026: 53.8%. This makes OA monitoring a central policy KPI, not a peripheral journal characteristic.
Country concentration is material
The top 10 harmonized countries account for 53.6% of all records. China contributes 22.1% and the USA 11.1%, after harmonizing obvious country-name variants.
Emerging-topic acceleration is visible but sparse
The fastest major-topic month-over-month increase meeting the reporting threshold is Molecular Dynamics Simulation: 1 to 4 records in Feb 2024 (5 total indexed records). Because 47.1% of records lack MeSH fields, these accelerators are early indexing signals rather than definitive prevalence estimates.
Collaboration intensity is moderately high
The median publication lists 6 authors (IQR 4–9), with country and research-type differences that can guide partnership and capacity-building strategies.

Open Access Adoption & Journal Landscape

The overall open-access rate is 65.1%. By publication year, rates are 2024: 64.1%, 2025: 74.1%, 2026: 53.8%. Among known-month records, the first observed month (Jan 2024) has an OA rate of 72.4%; the latest observed month (Mar 2026) has an OA rate of 23.5%. Month-to-month rates can be volatile in sparse 2026 months, so the policy-relevant readout is continued monitoring rather than over-interpreting a single month.

Methods & Data Integrity Notes

  • Source: `/app/benchmark/data.csv`, with dataset documentation from `/app/benchmark/source_brief.md` and report requirements from `/app/benchmark/task_input.json`.
  • Row-count discrepancy: The documentation describes 126,832 records, but the accessible file contains 4,886 rows. No values were scaled or imputed to match the documentation.
  • Monthly analysis: Monthly trend charts include only records with pub_month_num from 1 to 12; 754 records with unknown month are excluded only from month-level calculations.
  • Country analysis: Obvious aliases were harmonized for China, USA, United Kingdom, and South Korea. The country field represents first-author affiliation only.
  • Topic analysis: MeSH keyword terms were split on semicolons. Missing MeSH fields were not backfilled from abstracts or titles.
  • Open access: The open_access boolean was treated as the source of truth for PubMed Central availability.
