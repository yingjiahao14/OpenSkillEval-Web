Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

  • 2024 dominates output (3,924/4,886; 80.3%), consistent with known indexing lag; treat 2025–2026 as partial windows.
  • Open access is high at 65.1% overall and should be tracked monthly as a policy-sensitive indicator.
  • Geographic concentration is substantial: top 10 countries account for 48.1% of output.
  • Median collaboration size is 6.0 authors per publication, with a broad tail in team size.
  • Several major topics show strong month-over-month acceleration; momentum complements absolute volume in funding prioritization.
