Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

  • Indexing-lag dominates the apparent time series. 2024 contains 3,924 of 4,886 records; interpret 2025–2026 counts as incomplete coverage rather than a true output decline.
  • Open access is policy-relevant and time-variant in this dataset. Overall OA is 65.1%; monthly OA rate fluctuates materially across the observed window.
  • Country concentration remains high. The top 10 first-author countries account for 53.6% of publications, suggesting funding and capacity signals are still concentrated in a relatively small set of systems.
  • Topic acceleration signals are sensitive to sparse baselines. The highest observed month-over-month growth events often start from very small counts (e.g., 1 → 2+), so treat “emerging” flags as early indicators, not stable trajectories.
  • MeSH coverage is incomplete for recent records. 47.1% of records have no major_topic (marked as “Unindexed”), limiting topic attribution especially for the newest months.
