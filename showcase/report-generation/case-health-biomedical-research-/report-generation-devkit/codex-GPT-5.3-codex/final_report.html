Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

  • Volume concentration remains heavily backloaded to 2024, with 3,924 records (80.3%) versus 737 in 2025 and 225 through March 2026; year-on-year comparisons should be normalized for indexing lag.
  • Open-access penetration is substantial (65.1%) and should be treated as a policy lever, with monthly tracking useful for monitoring funder-aligned dissemination behavior.
  • Research output is geographically concentrated: top 10 countries account for 48.1%, suggesting strategic partnerships and capacity-building outside core hubs may diversify global contribution.
  • Topic momentum is distinct from topic size: emerging areas with high short-window growth (last 3 months vs prior 3 months) indicate where targeted funding can capture acceleration.
  • Collaboration signal is stable at median 6 authors, indicating multi-author team science remains the dominant production mode across publication types.
Important caveat: the dataset documentation explicitly states temporal imbalance by design (indexing accumulation in 2024), and unknown/missing metadata exists for some fields.
