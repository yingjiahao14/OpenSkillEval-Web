Publication Volume & Temporal Distribution

The publication time series shows strong 2024 dominance (indexing accumulation effect), with lower volumes in 2025 and early 2026. Monthly analysis excludes unknown months from trend lines to avoid calendar distortion and reports them separately.

Geographic Landscape of Biomedical Research

Country output is concentrated among a small group of high-volume contributors. The top-10 share quantifies this concentration and informs concentration risk and partnership opportunity assessment.

Research Topic & MeSH Keyword Analysis

Topic prevalence and acceleration are analyzed using major_topic. Emerging topic growth is computed as average month-over-month growth over the most recent comparable months with non-zero baselines, requiring a minimum activity threshold to reduce noise.

Open Access Adoption & Journal Landscape

Open-access adoption is measured by the open_access flag and tracked monthly and yearly. Journal concentration highlights where publication volume is clustered.

Research Type Distribution & Collaboration Indicators

Research type mix is extracted from multi-value publication type strings; author counts are used as collaboration proxies across the corpus and by top countries.

Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

    Method Notes

    • All values are computed directly from /app/benchmark/data.csv (n=4,886).
    • Monthly time-series uses valid month entries (pub_month_num 1–12); unknown months are excluded from trend charts but retained in totals.
    • Emerging topic growth is an inferential KPI and sensitive to low-volume baselines; thresholding is applied.
    • Given the known PubMed indexing lag, 2025–2026 values should be interpreted as partial/incomplete relative to 2024.
