Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

Temporal skew is the central analytic constraint. 2024 accounts for 80.3% of records, while 2025 and early 2026 account for 19.7% combined. Strategy teams should avoid interpreting lower later-year counts as a contraction without additional indexing-complete data.
Open access is policy-relevant but uneven over time. The overall open-access rate is 65.1%, with annual rates of 64.1% in 2024, 74.1% in 2025, and 53.8% in 2026. Monitoring OA at monthly resolution is warranted because the final observed month is highly volatile.
Geography is concentrated and non-standardized. The top 10 raw country values contribute 48.1% of all records; China alone contributes 19.5%. However, variants such as USA/United States and China/PR China require normalization before formal country benchmarking.
MeSH-based topic intelligence is incomplete for newer records. Major topics are present for 2,583 records and absent for 2,303. Emerging-topic signals should be treated as directional indicators from indexed records, not a complete map of the literature.
Collaboration intensity is moderate with a long tail. The median article has 6 authors, while the 95th percentile has 15; large-team outliers suggest some areas require institutional or cross-site coordination capacity.
