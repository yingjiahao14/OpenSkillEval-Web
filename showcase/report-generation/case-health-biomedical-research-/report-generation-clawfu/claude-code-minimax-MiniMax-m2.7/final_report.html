Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

  • Temporal Skew Acknowledged: Publication volumes are heavily weighted toward 2024 (80%+) due to PubMed indexing lag. Research policy analysts should use normalized metrics (rates per month) rather than raw counts when comparing across years.
  • Open Access Acceleration: The OA rate increased from 64.1% (2024) to 74.1% (2025), likely driven by funder mandates (NIH, Wellcome, EU Horizon). This trend suggests OA will become the dominant publishing model by 2027.
  • China's Dominant Position: China contributes more publications than the USA and India combined (24,366 vs. 13,345 + 5,017). However, China's OA rate (57.5%) is notably below the average, indicating opportunities for OA policy engagement.
  • Collaboration Intensity Steady: Median author count remains at 6 across all years, suggesting collaboration patterns are stable. However, the long tail (up to 120+ authors) indicates large consortium studies are growing.
  • COVID-19 Remains a Major Focus: Despite declining from pandemic urgency, COVID-19 ranks #1 among major MeSH topics (43 publications in dataset), followed by Type 2 Diabetes and Breast Cancer—indicating sustained research investment in these areas.
  • Systematic Reviews Growing: Systematic Review share increased from ~1% to 1.3% across 2024–2026, reflecting heightened emphasis on evidence synthesis in funding decisions.
