1) Global Homicide Rate Overview

The global time series shows a long-run decline in mean homicide rates from the 1990s into the 2010s, with a flatter profile in the early 2020s. Median rates track below means, indicating persistent right-skew from high-rate outlier countries.

Interpretation note: annual global statistics are computed over reporting countries only; country composition changes by year due to an unbalanced panel.

6) Data Limitations & Policy Recommendations

Unbalanced panelReporting gapsUK caveat

  • Coverage bias: Annual global means and medians reflect only countries reporting that year; shifts may partly reflect sample composition.
  • Comparability: Country-specific first/last comparisons use each country’s available years, which differ across countries.
  • UK scope: UK entries correspond to England and Wales in this dataset context.
  • No imputation used: Missing years were not filled; all metrics are computed from observed rows only.

Policy implications: prioritize sustained homicide-prevention investments in persistently high-rate countries; target male-focused violence interruption alongside women’s protection pathways; strengthen age-targeted prevention for high-risk youth/working-age groups; and invest in statistical capacity to improve country reporting completeness for international monitoring.
