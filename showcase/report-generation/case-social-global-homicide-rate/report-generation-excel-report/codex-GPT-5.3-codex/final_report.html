3) Gender Disparity in Homicide Victimization

Male victimization rates remain consistently above female rates. The male-to-female ratio is a persistent structural disparity requiring targeted intervention design.

4) Age Group Analysis

Risk concentration is highest in adolescent and young-adult groups, while rates are lower in children and older populations, supporting age-targeted prevention strategies.

5) Notable Country-Level Trends & Outliers

Outliers are identified by absolute change between each country’s first and latest observed year (minimum 8 observations).

6) Data Limitations & Policy Recommendations

  • Unbalanced panel: country-year availability is uneven across 1990–2023.
  • Coverage variability: global trajectory can be affected by changing reporting composition.
  • UK caveat: UK records correspond to England and Wales in source framing.
  • No imputation: missing observations are not filled.

Policy focus: prioritize high-rate outliers; sustain male-focused and youth-focused prevention; improve homicide data systems and standardized reporting to strengthen cross-country comparability.
