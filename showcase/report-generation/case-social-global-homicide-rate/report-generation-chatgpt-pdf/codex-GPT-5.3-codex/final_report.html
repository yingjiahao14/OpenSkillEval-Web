6) Data Limitations & Policy Recommendations

  • Unbalanced panel: coverage differs by country-year.
  • No imputation: missing years remain missing; no fabricated values.
  • Coverage sensitivity: global aggregates reflect both true movement and reporting mix changes.
  • Source caveat: UK data refer to England and Wales in this dataset context.

Policy recommendations: prioritize high-rate contexts for targeted prevention; scale sex-responsive intervention strategies given persistent male overexposure; focus youth/working-age risk prevention pathways; and strengthen statistical reporting systems to improve comparability and accountability.
