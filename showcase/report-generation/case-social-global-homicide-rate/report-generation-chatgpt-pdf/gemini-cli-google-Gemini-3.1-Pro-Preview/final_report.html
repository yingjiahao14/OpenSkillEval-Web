6. Data Limitations & Policy Recommendations

Data Limitations

  • Unbalanced Panel: The set of reporting countries varies each year, significantly affecting global averages. Missing data is not imputed.
  • Categorical Variations: Age groups and reporting standards vary between nations.
  • Geographic Caveats: United Kingdom data covers only England and Wales; territories may be reported separately.

Policy Recommendations

  • Targeted Interventions: The high male-to-female victimization ratio requires gender-specific violence prevention initiatives.
  • Demographic Focus: Age groups with disproportionately high rates (e.g., young adults) need prioritized community safety programs.
  • Capacity Building: International stakeholders should support improved crime data collection in under-reported nations.
