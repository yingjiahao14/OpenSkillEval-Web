6) Data Limitations & Policy Recommendations

Data limitations

  • Unbalanced panel: country participation varies by year, so year-to-year global shifts mix real change with reporting-composition effects.
  • Missing years are absent rather than imputed; this report does not interpolate values.
  • UK observations in the source refer to England and Wales only.
  • Territories may be listed separately from sovereign states in source reporting.

Policy-oriented implications

  • Prioritize high-rate countries in latest-year top decile for focused violence prevention and justice-capacity support.
  • Maintain sex-disaggregated monitoring as male victimization remains structurally higher than female victimization.
  • Target youth-centered interventions when younger cohorts remain above overall age-profile averages.
  • Invest in statistical capacity to improve continuity and completeness of annual reporting.
