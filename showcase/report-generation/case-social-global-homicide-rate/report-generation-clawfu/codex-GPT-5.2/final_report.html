6) Data Limitations & Policy Recommendations

Unbalanced panel • coverage gaps • action orientation

Key data limitations

  • Unbalanced panel: countries enter/exit the series over time; means/medians are conditional on reporters in each year.
  • Coverage decline at series end: reporting in 2023 is materially lower than peak coverage years in the late 2010s.
  • Age-group constraint: age bands are sex-disaggregated only (no both-sexes age totals), limiting direct cross-age comparisons for overall populations.
  • Territories included: some entries are territories; comparisons should consider administrative scope.
  • UK caveat (per dataset doc): United Kingdom entries may refer to England and Wales only.

Policy-relevant takeaways (data-driven)

  • Target high-rate hotspots: concentrate prevention and investigative capacity where latest rates are extreme (top decile), while monitoring spillover risks.
  • Design gender-responsive interventions: male victimization remains substantially higher, suggesting priority for violence reduction among men and boys while sustaining protections for women.
  • Invest in statistical systems: reporting completeness is itself a capability; gaps hinder trend detection and accountability.
  • Use multi-year smoothing for decisions: prefer 3–5 year windows where feasible to reduce sensitivity to single-year coverage changes.
  • Validate outlier shifts: large changes should trigger contextual review (methodology, conflict, organized crime, governance, or data breaks).
