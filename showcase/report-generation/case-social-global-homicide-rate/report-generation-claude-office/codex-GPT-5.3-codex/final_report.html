1) Global Homicide Rate Overview

Using records where sex=both and age_group=ALL, the global mean homicide rate changed from 6.33 (first available year 1990) to 8.29 (2023), a +30.9% change. The median series remains below the mean in most years, indicating right-skew from high-rate countries.

Interpretation note: yearly global aggregates are affected by changing country coverage.

3) Gender Disparity in Homicide Victimization

Across country-year observations with male and female data for age_group=ALL, males face substantially higher homicide rates. The global male-to-female ratio is 6.85x in the latest year with both series, compared with 4.58x in the earliest year with both series.

6) Data Limitations & Policy Recommendations

Data limitations

  • Unbalanced panel: country coverage varies materially by year, affecting trend comparability.
  • Missing years are absent rows (not zero), so naive averaging can mix data-availability effects with true level shifts.
  • Country scope caveats apply (e.g., UK refers to England and Wales in source notes).
  • No native region variable is present in data.csv; regional inference requires external mapping not used in this report.

Policy recommendations

  • Target high-rate countries with evidence-based violence interruption and justice-capacity investments.
  • Mainstream gender-sensitive prevention: male victimization burden remains persistently and substantially higher.
  • Prioritize high-risk age cohorts in community, school-to-work, and firearm-risk interventions.
  • Strengthen statistical systems to improve annual reporting completeness and cross-country comparability.
  • Use rolling multi-year averages in monitoring dashboards to reduce volatility from reporting gaps.
Method summary: all figures are computed directly from /app/benchmark/data.csv with no external data joins and no interpolation of missing rows.
