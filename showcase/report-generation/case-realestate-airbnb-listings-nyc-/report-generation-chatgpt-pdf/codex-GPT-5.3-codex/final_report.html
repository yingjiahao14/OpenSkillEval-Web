Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

  1. Pricing spread is material across markets: Bangkok leads median price at $1,400, while Rome is lowest at $131, implying distinct revenue-yield and affordability profiles by city.
  2. Commercialization differs sharply: Bangkok has the highest professional host share at 60.4%, signaling stronger multi-unit operator presence and potentially higher regulatory sensitivity.
  3. Room-type mix should guide entry strategy: cities with higher entire-home share tend to reflect more investment-style inventory, while higher private-room mix supports host-led, lower-price product strategy.
  4. Demand intensity is uneven: Rome shows the strongest review velocity at 1.77 reviews/month, indicating stronger booking activity proxy versus peer cities.
  5. Constraint patterns matter for operating model design: cross-city differences in mean availability and 30+ night minimum share indicate where short-stay flexibility is higher versus where listings tilt to longer stays.
  6. Data coverage caveat is decision-critical: the provided file includes 7 of 10 expected cities; conclusions should be treated as a partial-market benchmark, not a full 10-city global ranking.
