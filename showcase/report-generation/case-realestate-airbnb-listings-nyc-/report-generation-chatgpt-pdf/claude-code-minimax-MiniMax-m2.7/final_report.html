  • Bangkok (60.4%) and Barcelona (58.3%) have the highest professional host concentration — over half of all listings are controlled by multi-property operators, which often correlates with platform-oriented compliance strategies and regulatory pushback.
  • Amsterdam (6.8%) has the lowest professional host share, consistent with strict short-term rental licensing that has effectively removed large-scale operators from the market.
  • Sydney (42.3%) shows elevated professional host activity despite recent regulatory tightening, suggesting a still-commercialized market structure.
  • The correlation between high professional host share and high entire-home share (Bangkok, Barcelona) reinforces that commercialization is largely driven by multi-listing operators rather than individual sharers.
