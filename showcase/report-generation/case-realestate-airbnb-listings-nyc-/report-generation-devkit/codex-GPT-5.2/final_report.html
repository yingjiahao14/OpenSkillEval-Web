Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

Synthesize findings into actionable insights for investors, hosts, and policymakers, highlighting the most attractive markets and emerging trends.
  1. Pricing leader (median): Bangkok at $1,400 per night (computed on non-missing prices only).
  2. Price data completeness varies materially; the highest missingness is Paris at 100.0% of listings with null price (cross-city price comparisons exclude these rows).
  3. Professional hosts (≥5 listings) dominate most in Bangkok (60.4%) vs. lowest in Amsterdam (6.8%).
  4. Entire-home commercialization is highest in Paris where 88.2% of listings are Entire home/apt.
  5. Demand proxy (avg reviews/month) is strongest in Rome at 1.77.
Method note: The dataset is a snapshot (single scrape_date). Review metrics are proxies (not bookings) and should be interpreted directionally.
Dataset: 50,000 rows × 20 columns. Cities present: Amsterdam, Bangkok, Barcelona, London, Paris, Rome, Sydney.
