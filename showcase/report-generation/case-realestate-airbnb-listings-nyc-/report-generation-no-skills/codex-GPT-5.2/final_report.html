Global Airbnb Market Comparison: Pricing, Supply & Guest Demand Across 10 Major Cities

A cross-city snapshot of Airbnb listings focusing on pricing (primary), supply composition, professional host concentration, and demand proxies.

Dataset Inside Airbnb-derived snapshot Listings 50,000 Cities present 7 (expected 10) Scrape date 2026-03-29

Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

  1. Bangkok is the highest-priced market in this dataset (median nightly price $1,400 across priced listings; mean $2,337).
  2. Among cities with price data, Rome is the lowest on median pricing (median $131).
  3. Professional host concentration is highest in Bangkok: 60.4% of listings belong to hosts with 5+ listings (using `calculated_host_listings_count`).
  4. Guest-demand proxies are strongest in Rome by reviews/month (average 1.77; median 1.28).
  5. Booking constraints are most extreme in Paris on the upper end (P90 minimum nights = 365 nights), suggesting a bifurcated market with many long-minimum listings.
  6. Price-based rankings exclude Paris, Sydney due to 100% missing `price` values in the provided `data.csv`.
All figures are computed directly from benchmark/data.csv. Missing values are shown as “—” and are not imputed.
