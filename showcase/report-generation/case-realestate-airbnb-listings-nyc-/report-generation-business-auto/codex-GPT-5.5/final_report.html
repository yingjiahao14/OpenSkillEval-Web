Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

  1. Bangkok is the clear pricing outlier among cities with usable price data: median nightly price is $1,400, versus $222 in Amsterdam and $131 in Rome, the lowest median priced market. Treat Bangkok's premium cautiously as prices are described as local-equivalent USD but appear on a much larger numeric scale than European markets.
  2. Professional host concentration is highest in Bangkok and Barcelona: 60.4% of Bangkok listings and 58.3% of Barcelona listings are controlled by hosts with 5+ listings, indicating more institutionalized supply and stronger regulatory relevance.
  3. Amsterdam is the least professionalized supply base: only 6.8% of listings meet the 5+ listing threshold, despite 82.8% being entire homes. This suggests a more individual-host-heavy market than Barcelona or Bangkok.
  4. Paris has the strongest entire-home skew but no usable price data: 88.2% of Paris listings are entire homes, yet 100.0% of Paris price values are missing. Strategic pricing conclusions for Paris should not be drawn from this file.
  5. Rome shows the strongest review intensity among available cities: average reviews per month are 1.77, ahead of Sydney (1.44) and Barcelona (1.43), supporting demand resilience even at a lower median price point.
  6. Availability signals diverge sharply: Bangkok averages 253 available days per year, while Amsterdam averages only 92. High availability plus high professional share points to more commercially oriented supply; low availability may reflect regulation, part-time hosting, or constrained calendars.

Supply Structure & Host Concentration

Professional host share is calculated exactly as required: listings where calculated_host_listings_count >= 5 divided by total listings in the city. This metric reveals a sharp split between professionalized markets such as Bangkok and Barcelona and more casual-host markets such as Amsterdam and Paris.

Guest Demand & Review Activity

Review metrics are demand proxies, not direct booking counts. On average reviews per month, Rome is highest (1.77), followed by Sydney (1.44) and Barcelona (1.43). Bangkok has the highest pricing among priced cities but a lower review-per-month average (0.96), suggesting that price premium and observed review velocity do not move together in this snapshot.

Methodology

Definitions

Professional Host Share = share of listings where calculated_host_listings_count >= 5.

Entire Home Share = share of listings where room_type = Entire home/apt.

Average Reviews per Month = mean of non-null reviews_per_month values by city.

Price Statistics exclude null price values and are marked n/a when a city has no priced listings.

Data Limitations

Only cities present in data.csv are analyzed. New York, Tokyo, and Los Angeles are absent despite appearing in the report brief.

Paris and Sydney contain listings but no non-null prices, preventing valid price comparisons for those cities.

The latest review date precedes the scrape date by more than 90 days, so scrape-relative review recency is uniformly zero.
