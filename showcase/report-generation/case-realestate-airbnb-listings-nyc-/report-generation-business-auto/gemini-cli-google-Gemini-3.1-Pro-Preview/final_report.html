Global Airbnb Cross-Market Analysis

A Comparative Study of Pricing, Supply, and Demand Across Top Global Destinations

1. Market Overview

This analytical report targets hospitality industry analysts and short-term rental market strategists. It provides a data-driven comparison of Airbnb markets across major global tourist destinations, focusing on actionable metrics: cross-city pricing dynamics, professional host concentrations, room-type distributions, and robust guest demand proxies.

2. Pricing Dynamics Across Cities

Pricing reflects both the cost of living and the intense demand for short-term accommodation. The first chart details the median and average nightly prices. The second chart breaks down median pricing by room type to highlight structural differences in accommodation categories.

3. Supply Structure & Host Concentration

The share of professional hosts (those managing 5+ listings) indicates the commercialization level of a market. High concentrations heavily influence regulatory responses and signify mature B2B hospitality ecosystems.

4. Guest Demand & Review Activity

Average reviews per month act as a robust proxy for listing utilization and guest turnaround, reflecting varying levels of tourist flow and booking velocities.

5. Availability & Booking Constraints

Mean availability (days/year) showcases inventory liquidity—how often listings are available for booking versus occupied or structurally blocked.
