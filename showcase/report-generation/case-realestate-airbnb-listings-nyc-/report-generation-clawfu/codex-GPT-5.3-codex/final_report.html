Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

  1. Bangkok is the highest-priced market by median nightly rate at $1400.0, while Rome is the lowest at $131.0.
  2. Professionalized supply is most concentrated in Bangkok (60.4% of listings hosted by operators with 5+ listings), versus Amsterdam (6.8%).
  3. Guest-demand intensity is highest in Rome (1.77 average reviews/month) and lowest in Bangkok (0.96).
  4. Commercialization profile differs sharply: entire-home share peaks in Paris (88.2%) and is lowest in Barcelona (60.2%).
  5. Listing availability suggests different operating models/regulatory constraints: Bangkok averages 252.5 available days/year vs. Amsterdam at 92.5.

Method notes: Professional host share = % of listings with calculated_host_listings_count ≥ 5. All percentages are listing-weighted within each city. No imputation was used for missing prices or review dates.
