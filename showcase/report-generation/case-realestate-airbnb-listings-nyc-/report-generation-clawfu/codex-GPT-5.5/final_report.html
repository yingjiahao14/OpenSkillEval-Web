Key Takeaways & Strategic Implications

Bangkok is the price outlier among markets with usable prices: its median listed price is 1,400, more than 10.7x Rome's 131. Treat this as a direct comparison of the recorded price field because the dataset’s currency normalization cannot be independently verified from the CSV.
Professionalized supply is most concentrated in Bangkok and Barcelona: 60.4% of Bangkok listings and 58.3% of Barcelona listings are controlled by hosts with 5+ listings, signaling a more commercial competitive landscape and higher regulatory relevance.
Paris is the most whole-unit-oriented market: 88.2% of Paris listings are entire homes/apartments, followed by Amsterdam at 82.8%; this mix points to strong investor/second-home participation, even though Paris price data is fully missing.
Rome shows the strongest guest engagement signal: it leads on average reviews per month at 1.77 and also has 66.3% of listings reviewed within the last 12 months, indicating broad current demand rather than only legacy review accumulation.
Barcelona has the clearest booking-constraint signal: 40.1% of listings require 30+ nights, far above the next-highest available city, which materially affects addressable short-stay demand and should be benchmarked against local regulations before market entry.

Methodology & Data Integrity Notes

All metrics are derived from /app/benchmark/data.csv. No values are imputed. Missing prices are excluded from price means, medians, percentiles, and room-type price summaries. Percent shares use total listings in each city as the denominator unless otherwise noted.

Professional host share is listing-weighted and equals the percentage of listings with calculated_host_listings_count >= 5. Review recency is calculated as the share of listings whose last_review is on or after 2025-03-29, the 12-month window ending at the dataset scrape date of 2026-03-29.

Required but absent from the supplied CSV: New York, Tokyo, Los Angeles. Cities with fully missing prices: Paris, Sydney. The neighbourhood_group and license fields are substantially incomplete and are not used as core KPIs.
