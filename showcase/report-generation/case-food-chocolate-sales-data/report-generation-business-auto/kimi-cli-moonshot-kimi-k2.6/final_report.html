Executive Summary

Over the 2023–2024 period, the chocolate retail business generated $1,275,308.34 in total revenue with $510,547.86 in profit, maintaining a healthy average profit margin of 40.01%. The business showed modest year-over-year growth across all key metrics, with order volume, revenue, and profit all increasing between 1.5% and 1.7%.

Sales Trends Over Time

Monthly revenue remained relatively stable throughout the two-year period, fluctuating between approximately $48K and $56K per month. The strongest month was June 2024 ($55,980), while the weakest was February 2023 ($48,259). There is no pronounced seasonal peak, though Q3 tends to be slightly stronger than other quarters.

Quarterly Performance

Quarterly revenue shows a gradual upward trend from 2023 to 2024. Q2 2024 was the strongest quarter at $163,384, while Q2 2023 was the weakest at $155,061. The year-over-year quarterly growth ranged from -0.5% (Q4) to +5.4% (Q2).

Seasonality Insight: January and July show the highest combined revenue across both years, suggesting post-holiday restocking and mid-year purchasing patterns. February consistently underperforms, presenting an opportunity for targeted promotional campaigns.

Discount Impact & Profitability Analysis

Discounts are applied in 37.55% of all transactions, with four discount tiers: 0%, 10%, 15%, and 20%. Counter-intuitively, average profit margin remains stable at ~40% across all discount levels, suggesting that cost structure adjusts proportionally or that discounted transactions involve higher-margin products.

Actionable Insight: Discounted transactions show lower average order values ($21.44 at 20% off vs $27.15 at full price) but similar unit volumes. The margin stability is encouraging, yet the revenue per unit erosion ($9.04 → $7.15) means discounts must drive meaningful incremental volume to be profitable. Consider A/B testing 10% vs 15% promotions to find the optimal elasticity point.
