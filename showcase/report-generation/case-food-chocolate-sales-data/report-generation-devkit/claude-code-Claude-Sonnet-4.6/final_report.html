The chocolate retail division delivered $1,275,308 in total revenue over the 2023–2024 period, supported by 50,000 transactions and 149,961 units sold across the store network. Year-over-year revenue growth of +1.72% ($632,219 in 2023 vs. $643,089 in 2024) reflects steady organic growth in a competitive confectionery market.

Profit performance remained robust at $510,548 total (an average margin of 40.01%), underscoring strong cost discipline throughout the supply chain. The average order value of $25.51 was consistent across both years, indicating stable consumer spending behaviour.

A discount utilisation rate of 37.55% warrants management attention. While promotional pricing supports volume growth, margin integrity should be preserved by ensuring discounts are targeted rather than broad-based. The 11–20% discount tier accounted for 25% of all orders, representing the largest promotional cohort.

No-discount orders (62.45% of all transactions) generate the highest average order value at $27.15, compared to $23.90 for 1–10% discounts and $22.22 for 11–20% discounts. Crucially, profit margins remain consistent across all tiers (~40%), indicating that discounting is being applied at the product cost level rather than eating into net margin — a positive structural finding.

Management should continue to monitor whether discount volume growth is driving incremental revenue or merely accelerating purchases that would have occurred at full price. Targeted discount campaigns tied to seasonal peaks (Q1 and Q3) are recommended.
