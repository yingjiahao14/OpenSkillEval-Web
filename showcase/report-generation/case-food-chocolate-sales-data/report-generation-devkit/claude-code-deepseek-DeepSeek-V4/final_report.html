Discount Level vs. Per-Order Economics

Discount Strategy Insight: Discounts reduce per-order revenue proportionally (from $27.15 at 0% to $21.44 at 20%) but do not materially increase order quantity — average quantities stay flat at ~3.0 units regardless of discount level. This means discounts are pure margin giveaways with no volume offset. The profit margin percentage remains constant (~40%) because the discount is applied to revenue before cost, but absolute profit per order drops 21% (from $10.87 to $8.59). Recommendation: Re-evaluate the promotional strategy. If discounts don't drive incremental volume, consider reducing discount frequency or testing targeted offers (loyalty-only, bundling, minimum quantity thresholds) to preserve profit.
