Executive Summary

The provided fact table shows a stable, profitable chocolate retail business over 2023–2024. Revenue grew by 1.7% in 2024, profit moved by 1.6%, and order volume moved by 1.5%. Margins were highly consistent year over year, with average transaction margin staying near 40.0%.

What is working

  • Revenue and profit both increased modestly in 2024 while margins stayed stable.
  • Top stores and products are not overly concentrated; the top 10 stores represent 11.0% of revenue and the top 10 products represent 5.8%.
  • Repeat customers, identifiable by multiple purchases in this file, account for 63.5% of revenue.

Management watchouts

  • Discounted transactions do not show a meaningful quantity uplift: 20% discount orders average 3.00 units versus 3.00 for non-discounted orders.
  • Because product, store, and customer dimension tables are not included in `/app/benchmark`, category, brand, geography, demographic, and loyalty analyses cannot be directly supported.
  • Monthly results are comparatively even, so growth strategy should focus on proven store/product execution rather than assuming a large seasonal spike.

This is the priority management view. Monthly revenue stayed in a relatively narrow band, with 2024 outperforming 2023 by 1.7% overall. The strongest month-over-month comparison was June at 10.4% YoY, while December trailed most at -7.5%.

Actionable interpretation

  • Do not expand discount depth solely to drive unit volume; the 20% tier does not show higher average quantity than no discount.

Recommendations for FY Planning

  1. Protect margin while scaling modest growth. 2024 growth was positive but not explosive; prioritize availability and execution in top product/store IDs rather than broad margin sacrifice.
  2. Require incremental-lift measurement for promotions. Discounted tiers show lower AOV and no clear quantity lift, so future campaigns should include controls, pre/post lift, and SKU-level incrementality.
  3. Invest in repeat-customer activation. Repeat customers generate 63.5% of revenue despite being 42.0% of customer IDs; retention and replenishment nudges are likely high-leverage.
  4. Enrich the reporting pipeline with dimension tables. Product/category, store/geography, and loyalty/demographic conclusions require the missing product, store, and customer dimensions documented in the source brief.

Data Integrity Notes

  • Primary data source: `/app/benchmark/data.csv`, containing 50,000 rows and 19 columns.
  • Date range: 2023-01-01 through 2024-12-31; no missing values detected in the provided fact table.
