Key Observation

Revenue, Margin & Avg Order Value by Discount Level

Discount Effectiveness Assessment
Discounts do not erode margins — margins remain stable at ~40% across all discount levels. This indicates that either costs scale proportionally with discounts, or discounts are applied to the retail price only. However, average order value declines meaningfully with higher discounts ($27.15 no discount vs. $21.44 at 20% off), meaning customers spend less per visit when incentivized by price reductions. 62.5% of orders carry no discount and generate 66.5% of total revenue, confirming the base-price channel as the primary revenue driver. Discounts may drive volume but do not increase individual transaction value.

Loyalty Insight
Repeat customers are 2.4x more valuable per customer than new customers ($61.19 vs. $25.44), while maintaining identical profit margins (~40%). With 13,237 repeat customers generating $810K of the total $1.28M revenue (63.5%), the loyalty base is the single most important revenue driver. The dataset does not include customer demographics (age, gender), so further demographic segmentation is not possible. Acquiring new customers is critical, but retaining and growing the existing loyal base should be the primary strategic focus.
