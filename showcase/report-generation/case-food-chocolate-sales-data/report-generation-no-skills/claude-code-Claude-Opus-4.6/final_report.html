Monthly Discount Utilization Trend

Key Finding — Discounts Do Not Erode Margins: Profit margins remain virtually identical (~40%) regardless of discount level applied. This is because unit costs adjust proportionally, suggesting a cost-plus pricing model where discounts are absorbed in the cost structure. However, discounts do reduce average order value from $27.15 (full price) to $21.44 (16–20% discount) — a 21% decline — without generating meaningfully higher order quantities (avg ~3.0 units across all levels).
Actionable Insight: Since discounts lower AOV without driving volume uplift or eroding margins, they appear to be subsidizing price-insensitive purchases rather than generating incremental demand. Consider reducing discount frequency or testing whether a shift to volume-based incentives (buy-more-save-more) could increase basket size without further AOV dilution. The ~38% discount utilization rate is stable month-to-month, suggesting it is structurally embedded rather than promotional.
