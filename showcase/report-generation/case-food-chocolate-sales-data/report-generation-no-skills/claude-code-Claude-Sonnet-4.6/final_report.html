Critical Finding
Discount levels do not increase order quantity — avg qty/order is 3.00 at all levels. Discounts only erode revenue per order, not drive volume uplift.
Margin Resilience
Remarkably, profit margin holds at ~40% across all discount tiers. This indicates cost is adjusted proportionally — or the data reflects net-of-promotion cost accounting.
Strategic Implication
A 20% discount lowers AOV by $5.71 vs. no-discount ($21.44 vs $27.15) without measurable basket-size benefit. Promotional ROI should be scrutinized carefully.
