🎯 Strategic Recommendations

  1. Double down on Q3 momentum: Q3 is the strongest quarter in both years. Plan inventory and marketing spend to capitalize on this seasonal peak.
  2. Re-evaluate discount strategy: Discounts reduce AOV without improving volume or margin. Test smaller discount tiers (5%) or shift to bundling and loyalty rewards to protect per-unit profitability.
  3. Attack the one-time buyer problem: Launch a targeted post-purchase email/SMS campaign to convert the 18,294 one-time buyers into repeat purchasers. Even a 5% conversion would add ~$700K in revenue.
  4. Store consistency is a strength: With only a 30% spread between top and bottom stores, replicate best practices from S075 and S076 across the network rather than chasing outliers.
  5. Product portfolio is well-balanced: No over-dependence on a single SKU reduces risk. Continue diversified assortment strategy.
