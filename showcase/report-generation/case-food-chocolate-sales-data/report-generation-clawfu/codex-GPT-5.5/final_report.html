Appendix: Required Sections & KPI Definitions

All sections requested in task_input.json are included. Unsupported dimensions are explicitly identified rather than estimated.

KPI definitions used

  • Total Revenue: sum of revenue.
  • Total Profit: sum of profit.
  • Average Profit Margin: mean of profit / revenue by transaction.
  • Average Order Value: mean revenue per transaction.
  • Discount Utilization Rate: share of transactions where discount > 0.

Unavailable analyses

  • Category, brand, cocoa percentage: products.csv unavailable.
  • City, country, store type: stores.csv unavailable.
  • Age, gender, loyalty membership: customers.csv unavailable.
  • Calendar attributes beyond parsed date parts: calendar.csv unavailable, but month/quarter/year were derived from order_date.
