Key Highlights

Data-backed
  • YoY growth is modest: revenue increased by 1.72% from 2023 to 2024; profit increased by 1.57%.
  • Profitability is stable: blended profit margin is ~40% in both years, indicating costs scale proportionally with revenue.
  • Discounting trades profit for volume: discounted orders represent 37.55% of transactions and reduce realized revenue by $75,534.34 over 2023–2024 (vs pre-discount revenue).
  • Operational focus areas: improve gross margin economics of promotions (especially 15–20%) and lean into quarter-level momentum where YoY is strongest.
Important limitation: this benchmark dataset includes only the fact table (transactions). Dimension attributes (product category/brand, store geography, customer demographics/loyalty) are not present in data.csv, so category, geography, and customer segment analyses are constrained to ID-level rollups.

Volume & Margin Signals

What changes with discounts
  • Average order value (AOV) falls with higher discounts: 0% AOV $27.15 → 20% AOV $21.44.
  • Order-level margin % stays ~flat (~40%): costs scale with revenue, so margin % is stable across discount rates in this dataset.
  • Discount utilization is steady month-to-month: monthly discounted-order rate ranges 35.57%–40.13%.
  • Action: treat 15–20% discounts as “profit-funded” promotions; require measurable unit lift or strategic objective (acquisition, clearance) to justify.
