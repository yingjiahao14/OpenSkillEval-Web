Outlook & Conclusions

  • Regime shifts are visible in both level and dispersion. The 1970s and 2000s show materially higher within-decade dispersion than the 1990s, while the 2010s and 2020s sustain higher price levels than pre-2000 decades.
  • Shocks cluster. The most extreme monthly drawdowns concentrate in 2008 and 2020, while the largest YoY increases are concentrated in 1974.
  • Context for 2020s. The post-2020 recovery and 2022 spike are clearly visible in the series; however, the dataset itself does not label observations as “forecast” vs “actual,” so late-period points should be interpreted as provided.
  • Historical baseline matters. Percentage change metrics can be dominated by low-price starting points (early 1970s). For cross-era comparability, analysts often complement percentage moves with absolute USD moves and/or inflation adjustments (not available in this dataset).
