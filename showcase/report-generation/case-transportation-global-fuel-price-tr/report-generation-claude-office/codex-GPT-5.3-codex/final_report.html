Outlook & Conclusions

  • The 2020s segment in this dataset exhibits elevated regime uncertainty, with sharp rebounds and drawdowns relative to pre-2000 norms.
  • Historical behavior suggests crude oil prices are characterized by long plateaus interrupted by discrete structural breaks rather than smooth mean reversion.
  • For forward contexting, analysts should benchmark current moves against the empirical MoM/YoY shock distribution shown above and explicitly separate level effects from volatility effects.
  • The source brief indicates some late-period observations may be projected; interpretations of 2025–2026 should therefore account for potential forecast components.
