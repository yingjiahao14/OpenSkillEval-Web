Over the full sample (1970-01-01–2026-03-01), crude oil prices range from $1.21 to $132.83. The series exhibits punctuated regime shifts: low/stable prices in the early 1970s, structurally higher prices after the 1970s shocks, a mid-1980s collapse, a high-volatility supercycle culminating in 2008, and renewed shock-driven volatility in the 2020s. The CAGR computed directly from the first and last observations is 7.93%.
All statistics and values in this report are computed directly from benchmark/data.csv. No external price series is used.

Outlook & Conclusions

The 2020s segment in this dataset (through Mar 2026) shows a rapid demand shock and recovery pattern: prices fall from $53.35 in Feb 2020 to $21.04 in Apr 2020, then rise to $39.46 by Jun 2020. The dataset also captures renewed upward shock dynamics in early 2022 (e.g., Mar 2022 at $112.40). The final observation (Mar 2026) is $87.84. Decade-level averages show that the 2010s were the highest-average decade, while the 2000s contain the all-time monthly peak.
