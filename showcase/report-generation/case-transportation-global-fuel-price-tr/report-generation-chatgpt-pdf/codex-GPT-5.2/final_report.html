Outlook & Conclusions

Within this dataset, the final observation is 2026-03 at $87.84. The long-run profile is characterized by large regime shifts and episodic volatility; the single highest monthly level occurs in 2008-07 at $132.83, while the minimum occurs at the start of the sample in 1970-01 ($1.21).
