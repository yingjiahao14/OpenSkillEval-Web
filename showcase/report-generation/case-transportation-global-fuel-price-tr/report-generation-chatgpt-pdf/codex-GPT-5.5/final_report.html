Outlook & Conclusions

Recent data should be interpreted with the source documentation in mind: the dataset title extends through 2026, and the documentation notes that later observations may be estimated or projected rather than fully realized market history. Within the provided data, the 2020s average is $71.75, with a decade-to-date standard deviation of $18.80 and a range from $21.04 to $116.80.

  • Historical precedent matters: the largest shocks are not evenly distributed; volatility clusters around macro stress and geopolitical repricing episodes.
  • Nominal price regimes have shifted upward: the early sample is anchored near single-digit prices, while later regimes frequently sit far above the full-sample median.
  • Decade averages hide regime breaks: the 2010s and 2020s include substantial internal reversals, so analysts should combine decade summaries with event-window analysis.
  • Current prices require context: the March 2026 value of $87.84 is below the July 2008 maximum but far above the early-sample baseline and above the full-sample mean.
