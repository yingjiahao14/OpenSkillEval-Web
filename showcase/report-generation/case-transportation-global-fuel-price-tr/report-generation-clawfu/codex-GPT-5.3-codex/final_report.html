Executive Overview

The dataset captures a transition from a low-price, low-volatility regime in the early 1970s to structurally higher and more volatile pricing in later decades. Prices span from $1.21 to $132.83, with a total range of $131.62 per barrel.

Across 56.2 years, the series implies a nominal CAGR of 7.93%. The highest average decade is 2010s, while the largest one-month absolute move occurred in 2008-10 ($-26.96, -27.06%).

Long-Term Price Trajectory (1970–2026)

  • Structural escalation: the central tendency shifts materially upward after the 1970s.
  • Regime shifts: the 2000s onward show wider and faster swings than earlier decades.
  • Extremes: trough at 1970-01 ($1.21) and peak at 2008-07 ($132.83).
