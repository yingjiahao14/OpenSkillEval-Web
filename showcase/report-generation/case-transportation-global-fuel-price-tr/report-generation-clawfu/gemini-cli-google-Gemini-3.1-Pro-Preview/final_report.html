Long-Term Price Trajectory (1970–2026)

Over the past 56 years, crude oil has transitioned from a highly regulated, low-cost commodity in the early 1970s to a highly financialized and volatile global asset. Major inflection points correlate heavily with global macroeconomic crises, Middle Eastern conflicts, and demand shocks.

Decade-by-Decade Breakdown

Breaking down the data by decade reveals the structural shifts in the market's pricing environment. The 1970s initiated the era of shocks, the 1980s and 1990s saw prolonged price moderation, while the 2000s and 2010s ushered in super-cycles driven by emerging market demand and subsequent shale oil supply revolutions.
