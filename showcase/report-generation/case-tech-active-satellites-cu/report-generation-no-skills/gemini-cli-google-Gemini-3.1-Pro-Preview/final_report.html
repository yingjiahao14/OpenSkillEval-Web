Catalog Overview & Key Figures

This report analyzes the current population of active satellites orbiting Earth based on TLE catalog data. The dataset contains 14,875 operational objects, with orbital element epochs ranging from 2026-02-26 to 2026-03-30. The data reflects a rapidly evolving orbital environment heavily dominated by commercial mega-constellations.

Launch Year Trends

The historical growth curve reveals a dramatic acceleration in successful deployments of currently active satellites. A staggering 90.5% of all active satellites were launched in 2020 or later, illustrating the shift toward mass manufacturing and rapid deployment cadence.

Orbital Regime Classification

Satellites are classified into regimes utilizing the following MEAN_MOTION thresholds:

  • LEO: ≥ 11.25 rev/day (Low Eccentricity)
  • MEO: 1.05 - 11.25 rev/day
  • GEO: 0.95 - 1.05 rev/day
  • HEO: Eccentricity > 0.25

Major Constellations & Operators

The space environment is heavily skewed by a few dominant players. By parsing spacecraft names, we identify the largest constellations, which fundamentally alter orbit utilization statistics.

Inclination Distribution

Analysis of orbital parameters highlights structural choices in constellation deployment. The clustering around mid-inclination bands (median 53.2°) provides optimal global coverage for broadband networks.

Eccentricity Distribution

The vast majority of operational satellites inhabit highly circular orbits (Eccentricity < 0.01), crucial for maintaining consistent altitudes in LEO mega-constellations.

Key Takeaways & Outlook

  • Mega-Constellation Dominance: With 10,083 active units, Starlink accounts for 67.8% of the global orbital population, strongly shaping inclination and altitude metrics.
  • LEO Congestion: The overwhelming majority (94.6%) of operational satellites reside in LEO (≥11.25 rev/day). The stricter ">14 rev/day" threshold alone captures 86.5% of the catalog, pinpointing the most densely packed shells.
  • Launch Acceleration: The deployment rate post-2020 signifies a paradigm shift. With 13,461 satellites surviving from the last few years, orbital volume is expanding at an unprecedented rate.
