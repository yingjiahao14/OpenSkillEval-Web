Key Takeaways & Outlook

Launch acceleration is the central story.13,461 active satellites were launched since 2020, so recent launch cohorts constitute 90.5% of the current active catalog.
LEO is the dominant operating regime.12,860 satellites are in LEO by the MEAN_MOTION >14 rev/day threshold, equal to 86.5% of the catalog.
One constellation reshapes the statistics.Starlink contributes 10,083 satellites, or 67.8% of all active satellites, and 78.4% of the LEO population.

For policy and spectrum analysis, the catalog indicates a highly concentrated orbital environment: active satellites are clustered in LEO, launch growth is dominated by very recent cohorts, and large constellations materially alter aggregate measures such as regime share, inclination distribution, and congestion exposure.
