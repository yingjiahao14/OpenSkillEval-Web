Key Takeaways & Outlook (data-driven)

  • LEO is the dominant congestion regime. Using MEAN_MOTION > 14.0 rev/day, LEO contains 12,860 active satellites (86.5%).
  • Constellation concentration is extreme. STARLINK alone represents 10,083 satellites (67.8%), materially skewing total-regime shares and inclination statistics.
  • The active population is overwhelmingly recent. 13,461 active satellites (90.5%) have launch years ≥ 2020, consistent with rapid fleet growth and replenishment cycles.
  • Policy implication for licensing/spectrum coordination: any analysis of “typical” orbital parameters should be reported both with and without the dominant constellation to avoid misleading medians (e.g., inclination).
