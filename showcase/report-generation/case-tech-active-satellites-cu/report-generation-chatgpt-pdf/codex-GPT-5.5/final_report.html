Key Takeaways & Outlook

  • LEO congestion is the central operating environment: 12,860 satellites, or 86.5%, classify as LEO using MEAN_MOTION > 14 rev/day.
  • Constellation concentration is extreme: Starlink represents 67.8% of the full catalog and 78.4% of LEO satellites.
  • Recent deployments dominate the active catalog: 13,461 active satellites launched from 2020 onward, with 2025 the largest launch year in the data at 4,329 satellites.
  • Policy-relevant distributions are skewed: the median object is a low-eccentricity LEO satellite, but GEO/MEO/HEO populations remain material for spectrum coordination and long-lived orbital stewardship.
  • Licensing and debris analysis should separate Starlink from non-Starlink baselines: a single operator materially changes totals, LEO shares, and inclination-shell counts.
