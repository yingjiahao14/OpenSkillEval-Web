Launch Year Trends

Launch-year distribution of currently active satellites shows strong acceleration in the 2020s. Active spacecraft launched since 2020 total 13,461 (90.5% of all active satellites).

Orbital Parameter Deep Dive

Median inclination is 53.16°. Eccentricity is near-circular for most satellites, with a thin high-eccentricity tail associated with non-circular mission profiles.

Key Takeaways & Outlook

  • LEO dominates the active orbital environment, centralizing congestion risk in lower orbital shells.
  • Constellation concentration is high; one operator (Starlink) constitutes a large fraction of all active satellites.
  • Most active satellites were launched in the post-2020 period, confirming a persistent high-growth era.
  • Given concentration by shell and operator, spectrum coordination and debris-mitigation enforcement remain high-priority policy levers.
