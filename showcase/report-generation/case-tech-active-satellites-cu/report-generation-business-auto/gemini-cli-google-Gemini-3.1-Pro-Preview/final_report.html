Key Takeaways & Outlook

  • Unprecedented Growth: With 13,461 currently active satellites launched since 2020, the orbital population has expanded exponentially.
  • Constellation Dominance: The Starlink constellation accounts for 10,083 out of 14,875 satellites (67.8% of the total active catalog). This single operator significantly skews overall orbital distributions.
  • LEO Congestion: Low Earth Orbit is the overwhelmingly dominant regime (86.5% of active assets), requiring enhanced space traffic management and stricter orbital debris mitigation policies.
  • Common Orbital Shells: The median inclination of 53.2° reflects the popular mid-inclination orbits heavily populated by mega-constellations to serve global mid-latitudes.
