Key Takeaways & Outlook

  • LEO dominates the active population: 12,860 satellites (86.45%).
  • Starlink is the primary driver of both totals and LEO congestion in this snapshot: 10,083 satellites (67.78% of the catalog).
  • Recent launches dominate the active fleet: 13,461 satellites (90.49%) have launch years 2020+.
  • Policy implication (data-driven framing): Any spectrum/orbit-use analysis that treats “the satellite population” as homogeneous will be materially distorted unless it segments out large constellations (e.g., Starlink) and reports both with and without them.
