Key Takeaways & Outlook

  • Current active-orbit crowding is primarily a LEO issue: 12,860 of 14,875 satellites, or 86.5%, classify as LEO using MEAN_MOTION > 14.
  • Launch acceleration dominates the active fleet: 13,461 satellites, 90.5% of the catalog, have launch years of 2020 or later.
  • One operator materially shifts the statistics: Starlink’s 10,083 satellites account for 67.8% of all active satellites and 78.4% of LEO entries.
  • Regulatory workload is concentrated but not single-dimensional: Starlink dominates LEO counts, while non-Starlink satellites still populate 1,336 MEO, 595 GEO, and 84 HEO catalog entries.
  • Inclination and shell clustering matter for coordination: the median inclination is 53.2°, and 3,884 satellites fall between 80° and 100° inclination.
