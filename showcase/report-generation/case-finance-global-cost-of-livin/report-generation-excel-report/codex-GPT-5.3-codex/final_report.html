Key Findings and Policy Implications

  • Housing pressure is the primary policy lever: the global mean rent-to-salary ratio is 61.5%, with 80 cities beyond the severe threshold.
  • Prioritize tier-5 cities: 14 cities are in the highest crisis tier, indicating concentrated acute stress requiring targeted affordability interventions.
  • Differentiate policy by regional structure: regions with high rents but higher purchasing power require supply/competition reforms, while low purchasing power regions require income support and social protection.
  • Shock resilience matters: cities with high inflation and high oil exposure should be prioritized for transport-energy buffering and inflation-sensitive safety nets.
  • Further research: track longitudinal movement in crisis tier and simulate rent subsidy/zoning scenarios for high-burden cities identified above.
