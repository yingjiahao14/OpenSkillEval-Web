Key Findings and Policy Implications

Urgent Priorities

  • Housing affordability remains the primary driver of acute economic stress, with the most burdened cities seeing rent consume over 70% of average salaries.
  • There are 14 cities classified in the most severe crisis tier, necessitating immediate targeted interventions.
  • Significant regional disparities exist, with specific cities heavily deviating from their regional affordability baselines.
  • Cities with high oil shock exposure systematically exhibit varying inflationary pressures impacting overall purchasing power.

Policy Recommendations

  • Implement targeted housing subsidies or rent stabilization mechanisms in cities identified as having severe rent burdens.
  • Develop localized economic resilience strategies for cities highly exposed to oil shocks and high inflation.
  • Focus regional economic development funds on outliers that significantly lag behind their geographic peers in purchasing power.
