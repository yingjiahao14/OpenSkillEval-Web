Key Findings

  1. Global affordability crisis is pervasive: 14 cities (17.5%) face Tier 5 crisis conditions with average inflation of 38%.
  2. Rent burden exceeds sustainable thresholds globally: Average rent-to-salary ratio is 61.5%, far above the 30% benchmark.
  3. Swiss cities remain most expensive but most prosperous: Zurich, Geneva lead in COL but also in PPP, indicating high costs matched by high wages.
  4. South Asia faces compounding vulnerabilities: Low COL but highest regional inflation (16.6%) and significant oil shock exposure.
  5. High oil shock exposure is a leading crisis indicator: High-exposure cities show 6x higher inflation and significantly lower purchasing power.

Policy Recommendations

For Tier 4-5 Cities:
  • Implement rent control mechanisms and affordable housing programs
  • Address inflation through monetary policy support and currency stabilization
  • Prioritize social safety nets for lowest-income households
For High Oil Shock Exposure Regions:
  • Reduce dependency through energy diversification
  • Build emergency economic response frameworks
  • Develop regional economic cooperation to share shock absorption
