Features

Share what you're into with the people who get you with all our features

SnapFrame's suite of creative tools — from short-form video to disappearing stories and direct messaging — lets you express yourself and connect with the communities that matter most. Explore Clips, Stories, DMs, and Search & Explore to find your people.

features

Clips

Short-form video that captures attention. Create, edit, and share 60-second videos with music, effects, and filters.

Stories

Share fleeting moments that disappear after 24 hours. Perfect for authentic, in-the-moment updates with your closest friends.

DMs

Private conversations with friends and groups. Share photos, videos, and messages in a space that's just for you and your people.

Search & Explore

Discover trending content, new creators, and communities that match your interests. Your personalized window into what's happening.

Safety

Protect your account and peace-of-mind with our safety tools

SnapFrame is committed to keeping every member of our community safe. Our safety tools give you control over your experience — from content filters and restricted accounts to privacy settings and reporting features designed to protect your well-being.
