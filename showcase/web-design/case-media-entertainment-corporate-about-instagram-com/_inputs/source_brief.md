# SnapFrame — Capture, Create & Share What You Love

## Brand
SnapFrame is a social media platform that makes it easy to capture, create, and share what you love with the people who matter most. The corporate About page introduces the platform's mission, highlights key features, promotes safety and well-being tools, showcases creator opportunities, and surfaces the latest company news.

Tone: modern, bold, playful
Audience: general consumers, creators, parents, and businesses

Brand colors: #000000 (primary black), #7638FA (accent purple), #FF7A00 (accent orange)
