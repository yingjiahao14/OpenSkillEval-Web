Plus credits explained

How do Plus credits work?

You'll get one credit each month (monthly) or 12 credits per year (annual), starting the day you subscribe.

Exchange it now, or later

Use your credits as soon as you get them or save them. You have a whole year to use each credit.

Any course, any price

Credits can be exchanged for any course regardless of price. Once redeemed, the course is yours forever.

Credits expire after 1 year

Credits expire one year after receipt or when membership ends. Make sure to use them!

Earn a certificate with every course
