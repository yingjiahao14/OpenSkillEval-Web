How Plus credits work

Get 12 credits a year — each one redeemable for any course, any price.

🎟️

12 credits per year

You'll receive one credit per month with annual plans (or 12 at once), starting the day you subscribe. Monthly plan includes 1 credit/month.

🛒

Redeem for any course

Credits can be exchanged for any course on the platform regardless of price. A $94.99 masterclass costs the same one credit as a $19.99 intro course.

♾️

Yours forever

Once you redeem a credit for a course, that course is yours to keep forever — even if your Plus membership ends.

📅

Use now or save for later

Credits accumulate throughout the year. Use them immediately on the course you want today, or save them for something special later. Credits expire 1 year after receipt.

💰

Save at least 20%

When you buy additional credits (beyond your free annual allocation), you always save a minimum of 20% compared to regular course prices.
