Frequently Asked Questions

What courses can Plus members watch for free? +

Plus members can watch thousands of courses for free, with 100+ new courses added to the Plus catalog every week. Courses are free to watch for a limited time period. You can always buy a course to keep it forever.

How do Plus credits work? +

You'll get one credit each month (monthly) or 12 credits per year (annual), starting the day you subscribe. Credits accumulate and can be exchanged for any course regardless of price. Credits expire one year after receipt or when membership ends. Once redeemed, the course is yours forever.

When will I get my certificate? +

When you complete a purchased course, you'll instantly get your personalized certificate. Download as PDF or share online. Includes your name, course title, QR code linking to your project, and teacher's signature.

What's the difference between the yearly and monthly subscription? +

Benefits are exactly the same, but the yearly payment offers a substantial discount compared to standard monthly base pricing.
