FAQ

Questions about coaching on LearnForge

Can I offer both 1-on-1 and group coaching?
▼
Yes! LearnForge supports both individual and group coaching formats. You can create separate products for each format, set different pricing, and manage both from a single dashboard.
How does session scheduling work?
▼
LearnForge integrates with Calendly and Google Calendar to handle scheduling automatically. Clients book sessions from your availability, receive confirmation emails, and get reminders — all without you lifting a finger.
Can I sell coaching alongside courses and downloads?
▼
Absolutely. LearnForge is designed for multi-product creators. You can bundle coaching sessions with courses, offer coaching as an upsell after a course purchase, or sell coaching completely independently.
How do I screen and onboard new coaching clients?
▼
LearnForge's customizable intake block lets you add questions, requirements, and screening criteria to your coaching sales page. Only clients who meet your criteria can complete the purchase — giving you full control over who you work with.
