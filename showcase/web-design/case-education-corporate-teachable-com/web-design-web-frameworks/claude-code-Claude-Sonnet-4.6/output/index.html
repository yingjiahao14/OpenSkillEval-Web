FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel my account at any time?
▼
Yes, simply cancel your account if LearnForge isn't right for you. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. You can manage your subscription from your admin dashboard at any time.
Can I change my plan once I sign up?
▼
Yes, you can change your plan at any time from your admin dashboard. The new amount will be charged on your next billing date. Upgrades take effect immediately.
Are there any transaction fees?
▼
0% transaction fees on Builder, Growth, Advanced, and Unlimited plans when using LearnForge Pay. The Starter plan has a 7.5% transaction fee. Standard payment processing fees may apply depending on your payment provider.
Who uses LearnForge?
▼
Creators, experts, entrepreneurs, and businesses serious about education—from language teachers and fitness coaches to finance professionals and health & wellness leaders. Anyone who wants to turn their knowledge into a sustainable online business.
