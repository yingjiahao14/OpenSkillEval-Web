FAQ

Questions about online courses

How do I create my first course?
▼
Sign up for LearnForge, click "New Course," and use the drag-and-drop builder to add videos, text, quizzes, and downloads. You can publish immediately or save as a draft. Our AI tools help you write curriculum and content faster.
Can I offer free courses or trials?
▼
Yes! You can create completely free courses, offer free preview lessons within paid courses, or set up free trial periods for memberships. Free courses are a powerful way to build your audience and convert leads.
What video formats are supported?
▼
LearnForge supports all major video formats including MP4, MOV, and AVI. We host and stream your videos globally with unlimited storage and bandwidth on paid plans. We also support YouTube and Vimeo embeds.
How do I get paid?
▼
LearnForge pays out via direct bank transfer, PayPal, or Stripe. Payouts are processed on a regular schedule (typically every 2 weeks). You can also set up automatic payouts from your admin dashboard.
