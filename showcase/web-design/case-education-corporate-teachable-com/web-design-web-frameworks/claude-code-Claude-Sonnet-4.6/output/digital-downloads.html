FAQ

Questions about digital downloads

What file types can I sell?
▼
You can sell virtually any digital file — PDFs, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, Photoshop files, ZIP archives, MP3s, videos, ebooks, and more. If it's a file, you can sell it on LearnForge.
How do customers receive their downloads?
▼
After purchase, customers receive an instant download link via email and in their LearnForge account. Downloads are securely hosted and can be accessed immediately — no waiting required.
Can I bundle downloads with courses?
▼
Yes! You can bundle digital downloads with courses, coaching sessions, or membership products. Bundles are a powerful way to increase your average order value and provide more comprehensive value to your customers.
Can I offer digital downloads for free as a lead magnet?
▼
Absolutely. Free digital downloads are one of the most effective lead generation tools available. You can set any product to free, require an email address for access, and automatically add downloaders to your email list.
