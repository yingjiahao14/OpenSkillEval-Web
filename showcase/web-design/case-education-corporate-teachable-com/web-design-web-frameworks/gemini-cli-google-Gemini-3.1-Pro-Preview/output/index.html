Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my account at any time?

Yes, simply cancel your account if LearnForge isn't right for you. There are no long-term contracts unless you explicitly sign up for an annual plan.

Can I change my plan once I sign up?

Yes, change your plan at any time from your admin dashboard. The new amount will be charged at your next billing date.

Are there any transaction fees?

0% on Builder, Growth, Advanced, and Unlimited plans using LearnForge:pay. There is a 7.5% fee on the Starter plan. Standard payment gateway processing fees may apply.

Who uses LearnForge?

Creators, experts, entrepreneurs, and businesses serious about education—from language teachers to finance professionals to health and fitness leaders around the globe.
