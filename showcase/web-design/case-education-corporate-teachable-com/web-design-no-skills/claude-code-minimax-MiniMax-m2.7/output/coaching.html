FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does coaching work on LearnForge?
You create a coaching product, set your pricing and availability, and students can book sessions directly through your page. Manage assignments, track progress, and communicate all from your dashboard.
Can I offer both one-on-one and group coaching?
Yes! LearnForge supports both individual and small group coaching. You can create different products for each type and manage them from the same dashboard.
How do payments work?
LearnForge handles all payment processing. Clients pay upfront or in installments, and you receive direct payouts. Automated invoices and receipts included.
What is the average coaching price?
The average coaching price on LearnForge is over $200. You set your own pricing based on your expertise, market, and the value you provide to clients.
