FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I create my first course?
Simply use our drag-and-drop course builder to create your curriculum, add video lessons, set up quizzes, and publish. Our AI tools can help you generate content and sales pages too.
Can I use my own custom domain?
Yes! On our Pro and Business plans, you can connect your own custom domain for a seamless brand experience.
How do payments work?
LearnForge handles all payment processing. Students can pay via credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and more. You receive payouts directly to your account.
Do I need to handle tax compliance?
No! LearnForge calculates, collects, and remits sales tax, VAT, and GST on your behalf across 180+ countries.
