FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What file types can I sell?
You can sell any digital file including PDFs, spreadsheets, ebooks, design assets, templates, email templates, lesson plans, and more.
How do I deliver files to customers?
LearnForge automatically delivers files instantly after purchase. Customers receive a download link and can access their files from their account.
Can I use digital downloads as lead magnets?
Yes! You can offer free downloads to capture leads, then use email marketing to nurture them into paying customers for your other products.
Do I handle tax for digital products?
No! LearnForge calculates, collects, and remits sales tax, VAT, and GST for digital products across all supported countries.
