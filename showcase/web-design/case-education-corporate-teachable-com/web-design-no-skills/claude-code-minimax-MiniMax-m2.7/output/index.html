FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel my account at any time?
Yes, simply cancel your account if LearnForge isn't right for you. No long-term contracts or commitments.
Can I change my plan once I sign up?
Yes, change your plan at any time from your admin dashboard. The new amount will be charged at your next billing date.
Are there any transaction fees?
0% transaction fees on Builder, Growth, Advanced, and Unlimited plans when using LearnForge payments. 7.5% fee on Starter plan. Standard processing fees may apply.
