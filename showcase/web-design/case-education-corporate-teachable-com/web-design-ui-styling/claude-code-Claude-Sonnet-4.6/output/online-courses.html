FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell courses without technical skills?
+
Absolutely. LearnForge is designed for creators, not developers. Our drag-and-drop course builder, AI-powered tools, and customizable templates mean you can have a professional course live in minutes — no coding required.
What payment methods can my students use?
+
Students can pay via credit/debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Afterpay, and Klarna. We support 130+ currencies across 200+ countries so you can sell globally without friction.
How does LearnForge handle taxes and VAT?
+
LearnForge automatically calculates, collects, and remits sales taxes, VAT, and GST on your behalf in applicable regions. You focus on teaching — we handle the compliance paperwork.
Is there a limit on students or video storage?
+
No limits. LearnForge offers unlimited students and unlimited video hosting on all plans. Your course can scale from 10 students to 100,000+ without any platform restrictions.
