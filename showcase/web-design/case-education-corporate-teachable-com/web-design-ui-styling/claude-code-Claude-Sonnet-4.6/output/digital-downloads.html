Frequently asked questions

What file types can I sell?
+
Design files, spreadsheets, ebooks, stock imagery, email templates, lesson plans, and asset libraries — plus many more. If you can create it digitally, you can sell it on LearnForge.
Can I bundle digital downloads with other products?
+
Yes, you can bundle digital downloads with courses, memberships, or coaching packages for added value. Create irresistible offers by combining multiple products at a single price.
How do students access their downloads?
+
Students gain instant access to their downloads immediately after purchase, directly from their student dashboard. No waiting, no manual delivery — it's fully automated.
Are there transaction fees?
+
0% transaction fees on Builder, Growth, Advanced, and Unlimited plans. The Starter plan has a 7.5% transaction fee. Upgrade any time to keep more of what you earn.
