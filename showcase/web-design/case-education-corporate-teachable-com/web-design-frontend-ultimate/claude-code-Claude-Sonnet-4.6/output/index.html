Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel my account at any time?

Yes, simply cancel your account if LearnForge isn't right for you. No long-term contracts or cancellation fees.

Can I change my plan once I sign up?

Yes, change your plan at any time from your admin dashboard. The new amount will be charged at your next billing date.

Are there any transaction fees?

0% on Builder, Growth, Advanced, and Unlimited plans using LearnForge:pay. 7.5% on the Starter plan. Standard payment processing fees may apply.

Who uses LearnForge?

Creators, experts, entrepreneurs, and businesses serious about education—from language teachers to finance professionals to health and fitness leaders.
