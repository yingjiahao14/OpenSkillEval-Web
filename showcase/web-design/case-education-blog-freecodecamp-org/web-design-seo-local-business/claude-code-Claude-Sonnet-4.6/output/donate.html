Frequently Asked Questions

You can contact our donor support team at donors@openlearnhub.org. We respond to all inquiries within 2 business days. You can also visit our support forum for community assistance.
OpenLearnHub publishes its full financial statements, Form 990, and audited financials publicly on our website. We maintain a 4-star rating on Charity Navigator and are accredited by the Better Business Bureau. Our annual reports detail exactly how donations are spent.
OpenLearnHub is a highly efficient charity. The vast majority of our revenue goes directly to creating free educational content, with minimal administrative overhead. We consistently rank among the most efficient education nonprofits in the country.
You can make a one-time donation using the donation form above. Simply select your amount and click "Donate." You'll be directed to our secure payment processor where you can choose a one-time instead of monthly option.
Yes! OpenLearnHub accepts Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and other major cryptocurrencies via our crypto donation portal. Crypto donations may also be tax-deductible — please consult your tax advisor.
Yes! Make checks payable to "OpenLearnHub" and mail to our registered office address. Please include your email address so we can send you a tax receipt.
Many employers match charitable contributions. Check with your HR department about employer matching programs. For payroll deductions, contact us at donors@openlearnhub.org and we'll help you set it up.
Endowment gifts provide long-term support for our mission. Please contact our development team at giving@openlearnhub.org to discuss how to establish an endowment fund in your name or the name of a loved one.
A legacy gift (bequest) through your will or estate plan is one of the most meaningful ways to support OpenLearnHub's mission for generations to come. Contact our legacy giving team for more information and sample bequest language.
Donating appreciated stock can be a tax-efficient way to give. Contact us at donors@openlearnhub.org for our brokerage account information and wire transfer instructions.
You can manage your recurring donation by logging into your OpenLearnHub account and visiting your donor dashboard. From there, you can update your payment method, change the amount, or cancel your subscription at any time with no fees.
Our full donor guide is available on our website. You can also read our annual impact reports, follow us on social media, or join our community forums to see firsthand the difference your donation makes in people's lives around the world.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
