Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get help with my donations? ▼
If you need help with your donations, you can reach out to our support team through the contact form on our website or email us directly. We're here to assist you with any donation-related questions.
How transparent is OpenLearnHub? ▼
OpenLearnHub is committed to transparency. We publish annual reports detailing how donations are used, our financial statements, and the impact of our programs. We are a verified 501(c)(3) charitable organization.
How efficient is OpenLearnHub? ▼
OpenLearnHub is a highly efficient education charity. The vast majority of our expenses go directly toward creating educational content and maintaining our platform, ensuring your donation has maximum impact.
How can I make a one-time donation? ▼
You can make a one-time donation through our secure donation form. Simply choose your amount and select the one-time donation option before proceeding to payment.
Does OpenLearnHub accept donations in Bitcoin or other cryptocurrencies? ▼
Yes, OpenLearnHub accepts donations in Bitcoin and several other major cryptocurrencies. Visit our crypto donation page for wallet addresses and more details.
Can I mail a physical check? ▼
Yes, you can mail a physical check to our office. Please make checks payable to OpenLearnHub and include your email address so we can send you a receipt.
How can I set up matching gifts from my employer, or payroll deductions? ▼
Many employers offer matching gift programs that can double your donation. Check with your HR department to see if your company participates, and follow their process for submitting a matching gift request.
How can I set up an Endowment Gift to OpenLearnHub? ▼
For information about setting up an endowment gift, please contact our development team. We'll work with you and your financial advisor to establish a gift that supports our mission in perpetuity.
How can I set up a Legacy gift to OpenLearnHub? ▼
Legacy gifts can be arranged through your will or estate plan. Please contact our team for the appropriate language and beneficiary information to include OpenLearnHub in your legacy planning.
How can I donate stock to OpenLearnHub? ▼
You can donate appreciated stock to OpenLearnHub, which may offer tax benefits. Contact our development team for our brokerage account details and transfer instructions.
I set up a monthly donation, but I need to update or stop the monthly recurrence. How can I do this? ▼
You can update or cancel your monthly donation at any time by logging into your OpenLearnHub account and managing your donation settings, or by contacting our support team for assistance.
Is there anything else I can learn about donating to OpenLearnHub? ▼
For additional information about donating to OpenLearnHub, including planned giving, corporate sponsorships, and other ways to support our mission, please visit our support page or contact our development team.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
