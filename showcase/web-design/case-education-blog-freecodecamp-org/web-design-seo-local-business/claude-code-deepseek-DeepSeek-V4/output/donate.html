Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our donor support team at donors@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within one business day. You can also visit our donor help center for answers to common questions about receipts, account management, and payment methods.
OpenLearnHub is a fully transparent 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. We publish annual financial reports, and our IRS Form 990 is publicly available on our website. Every donation is tracked, and we provide detailed breakdowns of how funds are allocated across programs, curriculum development, server costs, and operations.
OpenLearnHub is one of the most efficient education charities worldwide. Over 90% of every dollar goes directly toward creating free educational resources, curriculum development, and running our learning platform. This means your donation has maximum impact on learners around the world.
You can make a one-time donation by selecting any amount on our donation page and choosing the one-time option at checkout. All major credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal are accepted. You'll receive an immediate email receipt for your tax records.
Yes, we accept cryptocurrency donations including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USD Coin (USDC), and several other major cryptocurrencies through our crypto donation portal. Crypto donations may provide tax benefits since they are treated as property donations.
Yes, checks can be mailed to our office: OpenLearnHub, 548 Market St, PMB 12345, San Francisco, CA 94104-5401. Please make checks payable to "OpenLearnHub" and include your email address for your donation receipt.
Many employers offer matching gift programs that can double or triple your donation. Check with your HR department or use our matching gift search tool. For payroll deductions, contact donors@openlearnhub.org and we'll provide your employer with the necessary setup instructions.
To discuss endowment giving options, please contact our major gifts team at major-gifts@openlearnhub.org. We can work with you and your financial advisor to structure an endowed gift that creates lasting impact and aligns with your philanthropic goals for generations of learners.
Legacy gifts can be arranged through your estate planning. Contact legacy@openlearnhub.org to discuss including OpenLearnHub in your will, trust, or as a beneficiary designation on retirement accounts or life insurance policies.
Stock donations can be made through our brokerage account. Contact stock-donations@openlearnhub.org for our DTC transfer instructions, brokerage account details, and the necessary forms. Donating appreciated securities may provide significant tax advantages.
You can manage your recurring donations through your donor dashboard at openlearnhub.org/account/donations. From there you can update your payment method, change the donation amount, pause, or cancel your monthly donation at any time. You can also email donors@openlearnhub.org for assistance.
Visit our full Donor FAQ at openlearnhub.org/donate/faq for additional information about workplace giving, donor-advised funds (DAFs), vehicle donations, and other ways to support our mission. You can also subscribe to our donor newsletter for updates on the impact of your giving.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
