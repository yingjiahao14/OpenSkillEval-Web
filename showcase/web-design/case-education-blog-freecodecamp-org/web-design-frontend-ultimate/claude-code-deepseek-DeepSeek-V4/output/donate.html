Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our donor support team by emailing donors@openlearnhub.org. We respond to all inquiries within 1-2 business days and are happy to help with any questions about your donation.
OpenLearnHub is fully transparent. We publish annual financial reports and our IRS Form 990 is publicly available. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, all our financials are open to public inspection.
OpenLearnHub is one of the most efficient education charities in the world. We keep operational costs extremely low through our volunteer-driven model, ensuring that the vast majority of every dollar goes directly to creating educational resources.
You can make a one-time donation by selecting any amount above and choosing the one-time option at checkout. One-time donations are processed securely via our payment partner and go directly toward funding our educational mission.
Yes, we accept cryptocurrency donations including Bitcoin, Ethereum, and several other major cryptocurrencies. Visit our crypto donation portal for wallet addresses and instructions.
Yes, you can mail a check payable to "OpenLearnHub" to our mailing address. Please include your email address so we can send you a donation receipt for tax purposes.
Many employers offer matching gift programs that can double or triple your donation. Check with your HR department to see if your company matches charitable contributions, and submit OpenLearnHub as the recipient organization.
Endowment gifts help ensure OpenLearnHub's long-term sustainability. Contact our development team at donors@openlearnhub.org to discuss endowment options, including named endowments and planned giving vehicles.
Legacy gifts, such as including OpenLearnHub in your will or trust, create a lasting impact. We recommend consulting with your estate planning attorney and contacting us for the correct legal language and tax ID information.
Donating appreciated stock can be a tax-efficient way to support our mission. Contact our donor support team for brokerage account details and transfer instructions. Stock donations may provide significant tax advantages.
You can manage your monthly donation through your donor portal account. Log in to view, modify, or cancel your recurring donation at any time. Changes take effect immediately and you'll receive a confirmation email.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
