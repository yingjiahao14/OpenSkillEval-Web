Frequently Asked Questions

You can email our support team at donors@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 24 hours during business days. You can also reach us through the support chat on our website.
OpenLearnHub is fully transparent about our finances and operations. We publish annual reports detailing our revenue, expenses, and impact metrics. Our Form 990 is publicly available, and we maintain a 4-star rating on Charity Navigator.
We are one of the most efficient education charities. Over 90% of donations go directly to program services. Our cost-per-learner is among the lowest in the education nonprofit space, enabling maximum impact per dollar.
You can make a one-time donation through our donation page by selecting the one-time option. We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. Simply choose your amount and complete the checkout process.
Yes, we accept Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other major cryptocurrencies through our partnership with The Giving Block. Crypto donations are tax-deductible and you can receive a receipt for your records.
Yes. Please make checks payable to "OpenLearnHub" and mail them to our office. Contact donors@openlearnhub.org for our current mailing address. Please include your email address so we can send you a tax receipt.
Many employers match charitable donations. Check with your HR department to see if your company participates. For payroll deductions, you can set up recurring donations through your employer's giving platform. Contact us if you need our EIN or other documentation.
Endowment gifts provide long-term sustainable funding for our mission. Please contact our development team at endowments@openlearnhub.org to discuss options for establishing an endowment gift.
Legacy gifts through your will or trust help ensure the future of free coding education. Please consult with your estate planning attorney and contact us at legacy@openlearnhub.org for our organization details.
Donating appreciated stock can provide tax benefits while supporting our mission. Contact our finance team at stock@openlearnhub.org for transfer instructions and our brokerage account details.
You can manage your recurring donations by logging into your account settings. From there, you can update the amount, change your payment method, or cancel the recurring donation. You can also email donors@openlearnhub.org for assistance.
Visit our "About" page to learn more about our mission, impact, and how your donations are used. You can also read our annual transparency report for a detailed breakdown of our finances and program outcomes.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
