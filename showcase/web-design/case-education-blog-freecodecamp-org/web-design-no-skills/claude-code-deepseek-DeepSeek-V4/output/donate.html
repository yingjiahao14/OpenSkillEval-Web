Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our donor support team at donors@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days. You can also visit our Support page for additional resources and guides on managing your donations.

We are a fully transparent 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our annual financial reports, IRS Form 990 filings, and audited financial statements are publicly available on our website. We believe donors deserve to know exactly how their contributions are being used.

OpenLearnHub is one of the most efficient education charities in the world. The vast majority of every dollar goes directly toward creating and maintaining free educational resources. Our lean team and open-source approach mean we can deliver thousands of hours of learning for just a few dollars per learner.

You can make a one-time donation by selecting any amount above and choosing the "one-time" option during checkout. You can also donate via PayPal, bank transfer, or by mailing a check. All one-time donations are tax-deductible to the full extent of the law.

Yes! We accept donations in Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and several other major cryptocurrencies through our crypto donation portal. This allows us to accept contributions from supporters around the world with minimal transaction fees.

Absolutely. Please make checks payable to "OpenLearnHub" and mail them to our office address listed on our Contact page. Please include your email address so we can send you a donation receipt for tax purposes.

Many employers offer matching gift programs that can double or even triple your donation's impact. Check with your HR department to see if your company participates. For payroll deductions, you can set up recurring donations through our portal or coordinate with your employer's giving program.

Endowment gifts provide lasting support for our mission. To discuss establishing an endowment, please contact our major gifts team at major-gifts@openlearnhub.org. We'll work with you and your financial advisor to structure a gift that aligns with your philanthropic goals.

Legacy gifts — also called planned gifts — can be made through your will, trust, or by naming OpenLearnHub as a beneficiary of a retirement account or life insurance policy. Contact legacy@openlearnhub.org for sample language and guidance on including us in your estate plans.

Donating appreciated stock can be a tax-efficient way to give. Contact stock-donations@openlearnhub.org for our brokerage account details and transfer instructions. Please include the stock symbol and number of shares in your message so we can properly acknowledge your gift.

You can manage your monthly donation by logging into your donor dashboard on our website. From there, you can update your payment method, change the amount, or cancel your recurring donation at any time. Alternatively, contact donors@openlearnhub.org and our team will assist you.

We're always happy to answer questions! Visit our comprehensive Donor FAQ page for more details, or reach out to our team directly at donors@openlearnhub.org. Thank you for your interest in supporting free coding education for everyone.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
