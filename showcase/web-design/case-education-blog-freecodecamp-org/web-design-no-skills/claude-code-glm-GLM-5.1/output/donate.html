Frequently Asked Questions

If you need help with your donations, please email us at support@openlearnhub.org and our team will assist you promptly.
OpenLearnHub publishes annual reports detailing how donations are used. We are committed to full transparency and our financial records are publicly available.
OpenLearnHub is one of the most efficient charities in the education sector. The vast majority of our expenses go directly toward creating educational content and tools.
You can make a one-time donation through our donation form by selecting your preferred amount and completing the payment process.
Yes, we accept cryptocurrency donations. Please visit our crypto donation page for wallet addresses and more details.
Yes, you can mail a check to our office. Please make it payable to OpenLearnHub and send it to our mailing address listed on our contact page.
Many employers offer matching gift programs. Check with your HR department, and they can help you set up payroll deductions or matching contributions to OpenLearnHub.
For endowment gifts, please contact our development team at giving@openlearnhub.org. We'll work with you to establish the gift according to your wishes.
Legacy gifts can be arranged through your estate planning attorney. Contact us for the relevant legal details and beneficiary information.
To donate stock, please reach out to us at giving@openlearnhub.org and we'll provide our brokerage account details for the transfer.
You can manage your monthly donation by logging into your OpenLearnHub account and visiting your donation settings, or by emailing support@openlearnhub.org.
For additional questions about donating, please visit our FAQ page or contact us directly. We're happy to help with any inquiries.
