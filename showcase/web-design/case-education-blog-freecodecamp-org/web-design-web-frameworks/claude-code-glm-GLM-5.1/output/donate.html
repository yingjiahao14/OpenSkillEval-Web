Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our donor support team by emailing donate@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 1–2 business days and are happy to help with any questions about your contribution.
OpenLearnHub publishes annual reports detailing how donations are used. Our financial statements are publicly available and we maintain full transparency about our operations and spending.
OpenLearnHub is one of the most efficient charities in the education space. The vast majority of every dollar donated goes directly toward creating educational content and supporting learners.
You can make a one-time donation through our secure payment form. Select your desired amount and choose the one-time option before completing your donation.
Yes, OpenLearnHub accepts donations in several major cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin and Ethereum. Visit our crypto donation page for wallet addresses and instructions.
Yes, you can mail a check to our office. Please make checks payable to OpenLearnHub and send them to our mailing address listed on the contact page.
Many employers offer matching gift programs. Check with your HR department to see if your company participates, and let us know if you need any documentation from our end to facilitate the match.
Endowment gifts help ensure the long-term sustainability of our mission. Please contact our development team at endowment@openlearnhub.org to discuss setting up an endowment.
Legacy gifts allow you to include OpenLearnHub in your estate planning. Contact our team for information about wills, trusts, and other legacy giving options.
We accept stock donations which can provide tax benefits. Contact our finance team for brokerage account details and transfer instructions.
You can manage your recurring donation through your OpenLearnHub account settings, or contact our support team and we'll help you update or cancel your monthly donation.
We're happy to answer any additional questions. Reach out to us at donate@openlearnhub.org and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
