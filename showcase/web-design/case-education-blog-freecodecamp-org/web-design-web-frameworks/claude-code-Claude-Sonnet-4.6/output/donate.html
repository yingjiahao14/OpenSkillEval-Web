Frequently Asked Questions

You can contact our donor support team at donate@openlearnhub.org. We're happy to assist with any questions about your contributions, payment methods, or donation history.
OpenLearnHub publishes annual reports and financial statements on our website. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, we file Form 990 with the IRS, which is publicly available. We believe in full financial transparency with our community.
OpenLearnHub is rated highly efficient by Charity Navigator and similar watchdogs. The vast majority of our expenses go directly to education programs, curriculum development, and content creation — not administrative overhead.
On our donation page, you can switch between monthly and one-time giving options. Simply select "One-time" and choose your preferred amount. We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers.
Yes! OpenLearnHub accepts Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other cryptocurrencies through our partner platform. Please visit our crypto donation page for wallet addresses and instructions.
Yes, checks can be made payable to "OpenLearnHub" and mailed to our registered address. Please include your email address so we can send you a receipt for tax purposes.
Many employers match charitable donations. Contact your HR department and provide them with our EIN (tax ID number) to initiate matching. For payroll deductions, check if your employer uses Benevity or YourCause platforms where OpenLearnHub is registered.
Endowment gifts provide sustainable long-term funding. Please reach out to our development team at endowment@openlearnhub.org to discuss how your contribution can create a lasting legacy for free coding education.
A legacy gift (bequest) can be arranged through your estate planning attorney. We recommend including our legal name "OpenLearnHub, Inc." and our EIN in your will or trust documents. Contact us for a sample bequest language.
Donating appreciated stock may provide significant tax advantages. Please contact our finance team for our brokerage account details and transfer instructions. Stock donations are processed within 3–5 business days.
You can manage your recurring donation by logging into your OpenLearnHub account and visiting the "Billing" section under settings. You can change the amount, payment method, or cancel at any time with no penalty.
For detailed information about donor programs, our impact reports, and all giving options, visit our full transparency page or reach out to our donor relations team. We're grateful for every contribution that helps us keep education free for everyone.
A OpenLearnHub

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
