Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our donor support team by emailing donors@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days.

We publish annual financial reports and are rated highly by charity watchdogs. As a 501(c)(3) organization, our IRS filings are publicly available.

We operate with a lean team and minimal overhead. The vast majority of every dollar goes directly to creating and maintaining educational resources.

You can make a one-time donation through our donation form. Select your preferred amount and choose the one-time option during checkout.

Yes, we accept cryptocurrency donations through our partner platform. Visit our crypto donation page for wallet addresses and supported currencies.

Yes, checks can be mailed to our office address. Please make checks payable to "OpenLearnHub" and include your email address for a receipt.

Many employers match charitable contributions. Check with your HR department and provide them with our EIN number. For payroll deductions, use our workplace giving portal.

Endowment gifts help sustain our mission long-term. Contact our planned giving team at plannedgiving@openlearnhub.org to discuss options.

Legacy gifts can be arranged through your estate planning. We recommend consulting with your financial advisor and contacting us for the necessary documentation.

Stock donations can be transferred through our brokerage account. Contact stockdonations@openlearnhub.org for transfer instructions and account details.

You can manage your recurring donation through your account settings on our website, or contact donor support for assistance with changes or cancellations.

Visit our full donation FAQ page for additional information, or reach out to our team directly. We're happy to answer any questions about supporting our mission.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
