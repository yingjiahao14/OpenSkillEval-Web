Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about donating to OpenLearnHub

For any questions about your donation, you can reach our donor support team at donors@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days. You can also find detailed information in our Donor Help Center.
OpenLearnHub is committed to full transparency. We publish annual financial reports, IRS Form 990 filings, and detailed breakdowns of how donations are used. All of our code is open source, and our curriculum is developed in public on GitHub.
OpenLearnHub is one of the most efficient education charities in the world. We operate with a lean team and leverage thousands of volunteer contributors. Over 90% of donations go directly to creating and maintaining educational resources. Our cost per student learning hour is among the lowest in the education nonprofit sector.
You can make a one-time donation by selecting any amount above and choosing the "one-time" option during checkout. You can donate via credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer. One-time donations are processed securely through Stripe.
Yes! We accept donations in Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and several other major cryptocurrencies through our partnership with The Giving Block. Visit our crypto donation page for wallet addresses and instructions.
Absolutely. Please make checks payable to "OpenLearnHub Inc." and mail them to: OpenLearnHub, 548 Market St, PMB 45678, San Francisco, CA 94104, United States. Please include your email address so we can send you a donation receipt.
Many employers match charitable donations, effectively doubling your contribution. Check with your HR department about matching gift programs, or use our matching gift tool on the donation page. For payroll deductions, we can provide the necessary documentation to your employer's giving platform (Benevity, Bright Funds, etc.).
Endowment gifts help sustain OpenLearnHub for the long term. To discuss endowment opportunities, please contact our development team at major-gifts@openlearnhub.org. We work with donors to structure gifts that align with their philanthropic goals while supporting our mission.
Including OpenLearnHub in your estate plans is a powerful way to support free education for future generations. You can name OpenLearnHub as a beneficiary in your will, trust, retirement account, or life insurance policy. Contact legacy@openlearnhub.org for sample language and assistance.
Donating appreciated securities is a tax-efficient way to support OpenLearnHub. You can transfer stocks, bonds, or mutual fund shares directly to our brokerage account. Contact stock-donations@openlearnhub.org for our DTC transfer instructions and account details.
You can manage your recurring donation at any time through your OpenLearnHub account settings under "Donation Management." From there, you can update your payment method, change the donation amount, pause donations, or cancel the recurring plan. Changes take effect immediately.
We encourage you to explore our annual report and impact dashboard for a comprehensive view of how donations transform lives. You can also join our Discord community to hear directly from learners whose careers have been changed by OpenLearnHub. For any additional questions, reach out to donors@openlearnhub.org.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
