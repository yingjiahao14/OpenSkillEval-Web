Donation FAQ

Everything you need to know about supporting OpenLearnHub.

You can contact our support team at donate@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days.
We publish annual reports and financial statements on our website. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, we file Form 990 publicly with the IRS each year. You can view all our financials on our Charity page.
The vast majority of our funds go directly to building and maintaining educational resources. We operate with a small, distributed team and minimal overhead to maximize our educational impact.
You can make a one-time donation by selecting your preferred amount above and clicking "Donate." On the payment page, select "One-time" instead of "Monthly."
Yes! We accept Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other major cryptocurrencies. Please visit our Crypto Donation page for wallet addresses and instructions.
Yes, checks can be made payable to "OpenLearnHub" and mailed to our registered address. Contact us for the current mailing address.
Many employers offer matching gift programs. Contact your HR department and provide them with our EIN number (available on our Charity page). For payroll deductions, check if your employer uses Benevity or similar platforms.
Endowment gifts create a permanent funding source for OpenLearnHub. Please contact our development team at endowment@openlearnhub.org to discuss options.
A legacy gift (bequest) allows you to include OpenLearnHub in your estate plan. Our EIN and legal name can be provided upon request to share with your estate attorney.
Stock donations can provide significant tax advantages. Please contact giving@openlearnhub.org with details about the stock you wish to donate, and we'll provide brokerage transfer instructions.
You can manage your recurring donation by logging into your account and visiting the Billing section, or by contacting us at donate@openlearnhub.org and we'll assist you promptly.
