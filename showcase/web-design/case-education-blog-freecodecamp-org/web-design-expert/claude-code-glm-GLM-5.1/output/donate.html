Frequently Asked Questions

If you need help with your donations, please email our support team and we'll get back to you within 24 hours. We're here to help with any questions about your contribution.
OpenLearnHub is committed to full transparency. We publish annual reports detailing how donations are used, our financial statements, and impact metrics. You can find all of this information on our transparency page.
OpenLearnHub is one of the most efficient charities in the education space. The vast majority of every dollar donated goes directly toward creating educational content and supporting learners worldwide.
You can make a one-time donation through our secure donation form. Simply select your preferred amount and complete the payment process. We accept all major credit cards and PayPal.
Yes, OpenLearnHub accepts donations in Bitcoin and several other major cryptocurrencies. Please visit our cryptocurrency donation page for wallet addresses and instructions.
Yes, you can mail a check to our office. Please make checks payable to OpenLearnHub and send them to our mailing address listed on the contact page.
Many employers offer matching gift programs. Check with your HR department to see if your company participates. We can provide any documentation needed to process matching gifts or payroll deductions.
For endowment gifts and other major contributions, please contact our development team directly. We'll work with you and your financial advisors to structure the gift in the most beneficial way.
Legacy gifts help ensure OpenLearnHub can continue its mission for generations to come. Please contact us for information about including OpenLearnHub in your will or estate plan.
We accept stock donations. Please contact our team for brokerage account details and transfer instructions. Donating appreciated stock can provide significant tax benefits.
You can manage your monthly donation through your account settings or by contacting our support team. We make it easy to update your amount, change payment methods, or cancel at any time.
We're happy to answer any additional questions. Feel free to reach out to our team via email or through our community forums. We believe in complete transparency and are always available to discuss how your contributions make a difference.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
