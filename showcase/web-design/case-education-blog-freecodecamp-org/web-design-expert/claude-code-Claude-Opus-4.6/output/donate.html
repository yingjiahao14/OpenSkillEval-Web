Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our support team at donors@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 24 hours and are happy to help with any questions about your donations.
OpenLearnHub is fully transparent. We publish our annual tax filings, audited financial statements, and quarterly reports. You can review our finances on our transparency page.
We operate with minimal overhead. The vast majority of donations go directly toward creating educational content, maintaining our platform, and supporting our mission of free coding education for all.
You can make a one-time donation by selecting an amount above and choosing the one-time option during checkout. We accept major credit cards and PayPal.
Yes, we accept Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other major cryptocurrencies. Please contact us for wallet details and instructions.
Yes, you can mail checks to our office. Please contact our team for the current mailing address and instructions.
Many employers offer matching gift programs. Check with your HR department to see if your company participates. We can provide any documentation needed to process the match.
For endowment gifts, please reach out to our development team. We can work with you and your financial advisor to structure a gift that meets your philanthropic goals.
Legacy gifts can be arranged through your estate planning attorney. Contact us for the information needed to include OpenLearnHub in your will or trust.
We accept stock donations. Donating appreciated stock can provide tax benefits. Contact our team for brokerage transfer instructions.
You can manage your recurring donations from your account settings. If you need help, our support team is available to assist with any changes.
Visit our donation FAQ page for more details, or reach out to our team directly. We're always happy to answer questions and help you find the best way to support our mission.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
