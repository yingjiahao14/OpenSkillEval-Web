Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our donor support team at donations@openlearnhub.org or through our contact form. We typically respond within 1-2 business days.

We publish annual reports with detailed financials, impact metrics, and strategic goals. All our financial statements are available on our website.

Over 85% of all donations go directly to educational programs and content creation. We maintain low overhead costs to maximize impact.

You can make a one-time donation using our secure donation form. Simply select "One-time" instead of "Monthly" and enter your desired amount.

Yes, we accept cryptocurrency donations. Please contact us at giving@openlearnhub.org for instructions on how to make a crypto donation.

Yes, we accept checks. Please make them payable to OpenLearnHub and mail to our donor relations office at 123 Education Lane, San Francisco, CA 94102.

Many employers offer matching gift programs. Check with your HR department for eligibility and submission guidelines. We can provide all necessary documentation.

Contact our planned giving team at plannedgiving@openlearnhub.org to discuss endowment options that align with your charitable goals.

Legacy gifts can be arranged through our planned giving program. Please contact plannedgiving@openlearnhub.org for more information.

Stock donations can be transferred electronically. Contact our donor relations team for the necessary brokerage account information.

You can manage your recurring donation through our donor portal or by contacting donations@openlearnhub.org. Changes take effect immediately.

Visit our donor FAQ page or contact us directly at donations@openlearnhub.org. We're happy to answer any questions about making a gift.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
