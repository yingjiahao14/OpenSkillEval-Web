Frequently Asked Questions

You can email us at support@openlearnhub.org and our team will be happy to help with any donation-related questions or issues.

OpenLearnHub publishes annual reports detailing how donations are used. We are committed to full transparency and our financial records are publicly available.

OpenLearnHub is one of the most efficient education charities. The vast majority of our expenses go directly toward creating educational content and maintaining our platform.

You can make a one-time donation through our donation form. Simply select your preferred amount and choose the one-time option instead of monthly.

Yes, we accept donations in several major cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin and Ethereum. Contact us for wallet addresses.

Yes, you can mail a check to our office. Please make it payable to OpenLearnHub and send it to our mailing address listed on our contact page.

Many employers offer matching gift programs. Check with your HR department and provide them with our EIN. We can also help set up payroll deductions.

For endowment gifts, please contact our development team at giving@openlearnhub.org. We can work with you and your financial advisor to structure the gift.

Legacy gifts can be arranged through your will or estate plan. Contact us for sample bequest language and to discuss your intentions.

We gladly accept stock donations. Please contact our finance team for brokerage account details and transfer instructions.

You can manage your recurring donation by logging into your account or emailing us at support@openlearnhub.org. We'll process changes or cancellations promptly.

We encourage you to visit our About page for more details about our mission, financials, and impact. You can also reach out to us directly with any questions.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
