Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach out to our support team at donate@openlearnhub.org and we'll be happy to assist you with any donation-related questions.

We publish annual reports detailing how every dollar is spent. Our financials are audited by independent third parties and available on our website.

We maintain a lean operating model with minimal overhead. The vast majority of donations go directly toward creating educational content and maintaining our platform.

You can switch to a one-time donation by clicking "edit amount" below the donate button and selecting the one-time option.

Yes, we accept donations in Bitcoin, Ethereum, and several other cryptocurrencies. Please contact us for wallet addresses and instructions.

Yes, checks can be mailed to our headquarters. Please make checks payable to "OpenLearnHub" and include your email address so we can send a receipt.

Many employers offer matching gift programs. Contact your HR department to see if they participate, and forward the matching gift form to us.

Endowment gifts help secure our long-term future. Please contact our development team to discuss endowment options and recognition.

Legacy gifts through wills, trusts, or beneficiary designations are a meaningful way to support future learners. Reach out to learn more.

Stock donations can be tax-advantaged. Contact us and we'll provide transfer instructions for your brokerage.

You can manage your recurring donation by signing into your donor portal or by emailing us directly with your request.

We're always happy to talk. Reach out to donate@openlearnhub.org and a member of our team will get back to you promptly.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
