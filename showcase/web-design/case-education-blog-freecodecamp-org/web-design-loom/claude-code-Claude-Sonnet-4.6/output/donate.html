Frequently Asked Questions

You can contact our donor support team at donors@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within one business day and are happy to assist with any questions about your donation.
We publish detailed annual reports, financial statements, and 990 tax filings publicly. We're rated highly by charity watchdogs and maintain full transparency about how donor funds are allocated.
Over 90% of donations go directly to our educational programs. We keep our overhead extremely low by relying on open-source tools, volunteer contributions, and an efficient remote team.
You can make a one-time donation using the form above — simply select your preferred amount and choose "one-time" from the payment options. We accept all major credit cards.
Yes! We accept Bitcoin and several other cryptocurrencies. Visit our crypto donation page or contact us for wallet addresses and instructions.
Yes, you can mail a check payable to "OpenLearnHub" to our registered address. Please contact us for our current mailing address and to ensure proper processing.
Many employers offer gift-matching programs. Check with your HR department and use our EIN (tax ID) to submit the matching gift request. We're happy to provide documentation needed for your employer.
Endowment gifts create a lasting legacy. Contact our development team to discuss endowment options, minimum amounts, and how your gift will be invested and used to fund education in perpetuity.
A legacy gift (bequest) allows you to include OpenLearnHub in your estate plan. Please consult with your estate attorney and contact us so we can properly acknowledge your generous intention.
Stock donations offer tax advantages — you avoid capital gains tax and can deduct the full fair market value. Contact us for our brokerage account details to transfer appreciated stock.
Log in to your OpenLearnHub account and visit the "Billing" section in your profile settings to update or cancel your recurring donation at any time. You can also email us for assistance.
Visit our full Donor FAQ page on our website for comprehensive information. You can also read our annual transparency reports to see exactly how your donations are making an impact around the world.
