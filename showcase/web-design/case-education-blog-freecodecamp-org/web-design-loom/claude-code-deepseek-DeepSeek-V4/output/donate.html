Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our donor support team at donors@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days.

We publish annual financial reports and our IRS Form 990 is publicly available. Our finances are openly shared with the community.

Over 90% of donations go directly to our educational programs and content creation. We keep administrative costs minimal through our volunteer-driven model.

You can make a one-time donation via credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer. Visit our donation page and select "One-time" instead of "Monthly" when prompted.

Endowment gifts help sustain our mission long-term. Contact our development team at endowment@openlearnhub.org for information about endowment options.

Legacy gifts through your will or estate plan make a lasting impact. Email legacy@openlearnhub.org to discuss planned giving options.

Stock donations can be tax-efficient. Contact stock@openlearnhub.org and we'll provide transfer instructions for your brokerage.

We're happy to answer any additional questions. Visit our full donation FAQ or reach out to donors@openlearnhub.org for personalized assistance.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
