Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our support team by emailing donate@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 24 hours and are happy to help with any questions about your donation.
OpenLearnHub is committed to full transparency. We publish annual reports detailing how donations are used, our financial statements are publicly available, and we maintain a Platinum Seal of Transparency on GuideStar.
OpenLearnHub is one of the most efficient charities in the education sector. More than 97% of our expenses go directly toward our educational programs, with minimal overhead costs.
You can make a one-time donation through our donation form by selecting your preferred amount and choosing the one-time payment option. We accept all major credit cards and PayPal.
Yes, we accept donations in Bitcoin, Ethereum, and several other major cryptocurrencies through our partnership with The Giving Block. Cryptocurrency donations are tax-deductible.
Yes, you can mail a check payable to OpenLearnHub to our mailing address. Please include your email address so we can send you a receipt for tax purposes.
Many employers offer matching gift programs that can double your donation. Check with your HR department about matching gifts and payroll deduction options. We are registered with most major matching gift platforms.
Endowment gifts help secure the long-term future of OpenLearnHub. Please contact our development team at endowment@openlearnhub.org to discuss setting up an endowment gift tailored to your philanthropic goals.
Legacy gifts ensure that future generations can learn to code for free. You can include OpenLearnHub in your will or estate plan. Contact legacy@openlearnhub.org for more information about planned giving options.
Donating appreciated stock is a tax-efficient way to support OpenLearnHub. You can avoid capital gains tax while receiving a charitable deduction. Contact donate@openlearnhub.org for our brokerage account details and transfer instructions.
You can manage your monthly donation by logging into your OpenLearnHub account and navigating to the donation settings. Alternatively, email donate@openlearnhub.org and we'll help you update or cancel your recurring donation.
We're happy to answer any additional questions you may have. Please reach out to donate@openlearnhub.org and our team will provide you with all the information you need about supporting OpenLearnHub's mission.
