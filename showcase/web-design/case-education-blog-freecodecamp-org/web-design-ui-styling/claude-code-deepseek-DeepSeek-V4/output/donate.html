Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our donor support team at support@openlearnhub.org. We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. You can also visit our Support page for immediate answers to common questions.
We publish annual financial reports and maintain full transparency about how donations are used. As a 501(c)(3) organization, our tax filings are publicly available. Over 80% of every dollar goes directly to educational program creation and maintenance.
We are one of the most efficient education charities in the world. With a lean team and volunteer contributors, we maximize the impact of every donation. Our cost per learner is among the lowest in the nonprofit education sector.
On the donation form above, you can choose any amount — simply click "edit amount" to enter a custom value. Select the one-time option during checkout instead of the monthly recurring option.
Yes, we accept cryptocurrency donations including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and several other major cryptocurrencies. Visit our crypto donation portal for wallet addresses and instructions.
Yes, we accept physical checks. Please make checks payable to "OpenLearnHub Inc." and mail them to our mailing address listed on our Contact page. Please include your email address so we can send you a donation receipt.
Many employers offer donation matching programs. Search for OpenLearnHub in your employer's giving portal, or email us for assistance. For payroll deductions, check if your company offers this through platforms like Benevity or United Way.
Endowment gifts help ensure OpenLearnHub's long-term sustainability. Contact our development team at endowment@openlearnhub.org to discuss endowment options, including named endowments and planned giving strategies.
Including OpenLearnHub in your estate planning creates a lasting impact. We recommend consulting with your attorney or financial advisor. Contact legacy@openlearnhub.org for assistance with bequest language and beneficiary designations.
Donating appreciated securities can be tax-efficient. Contact stockdonations@openlearnhub.org for our DTC transfer instructions and brokerage account details. Please include the stock symbol and number of shares you wish to transfer.
You can manage your recurring donation through your OpenLearnHub account settings under "Donations." From there, you can update the amount, change payment methods, or cancel the recurrence at any time. Changes take effect immediately.
Thank you for your interest in supporting OpenLearnHub! Visit our Donor Resources page for comprehensive information about all giving options, tax receipts, and impact reports. You can also subscribe to our donor newsletter for updates on how your contributions make a difference.
O OpenLearnHub

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
