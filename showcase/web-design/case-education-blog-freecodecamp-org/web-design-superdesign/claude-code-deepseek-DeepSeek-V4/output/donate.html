Frequently Asked Questions

If you need help with your donation, please contact our support team at support@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 24 hours. You can also visit our Help Center for immediate answers to common questions about donations, receipts, and account management.
OpenLearnHub is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. We publish annual financial reports and are committed to full transparency. You can view our IRS Form 990 filings and annual reports on our About page.
OpenLearnHub is one of the most efficient education charities. The vast majority of our donations go directly toward creating educational resources, maintaining our platform, and supporting the community. Our lean team and open-source approach ensure maximum impact per dollar.
You can make a one-time donation by selecting any amount above and choosing the one-time option during checkout. One-time donations are processed securely through our payment partner and you'll receive a receipt immediately via email.
Yes, we accept cryptocurrency donations including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and several other major cryptocurrencies. Visit our crypto donation page for wallet addresses and instructions.
Yes, we accept physical checks. Please make them payable to "OpenLearnHub" and mail to our office address. Include your email address so we can send you a donation receipt.
Many employers offer matching gift programs that can double or triple your donation. Check with your HR department to see if your company participates. For payroll deductions, you can set up recurring donations through our platform that align with your pay schedule.
Endowment gifts help ensure OpenLearnHub's long-term sustainability. Please contact our development team at endowment@openlearnhub.org to discuss endowment options and naming opportunities.
Legacy gifts can be arranged through your estate planning. You can include OpenLearnHub in your will or trust, or designate us as a beneficiary. Contact legacy@openlearnhub.org for sample language and assistance.
Stock donations are a tax-efficient way to support our mission. Contact stockdonations@openlearnhub.org for our brokerage account information and transfer instructions.
You can manage your recurring donation through your account settings. Log in, navigate to the Donations section, and you'll find options to update the amount, payment method, or cancel the recurrence at any time.
We appreciate your interest in supporting OpenLearnHub! Beyond monetary donations, you can contribute by volunteering, contributing to our open source projects, or spreading the word. Visit our Get Involved page to learn more about all the ways you can help.

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
