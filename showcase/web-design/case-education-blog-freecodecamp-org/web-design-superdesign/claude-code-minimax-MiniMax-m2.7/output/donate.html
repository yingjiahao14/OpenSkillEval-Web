Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our support team by emailing donate@openlearnhub.org or visiting our Discord server for real-time assistance with any donation-related questions.

We publish annual reports detailing how donations are used. Our financial statements are available on our website, and we maintain full transparency about our operations and impact.

Over 85% of our revenue goes directly to educational programs and resources. We maintain low overhead costs while maximizing the impact of every donation.

You can make a one-time donation using the donation form above. Simply select your preferred amount and click Donate. You can also cancel or modify your recurring donation at any time.

Yes, we accept cryptocurrency donations. Please contact our development team for details on how to make a cryptocurrency contribution to OpenLearnHub.

Yes, we accept checks made payable to OpenLearnHub. Please send them to our PO Box address listed on our Contact page.

Many employers offer matching gift programs. Please check with your HR department to see if your employer matches donations to 501(c)(3) organizations like OpenLearnHub.

To set up an endowment gift, please contact our development team at donate@openlearnhub.org for more information on how to establish a lasting legacy.

Legacy gifts can be arranged through our planned giving program. Please reach out to our team for more information about including OpenLearnHub in your will or estate planning.

Stock donations can be processed through our brokerage account. Please contact us for transfer instructions and to ensure proper acknowledgment of your generous contribution.

You can manage your recurring donation at any time through our donor portal, or contact our support team to update or cancel your monthly contribution.

Visit our Donors page for more information about giving options, tax deductibility, and the impact of your support. We're grateful for every contribution!

OpenLearnHub is a donor-supported tax-exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Our mission: to help people learn to code for free. We accomplish this by creating thousands of videos, articles, and interactive coding lessons — all freely available to the public.
