Frequently Asked Questions

You can reach our support team at donors@openlearnhub.org. We typically respond within 2 business days. You can also visit our donor support page for common questions and self-service options.
OpenLearnHub publishes annual reports and financial statements publicly. We maintain a Charity Navigator profile and share detailed breakdowns of how donations are allocated. Our financials are audited by an independent CPA firm each year.
Over 90 cents of every dollar donated goes directly to our educational mission. We keep overhead extremely low by operating as a distributed remote team and leveraging open source tools. We consistently earn 4-star ratings from top charity watchdog organizations.
Use the donation form above — simply choose your amount and select "one-time" instead of monthly. You can also donate via PayPal, check, or wire transfer. Contact us at donors@openlearnhub.org for alternative payment arrangements.
Yes! OpenLearnHub accepts donations in Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other major cryptocurrencies through The Giving Block. Crypto donations are tax-deductible and you'll receive a donation receipt for your records.
Yes. Make checks payable to "OpenLearnHub" and mail to: OpenLearnHub, 3905 Hedgcoxe Rd, Suite 160, Plano, TX 75025. Please include your email address so we can send a donation receipt.
Many employers match employee donations. Contact your HR department to check eligibility. For payroll deductions, search for "OpenLearnHub" in your employer's giving portal. We're registered with most major corporate giving platforms.
Endowment gifts permanently support our mission. Please contact our development team at legacy@openlearnhub.org to discuss options, including named endowment funds. We work with donors and their financial advisors to structure the gift to meet your philanthropic goals.
A legacy gift (bequest) allows you to leave a lasting impact. You can designate OpenLearnHub as a beneficiary in your will, trust, or retirement account. Contact legacy@openlearnhub.org for sample bequest language and more information.
Donating appreciated stock is one of the most tax-efficient ways to give. You avoid capital gains tax and can deduct the full fair market value. Contact donors@openlearnhub.org for our brokerage account details and DTC transfer instructions.
You can update or cancel your recurring donation anytime. Log in to your OpenLearnHub account and visit Donation Settings, or email donors@openlearnhub.org with your request. We'll process changes within 2 business days — before your next billing date.
Visit our full donor FAQ at openlearnhub.org/donors, or email us at donors@openlearnhub.org. You can also explore our annual impact reports and community achievements to see exactly how your donations are used to help learners worldwide.
