PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Features Designed for Energy

Explore the capabilities that make WellStream the leading choice for energy data management.

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. Our streamlined onboarding process gets your team operational faster than any legacy system migration — without compromising on data integrity or feature depth.
SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. We maintain rigorous security controls across every layer of the platform — from network perimeter to application logic — so your sensitive operational data stays protected.
Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. Engineers and field operators can capture well data, inspection reports, and production readings on-site — with automatic synchronization once connectivity is restored.
Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Build custom views for production volumes, allocation tracking, downtime analysis, and compliance metrics — all updated in real time from a single source of truth.
Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Connect SCADA systems, accounting platforms, regulatory databases, and BI tools — creating a unified data ecosystem without replacing the tools your team already knows.
