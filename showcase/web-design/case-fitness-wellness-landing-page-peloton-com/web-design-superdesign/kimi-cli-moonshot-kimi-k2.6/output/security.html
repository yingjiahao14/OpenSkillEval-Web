Security Overview

Built for Enterprise Trust

WellStream's security posture is designed from the ground up for the energy industry's most demanding compliance and operational requirements.

Defense in Depth

WellStream employs a multi-layered security architecture that protects your data at every layer—from physical infrastructure to application-level access controls. Our security model follows NIST Cybersecurity Framework guidelines and is continuously validated by third-party auditors.

SOC 1 & 2 Certified Compliance
Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial and operational data handling.
Powered by Microsoft Azure
Enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers and 99.95% uptime SLA.
Performance Monitoring
Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting and incident response.
Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication
Integrate with your corporate identity provider for seamless, secure access management.
Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)
Real-time log aggregation, threat detection, and forensic analysis across all platform components.

Encryption Everywhere

AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Your data is encrypted from the moment it enters our systems until it reaches authorized users.

Continuous Monitoring

24/7 Security Operations Center (SOC) with automated threat detection, anomaly alerting, and incident response playbooks.

Identity & Access

Role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and integration with Azure AD, Okta, and other IdPs.

SOC 1 & 2 Type II Certified

WellStream undergoes annual SOC 1 Type II and SOC 2 Type II audits conducted by independent third-party assessors. These certifications validate our internal controls over financial reporting (SOC 1) and the Trust Services Criteria for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy (SOC 2).
