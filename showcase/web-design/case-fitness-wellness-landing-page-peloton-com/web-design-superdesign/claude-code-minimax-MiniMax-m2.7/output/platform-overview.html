Platform Features

Everything you need to manage energy data at scale

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. WellStream's modern infrastructure eliminates the long implementation cycles typical of legacy systems, getting your team operational faster.
SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is protected by enterprise-grade security controls, continuous threat monitoring, and compliance audits.
Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. Whether your team is on the rig, in the office, or traveling, WellStream keeps everyone connected with seamless mobile and desktop experiences.
Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Build custom views, set up automated alerts, and make data-driven decisions with real-time visibility across your entire operation.
Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Connect to SCADA systems, accounting software, regulatory databases, and business intelligence tools without custom development.
