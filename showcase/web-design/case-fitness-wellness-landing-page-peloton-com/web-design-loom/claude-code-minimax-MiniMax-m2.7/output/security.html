Security

Enterprise-Grade Security for Critical Energy Data

WellStream is built on Microsoft Azure with SOC 1 & 2 compliance, SIEM monitoring, and enterprise SSO — so your most sensitive operational data stays protected.

SECURITY FOUNDATIONS

Built on Trust, Powered by Azure

WellStream's infrastructure is engineered for the highest levels of data protection required in the energy sector. Every layer of the platform — from data ingestion through storage and transmission — is secured by Microsoft's enterprise cloud infrastructure.

Our multi-layered approach combines preventive controls, real-time monitoring, and rapid incident response to deliver defense-in-depth that meets or exceeds the security requirements of major operators worldwide.

PLATFORM SECURITY FEATURES

Comprehensive Protection Across Every Layer

SOC 1 & 2 Certified Compliance

Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial and operational data handling, ensuring your data governance meets the strictest standards.

Powered by Microsoft Azure

Enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers and 99.95% uptime SLA, leveraging Microsoft's global security footprint.

Performance Monitoring

Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting and incident response teams available 24/7.

Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication

Integrate with your corporate identity provider for seamless, secure access management across your entire organization.

SIEM System

Real-time log aggregation, threat detection, and forensic analysis across all platform components for complete visibility.

Encryption at Rest & In Transit

AES-256 encryption protects your data at rest; TLS 1.3 secures all data in transit between clients and our infrastructure.

SOC COMPLIANCE

Third-Party Audited Security Controls

WellStream maintains SOC 1 Type II and SOC 2 Type II compliance, with annual audits conducted by independent third-party assessors. These audits evaluate the design and operating effectiveness of our security, availability, and confidentiality controls.

Our compliance program covers controls across infrastructure, access management, change management, monitoring, and incident response — providing our clients with the assurance that their data is handled by a platform built for enterprise accountability.
