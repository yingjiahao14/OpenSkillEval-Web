SOC COMPLIANCE

Certified Compliance You Can Count On

Annual third-party audits verify our controls over financial and operational data handling. WellStream maintains both SOC 1 and SOC 2 certifications to meet the rigorous requirements of enterprise energy operators.

🛡️

SOC 1 — Type II

Covers internal controls over financial reporting. Annual independent audits validate that WellStream's controls are operating effectively to protect the integrity of financial data processed through the platform.

Annual Audit Scope
Financial data controls, access management, change management, data backup and recovery
🔒

SOC 2 — Type II

Covers security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy (Trust Services Criteria). Our SOC 2 certification demonstrates our commitment to protecting operational data and maintaining platform availability.

Trust Service Criteria
Security · Availability · Processing Integrity · Confidentiality · Privacy
