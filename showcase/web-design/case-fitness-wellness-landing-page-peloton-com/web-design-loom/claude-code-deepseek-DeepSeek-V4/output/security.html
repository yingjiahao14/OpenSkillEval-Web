SOC 1 & 2 Compliance Framework

WellStream undergoes annual SOC 1 Type II and SOC 2 Type II audits conducted by independent third-party assessors. These examinations verify that our controls over financial reporting (SOC 1) and trust services criteria — security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy (SOC 2) — are suitably designed and operating effectively.

SOC 1 Type II

Validates controls relevant to your financial reporting. Essential for operators subject to SEC reporting and SOX compliance requirements.

SOC 2 Type II

Covers security, availability, and confidentiality. Demonstrates ongoing operational excellence across all trust services criteria.
