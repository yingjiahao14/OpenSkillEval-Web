Platform Capabilities

Everything Your Operation Needs

Click each section to explore how WellStream delivers speed, security, and connectivity.

Get Speed

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. No servers to provision, no data centers to manage — just log in and start working. Our average independent operator goes live in under three weeks from contract signature.

Get Secure

SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is protected by Microsoft's enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers, 99.95% uptime SLA, and continuous third-party security audits.

Get Mobility

Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. WellStream's offline-first mobile architecture ensures your field teams stay productive regardless of network conditions — data captured offline syncs automatically when connectivity returns.

Get Real-Time Reporting

Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Create role-specific views that surface exactly what each stakeholder needs — from production decline curves to safety compliance metrics, all updated in real time.

Get Connected

Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Whether you run SCADA, accounting, or regulatory reporting systems, our integration layer ensures data flows seamlessly across your entire technology ecosystem.
