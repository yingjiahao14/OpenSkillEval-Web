CAPABILITIES

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

Purpose-built features that address the real challenges energy operators face every day.

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. Our implementation team works alongside your operations staff to configure workflows, migrate data, and train users — all without disrupting ongoing operations.
SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Annual third-party audits verify our controls. Role-based access control, SSO integration, and comprehensive audit logging ensure your data is protected at every layer.
Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. Capture well data, site observations, and inspection results offline. When connectivity returns, data syncs automatically to the central platform — no double entry, no lost information.
Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Build custom visualizations, set threshold alerts, and share reports across your organization. From daily drilling summaries to monthly production allocations — it's all available instantly.
Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Connect SCADA systems, accounting platforms, regulatory databases, and BI tools. Our managed integration layer handles data mapping, transformation, and delivery on your schedule.
