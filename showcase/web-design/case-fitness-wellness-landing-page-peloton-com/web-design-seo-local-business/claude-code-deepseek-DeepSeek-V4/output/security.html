Enterprise-Grade Protection for Your Critical Data

SOC 1 & 2 Certified Compliance

Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial and operational data handling. Our SOC reports are available to customers under NDA.

Powered by Microsoft Azure

Enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers and 99.95% uptime SLA. Your data stays in the region you choose.

Performance Monitoring

Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting and incident response. We detect issues before they impact operations.

Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication

Integrate with your corporate identity provider for seamless, secure access management. Support for SAML 2.0, OAuth 2.0, and OpenID Connect.

Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)

Real-time log aggregation, threat detection, and forensic analysis across all platform components. Anomalies trigger immediate investigation.

Encryption at Rest and in Transit

AES-256 encryption protects stored data. TLS 1.3 secures all data in transit. Customer-managed encryption keys available for regulatory requirements.
