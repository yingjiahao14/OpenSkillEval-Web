Security Features

Multi-Layered Protection at Every Level

WellStream's security framework encompasses infrastructure, application, data, and access control layers — delivering defense-in-depth for energy operators worldwide.

SOC 1 & 2 Certified Compliance
Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial and operational data handling. Our SOC reports are available to clients under NDA, demonstrating our commitment to transparent security governance across all platform components.
Powered by Microsoft Azure
Enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers and a 99.95% uptime SLA. Azure's global network of data centers ensures your data is always available and protected by Microsoft's industry-leading cloud security standards.
Performance Monitoring
Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting and incident response. Our security operations team responds to anomalies 24/7, ensuring rapid containment and resolution of any potential threats before they impact operations.
Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication
Integrate with your corporate identity provider for seamless, secure access management. WellStream supports SAML 2.0 and OAuth 2.0, connecting with Active Directory, Azure AD, Okta, and other enterprise identity platforms without compromising your security policies.
Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)
Real-time log aggregation, threat detection, and forensic analysis across all platform components. WellStream's SIEM system correlates events across infrastructure layers to detect sophisticated attack patterns and provide complete audit trails for compliance reporting.
