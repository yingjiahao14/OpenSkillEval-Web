Platform Features

Everything You Need, Built In

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. Get up and running faster than any on-premise solution, with automatic updates that keep you on the latest version without downtime.
SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is protected by enterprise-grade security that meets the most rigorous compliance requirements in the energy industry.
Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. Capture data on-site, review dashboards on the go, and stay connected to your operations whether you're at the rig or in the office.
Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. No more waiting for end-of-month reports — see your data as it happens and make decisions with confidence.
Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Bridge legacy systems and modern applications with managed data orchestration that keeps everything in sync.
