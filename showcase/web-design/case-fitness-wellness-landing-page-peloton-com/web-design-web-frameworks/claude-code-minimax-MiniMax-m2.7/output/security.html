Enterprise-Grade Security for the Energy Industry

WellStream is built on SOC 1 & 2 certified infrastructure powered by Microsoft Azure. Your data is protected by continuous monitoring, encryption at rest and in transit, and enterprise-grade access controls.

SECURITY FOUNDATIONS

Built on Layers of Protection

Five pillars of security that protect your critical energy data at every layer of the platform.

SOC 1 & 2 Certified Compliance

Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial and operational data handling. Our certification demonstrates our commitment to the highest security standards in the industry.

Powered by Microsoft Azure

Enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers and 99.95% uptime SLA. Azure's global network ensures your data is always available when you need it.

Performance Monitoring

Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting and incident response. Our 24/7 NOC detects and resolves issues before they impact your operations.

Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication

Integrate with your corporate identity provider for seamless, secure access management. SSO reduces password fatigue and ensures consistent security policies across your organization.

Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)

Real-time log aggregation, threat detection, and forensic analysis across all platform components. Our SIEM correlates events across your entire deployment to identify sophisticated threats.
