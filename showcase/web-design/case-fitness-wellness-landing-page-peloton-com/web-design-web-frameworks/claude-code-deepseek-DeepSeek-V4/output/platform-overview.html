Features Deep Dive

Everything Your Team Needs

Explore the capabilities that make WellStream the most trusted platform in energy data management.

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. WellStream's modern infrastructure eliminates the procurement, provisioning, and configuration delays of traditional on-premise solutions. Start with core modules and expand as your needs grow — all without downtime or disruption.

SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is protected by multiple layers of security — from physical datacenter controls to application-level encryption. Annual third-party audits verify our controls, so you can focus on operations while we handle compliance.

Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. WellStream's mobile suite — including SiteView Go and WellStream Water Go — lets field teams capture data, submit reports, and access critical information from any location. Offline-first architecture ensures no data is lost, even when connectivity drops.

Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Build custom views for every role in your organization — from high-level production summaries for the boardroom to granular well performance metrics for the engineering team. No more waiting for end-of-month reports.

Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Whether you're running legacy SCADA systems, modern cloud analytics platforms, or a combination of both, WellStream's integration layer ensures data flows seamlessly across your entire technology ecosystem.
