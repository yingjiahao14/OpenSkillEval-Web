SECURITY DETAILS

Comprehensive Protection

Explore our multi-layered security framework and compliance certifications that safeguard your energy data.

Encryption at Rest & In Transit

All data is encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit, ensuring your information is protected at every stage of the data lifecycle.

24/7 Monitoring & Incident Response

Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting ensures threats are detected and addressed before they impact your operations.

Role-Based Access Control

Granular permission management ensures users only access the data and functions relevant to their role, minimizing exposure and maintaining operational integrity.

Geo-Redundant Data Centers

Data is replicated across geographically distributed Azure data centers, providing disaster recovery and ensuring 99.95% uptime SLA for your mission-critical applications.

SOC 1 & SOC 2 Certified

Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial and operational data handling, ensuring WellStream meets the highest standards of security, availability, and confidentiality.

SOC 1 — Type II

Validates controls relevant to financial reporting, ensuring data integrity for production volumes, lease payments, and revenue allocation.

SOC 2 — Type II

Validates security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy controls across WellStream's cloud infrastructure.

Continuous Compliance: WellStream maintains SOC compliance year-round — not just during audit periods. Automated controls monitoring ensures standards are met continuously.
