CAPABILITIES

What You Get with WellStream

Expand each section to learn how WellStream delivers on the promises that matter most to operators.

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. WellStream's SaaS delivery model eliminates the procurement, installation, and maintenance cycles of traditional on-premise systems — getting your team productive faster than any alternative.

SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is protected by the same enterprise-grade security trusted by Fortune 500 companies, with continuous compliance verification and automated threat detection.

Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. Whether your teams are on the rig floor, at the wellsite, or in the field, WellStream's mobile applications ensure data capture never stops — with offline capabilities that sync automatically when connectivity is restored.

Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Build custom views that surface the metrics that matter most to each stakeholder, from production decline curves to drilling cost tracking, updated in real time as data flows into the platform.

Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Whether you're connecting to SCADA systems, accounting software, or regulatory databases, WellStream's integration framework ensures seamless data flow across your entire technology ecosystem.
