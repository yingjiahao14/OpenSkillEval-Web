SOC 1 & SOC 2 Compliance

WellStream undergoes annual third-party audits conducted by accredited CPA firms to verify our controls over financial reporting integrity and operational security.

SOC 1 TYPE II

Financial Controls Audit

SOC 1 Type II reports verify that WellStream's internal controls relevant to your financial reporting are designed effectively and operating as intended over the audit period. Essential for operators with public reporting obligations.

  • ✓ Data integrity controls
  • ✓ Access and authorization
  • ✓ Change management procedures
SOC 2 TYPE II

Trust Service Criteria Audit

SOC 2 Type II examines WellStream's controls across security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy. This is the gold standard for cloud-based SaaS platforms handling sensitive operational data.

  • ✓ Security (CC series criteria)
  • ✓ Availability & confidentiality
  • ✓ Processing integrity

Audit Frequency & Availability

WellStream conducts annual SOC audits with a bridge letter process for periods between audit cycles.
