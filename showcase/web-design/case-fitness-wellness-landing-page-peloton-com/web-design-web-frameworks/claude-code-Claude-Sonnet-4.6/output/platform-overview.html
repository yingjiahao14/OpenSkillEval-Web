Platform Features

Get Everything Your Operation Needs

WellStream delivers speed, security, and mobility in a single platform — replacing fragmented legacy systems with one cloud-native solution.

Request A Demo
Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware procurement cycles, zero server configuration overhead, and instant scalability as your operation grows. Go from demo to live data in as little as three weeks.
SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Enterprise SSO, role-based access controls, and continuous SIEM threat monitoring protect your most sensitive operational data.
Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. SiteView Go and WellStream Water Go bring full data capture capabilities to the wellsite, eliminating paper tickets and manual transcription errors.
Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Pre-built report templates accelerate daily reporting while custom views let each team see exactly what they need, when they need it.
Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack — including SCADA systems, accounting platforms, regulatory reporting tools, and BI warehouses like Power BI, Tableau, and Spotfire.
