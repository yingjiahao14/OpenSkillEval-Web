Security & Compliance

Enterprise-Grade Security,
Built Into Everything We Do

Your operational data is your most critical asset. That's why security isn't an add-on at WellStream — it's foundational architecture.

Powered by Microsoft Azure

Enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers and 99.95% uptime SLA. Your data resides in SOC-compliant Azure regions with automatic failover and disaster recovery.

Performance Monitoring

Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting and incident response. Our operations team detects and resolves issues before they impact your workflows.

Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication

Integrate with your corporate identity provider for seamless, secure access management. Support for SAML 2.0, OAuth 2.0, and OpenID Connect — no separate credentials to manage.

SIEM System

Real-time log aggregation, threat detection, and forensic analysis across all platform components. Security Information and Event Management provides 24/7 visibility into platform activity.

SOC 1 & 2 Certified Compliance

WellStream undergoes annual third-party audits to verify controls over financial and operational data handling. Our SOC 1 and SOC 2 certifications provide independent assurance that our security controls meet the highest industry standards.

SOC 1 Type II

Validates controls relevant to financial reporting — critical for operators subject to SEC regulations and investor scrutiny.

SOC 2 Type II

Verifies security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy controls — the gold standard for SaaS platforms.

Annual Re-certification

Independent auditors re-certify our controls every year, with continuous monitoring between audit cycles to maintain compliance.

Audit Reports Available

SOC reports are available to customers under NDA. Your security and compliance teams can review our controls in detail.
