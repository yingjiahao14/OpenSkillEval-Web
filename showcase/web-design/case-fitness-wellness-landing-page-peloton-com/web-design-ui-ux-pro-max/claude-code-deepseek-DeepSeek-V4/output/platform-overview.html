Platform Capabilities

Everything You Need to Run Smarter

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. Our platform eliminates infrastructure bottlenecks so your team can focus on operations, not IT maintenance.
SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Your critical operational data is protected by enterprise-grade security controls and continuous compliance verification.
Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. Capture data at the wellsite, on the pipeline, or in the field — and watch it populate dashboards instantly when connectivity returns.
Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. No more waiting for end-of-month reports — make decisions with current data, not last quarter's.
Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Connect SCADA systems, accounting platforms, regulatory databases, and BI tools without custom development.
