Security Foundations

How We Protect Your Data

Explore our security architecture and compliance certifications.

SOC 1 & 2 Certified Compliance

Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial and operational data handling. Our certifications demonstrate the highest standards of trust and transparency.

Powered by Microsoft Azure

Enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers and 99.95% uptime SLA. Your data lives in world-class facilities with redundant power, cooling, and networking.

Performance Monitoring

Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting and incident response. We detect anomalies before they impact your operations.

Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication

Integrate with your corporate identity provider for seamless, secure access management. Support for SAML 2.0 and OIDC ensures compatibility with your existing IAM stack.

Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)

Real-time log aggregation, threat detection, and forensic analysis across all platform components. Our SIEM pipeline correlates events to identify and neutralize threats.

SOC 1 Type II

Independent auditor attestation of internal controls over financial reporting. Validates that our controls are designed and operating effectively to protect client financial data.

SOC 2 Type II

Comprehensive evaluation of security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy controls. Demonstrates our commitment to the Trust Services Criteria.

Encryption at Rest & In Transit

All data is encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Keys are managed through Azure Key Vault with strict access controls and rotation policies.

Annual Penetration Testing

Third-party security firms conduct rigorous penetration tests annually. Findings are remediated promptly and verified through re-testing to ensure continuous hardening.
