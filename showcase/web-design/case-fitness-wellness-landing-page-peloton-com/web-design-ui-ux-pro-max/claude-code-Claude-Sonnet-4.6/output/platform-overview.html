PLATFORM FEATURES

Everything Your Operation Needs

Explore our core platform capabilities — each engineered to solve the real challenges energy operators face every day.

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware procurement, instant scalability, and automatic software updates without downtime windows. Your team is productive from day one.
SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Annual third-party audits, SIEM threat detection, and SSO integration protect your most sensitive operational data.
Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. Your crews can capture data on-site, reducing paper tickets and eliminating transcription errors before they compound.
Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. No more waiting days for reports — production volumes, well integrity, and compliance status update in real time.
Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack — SCADA systems, accounting platforms, BI tools like Power BI and Tableau, and regulatory reporting systems.
