Features in Depth

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

Get Speed

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. Our dedicated implementation team has operators live in as little as three weeks — without the burden of on-premise infrastructure or lengthy IT procurement cycles.

Get Secure

SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Our security framework undergoes annual third-party audits, ensuring your critical operational data meets the highest standards of protection and compliance.

Get Mobility

Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. SiteView Go and WellStream Water Go enable your field teams to capture data at the wellhead, with automatic synchronization when connectivity is restored — no more paper tickets or duplicate entry.

Get Real-Time Reporting

Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. From production volumes to well integrity status, every stakeholder sees the metrics that matter most — with drill-down capabilities to investigate anomalies in seconds.

Get Connected

Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Connect SCADA systems, accounting platforms, regulatory databases, and BI tools through managed integration layers that maintain data integrity across your entire ecosystem.
