Security Architecture

Comprehensive Protection at Every Layer

From the network perimeter to the application layer, WellStream applies defense-in-depth principles to protect energy operators' critical data.

🔐

SOC 1 & 2 Certified Compliance

Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial reporting (SOC 1) and operational data handling (SOC 2) — the highest standard for SaaS security assurance.

☁️

Powered by Microsoft Azure

Enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers and 99.95% uptime SLA. Azure's global network ensures your data is always available when you need it.

📡

Performance Monitoring

Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting and incident response. Issues are detected and resolved before they impact your operations.

🔑

Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication

Integrate with your corporate identity provider (Active Directory, Okta, Azure AD) for seamless, secure access management across your entire organization.

🛡️

SIEM System

Security Information and Event Management: real-time log aggregation, threat detection, and forensic analysis across all platform components to identify and respond to security events.

🔒

Encryption at Rest & In Transit

All data is encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Your well data, production figures, and land records are protected at every stage of their lifecycle.

SOC Compliance

Independently Verified, Annually Audited

WellStream undergoes rigorous third-party audits to maintain SOC 1 and SOC 2 certifications — giving your compliance and security teams verified assurance.

📋

SOC 1 Type II

Controls relevant to financial reporting. Covers data integrity, availability, and processing accuracy for production volumes, allocation records, and reporting outputs.

SOC 2 Type II

Controls across the five Trust Service Criteria: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy of operational data.
