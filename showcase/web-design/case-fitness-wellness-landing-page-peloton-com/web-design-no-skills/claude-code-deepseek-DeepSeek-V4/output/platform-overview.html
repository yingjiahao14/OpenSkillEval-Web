PLATFORM FEATURES

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

Click each section to learn how WellStream delivers value across speed, security, mobility, reporting, and connectivity.

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware procurement and instant scalability. Our implementation team configures the platform to match your existing workflows, and automated data migration tools accelerate the transition from legacy systems. Independent operators are typically live within 3 weeks of signing.

SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Multi-factor authentication, role-based access controls, and SSO integration ensure only authorized personnel access your data. Automated compliance reporting and continuous third-party auditing give you and your stakeholders complete confidence.

Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. Engineers and field crews can capture inspections, maintenance records, and operational data directly from mobile devices. Offline mode stores data locally and syncs automatically when connectivity is restored—no more paper tickets or double entry.

Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Build custom views for production volumes, well status, compliance metrics, and operational trends—all updating in real time as data flows into the platform. No more waiting for end-of-month reports.

Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Connect SCADA systems, accounting platforms, regulatory databases, and BI tools like Power BI and Tableau. Our managed ETL orchestration handles data mapping, transformation, and scheduling so your teams can focus on analysis instead of data wrangling.
