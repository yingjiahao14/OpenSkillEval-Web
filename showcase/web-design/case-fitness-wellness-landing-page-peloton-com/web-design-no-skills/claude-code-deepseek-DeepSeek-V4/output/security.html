Security & Compliance

Enterprise-grade protection your critical energy data deserves. SOC certified, Azure powered, and continuously monitored.

SOC 1 & 2 Certified Compliance

Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial and operational data handling. Our SOC reports are available to clients under NDA, providing transparency into our security posture.

Powered by Microsoft Azure

Enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers and 99.95% uptime SLA. Your data resides in your choice of Azure regions to meet data sovereignty requirements.

Performance Monitoring

Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting and incident response. Our operations team responds to critical alerts within 15 minutes, 24/7/365.

Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication

Integrate with your corporate identity provider (Azure AD, Okta, Ping, etc.) for seamless, secure access management. Support for SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect protocols.

Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) System

Real-time log aggregation, threat detection, and forensic analysis across all platform components. Automated correlation rules surface anomalous behavior patterns before they become incidents.

SOC 1 & SOC 2 Compliance

WellStream undergoes annual SOC 1 (SSAE 18) and SOC 2 Type II audits conducted by an independent AICPA-accredited firm. These audits validate the design and operating effectiveness of our controls over financial reporting and data security, availability, and confidentiality.

Our SOC 2 report covers the Trust Services Criteria for Security, Availability, and Confidentiality. Clients may request access to our latest SOC reports under a non-disclosure agreement.

SOC 1 (SSAE 18)

Validates controls relevant to your financial reporting. Essential for publicly traded operators and those with SOX compliance obligations.

SOC 2 Type II

Tests operating effectiveness over time for security, availability, and confidentiality controls. Renewed annually with continuous monitoring.
