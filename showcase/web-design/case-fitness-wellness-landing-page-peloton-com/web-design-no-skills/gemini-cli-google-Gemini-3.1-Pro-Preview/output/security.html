Enterprise-Grade Security

Protecting your critical energy data with industry-leading infrastructure, continuous monitoring, and rigorous compliance.

Secure By Design

Our platform architecture incorporates security at every layer, from network infrastructure to application code. We utilize Microsoft Azure's enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers to ensure 99.95% uptime and data durability.

Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring paired with automated alerting means potential threats are identified and mitigated before they can impact your operations. Our Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) system provides real-time log aggregation and forensic analysis across all platform components.

SOC 1 & 2 Certified

WellStream maintains rigorous SOC 1 and SOC 2 Type II certifications. Annual third-party audits verify our internal controls over financial reporting and operational data handling, ensuring that your data is managed securely and transparently.

Our commitment to compliance gives you the confidence that your organizational data, processes, and user access are protected according to the highest industry standards.
