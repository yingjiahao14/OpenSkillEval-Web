SECURITY OVERVIEW

How We Protect Your Data

SOC 1 & 2 Certified Compliance

Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial and operational data handling. Our certifications demonstrate our commitment to the highest standards of security and operational excellence.

Powered by Microsoft Azure

Enterprise cloud infrastructure with geo-redundant data centers and 99.95% uptime SLA. Your data is stored in world-class facilities with multiple layers of physical and digital protection.

Performance Monitoring

Continuous application and infrastructure monitoring with automated alerting and incident response. Our operations team responds to anomalies before they impact your business.

Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication

Integrate with your corporate identity provider for seamless, secure access management. Support for SAML 2.0 and OIDC ensures compatibility with your existing authentication stack.

Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)

Real-time log aggregation, threat detection, and forensic analysis across all platform components. Our SIEM system correlates events to identify and neutralize threats proactively.

SOC 1 Type II

Our SOC 1 Type II report covers controls relevant to internal control over financial reporting (ICFR). This is critical for operators who rely on WellStream for production allocation and revenue reporting.

  • Controls over data processing integrity
  • Change management and release procedures
  • Logical and physical access controls
  • System operations and monitoring

SOC 2 Type II

Our SOC 2 Type II report addresses the Trust Services Criteria for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy. This comprehensive audit validates our operational excellence.

  • Security: Protection against unauthorized access
  • Availability: System uptime and redundancy
  • Processing Integrity: Complete, valid, and accurate processing
  • Confidentiality: Designated access to information
  • Privacy: Collection, use, and retention of personal information
