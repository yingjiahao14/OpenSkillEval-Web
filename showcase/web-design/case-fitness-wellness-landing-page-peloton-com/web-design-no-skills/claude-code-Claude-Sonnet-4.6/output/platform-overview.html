Platform Features

Everything You Need. Nothing You Don't.

WellStream delivers the capabilities that matter most to energy operators — built into one cohesive, cloud-native platform.

Zero Hardware

Cloud-native deployment means instant setup and infinite scale.

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. WellStream eliminates months-long implementation cycles that plague on-premise alternatives. Your team can be live and productive in a fraction of the time.
SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Annual third-party audits, enterprise SSO, and SIEM integration ensure your data is protected at every layer.
Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. From the wellsite to the boardroom, WellStream travels with your team. Capture data where work happens — not just at a desk.
Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. No more waiting for end-of-day reports or chasing data across spreadsheets. Decision-makers get the information they need instantly.
Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Bridge SCADA systems, accounting platforms, regulatory portals, and BI tools — WellStream speaks every protocol.
