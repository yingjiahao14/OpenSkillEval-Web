Capabilities

What You Get with WellStream

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. WellStream's SaaS model eliminates server rooms and IT overhead—your team is up and running before the ink dries on the contract.
SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Every data packet is protected by enterprise-grade security controls and continuous threat monitoring.
Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. Your field teams can capture data, submit reports, and access well information from anywhere—no more paper tickets or delayed uploads.
Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Build the views you need, share them across roles, and never wait for a manual report again.
Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Whether it's SCADA, accounting, or BI tools, WellStream fits seamlessly into your ecosystem.
