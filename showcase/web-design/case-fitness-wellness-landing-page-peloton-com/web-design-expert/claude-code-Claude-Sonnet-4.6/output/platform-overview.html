Core Features

Everything You Need to
Run at Full Speed

Deploy in weeks, not years. Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. WellStream's infrastructure is pre-built, pre-configured, and ready to connect to your existing data sources — eliminating months of IT procurement and setup.

SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Our security posture is continuously validated by independent third-party auditors to meet the most stringent enterprise requirements.

Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. From wellsite inspections to completion sign-offs, your field teams capture data in WellStream Go apps and it instantly appears in the platform — no paper tickets, no data loss.

Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Build custom views with drag-and-drop tools, or use pre-built industry templates. Data updates in real time — no scheduled refreshes, no stale reports.

Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. Connect to SCADA systems, accounting platforms, regulatory databases, and BI tools like Power BI, Tableau, and Spotfire with managed integration pipelines.
