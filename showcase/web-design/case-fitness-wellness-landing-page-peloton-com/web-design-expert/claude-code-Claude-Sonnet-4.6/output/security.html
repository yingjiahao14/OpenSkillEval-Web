SOC 1 & SOC 2 Certified Compliance

Annual third-party audits verify controls over financial and operational data handling — providing your legal, compliance, and audit teams with the evidence they need.

SOC 1

SOC 1 — Financial Reporting Controls

Type II report covering controls relevant to financial data integrity. Critical for operators with public reporting obligations or audit requirements over operational accounting systems.

SOC 2

SOC 2 — Operational Data Security

Type II report covering the five Trust Service Criteria: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy — the gold standard for SaaS security assurance.
