Platform Capabilities

Cloud-native architecture means zero hardware and instant scalability. WellStream's modern infrastructure eliminates the lengthy deployment cycles of traditional enterprise software. Your team can be operational in weeks, not the 12-18 months typical of legacy systems.

  • No hardware to purchase or maintain
  • Automatic updates and feature releases
  • Scalable resources that grow with your operations

SOC 1 & 2 certified, Azure-powered infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring and encryption at rest and in transit. Security isn't an afterthought—it's baked into every layer of the platform.

  • Annual third-party security audits
  • 99.95% uptime SLA with geo-redundant data centers
  • End-to-end encryption for all data

Field-ready mobile apps that sync in real time, even in low-connectivity environments. Your field teams can capture data directly from well sites, reducing errors and eliminating paper-based workflows.

  • Offline capability for remote locations
  • Real-time sync when connectivity returns
  • Native iOS and Android applications

Configurable dashboards deliver live KPIs to executives, engineers, and field teams simultaneously. Make decisions based on current data, not yesterday's reports.

  • Customizable dashboard views
  • Role-based data visibility
  • Automated report generation and distribution

Open APIs, ETL pipelines, and pre-built connectors integrate WellStream with your existing tech stack. We meet you where you are.

  • RESTful APIs for custom integrations
  • Pre-built connectors for major enterprise systems
  • Automated ETL pipelines on schedule
