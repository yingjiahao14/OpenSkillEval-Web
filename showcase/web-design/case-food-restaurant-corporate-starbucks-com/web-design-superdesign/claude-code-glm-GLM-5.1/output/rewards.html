How it works

Getting started is easy — collect Stars, enjoy free treats, and unlock benefits.

1

Collect Stars

Earn Stars by making eligible purchases and participating in challenges, offers and games.

2

Enjoy free treats

Redeem your Stars for free food, drinks and more. Look forward to a birthday treat on us every year.

3

Unlock benefits

Keep earning Stars to increase your status and help you get to free faster. Discover more benefits at every level.

Status tiers

The more Stars you earn, the more benefits you unlock.

Green

Less than 500 Stars per year

  • Free birthday treat
  • Earn 1 Star per $1
  • Free Mod Mondays
  • Double Stars with personal cup

Gold

500+ Stars per year

  • 7-day birthday window
  • Earn 1.2 Stars per $1
  • Stars won't expire
  • 4 extra Double Star Days

Reserve

2,500+ Stars per year

  • 30-day birthday window
  • Earn 1.7 Stars per $1
  • Exclusive global coffee experiences
  • Free personalized Reserve Card
  • 6 extra Double Star Days

Terms & Conditions

GreenBean Rewards is available at participating stores. Stars and benefits are subject to the GreenBean Rewards Terms of Use. Star amounts and reward availability may vary by location.

To earn Stars, you must pay for your purchase using a registered GreenBean Card, GreenBean app, or by scanning your app at the register before paying with cash, credit/debit card, or mobile wallet.

Birthday rewards require prior registration. Tier status is calculated based on Stars earned in the preceding 12-month period. Benefits are subject to change. Visit greenbean.com/rewards for full terms.
