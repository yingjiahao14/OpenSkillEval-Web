Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my gift card balance?

You can check your balance on our website or mobile app by entering your card number. You can also call the number on the back of your card.

Can I reload my gift card?

Yes! Sign in to your GreenBean account or use our mobile app to add funds to your existing gift card at any time.

Do gift cards expire?

No, GreenBean gift cards do not expire. Any remaining balance will remain available for future use.

Can I earn Stars when using a gift card?

Yes! When you register your gift card to your GreenBean Rewards account, you'll earn Stars on qualifying purchases.
