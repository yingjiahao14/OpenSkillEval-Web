# GreenBean Coffee — Your Daily Coffee Ritual

## Overview

Build a 5-page corporate website for GreenBean Coffee, a premium coffeehouse chain. The site should feel like walking into a warm, well-designed coffee shop — inviting, modern, and just a touch playful. The audience spans everyday coffee lovers, loyalty members, gift card shoppers, and casual browsers. The primary goals are driving online ordering, showcasing seasonal drinks, growing the rewards program, and making gift card purchases effortless.

## Visual Direction

Think earthy greens, warm creams, and rich coffee browns. Use a clean sans-serif typeface (like Inter or DM Sans) for body text, paired with a slightly more expressive heading font. Generous whitespace, rounded corners, and soft shadows should give the site a friendly, approachable feel. Photography placeholders should suggest lifestyle coffee imagery — hands wrapped around mugs, latte art, cozy café interiors. Keep the layout modern and airy, never cluttered.

## Key Priorities

- **Home page impact**: The hero and seasonal drink highlights are the first impression — they should feel vibrant and immediately actionable with clear ordering CTAs.
- **Rewards redemption tabs**: The tabbed star-level redemption display (25 through 400 Stars) is a core interactive element. Make the tab switching feel smooth and the content instantly clear — this is what convinces people to join.
- **Gift card carousels**: Both the featured and category-specific carousels need polished next/previous navigation. Six occasion categories should be easy to browse without overwhelming the page.
- **Store locator interactivity**: The search, filter toggle, and pickup/delivery switching should all feel responsive and functional, even with placeholder data.
- **Menu organization**: The sidebar + category layout must be intuitive. Users should quickly scan across drinks, food, and at-home coffee sections.
- **Responsive design** is required across all pages — pay special attention to the footer accordion behavior on mobile.
