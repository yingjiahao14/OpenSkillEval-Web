Frequently asked questions

How do I send an eGift card?

Select a gift card design, choose your amount (from $5 to $100), add a personal message, and enter the recipient's email. They'll receive it instantly!

Can I reload a gift card?

Yes! You can reload any registered GreenBean Gift Card through the app or website, with amounts between $5 and $100.

Do gift cards expire?

No, GreenBean Gift Cards do not have an expiration date. Your balance stays until you use it.

Can I earn Stars with a gift card?

Yes! Register your gift card to your GreenBean Rewards account and you'll earn Stars on every qualifying purchase.
