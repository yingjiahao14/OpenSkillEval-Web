Terms & Conditions

GreenBean Rewards is a loyalty program offered by GreenBean Coffee Company. Stars are earned on eligible purchases made at participating GreenBean locations and expire 6 months after the calendar month in which they were earned, unless you maintain Gold or Reserve status. Stars cannot be earned on purchases of gift cards, alcohol, or taxes. GreenBean reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the program at any time with notice. See full terms on the GreenBean app or website for complete details including redemption exclusions and restrictions.
