Terms & Conditions

Rewards Program Terms

  • Stars are earned on eligible purchases only. Excludes alcohol, fees, and gratuity.
  • Stars are calculated on the total before tax and other fees.
  • Stars will be added to your account within 24 hours of your purchase.
  • Reward certificates must be redeemed at participating locations.
  • Rewards have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Your GreenBean Rewards account may be terminated if fraud or abuse is suspected.
  • GreenBean reserves the right to modify or cancel the program at any time.
