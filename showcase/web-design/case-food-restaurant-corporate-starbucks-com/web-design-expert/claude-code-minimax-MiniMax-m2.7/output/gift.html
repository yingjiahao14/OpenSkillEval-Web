Frequently Asked Questions

You can purchase gift cards online or at any GreenBean location. Choose between physical cards or digital eGifts that can be sent via email. For bulk orders of 15+ cards, visit our Business Gifting section.

You can check your gift card balance by visiting any GreenBean location, or by entering your card details on our website. The balance will be displayed along with recent transaction history.

Yes! Gift cards can be reloaded at any GreenBean location or online. You can also register your card to your GreenBean Rewards account for easier management and to earn Stars on purchases.

Gift cards do not expire, and any remaining balance remains available for future use. However, if the card has been inactive for 12 months, a small monthly service fee may apply.

Business gifting orders require a minimum of 15 cards per order. This applies to both physical and digital gift cards. Bulk discounts may be available for orders over 50 cards.
