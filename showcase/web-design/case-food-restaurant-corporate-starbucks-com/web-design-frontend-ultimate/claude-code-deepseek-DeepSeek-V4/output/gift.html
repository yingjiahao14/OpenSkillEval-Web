About GreenBean eGift Cards

A GreenBean eGift card is a GreenBean Gift Card that is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase. Select a design to start! Received a gift card? Add or reload, check balance, or view Card Terms & Conditions.

Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean® Rewards account and members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.

Gift Card FAQ

Choose a design, select an amount, enter the recipient's email, add a personal message, and complete your purchase. The eGift is delivered digitally within minutes.

Yes! You can reload any registered GreenBean Gift Card through your account or the app. Simply go to your card management page and select "Reload."

GreenBean Gift Cards do not expire. eGift cards also have no expiration date, so recipients can use them whenever they like.

Register your gift card to a GreenBean Rewards account and you'll earn Stars on qualifying purchases made with that card. Stars are earned at the time of purchase, not when buying the gift card.
