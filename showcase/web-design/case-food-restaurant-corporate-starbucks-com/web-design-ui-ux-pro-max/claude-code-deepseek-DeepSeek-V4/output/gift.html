About eGift Cards

A GreenBean eGift card is a GreenBean Gift Card that is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase. Select a design to start!

Received a gift card? Add or reload, check balance, or view Card Terms & Conditions.

Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean® Rewards account and members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send an eGift?
Choose a design, select an amount, add a personal message, and enter the recipient's email. They'll receive it instantly — or you can schedule delivery for a specific date.

Do gift cards expire?
GreenBean Gift Cards and eGifts never expire. There are no dormancy fees or service charges.

Can I reload a gift card?
Yes! Register your card to a GreenBean Rewards account and you can reload it online, in the app, or in any store.
