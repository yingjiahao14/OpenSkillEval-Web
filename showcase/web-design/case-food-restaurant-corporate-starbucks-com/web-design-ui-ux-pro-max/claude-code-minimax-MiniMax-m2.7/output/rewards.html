GreenBean Rewards Program Terms

  • Stars are earned on eligible purchases at participating GreenBean stores.
  • Stars expire 12 months after the last day of the month in which they were earned.
  • Status tiers are calculated based on Stars earned within a calendar year.
  • Benefits and rewards are subject to change and may vary by location.
  • Members must present their membership card or provide their phone number to earn Stars.
  • GreenBean reserves the right to modify program terms with 30 days notice.
