Frequently asked questions

Where can I use my GreenBean Gift Card?

GreenBean Gift Cards and eGifts can be used at participating GreenBean locations in the U.S. and Canada, and on the GreenBean app or website for eligible purchases.

Do GreenBean Gift Cards expire?

GreenBean Gift Cards do not expire and there are no fees. Your balance will remain available as long as you need it.

Can I reload a GreenBean Gift Card?

Yes! You can add funds to a registered physical or digital GreenBean Gift Card in the app, online, or at any participating store.

How do I earn Stars with a Gift Card?

Register your GreenBean Gift Card or eGift to your GreenBean Rewards account and you'll automatically earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.

Can I send an eGift internationally?

eGifts can be sent to recipients in the U.S. and Canada. For international gifting, please contact our business gifting team for options.

What is the minimum for bulk business orders?

Corporate and bulk Gift Card orders have a minimum of 15 cards per order. Contact our business gifting team for volume pricing and customization options.
