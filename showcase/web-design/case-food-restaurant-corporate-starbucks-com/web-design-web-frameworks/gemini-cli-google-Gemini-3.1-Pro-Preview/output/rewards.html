Earn 1 Star per $1 spent when you scan your member barcode in the app, then pay with cash, credit/debit cards or mobile wallets at participating stores. You can also earn 1 Star per $1 spent when you link a payment method and pay directly through the app.

Earn 2 Stars per $1 spent when you load funds and pay with your digital GreenBean Card in the app. You can also earn 2 Stars per $1 spent when you pay in-person at a participating store with your registered physical GreenBean Card or scan your member barcode in the app, and then use any physical GreenBean Card (regardless of whether it is registered) to complete the purchase.
