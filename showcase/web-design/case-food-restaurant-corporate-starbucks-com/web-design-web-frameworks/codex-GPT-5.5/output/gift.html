Good to know

What is a GreenBean eGift card?

A GreenBean eGift card is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase.

Can I add or reload a received card?

Yes. Add or reload, check balance, or view Card Terms & Conditions after receiving a gift card.

Do gift cards earn Stars?

Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean Rewards account to earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.
