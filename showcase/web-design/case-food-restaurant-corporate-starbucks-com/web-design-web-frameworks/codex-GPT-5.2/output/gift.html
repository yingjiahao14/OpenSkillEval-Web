About eGifts

Digital gifting made easy

A GreenBean eGift card is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase. Select a design to start! Received a gift card? Add or reload, check balance, or view Card Terms & Conditions.

Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean® Rewards account and members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.

FAQ

Quick answers

Can I send more than one eGift?

Yes — up to 10 eGifts per purchase (prototype behavior).

Do recipients need an account?

No, but registering to GreenBean® Rewards helps members earn Stars when paying with that card.

Can I use gift cards for pickup or delivery?

Yes — apply at checkout for eligible orders (placeholder flow).
