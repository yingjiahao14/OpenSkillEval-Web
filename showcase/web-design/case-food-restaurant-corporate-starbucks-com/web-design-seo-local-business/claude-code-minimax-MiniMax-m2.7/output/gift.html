Frequently asked questions

How do I purchase a GreenBean Gift Card?

You can purchase gift cards online at greenbeancoffee.com/gift or through the GreenBean mobile app. Choose between digital cards (delivered instantly via email) or physical cards (mailed to your recipient). Digital cards are available in amounts from $5 to $500, while physical cards come in $10, $25, $50, and $100 denominations.

How do I check my Gift Card balance?

You can check your balance online at greenbeancoffee.com/giftcard or through the GreenBean mobile app. You'll need the gift card number and PIN found on the back of your card. You can also register your gift card to your GreenBean Rewards account to track your balance and earn Stars on purchases.

Can I reload my Gift Card?

Yes! You can reload your gift card online or through the GreenBean app. Registered gift card members can add funds instantly. Physical gift cards can be reloaded at any GreenBean store by presenting the card to a barista.

How do I send a gift card to someone?

For digital gift cards, select your design and amount, enter the recipient's email address, add a personal message, and complete your purchase. The eGift will be delivered within minutes. For physical cards, you can have them mailed directly to your recipient or to yourself to personally deliver.

What is the minimum for bulk corporate orders?

Corporate bulk orders require a minimum of 15 gift cards per order. We offer both physical and digital card options for business gifting. Contact our corporate sales team for volume pricing and custom branding options.

Do Gift Cards earn Stars?

Yes! When you register your gift card to a GreenBean Rewards account, you'll earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card. This is a great way to earn rewards faster on your favorite drinks and food items.
