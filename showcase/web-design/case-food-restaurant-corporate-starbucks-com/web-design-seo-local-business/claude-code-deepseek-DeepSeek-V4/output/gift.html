Frequently Asked Questions

What is a GreenBean eGift card?

A GreenBean eGift card is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase. Select a design and the recipient gets it instantly via email.

Can I earn Stars with a GreenBean gift card?

Yes. Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean Rewards account and you'll earn Stars on qualifying purchases when paying with that card.

Can I buy GreenBean gift cards in bulk?

Yes. Bulk send physical or digital GreenBean Cards to gift, reward, incentivize, or show appreciation. Minimum 15 cards per order.
