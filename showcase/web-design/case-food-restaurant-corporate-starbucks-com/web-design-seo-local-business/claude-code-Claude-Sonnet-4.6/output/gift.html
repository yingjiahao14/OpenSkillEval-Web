FAQ

Common questions

What is a GreenBean eGift card?

+
A GreenBean eGift card is a GreenBean Gift Card that is purchased and sent digitally. You can send up to 10 eGifts per purchase. Select a design to start your gift.

Can I earn Stars with a GreenBean Gift Card?

+
Yes! Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean® Rewards account and members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.

What is the minimum order for business gifting?

+
Business gifting requires a minimum of 15 cards per order. You can bulk send physical or digital GreenBean Cards to gift, reward, incentivize, or show appreciation towards your customers, clients, and team members.

How do I check my gift card balance?

+
You can add or reload, check balance, or view Card Terms & Conditions through the GreenBean website or app. Sign in to your Rewards account for the full card management experience.

Can gift cards be used in-store and online?

+
Yes. GreenBean Gift Cards and eGifts can be used at participating GreenBean stores nationwide, as well as when ordering through the app or website.
