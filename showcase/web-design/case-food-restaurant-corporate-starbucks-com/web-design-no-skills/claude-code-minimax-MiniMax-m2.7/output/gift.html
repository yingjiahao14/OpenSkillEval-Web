Business gifting — simplified

Bulk send physical or digital GreenBean Cards to gift, reward, incentivize, or show appreciation towards your customers, clients and team members. Minimum 15 cards per order.

About eGift Cards

A GreenBean eGift card is a GreenBean Gift Card that is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase. Select a design to start!

Received a gift card? Add or reload, check balance, or view Card Terms & Conditions. Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean® Rewards account and members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.

Frequently asked questions

How do I purchase a gift card?

Select a design, choose your amount, and send it via email or schedule delivery for a special date.

Can I reload my gift card?

Yes! Create an account or sign in to reload your card balance at any time.

How long does delivery take?

eGift cards are delivered within minutes of purchase. Physical cards ship within 3-5 business days.

Can I use my gift card in-store?

Yes, your gift card can be used at any GreenBean location for in-store purchases.
