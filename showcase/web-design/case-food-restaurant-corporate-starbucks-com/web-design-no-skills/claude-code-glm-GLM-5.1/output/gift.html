GreenBean eGift cards

Frequently asked questions

Yes, you can reload your GreenBean Gift Card at any participating store, online, or through the GreenBean app. Simply provide your card number and the amount you'd like to add.
You can check your balance online at greenbean.com/gift-cards/balance, through the GreenBean app, or by asking a barista at any participating store.
GreenBean Gift Cards can be used at most participating GreenBean stores in the U.S. and Canada. Some licensed store locations may not accept gift cards.
A physical gift card is a plastic card purchased in-store or online and shipped to the recipient. An eGift is a digital gift card sent via email — perfect for last-minute gifting. Both can be registered to your GreenBean Rewards account.
No, GreenBean Gift Cards do not expire. The value on the card remains until it's used. However, we recommend registering your card to protect the balance in case it's lost or stolen.
