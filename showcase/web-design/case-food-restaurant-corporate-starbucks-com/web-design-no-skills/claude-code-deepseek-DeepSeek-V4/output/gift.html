About GreenBean eGift Cards

A GreenBean eGift card is a GreenBean Gift Card that is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase. Select a design to start! Received a gift card? Add or reload, check balance, or view Card Terms & Conditions.

Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean® Rewards account and members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.

Frequently asked questions

How do I send an eGift card?
Select a design, choose an amount between $5 and $500, add a personal message, and enter the recipient's email address. They'll receive it instantly — or you can schedule delivery for a future date.
Can I use a gift card at any GreenBean location?
Yes! GreenBean Gift Cards and eGift Cards are accepted at all participating GreenBean locations in the U.S. and Canada.
Do gift cards expire?
Nope. GreenBean Gift Cards never expire and there are no dormancy fees. Your balance stays safe until you're ready to use it.
Can I reload a gift card?
Yes, you can reload any registered GreenBean Gift Card through your GreenBean account or the mobile app. You can also set up auto-reload so you never run low.
Do I earn Stars when paying with a gift card?
Yes! Register your Gift Card or eGift to your GreenBean Rewards account and you'll earn Stars on all qualifying purchases made with that card.
