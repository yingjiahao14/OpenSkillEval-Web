Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send an eGift card?

Choose a gift card design, enter the recipient's email or phone number, add a personal message, select an amount, and complete your purchase. The eGift will be delivered instantly!

Can I reload a gift card?

Yes! You can add funds to any registered GreenBean Gift Card through your account online or in the GreenBean app.

Do gift cards expire?

No, GreenBean Gift Cards do not have an expiration date and there are no fees for non-use.

Can I earn Stars with a gift card?

Yes! Register your Gift Card to your GreenBean Rewards account and you'll earn Stars on every qualifying purchase.
