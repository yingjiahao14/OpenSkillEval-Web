GreenBean Rewards terms and conditions apply. Stars earned may vary based on purchase amount, location, and payment method. Stars expire 6 months after the calendar month in which they were earned for Green tier members. Gold and Reserve tier members enjoy Stars that do not expire as long as tier status is maintained.
