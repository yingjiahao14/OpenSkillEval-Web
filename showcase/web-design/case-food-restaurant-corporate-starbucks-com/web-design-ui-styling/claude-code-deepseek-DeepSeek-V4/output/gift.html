About eGift Cards

A GreenBean eGift card is a GreenBean Gift Card that is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase. Select a design to start!

Get rewarded for using gift cards

Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean Rewards account and members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.

Frequently asked questions

How do eGift cards work?
eGift cards are purchased online and delivered digitally via email. You can send up to 10 eGifts per purchase, each with a personalized message. Recipients can use them in-store or online by showing the barcode or entering the card number.
Can I reload a gift card?
Yes! Registered gift cards can be reloaded through the GreenBean app or website. Simply add the card to your account and select "Reload" to add funds.
Do gift cards earn Stars?
When you register your gift card or eGift to a GreenBean Rewards account, you earn Stars on qualifying purchases made with that card — just like any other payment method linked to your account.
Where can I check my balance?
Check your gift card balance on our website, in the GreenBean app, or at any GreenBean location. You'll need your card number and PIN (if applicable).
