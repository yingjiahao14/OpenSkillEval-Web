About eGifts

What is an eGift?

A GreenBean eGift card is a GreenBean Gift Card that is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase. Select a design to start!

Received a gift card?

Add or reload, check balance, or view Card Terms & Conditions. Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean® Rewards account and members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.
