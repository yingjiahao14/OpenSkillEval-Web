About eGifts

Everything you need to know about digital gift cards

Send digitally

A GreenBean eGift card is a GreenBean Gift Card that is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase.

Choose a design

Select from our collection of beautiful designs to match any occasion — birthdays, thank you, congratulations, and more.

Earn Stars

Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean® Rewards account and members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.
