Terms and Conditions

GreenBean Rewards is free to join. You must be a member to earn and redeem Stars. Stars expire after 12 months of inactivity. Status tier is calculated based on Stars earned within a calendar year. Visit greenbeancoffee.com/rewards for full program details.

Earning Stars

  • Earn 1 Star per $1 spent on qualifying purchases at GreenBean Coffee stores.
  • Stars are credited to your account within 24 hours of purchase.
  • Stars cannot be earned on taxes, tips, or gift card purchases.

Redeeming Stars

  • Stars may be redeemed for rewards at participating GreenBean Coffee stores.
  • Rewards are subject to availability and may vary by location.
  • Minimum redemption amounts apply. See reward tiers for details.

Birthday Rewards

Birthday reward is valid for one complimentary handcrafted beverage or food item of your choice. Reward must be claimed during your birthday month. Green and Gold members receive a free treat on their birthday. Reserve members enjoy a 30-day birthday window.
