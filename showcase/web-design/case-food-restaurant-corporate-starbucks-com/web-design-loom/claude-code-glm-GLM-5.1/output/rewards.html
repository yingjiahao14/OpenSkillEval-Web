How it works

Three easy steps to start earning your way to free coffee and more.

1

Collect Stars

Earn Stars by making eligible purchases and participating in challenges, offers and games.

2

Enjoy free treats

Redeem your Stars for free food, drinks and more. Look forward to a birthday treat on us every year.

3

Unlock benefits

Keep earning Stars to increase your status and help you get to free faster. Discover more benefits at every level.
