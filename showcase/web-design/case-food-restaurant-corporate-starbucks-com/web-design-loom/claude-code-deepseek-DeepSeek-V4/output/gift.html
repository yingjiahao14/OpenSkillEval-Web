About eGift Cards

A GreenBean eGift card is a GreenBean Gift Card that is purchased and sent digitally. Send up to 10 eGifts per purchase. Select a design to start!

Received a gift card? Add or reload, check balance, or view Card Terms & Conditions.

Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a GreenBean® Rewards account and members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send an eGift card?

Select a design, choose an amount ($5–$100), enter the recipient's email, and add a personal message. Your eGift will be delivered within minutes — or schedule it for later.

Do gift cards expire?

No, GreenBean Gift Cards never expire. You can use them anytime at any participating GreenBean location.

Can I reload a gift card?

Yes! Register your card to a GreenBean Rewards account and you can reload it online, in the app, or at any store.
