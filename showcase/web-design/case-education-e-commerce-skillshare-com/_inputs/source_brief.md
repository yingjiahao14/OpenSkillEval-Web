## Brand
CreativeHub is an online learning community offering thousands of creative classes taught by industry professionals, from illustration and design to photography and filmmaking. The platform empowers creative professionals, hobbyists, and aspiring artists with hands-on video lessons, structured learning paths, and a vibrant peer community. The tone is modern, playful, and bold.

## FAQ
Section title: Frequently Asked Questions

- **What is CreativeHub?** — CreativeHub is an online learning community with thousands of classes for creative and curious people, on topics including illustration, design, photography, video, freelancing, and more. On CreativeHub, you'll find inspiration from hands-on classes and teachers at the top of their creative fields, so you can take the next step in your creative journey.
- **What is included in my membership?** — As a CreativeHub member, you'll have unlimited access to all classes to watch when and where you want, plus additional features such as offline viewing, access to a vibrant community of lifelong learners, and so much more.
- **What can I learn?** — CreativeHub has thousands of classes in everything from graphic design to cooking, productivity, filmmaking, content creation, UI/UX design, marketing, crafts, music, social media, and entrepreneurship. If it's something creative, you can learn it on CreativeHub.
- **What happens after my trial is over?** — After your trial ends, your annual CreativeHub membership begins. You'll be billed for the year in full, so you can enjoy continuous access to creative classes year-round.
- **Can I teach on the platform?** — Yes! CreativeHub teachers are everyday creatives and professionals who want to share their passion and the skills they've gained with a community of eager learners. Visit our Help Center to learn more.
