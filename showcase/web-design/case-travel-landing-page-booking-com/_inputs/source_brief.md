- **What car do people usually book in the United States?** — Premium is the most popular car group, followed by SUV and medium.
- **How much does it cost to rent a premium car?** — On average, $55 a day.
- **How much does it cost to rent a SUV?** — On average, $54 a day.
- **What car rental companies are available?** — 55 companies including Avis, Budget, and Alamo.
- **Can I return the car to a different location?** — Usually possible but depends on company policy and may cost extra.
- **How far in advance should I book?** — The cheapest time is within the same week for an average of $34/day.
- **What do I need to rent a car?** — Debit or credit card to book; passport, voucher, driver's license, and main driver's credit card at the counter.
