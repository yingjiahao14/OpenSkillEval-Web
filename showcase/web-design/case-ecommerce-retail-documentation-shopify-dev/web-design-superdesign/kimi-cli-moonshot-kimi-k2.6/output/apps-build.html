Best Practices

Follow these guidelines to build high-quality, secure, and performant apps.

Optimize speed across admin, checkout, storefront, and POS. Use lazy loading, minimize bundle sizes, and leverage caching strategies to ensure your app feels instant.

Make your app usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. Follow WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines, ensure keyboard navigation, and test with screen readers.

Protect your app and merchant data from common vulnerabilities. Validate all inputs, use secure authentication flows, and keep dependencies up to date.

Follow API terms and privacy law requirements. Ensure your app complies with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable regulations.

Translate and adapt your app for international merchants. Use i18n frameworks, support multiple currencies, and respect regional formatting.

Meet requirements for transparent and honest app behavior. Avoid dark patterns, clearly disclose data usage, and respect merchant consent.
