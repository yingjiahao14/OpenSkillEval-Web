Best Practices

Follow these guidelines to build high-quality apps that meet CommerceForge standards.

Performance

Optimize speed across admin, checkout, storefront, and POS. Keep extension load times under budget.

Accessibility

Make your app usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. Follow WCAG 2.1 AA standards.

Security

Protect your app and merchant data from common vulnerabilities. Use HTTPS, validate inputs, and rotate credentials.

Compliance

Follow API terms and privacy law requirements including GDPR and CCPA where applicable.

Localization

Translate and adapt your app for international merchants with proper locale handling.

Non-deceptive code

Meet requirements for transparent and honest app behavior. No dark patterns or misleading UX.
