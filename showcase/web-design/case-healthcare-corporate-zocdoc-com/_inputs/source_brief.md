# MedBook — Find and Book Top Doctors Instantly

## Brand
MedBook is an online healthcare appointment marketplace that connects patients with local doctors, dentists, and specialists for easy same-day booking. The platform empowers patients to search by specialty, location, and insurance, read verified reviews, and book appointments online in minutes — all completely free for patients. The tone is modern, professional, and playful, making healthcare access feel simple and approachable.

Brand colors: #1B1464 (primary navy), #FFD930 (accent yellow), #FFFFFF (background white)

## Search Bar
The primary search interface includes three fields:
- **Specialty / Condition / Doctor** — autocomplete text input with dropdown suggestions (e.g., "Dentist," "Back pain," "Dr. Sarah Chen")
- **Location** — city, state, or zip code with geolocation auto-detect option
- **Insurance** — dropdown selector with major insurance carriers (Aetna, Blue Cross, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, etc.)

CTA Button: "Search"

## Popular Specialties
A grid of clickable specialty cards that navigate to filtered search results:

- **Primary Care** — Annual checkups, sick visits, and preventive care
- **Dentist** — Cleanings, fillings, and oral health exams
- **Dermatologist** — Skin conditions, acne, and cosmetic consultations
- **Psychiatrist** — Mental health evaluations and medication management
- **Eye Doctor** — Vision exams, glasses prescriptions, and eye health
- **Orthopedic Surgeon** — Joint pain, fractures, and sports injuries

Each card includes a specialty icon and label. Clicking a card triggers a filtered search for that specialty.

## How It Works
A three-step visual guide explaining the MedBook booking flow:

1. **Search for a provider** — Enter your specialty, condition, or doctor name along with your location and insurance. Browse detailed provider profiles with photos, credentials, and office hours.
2. **Choose a time that works for you** — View real-time availability and pick an appointment slot that fits your schedule — including same-day and next-day options.
3. **Book your appointment instantly** — Confirm your visit in just a few clicks. Receive instant confirmation and reminders so you never miss an appointment.
