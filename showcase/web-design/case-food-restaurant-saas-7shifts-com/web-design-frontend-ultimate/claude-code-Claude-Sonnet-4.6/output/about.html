Our Story

Built by Restaurant
Operators, for Operators

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by people who've worked the floor, managed the chaos, and wished there was a better way.

Our Mission

Empowering every restaurant to run their best

Empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. After years of losing Sunday evenings to spreadsheet scheduling and receiving 2am texts about uncovered shifts, we knew there had to be a better way.

Today, ShiftWise helps over 35,000 restaurant managers reclaim their time and run more compliant, more connected operations — whether they're running one location or one hundred.

What We Stand For

Our values drive everything we build

Operators First
Every feature starts with a real problem faced by a real restaurant manager. We listen before we build.
Radical Simplicity
Powerful software shouldn't require a manual. If a new manager can't figure it out in 10 minutes, we haven't done our job.
Compliance Without Compromise
Labor law compliance should be automatic, not an afterthought. We stay ahead of regulations so you don't have to.
