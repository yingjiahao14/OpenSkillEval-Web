Core Features

Everything your team needs to run smoothly

From building schedules to keeping everyone compliant — ShiftWise handles it all in one place.

Drag-and-Drop Scheduling

Build and publish weekly schedules with an intuitive visual editor. Auto-fill based on availability and roles — done in minutes, not hours.

Team Messaging

Communicate with individuals, shift groups, or your entire staff in one hub. No more lost group texts. Read receipts ensure messages are seen.

Labor Law Compliance

Automatic notifications for overtime thresholds, predictive scheduling laws, and mandatory break periods — before violations happen.

POS Integrations

Connect with Toast, Square, Clover, and more to align your labor costs with real-time sales data and demand forecasting.

Time Clocking

GPS-enabled clock-in/clock-out from any mobile device. Eliminate buddy punching with photo verification for accurate timesheets.
