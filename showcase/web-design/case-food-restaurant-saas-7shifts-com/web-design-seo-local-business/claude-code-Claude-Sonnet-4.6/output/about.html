Our Story

Our Mission

Empowering Every Restaurant with Better Tools

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. Juggling spreadsheets, group texts, and paper timesheets while trying to run a great restaurant — we knew there had to be a better way.

Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

People First

We build technology that respects and empowers every person in your restaurant — managers and staff alike.

Simplicity Over Complexity

Every feature we build must be intuitive enough to use during a busy Friday dinner rush — no training manual required.

Trust Through Transparency

Clear pricing, honest communication, and software that does exactly what we say it does — every time.
