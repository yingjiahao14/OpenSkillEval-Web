Core Features

Everything You Need to Run Your Team

From scheduling to compliance, ShiftWise covers every aspect of workforce management so you can focus on what matters — great food and happy guests.

Drag-and-Drop Scheduling

Build and publish weekly schedules with an intuitive visual editor. Auto-fill based on availability and roles — done in minutes, not hours.

Team Messaging

Communicate with individuals, shift groups, or your entire staff. No more lost group texts — read receipts ensure critical messages are seen.

Labor Law Compliance

Automatic alerts for overtime thresholds, predictive scheduling laws, and mandatory breaks — flag violations before they happen.

POS Integrations

Connect with Toast, Square, Clover, and Lightspeed to align your labor costs with real-time sales data for smarter staffing decisions.

Time Clocking

GPS-enabled clock-in/clock-out from any mobile device. Eliminate buddy punching with photo verification and accurate time tracking.

Analytics & Forecasting

Monitor labor costs, attendance trends, and team performance from a single dashboard. Make data-driven decisions every week.
