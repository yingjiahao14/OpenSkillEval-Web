About ShiftWise

Empowering restaurant teams with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

Our Mission

Built by Restaurant People, For Restaurant People

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. We lived through the late nights, the scheduling spreadsheets, the compliance scares, and the communication breakdowns.

Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests. We believe that when your team is in sync, everything runs better.
