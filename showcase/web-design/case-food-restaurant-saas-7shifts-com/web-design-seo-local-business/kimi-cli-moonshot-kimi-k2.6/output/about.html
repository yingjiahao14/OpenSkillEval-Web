Our Mission

Empowering Every Restaurant to Thrive

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

We believe that when restaurant teams are in sync, everything else falls into place: happier staff, smoother service, and healthier margins. That's why we've built a platform that handles the complexity of scheduling, communication, and compliance — so you can focus on what matters most.
