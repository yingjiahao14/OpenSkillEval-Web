Our Mission

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

We believe that when restaurant teams are well-scheduled, well-informed, and well-supported, they create better experiences for every guest who walks through the door. That's why we obsess over the details that matter: intuitive design, reliable compliance, and tools that actually save time instead of creating more work.
