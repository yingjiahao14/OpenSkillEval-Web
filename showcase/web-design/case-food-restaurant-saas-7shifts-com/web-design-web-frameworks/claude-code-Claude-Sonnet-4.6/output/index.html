Core Features

Everything Your Team Needs, Nothing They Don't

From building the perfect schedule to staying compliant with labor laws, ShiftWise covers every aspect of restaurant workforce management.

Drag-and-Drop Scheduling

Build and publish weekly schedules with an intuitive visual editor. Auto-fill based on availability and roles — done in minutes, not hours.

Team Messaging

Communicate with individuals, shift groups, or your entire staff. Read receipts ensure critical messages are always seen.

Labor Law Compliance

Automatic alerts for overtime thresholds, predictive scheduling laws, and mandatory break periods — before violations happen.

POS Integrations

Connect with Toast, Square, Clover, and more to align labor costs with real-time sales data. One source of truth.

Time Clocking

GPS-enabled clock-in/out from any mobile device. Eliminate buddy punching with photo verification built right in.

Labor Analytics

Monitor labor costs, attendance trends, and team performance from a single dashboard. Optimize with real data.
