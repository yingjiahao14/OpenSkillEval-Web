Core Features

Five Tools. One Platform. Zero Chaos.

ShiftWise brings together everything restaurant teams need to run smoothly — in a single, unified experience.

Drag-and-Drop Scheduling

Build and publish weekly schedules with an intuitive visual editor. Auto-fill based on availability and roles.

Team Messaging

Communicate with individuals, shift groups, or your entire staff. No more lost group texts or missed updates.

Labor Law Compliance Alerts

Automatic notifications for overtime thresholds, predictive scheduling laws, and mandatory break periods.

POS Integrations

Connect with leading point-of-sale systems to align labor costs with real-time sales data seamlessly.

Time Clocking

GPS-enabled clock-in/clock-out from any mobile device. Eliminate buddy punching with photo verification.

Demand Forecasting

Use historical sales data and AI to predict staffing needs — so you're always properly staffed, never overstaffed.
