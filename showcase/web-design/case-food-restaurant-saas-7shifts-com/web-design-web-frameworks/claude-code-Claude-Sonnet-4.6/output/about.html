Our Story

Built by Restaurant People, for Restaurant People

ShiftWise was founded by a team who lived the chaos of restaurant workforce management firsthand — and decided to fix it.

Our Mission

Empowering Every Restaurant to Run at Its Best

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. We watched managers spend 6-hour stretches building schedules in spreadsheets, texting shift reminders into the void, and getting blindsided by compliance violations they didn't know existed.

Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests. We believe when your team is in sync, your guests feel it.
