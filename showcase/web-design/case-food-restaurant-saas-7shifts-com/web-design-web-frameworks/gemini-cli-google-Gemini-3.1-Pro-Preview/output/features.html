Conflict-Free Scheduling

Create conflict-free schedules in minutes. ShiftWise automatically factors in employee availability, time-off requests, certifications, and labor budget targets.

  • Intuitive drag-and-drop visual editor
  • Copy previous weeks or use custom templates
  • Smart auto-scheduler to do the heavy lifting
  • Instant mobile notifications for published schedules

Centralized Team Communication

Replace scattered text threads with a centralized messaging hub. Keep everyone on the same page without the chaos of group chats.
