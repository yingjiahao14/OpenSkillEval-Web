About ShiftWise

Empowering restaurant teams with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

Our Mission

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand.

Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

We believe that when your team is in sync, your restaurant thrives. That's why we're committed to building tools that save time, reduce stress, and help restaurant professionals focus on what they do best: creating memorable guest experiences.
