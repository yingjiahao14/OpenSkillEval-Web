Powerful Tools Built for Restaurants

Everything your team needs to stay organized, connected, and compliant — in one platform designed specifically for the hospitality industry.

Scheduling

Create Conflict-Free Schedules in Minutes

ShiftWise automatically factors in employee availability, time-off requests, certifications, and labor budget targets. Managers can copy previous weeks, use templates, or let the auto-scheduler do the heavy lifting.

  • Drag-and-drop visual editor
  • Auto-fill based on availability and roles
  • Instant mobile notifications on publish
  • Reusable templates and copy-from-week
Team Communication

Replace Scattered Texts with a Central Hub

Send shift reminders, broadcast announcements, and manage shift swap requests — all within the app. Read receipts ensure critical messages are seen, and managers can pin important updates for new hires.

  • Individual, group, and all-staff channels
  • Read receipts on critical messages
  • Instant shift swap requests
  • Pinned announcements for onboarding
Labor Compliance

Navigate Complex Labor Regulations with Confidence

ShiftWise monitors federal, state, and local labor laws — including predictive scheduling ordinances, minor work-hour restrictions, and overtime rules — and flags potential violations before they happen.

  • Federal, state, and local law monitoring
  • Predictive scheduling compliance
  • Overtime and break violation alerts
  • Minor work-hour restrictions
