Core Features

Everything in One Platform

No more juggling separate tools. ShiftWise brings scheduling, communication, compliance, and analytics under one roof.

📋

Drag-and-Drop Scheduling

Build and publish weekly schedules with an intuitive visual editor. Auto-fill based on availability and roles. Managers can copy previous weeks, use templates, or let the auto-scheduler do the heavy lifting.

💬

Team Messaging

Communicate with individuals, shift groups, or your entire staff. No more lost group texts. Read receipts ensure critical messages are seen, and managers can pin important updates for new hires.

⚖️

Labor Law Compliance Alerts

Automatic notifications for overtime thresholds, predictive scheduling laws, and mandatory break periods. Navigate complex labor regulations with confidence.

🔗

POS Integrations

Connect with leading point-of-sale systems to align labor costs with real-time sales data. Works with Toast, Square, Clover, Lightspeed, and more.

⏱️

Time Clocking

GPS-enabled clock-in/clock-out from any mobile device. Eliminate buddy punching with photo verification. Track hours accurately and export directly to payroll.

📊

Demand Forecasting

Use historical sales data and POS integrations to forecast demand and build smarter, leaner schedules. Available on the Premium plan.
