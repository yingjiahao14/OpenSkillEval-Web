Our Story

Built by Restaurant People, for Restaurant People

ShiftWise was founded by operators who lived the chaos of workforce management firsthand. We built the tool we always wished we had.

Our Mission

Empowering Every Restaurant to Run at Its Best

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. Spreadsheets, group texts, missed shifts, compliance fines — we've been there.

Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

Today, over 35,000 managers trust ShiftWise to run their teams. We're proud to be the platform that helps operators get back on the floor — where they belong.
