Our Mission

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 with a simple mission: to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

We believe that great restaurants are built by great teams, and great teams deserve great tools. That's why we've built an all-in-one platform that handles scheduling, communication, and compliance — so restaurant owners and managers can focus on what matters most: creating memorable experiences for their guests.
