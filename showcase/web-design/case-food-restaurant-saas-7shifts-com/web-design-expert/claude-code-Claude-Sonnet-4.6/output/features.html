Core Features

Powerful Tools Built for
Restaurant Teams

From the kitchen to the front of house, ShiftWise handles every aspect of workforce management — so you can focus on what matters most.

Drag-and-Drop Scheduling

Build conflict-free weekly schedules with a visual editor. Automatically factors in availability, certifications, and labor budgets.

Team Messaging

Centralized messaging for individuals, shift groups, or the whole team. Read receipts ensure critical updates are never missed.

Labor Compliance

Real-time alerts for overtime, predictive scheduling laws, and break violations — before they become costly violations.

POS Integrations

Connect with Toast, Square, Clover, and more to align labor costs with real-time sales data and smarter forecasting.

Time Clocking

GPS-enabled mobile clock-in with photo verification. Eliminate buddy punching and ensure accurate payroll every time.

Analytics & Reporting

Monitor labor costs, attendance trends, and team performance from a single dashboard. Make data-driven scheduling decisions.
