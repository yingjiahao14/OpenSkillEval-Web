Our Mission

Empowering Every Restaurant to Manage People as Seamlessly as Serving Guests

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. Late nights with spreadsheets, missed shift swaps, compliance violations caught too late — they knew there had to be a better way.

Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.
