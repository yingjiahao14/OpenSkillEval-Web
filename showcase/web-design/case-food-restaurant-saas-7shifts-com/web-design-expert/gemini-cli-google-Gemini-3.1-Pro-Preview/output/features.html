Powerful Tools for Hospitality Teams

Discover how ShiftWise helps you manage schedules, communicate with your staff, and stay compliant with labor laws.

Drag-and-Drop Scheduling

Create conflict-free schedules in minutes. ShiftWise automatically factors in employee availability, time-off requests, certifications, and labor budget targets. Managers can copy previous weeks, use templates, or let the auto-scheduler do the heavy lifting. Employees receive instant mobile notifications when schedules are published or updated.

Team Communication

Replace scattered text threads with a centralized messaging hub. Send shift reminders, broadcast announcements, and manage shift swap requests — all within the app. Read receipts ensure critical messages are seen, and managers can pin important updates for new hires.

Labor Compliance Alerts

Navigate complex and ever-changing labor regulations with confidence. ShiftWise monitors federal, state, and local labor laws — including predictive scheduling ordinances, minor work-hour restrictions, and overtime rules — and flags potential violations before they happen.
