Scheduling

Create Conflict-Free Schedules in Minutes

ShiftWise automatically factors in employee availability, time-off requests, certifications, and labor budget targets. Managers can copy previous weeks, use templates, or let the auto-scheduler do the heavy lifting.

Employees receive instant mobile notifications when schedules are published or updated — no more "I didn't know I was working today."

Communication

One Place for Every Message

Replace scattered text threads with a centralized messaging hub. Send shift reminders, broadcast announcements, and manage shift swap requests — all within the app.

Read receipts ensure critical messages are seen, and managers can pin important updates for new hires so nothing gets lost in the noise.
