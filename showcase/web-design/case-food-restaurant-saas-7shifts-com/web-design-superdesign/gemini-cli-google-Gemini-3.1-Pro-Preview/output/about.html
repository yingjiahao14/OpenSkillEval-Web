Our Mission

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers. We knew the pain of late-night scheduling, frantic group texts looking for shift coverage, and the anxiety of compliance audits.

Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests. We believe that when your team is in sync, your restaurant thrives.
