About ShiftWise

Empowering restaurant teams with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

Our Mission

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

We believe that great restaurant experiences start with well-managed teams. When your staff is scheduled fairly, communicated with clearly, and supported properly, they deliver better service, creating happier guests and more successful restaurants.
