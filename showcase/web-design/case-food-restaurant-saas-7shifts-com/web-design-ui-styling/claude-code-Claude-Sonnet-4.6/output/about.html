Our Story

ShiftWise was founded by a team who lived the chaos of restaurant operations firsthand — and decided to fix it.

Our Mission

Empowering Every Restaurant with the Right Tools

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. We watched managers spend hours every week wrestling with spreadsheets, chasing down shift swaps over text, and getting hit with compliance violations they didn't even know were coming.

So we built the tool we wished we'd had. Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

Today, ShiftWise is used by over 35,000 restaurant managers across the country. We're proud of every hour saved, every violation avoided, and every team that's a little more connected because of our platform.
