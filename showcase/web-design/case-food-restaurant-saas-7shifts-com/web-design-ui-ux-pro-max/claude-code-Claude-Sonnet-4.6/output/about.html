Our Mission

Empowering Every Restaurant with Technology That Works

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand.

Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests.

We believe that when managers spend less time wrestling with spreadsheets and group texts, they spend more time building the teams and experiences that make restaurants thrive.

Our Story

From the Floor to the Cloud

2019

ShiftWise Founded

Rachel and Marcus leave their restaurant and tech jobs to solve workforce management for restaurants. The first version is built in a San Francisco co-working space.

2020

First 500 Restaurants

Despite a challenging year for hospitality, ShiftWise grows to 500 restaurants and raises a seed round to build out compliance and team messaging features.

2022

10,000 Managers Strong

ShiftWise hits 10,000 restaurant managers and launches POS integrations with Toast and Square, connecting scheduling directly to labor cost data.

Today

35,000+ Managers, 3 Offices

With teams in San Francisco, Austin, and Toronto, ShiftWise serves 35,000+ managers scheduling 750,000+ shifts monthly. We're just getting started.
