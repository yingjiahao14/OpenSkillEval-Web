Powerful Features for Modern Restaurants

Everything you need to schedule smarter, communicate better, and stay compliant — all in one platform.

Drag-and-Drop Scheduling

Create conflict-free schedules in minutes. ShiftWise automatically factors in employee availability, time-off requests, certifications, and labor budget targets.

  • Copy previous weeks or use templates to speed up creation
  • Auto-scheduler fills shifts based on roles and availability
  • Instant mobile notifications when schedules are published
  • Visual conflict detection prevents double-booking

Team Communication

Replace scattered text threads with a centralized messaging hub. Send shift reminders, broadcast announcements, and manage shift swap requests — all within the app.

  • Message individuals, groups, or your entire staff
  • Read receipts ensure critical messages are seen
  • Pin important updates for new hires
  • Built-in shift swap request workflow

Labor Compliance

Navigate complex and ever-changing labor regulations with confidence. ShiftWise monitors federal, state, and local labor laws and flags potential violations before they happen.

  • Predictive scheduling ordinance alerts
  • Minor work-hour restriction monitoring
  • Overtime threshold warnings
  • Mandatory break period tracking

Seamless Integrations

ShiftWise connects with the tools restaurants already use. Sync data automatically and eliminate double entry across your tech stack.

  • Point of Sale: Toast, Square, Clover, Lightspeed
  • Payroll: ADP, Gusto, Paychex, QuickBooks
  • Accounting: Xero, FreshBooks, Sage
  • Delivery: DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub
