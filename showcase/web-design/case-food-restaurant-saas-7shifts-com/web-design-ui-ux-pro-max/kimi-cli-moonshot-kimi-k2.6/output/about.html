Our Story

Built by restaurant people, for restaurant people. We're on a mission to make workforce management effortless.

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand.

Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests. We believe that when restaurant teams are in sync, everyone wins: owners save time, managers stay compliant, and staff feel valued.
