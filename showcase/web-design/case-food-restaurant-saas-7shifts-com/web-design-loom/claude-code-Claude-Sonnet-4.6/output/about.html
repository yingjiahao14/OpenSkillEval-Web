Our Story

Built by Restaurant Operators, For Restaurant Operators

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by people who lived the chaos of restaurant workforce management — and decided to fix it once and for all.

Our Mission

Empowering Every Restaurant to Thrive

ShiftWise was founded in 2019 by a team of former restaurant operators and software engineers who experienced the chaos of workforce management firsthand. Managing schedules on spreadsheets, juggling group texts for shift swaps, and losing sleep over compliance violations — they knew there had to be a better way.

Our mission is to empower every restaurant — from single-unit independents to national chains — with technology that makes managing people as seamless as serving guests. We believe when your team runs smoothly, your restaurant does too.
