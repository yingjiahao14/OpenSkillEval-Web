## How It Works
1. **Build Your Schedule** — Use drag-and-drop tools to create optimized weekly schedules in minutes, not hours.
2. **Keep Everyone Connected** — Broadcast shift updates, swap requests, and announcements through built-in team messaging.
3. **Stay Compliant Automatically** — Get real-time alerts for overtime, break violations, and local labor law requirements.
4. **Track & Optimize** — Monitor labor costs, attendance trends, and team performance from a single dashboard.

## Core Features
- **Drag-and-drop scheduling** — Build and publish weekly schedules with an intuitive visual editor. Auto-fill based on availability and roles.
- **Team messaging** — Communicate with individuals, shift groups, or your entire staff. No more lost group texts.
- **Labor law compliance alerts** — Automatic notifications for overtime thresholds, predictive scheduling laws, and mandatory break periods.
- **POS integrations** — Connect with leading point-of-sale systems to align labor costs with real-time sales data.
- **Time clocking** — GPS-enabled clock-in/clock-out from any mobile device. Eliminate buddy punching with photo verification.

## Feature: Scheduling
Create conflict-free schedules in minutes. ShiftWise automatically factors in employee availability, time-off requests, certifications, and labor budget targets. Managers can copy previous weeks, use templates, or let the auto-scheduler do the heavy lifting. Employees receive instant mobile notifications when schedules are published or updated.

## Feature: Team Communication
Replace scattered text threads with a centralized messaging hub. Send shift reminders, broadcast announcements, and manage shift swap requests — all within the app. Read receipts ensure critical messages are seen, and managers can pin important updates for new hires.

## Feature: Labor Compliance
Navigate complex and ever-changing labor regulations with confidence. ShiftWise monitors federal, state, and local labor laws — including predictive scheduling ordinances, minor work-hour restrictions, and overtime rules — and flags potential violations before they happen.
