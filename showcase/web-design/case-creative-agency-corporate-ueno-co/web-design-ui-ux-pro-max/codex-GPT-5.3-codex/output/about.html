About

A boutique agency with global perspective.

Volta Studio is not an old agency and a startup agency at the same time. We are all experienced old timers and all curious youngsters too. We are a lot of things, but mostly we are just very fortunate because we get to do what we love every day with people we deeply care about.

And what we love to do is to build and improve digital products, services and user experiences in all forms and all channels. Whatever that means these days. In short, we make things pretty and useful at the same time and obsess over every little detail in between.

We've had this luck now for over ten years and have worked with tiny startups and giant corporations. We have won and lost and won and lost and learned and made mistakes and had occasional breakthroughs. And one thing has remained true through all of it: if people don't feel anything, it doesn't matter how clever the system is.

So we blend strategic thinking with craft and momentum. We care about market context and boardroom realities, but we are equally serious about copy lines, motion curves, and button states. That's where trust is won.
