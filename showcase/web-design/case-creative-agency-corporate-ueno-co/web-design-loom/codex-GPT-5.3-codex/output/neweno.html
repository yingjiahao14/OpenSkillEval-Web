Founder Letter

A Letter from Neweno.

Volta began in a small studio in Reykjavík. I wanted to build a place where strategy could feel human, and design could carry real emotional weight. We started with very little, but we had standards, curiosity, and a stubborn belief that details matter.

Over the years, our work reached teams far beyond Iceland. We partnered with leaders in healthcare, finance, mobility, and technology. Each engagement taught me the same thing: people remember how systems make them feel, not just how they function.

In 2021, I stepped inside a major technology company. It was a meaningful chapter. I learned scale from the inside. I learned what breaks when organizations move quickly. I learned where good ideas lose their shape before they reach customers.

In 2025, I reopened Volta as a curated studio. Smaller by design. More intentional. Built for enterprise teams that need a trusted creative partner, not a production pipeline. We choose a limited number of collaborations each year so every project gets real senior attention.

Our process is simple. We listen until the real problem appears. We bring order to ambiguity. We design brands, products, and experiences that are both useful and alive. Function and feeling, together. Always.

If you are building something significant and want a partner who cares about craft as much as outcomes, we should talk.

— Neweno, Founder
