Services

Function + Feeling.

We believe things should work — and feel like people made them. So we start every project with two questions: what does it do, and how does it make people feel?

Philosophy

On purpose. On craft.

Function is the thing doing the thing it’s supposed to do — in the simplest way possible.

Feeling is the care you can sense — the connection from the creators to the person who uses it.

We hold both. We don’t compromise either.

Service Details

That’s nice. But what do you actually do?

Branding.

All the elements about a company, product, experience — or to simplify it, brands — that make us feel something. Yes, it’s a logo. And yes it’s colors. And typography. And how the brand talks. And photos, videos, illustrations. How it moves, shakes and bakes. We do all that stuff.

Products.

The ones we make are usually digital and they are usually pretty heavy on that function thing. It’s an app, or a website, or a thingamajig. But it’s also the feeling thing. Why do you keep coming back? Why does it feel better? That’s sort of what we do for some of the apps you probably use every day.

Experiences.

The ones we make are again usually digital. Sometimes they are websites. Maybe it’s a game, or a place you go to have some fun. Sometimes it’s to sell something, usually there’s a story to be told, usually there’s a fair bit of branding involved. And you guessed it, we’ve done a lot of that for some of the biggest brands in the world.
