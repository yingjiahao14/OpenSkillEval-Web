# Fresh Pantry — Discover Everyday Deliciousness

## Overview

Build a single-page e-commerce landing site for **Fresh Pantry**, a neighborhood grocery brand that celebrates unique, high-quality food products at great prices. The audience is health-conscious grocery shoppers who love discovering new finds — think the excitement of wandering a well-curated store aisle, translated to the web. The tone should feel **warm, playful, and modern** — approachable but polished, never corporate or sterile.

## Visual & Style Direction

- **Color palette:** Think fresh greens, warm cream/off-white backgrounds, pops of sunny yellow or terracotta for accents. Avoid harsh neons or overly dark tones.
- **Typography:** A friendly rounded sans-serif for headings (something like Nunito or Poppins) paired with a clean body font. Keep it legible and inviting.
- **Imagery mood:** Bright, natural-light food photography vibes. Use placeholder images that suggest colorful produce, pantry staples, and cozy store interiors.
- **Layout:** Generous whitespace, rounded corners on cards, subtle shadows — the whole page should feel like a pleasant stroll through a sunlit market.

## Key Priorities

1. **The featured products carousel** is the centerpiece interaction — make sure the left/right navigation feels smooth and the product cards (with images, names, and prices) are visually appealing and easy to scan.
2. **The store locator** should feel functional and responsive: a zip code input with a search button that reveals mock store results. This drives the core business goal of in-store visits.
3. **Brand highlights stats** (500+ stores, 4,000+ products, etc.) should be presented as an eye-catching banner — use bold numbers with supporting labels to build credibility at a glance.
4. **Category hover effects** should feel delightful but not distracting — a gentle scale-up with an overlay is the target.
