How Do We Calculate Cryptocurrency Prices?

We receive updated cryptocurrency prices directly from exchanges based on their trading pairs, then convert to USD using a volume-weighted average across the most liquid markets. This ensures prices reflect actual trading conditions rather than any single exchange's quote.

How Do We Calculate Crypto Valuations?

Market capitalizations are calculated by multiplying the total circulating supply by the current reference price. We exclude locked, reserved, or unreleased tokens to present the most accurate reflection of freely traded market value.
