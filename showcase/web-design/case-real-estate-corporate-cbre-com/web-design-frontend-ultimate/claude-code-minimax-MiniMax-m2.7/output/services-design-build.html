Design & Build

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate that meets your operational and financial objectives.

From concept to occupancy, our integrated design and build services streamline the development process, reduce risk, and deliver projects that exceed expectations on time and on budget.

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our experienced managers ensure quality, budget, and timeline compliance at every stage.

  • Design coordination and contractor selection
  • Construction oversight and quality control
  • Project closeout and occupancy support
