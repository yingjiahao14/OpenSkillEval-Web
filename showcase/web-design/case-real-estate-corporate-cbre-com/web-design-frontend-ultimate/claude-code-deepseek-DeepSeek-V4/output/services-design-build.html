Services

Design & Build

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate that performs.

From feasibility studies and design management to construction oversight and delivery, our integrated project delivery platform ensures your vision is realized on time, on budget, and to the highest standards of quality.

Capabilities

Integrated Project Delivery

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout.

  • Feasibility studies & business case development
  • Design team selection & coordination
  • Schedule, budget & risk management
  • Contractor procurement & contract administration
  • Quality assurance & commissioning oversight
  • Project closeout & transition management

Supply Chain Sourcing

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines.

  • Strategic sourcing & vendor pre-qualification
  • Volume-based pricing & framework agreements
  • Supply chain risk assessment & mitigation
  • Sustainable materials sourcing & compliance
  • Logistics coordination & materials management
  • Cost benchmarking & value engineering
