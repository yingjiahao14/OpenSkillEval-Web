Invest, Finance & Value

As the global commercial real estate brokerage leader, GlobalStone is your strategic partner across the entire real estate investment lifecycle.

From capital markets execution to independent valuation and global forecasting, our integrated investment platform delivers the insight, access, and execution certainty that institutional and private investors demand in today's complex markets.

Comprehensive Investment Services

Capital Markets

Global expertise in investment sales, debt and structured finance, and recapitalization services across all property types and geographies.

Valuation & Advisory Services

Global Forecasting & Analytics

Proprietary econometric models and market intelligence that inform investment timing, asset selection, and portfolio construction.
