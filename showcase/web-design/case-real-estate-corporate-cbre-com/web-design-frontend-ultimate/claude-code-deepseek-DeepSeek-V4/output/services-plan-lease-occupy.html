Plan, Lease & Occupy

As the #1 advisory and transactions firm in the world, GlobalStone offers unique insight, experience, and solutions to serve the needs of occupiers and owners.

Whether you're a multinational corporation optimizing a global portfolio or a growing business securing your next location, our integrated planning and leasing platform delivers better outcomes — faster, smarter, and with less risk.

What We Do

Our Approach to Occupancy

We bring a data-driven, consultative methodology to every engagement — from single-asset lease negotiations to global portfolio transformation.

We analyze your entire real estate footprint against business objectives, workforce demographics, and market conditions to develop optimized portfolio strategies that reduce costs, improve flexibility, and enhance employee experience.
Our market-leading transaction professionals bring unparalleled local knowledge and global reach, negotiating superior terms on leases, renewals, purchases, and dispositions across every major market worldwide.
We design workplaces that attract talent, foster collaboration, and support evolving work patterns — backed by change management programs that ensure adoption and measure outcomes.
Through lease administration, critical date management, and continuous portfolio analytics, we ensure your occupancy strategy evolves with your business — identifying savings opportunities and mitigating risk at every turn.
