Design & Build

From concept to occupancy — we plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate that drives your business forward.

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate. Our integrated approach brings together project management, design coordination, and supply chain expertise to reduce timelines, control costs, and deliver exceptional outcomes.

Capabilities

Design & Build Expertise

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our project managers bring deep technical expertise and a rigorous process to every engagement, ensuring projects are delivered on time, on budget, and to the highest quality standards.

Supply Chain Sourcing

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines. We leverage our global scale and deep vendor relationships to negotiate best-in-class pricing, ensure quality control, and build resilient supply chains for projects of any size.

Strategic Partnership

Integrated Delivery, Exceptional Results

GlobalStone's Design & Build division partners with leading architecture, engineering, and construction firms worldwide to deliver turnkey solutions. Our integrated approach ensures seamless coordination from concept through occupancy, reducing timelines by an average of 18% and delivering cost savings of up to 12%.
