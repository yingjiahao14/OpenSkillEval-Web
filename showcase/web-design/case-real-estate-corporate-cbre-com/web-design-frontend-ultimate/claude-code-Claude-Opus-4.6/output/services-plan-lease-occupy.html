Plan, Lease & Occupy

The #1 advisory and transactions firm serving the needs of occupiers and owners worldwide.

As the #1 advisory and transactions firm in the world, GlobalStone offers unique insight, experience, and solutions to serve the needs of occupiers and owners. Whether you're finding the right space, negotiating the best terms, or transforming your workplace strategy, our integrated approach delivers measurable results.

Capabilities

Transaction Services
