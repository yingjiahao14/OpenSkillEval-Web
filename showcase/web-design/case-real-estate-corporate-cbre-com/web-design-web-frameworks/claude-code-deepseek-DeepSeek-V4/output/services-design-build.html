Design & Build

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate that performs.

Service Overview

From Vision to Occupancy, Delivered with Precision

GlobalStone's Design & Build division provides end-to-end project leadership that transforms real estate strategies into built reality. We manage every phase — feasibility, design coordination, procurement, construction oversight, and delivery — with a singular focus on quality, timeline, and budget.

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our team ensures every milestone is met, every risk is managed, and every stakeholder is aligned throughout the project lifecycle.

  • Feasibility studies and due diligence
  • Design team coordination and value engineering
  • Schedule, budget, and risk management
  • Quality assurance and commissioning
  • Closeout and transition planning

Supply Chain Sourcing

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines. Our global supply chain network ensures the right materials, at the right price, delivered at the right time — even in the most complex market conditions.

  • Strategic sourcing and bid management
  • Vendor prequalification and selection
  • Contract negotiation and administration
  • Materials procurement and logistics
  • Supplier diversity program integration

Strategic Partnerships

Delivering Turnkey Solutions Through Global Collaboration

GlobalStone's Design & Build division partners with leading architecture, engineering, and construction firms worldwide to deliver turnkey solutions. Our integrated approach ensures seamless coordination from concept through occupancy, reducing timelines by an average of 18% and delivering cost savings of up to 12%.
