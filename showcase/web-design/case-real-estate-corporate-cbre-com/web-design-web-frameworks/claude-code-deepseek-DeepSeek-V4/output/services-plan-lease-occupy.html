Plan, Lease & Occupy

As the #1 advisory and transactions firm in the world, GlobalStone offers unique insight, experience, and solutions to serve the needs of occupiers and owners across every market.

Service Overview

Strategic Occupancy for a Changing World

In today's dynamic environment, how you plan, lease, and occupy space directly impacts your bottom line, your talent strategy, and your brand. GlobalStone's integrated platform combines transaction expertise, advanced analytics, workplace strategy, and portfolio optimization to turn real estate into a competitive advantage.

Transaction Services

Tenant and landlord representation for office, industrial, retail, and specialty property leasing across global markets.

Site Selection & Location Strategy

Data-driven analysis combining labor analytics, incentives evaluation, and market benchmarking to optimize location decisions.

Workplace Solutions

Workplace strategy, change management, and space programming that align physical environments with workforce needs.

Experience Services

Hospitality-inspired facility experiences that elevate employee engagement and workplace satisfaction.

Lease Administration

Technology-enabled lease abstraction, compliance tracking, and critical date management for complex portfolios.

Portfolio Strategy

Holistic occupancy planning, scenario modeling, and cost optimization across multi-market portfolios.
