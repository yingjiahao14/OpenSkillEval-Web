About Us

Invest, Finance & Value

Your strategic partner across the entire real estate investment lifecycle — from acquisition through disposition, with unmatched global expertise.

As the global commercial real estate brokerage leader, GlobalStone is your strategic partner across the entire real estate investment lifecycle. We combine deep market intelligence, proprietary data, and an unmatched global platform to deliver exceptional outcomes for investors and owners.

Our Capabilities

Capital Markets

Global expertise in investment sales, debt and structured finance, and recapitalization services across all property types and geographies.

Valuation & Advisory Services

Independent, defensible valuations and strategic advisory powered by the industry's largest team of credentialed professionals.

Global Forecasting & Analytics

Proprietary econometric models and market intelligence that inform investment timing, asset selection, and portfolio construction.

Sustainability Solutions

ESG strategy, green building certification, and decarbonization advisory to future-proof assets and meet regulatory requirements.

Transform Business Outcomes

Data-driven consulting that connects real estate decisions to enterprise performance metrics.

Strategic Investment Consulting

Portfolio strategy, fund structuring, and capital allocation advisory for institutional investors and sovereign wealth funds.
