About Us

Design & Build

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate that exceeds expectations.

From concept through occupancy, our Design & Build team delivers turnkey solutions that align your real estate with strategic objectives. We bring together project management expertise, supply chain intelligence, and an integrated approach that reduces timelines and maximizes value.

Our Capabilities

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our experienced project managers ensure every milestone is met on time and within budget, while maintaining the highest quality standards.

Supply Chain Sourcing

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines. Our global sourcing network and data-driven approach deliver measurable savings while ensuring quality and reliability across every phase of construction.

Strategic Partnerships That Deliver

GlobalStone's Design & Build division partners with leading architecture, engineering, and construction firms worldwide to deliver turnkey solutions.

Our integrated approach ensures seamless coordination from concept through occupancy, reducing timelines by an average of 18% and delivering cost savings of up to 12%.
