Invest, Finance & Value

Strategic capital solutions for every stage of the investment lifecycle

As the global commercial real estate brokerage leader, GlobalStone is your strategic partner across the entire real estate investment lifecycle — from acquisition and financing to valuation and disposition.

Industry-leading investment expertise

With the largest team of credentialed professionals in the industry, proprietary econometric models, and a global platform spanning 100+ countries, GlobalStone delivers the capital markets execution, valuation precision, and strategic advisory that institutional and private investors demand.

Investment Capabilities

Six core capabilities powering better investment decisions.

Capital Markets

Global expertise in investment sales, debt and structured finance, and recapitalization services across all property types and geographies.

Valuation & Advisory Services

Independent, defensible valuations and strategic advisory powered by the industry's largest team of credentialed professionals.

Global Forecasting & Analytics

Proprietary econometric models and market intelligence that inform investment timing, asset selection, and portfolio construction.
