Plan, Lease & Occupy

The #1 advisory and transactions firm for occupiers and owners worldwide

GlobalStone offers unique insight, experience, and solutions to serve the needs of occupiers and owners — from site selection and lease negotiation to workplace strategy and portfolio optimization.

Where workplace meets strategy

In today's dynamic market, the spaces you occupy directly impact your ability to attract talent, serve customers, and operate efficiently. GlobalStone's planning and leasing professionals bring data-driven insight and unmatched transaction experience to every engagement — ensuring your real estate is a competitive advantage, not just a cost center.

Planning & Leasing Capabilities

Six integrated capabilities to optimize your occupancy strategy.

Transaction Services

Tenant and landlord representation for office, industrial, retail, and specialty property leasing across global markets.

Site Selection & Location Strategy

Data-driven analysis combining labor analytics, incentives evaluation, and market benchmarking to optimize location decisions.

Workplace Solutions

Workplace strategy, change management, and space programming that align physical environments with workforce needs.

Experience Services

Hospitality-inspired facility experiences that elevate employee engagement and workplace satisfaction.

Lease Administration

Technology-enabled lease abstraction, compliance tracking, and critical date management for complex portfolios.

Portfolio Strategy

Holistic occupancy planning, scenario modeling, and cost optimization across multi-market portfolios.
