Design & Build

Turnkey development solutions from concept through occupancy

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate — reducing timelines and costs through integrated project delivery.

Built on expertise, delivered with precision

From ground-up developments to complex renovations, GlobalStone's Design & Build division brings together the world's best architects, engineers, and construction partners to deliver projects on time and on budget. Our integrated approach eliminates the friction between design intent and construction reality.

Design & Build Capabilities

Two integrated capabilities to bring your vision to life.

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our project managers serve as the single point of accountability, ensuring quality, schedule, and budget targets are met at every phase.

Supply Chain Sourcing

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines. Our global sourcing network and pre-qualified vendor database ensure the right materials and partners are in place before breaking ground.

Strategic Partnerships

Delivering better outcomes through world-class collaboration.

Integrated delivery, exceptional results

GlobalStone's Design & Build division partners with leading architecture, engineering, and construction firms worldwide to deliver turnkey solutions. Our integrated approach ensures seamless coordination from concept through occupancy, reducing timelines by an average of 18% and delivering cost savings of up to 12%.
