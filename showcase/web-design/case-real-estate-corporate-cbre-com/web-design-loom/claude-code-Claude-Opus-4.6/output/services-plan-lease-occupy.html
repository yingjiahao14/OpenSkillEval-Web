Plan, Lease & Occupy

As the #1 advisory and transactions firm in the world, GlobalStone offers unique insight, experience, and solutions to serve the needs of occupiers and owners.

Whether you're an occupier seeking the ideal workspace or an owner looking to maximize asset performance, our global platform delivers unmatched advisory, transaction, and workplace expertise. We combine local market knowledge with global reach to help you plan, lease, and occupy commercial real estate with confidence.
