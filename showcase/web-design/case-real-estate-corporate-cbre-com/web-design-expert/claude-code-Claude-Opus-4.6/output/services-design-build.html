Design & Build

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate.

End-to-End Delivery

From Concept to Completion

Our Design & Build division delivers turnkey real estate solutions that align with your strategic objectives. We manage the entire project lifecycle — from initial planning and design coordination through construction delivery and occupancy.

Design & Build Capabilities

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our project managers bring deep technical expertise and a relentless focus on schedule, budget, and quality to every engagement.

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines. We leverage global relationships and data-driven sourcing to secure optimal pricing, quality, and delivery performance.
