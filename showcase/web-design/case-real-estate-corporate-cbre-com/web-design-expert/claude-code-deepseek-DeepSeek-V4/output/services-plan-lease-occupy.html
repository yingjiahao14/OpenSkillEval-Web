Plan, Lease & Occupy

Advisory and transaction expertise for occupiers and owners

As the #1 advisory and transactions firm in the world, GlobalStone offers unique insight, experience, and solutions to serve the needs of occupiers and owners.

Connect with a Specialist

We execute over 45,000 lease transactions annually across 100+ countries, representing the world’s most dynamic companies. From single-site leases to global portfolio restructuring, our approach is grounded in data, market intelligence, and relentless advocacy for our clients.

Capabilities

End-to-end planning and leasing capabilities

Transaction Services

Tenant and landlord representation for office, industrial, retail, and specialty property leasing across global markets.

Site Selection & Location Strategy

Data-driven analysis combining labor analytics, incentives evaluation, and market benchmarking to optimize location decisions.

Workplace Solutions

Workplace strategy, change management, and space programming that align physical environments with workforce needs.

Experience Services

Hospitality-inspired facility experiences that elevate employee engagement and workplace satisfaction.

Lease Administration

Technology-enabled lease abstraction, compliance tracking, and critical date management for complex portfolios.

Portfolio Strategy

Holistic occupancy planning, scenario modeling, and cost optimization across multi-market portfolios.

Our Approach

Our integrated approach combines transaction expertise with deep analytics. We don’t just find space — we align real estate with talent strategy, operational efficiency, and brand experience.

Whether you’re expanding into new markets, consolidating your footprint, or transforming your workplace, our team of 4,000+ transaction professionals delivers measurable results.
