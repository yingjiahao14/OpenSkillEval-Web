Design & Build

From concept to completion, we deliver projects that perform

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate.

Start Your Project

Our Design & Build division combines strategic planning, project management, and supply chain expertise to deliver projects on time and on budget. With $12B+ in active projects globally, we bring the scale and precision that complex developments demand.

Capabilities

Comprehensive design and build capabilities

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our certified project managers bring deep sector expertise and rigorous methodology to every engagement, ensuring milestones are met and budgets are respected.

Supply Chain Sourcing

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines. We leverage global sourcing relationships and volume purchasing power to secure favorable terms across materials, equipment, and contractor services.

Strategic Partnership

Delivering turnkey solutions through world-class partnerships

GlobalStone’s Design & Build division partners with leading architecture, engineering, and construction firms worldwide to deliver turnkey solutions. Our integrated approach ensures seamless coordination from concept through occupancy, reducing timelines and delivering measurable cost savings.
