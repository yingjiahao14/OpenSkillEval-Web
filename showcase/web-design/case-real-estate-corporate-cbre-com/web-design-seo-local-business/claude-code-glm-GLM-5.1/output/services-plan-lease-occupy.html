As the #1 advisory and transactions firm in the world, GlobalStone offers unique insight, experience, and solutions to serve the needs of occupiers and owners across every market and sector.

GlobalStone brings unmatched planning and leasing expertise to every engagement, combining deep market intelligence with a global platform to help organizations and investors make real estate decisions that drive competitive advantage and long-term value.

What We Do

Tailored representation and consulting to achieve the best outcomes for tenants, landlords, and organizations.

Advocating for Tenants, Everywhere

GlobalStone's tenant representation professionals serve as your exclusive advocate in every aspect of the leasing process. From space identification and market analysis to lease negotiation and build-out coordination, we leverage data-driven insights and unparalleled market access to secure optimal terms and spaces that align with your business strategy. Our teams operate across every major market, giving you a consistent, trusted advisor wherever your footprint extends.
