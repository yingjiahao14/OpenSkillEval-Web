Design & Build

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate that drives performance and creates lasting value.

GlobalStone's Design & Build capabilities bring together world-class project management, strategic sourcing, and integrated delivery to transform real estate concepts into high-performing assets. Our team oversees every phase — from initial feasibility and design coordination through construction, fit-out, and final handover — ensuring quality, cost certainty, and on-time delivery at every stage.

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our dedicated project managers align stakeholders, control budgets, mitigate risks, and ensure every milestone is met on schedule and to the highest standards of quality.

Supply Chain Sourcing

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines. Leveraging global supplier relationships and data-driven market intelligence, we source materials, systems, and services at optimal value while maintaining rigorous quality and compliance standards.

Strategic Partnerships

Delivering Turnkey Solutions Through Integrated Partnerships

GlobalStone's Design & Build division partners with leading architecture, engineering, and construction firms worldwide to deliver turnkey solutions. Our integrated approach ensures seamless coordination from concept through occupancy, reducing timelines by an average of 18% and delivering cost savings of up to 12%.
