Design & Build

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate that performs.

From concept to completion — we deliver

Our Design & Build practice brings together project management excellence and strategic supply chain sourcing to deliver capital projects on time, on budget, and to the highest standards of quality. Whether you are building a flagship headquarters, renovating a multi-site portfolio, or developing a ground-up asset, our integrated approach eliminates friction at every stage.

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our project managers bring deep technical expertise, rigorous budget controls, and proactive risk management to every engagement — ensuring your capital projects meet schedule, cost, and quality targets.

Our team has managed over $12 billion in capital projects across 40 countries, delivering office towers, industrial facilities, data centers, and mixed-use developments for the world's most demanding occupiers and investors.

Supply Chain Sourcing

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines. We leverage GlobalStone's global purchasing power and deep supplier relationships to secure favorable pricing, ensure material availability, and maintain quality standards across every project.

Our supply chain platform provides real-time visibility into procurement status, material lead times, and vendor performance — giving you confidence that your project stays on track from design through delivery.

Strategic Partnership

A global network of design and construction excellence

Integrated delivery through world-class partnerships

GlobalStone's Design & Build division partners with leading architecture, engineering, and construction firms worldwide to deliver turnkey solutions. Our integrated approach ensures seamless coordination from concept through occupancy, reducing timelines by an average of 18% and delivering cost savings of up to 12%.

Through these strategic alliances, we combine GlobalStone's real estate strategy and project management expertise with the specialized capabilities of the world's most respected design and construction firms. The result: a single point of accountability, streamlined decision-making, and consistently exceptional outcomes.
