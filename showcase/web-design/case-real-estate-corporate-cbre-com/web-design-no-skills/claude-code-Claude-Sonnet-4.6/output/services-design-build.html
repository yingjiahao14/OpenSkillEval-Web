Design & Build

From Concept to Occupancy — Delivered With Precision

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate — integrating architecture, engineering, and construction expertise under one accountable platform.

Explore Capabilities

Overview

Integrated Project Delivery for Every Scale and Sector

GlobalStone's Design & Build practice delivers end-to-end project management and construction services that transform real estate visions into fully realized, high-performing spaces. Our integrated approach coordinates every stakeholder — architects, engineers, contractors, and vendors — to ensure projects are delivered on time, on budget, and to specification.

From a single workplace fit-out to a multi-country campus development, our experienced teams apply disciplined program management and strategic procurement to maximize value at every stage.

Our Capabilities

01

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our project managers serve as the single point of accountability — managing risk, schedule, budget, and quality across every phase of the project lifecycle.

We deploy proven methodologies and integrated technology platforms to ensure real-time visibility into project status, cost performance, and schedule adherence for every stakeholder.

02

Supply Chain Sourcing

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines. GlobalStone's global supply chain network provides access to preferred pricing, pre-qualified contractors, and expedited material sourcing across 100+ markets.

Strategy & Feasibility
