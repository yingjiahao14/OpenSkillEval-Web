Project Delivery

Design & Build

GlobalStone examines every aspect of your business or investment strategy to plan, design, develop, and deliver real estate — from concept through occupancy, with an integrated approach that reduces timelines and delivers measurable cost savings.

Our Approach

Integrated Project Delivery

Our Design & Build division brings together architecture, engineering, procurement, and construction management under one accountable platform. By integrating these functions, we eliminate friction, reduce risk, and deliver superior outcomes — consistently achieving project completions that come in on time and within budget.

Core Capabilities

What We Deliver

01

Project & Program Management

End-to-end oversight of construction and renovation projects, from feasibility and design coordination through delivery and closeout. Our project managers bring deep technical expertise and a systematic approach to every engagement.

  • Feasibility studies and investment case development
  • Design coordination and quality control
  • Construction management and site supervision
  • Cost management and value engineering
  • Risk mitigation and schedule optimization
  • Handover, closeout, and post-occupancy evaluation
02

Supply Chain Sourcing

Strategic procurement and vendor management that reduce costs, mitigate risk, and accelerate project timelines. Our global sourcing network and scale give clients access to best-in-class contractors, materials, and equipment at competitive pricing.

  • Global contractor and vendor prequalification
  • Competitive tender management and evaluation
  • Long-lead item procurement and logistics
  • Sustainable materials sourcing and specification
  • Contract negotiation and administration
  • Vendor performance monitoring and audit

How We Work

Our Delivery Process

01

Strategy & Feasibility

Define objectives, assess options, and develop the investment case that underpins every project decision.

02

Design & Planning

Coordinate architecture, engineering, and planning to develop a design that meets your brief, budget, and regulatory requirements.

03

Procurement & Build

Source the right contractors and materials at the right price, then manage construction to deliver on time and on budget.

04

Handover & Occupy

Complete final inspections, coordinate fit-out, and ensure a smooth transition to occupancy with full documentation and warranties.
