Occupier & Owner Services

Plan, Lease & Occupy

As the #1 advisory and transactions firm in the world, GlobalStone offers unique insight, experience, and solutions to serve the needs of occupiers and owners across every market and property type.

Our Approach

Unmatched Occupier Advisory Expertise

Whether you're a global enterprise rationalizing a multi-market portfolio or a growing company securing your first office, GlobalStone brings the market intelligence, transaction expertise, and strategic counsel to deliver optimal outcomes. Our occupier advisory practice spans every geography and every property type — giving you a single, accountable partner for your entire real estate program.

What We Do

Planning & Leasing Capabilities

Transaction Services

Tenant and landlord representation for office, industrial, retail, and specialty property leasing across global markets. Our transactional expertise and market access deliver superior lease terms and economics.

Site Selection & Location Strategy

Data-driven analysis combining labor analytics, incentives evaluation, and market benchmarking to optimize location decisions. Our proprietary models identify optimal sites aligned with your talent, cost, and growth objectives.

Workplace Solutions

Workplace strategy, change management, and space programming that align physical environments with workforce needs. We help organizations create spaces that attract talent, foster collaboration, and drive productivity.

Experience Services

Hospitality-inspired facility experiences that elevate employee engagement and workplace satisfaction. We design and manage amenity programs, concierge services, and workplace experiences that set your environment apart.

Lease Administration

Technology-enabled lease abstraction, compliance tracking, and critical date management for complex portfolios. Our platform eliminates risk, reduces costs, and provides complete visibility across your entire lease estate.

Portfolio Strategy

Holistic occupancy planning, scenario modeling, and cost optimization across multi-market portfolios. We align your real estate footprint with your business strategy, identifying opportunities to reduce costs and enhance flexibility.
