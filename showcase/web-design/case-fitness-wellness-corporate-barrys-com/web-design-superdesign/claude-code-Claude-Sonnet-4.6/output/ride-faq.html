Frequently Asked
Questions

RIDE x LIFT is RedRoom's signature cycling class that combines a high-energy spin session with strength training on the floor. It's 50 minutes of total-body work set to motivating playlists in our red-lit studio.
Wear moisture-wicking athletic clothing and supportive sneakers. Cycling shoes are available at the studio but not required. We recommend form-fitting clothing to ensure safety on the bike and comfort during floor work.
No. We provide complimentary cycling shoes at every RIDE x LIFT studio. You're welcome to bring your own SPD-compatible shoes if you prefer. Our team will help you get set up and clipped in before class.
Each RIDE x LIFT class is 50 minutes — approximately 25 minutes on the bike and 25 minutes of strength work on the floor. We recommend arriving 10 minutes early to set up your bike and get acquainted with the space.
Absolutely. Our instructors coach every level. You control your resistance and speed on the bike, so the workout scales to your fitness level. Our coaching staff is trained to offer modifications during the floor section as well. First-timers are always welcome.
RIDE x LIFT is currently available at our Chelsea, NYC studio with plans to expand to additional locations. Stay tuned to our newsletter and social channels for announcements about new cities coming soon.
