RedRoom RIDE FAQ

Everything you need to know about our signature cycling + strength experience.

RIDE x LIFT is RedRoom's signature cycling class that combines a high-energy spin session with strength training on the floor. It's 50 minutes of total-body work set to motivating playlists in our red-lit studio.

Wear moisture-wicking athletic clothing and supportive sneakers. Cycling shoes are available at the studio but not required.

No. We provide complimentary cycling shoes at every RIDE x LIFT studio. You're welcome to bring your own SPD-compatible shoes if you prefer.

Each RIDE x LIFT class is 50 minutes — approximately 25 minutes on the bike and 25 minutes of strength work on the floor.

Absolutely. Our instructors coach every level. You control your resistance and speed, so the workout scales to your fitness level.

RIDE x LIFT is currently available at our Chelsea, NYC studio with plans to expand to additional locations.
