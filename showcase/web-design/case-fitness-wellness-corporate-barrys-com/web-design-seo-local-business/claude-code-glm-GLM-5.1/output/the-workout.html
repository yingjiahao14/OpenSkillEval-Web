THE REDROOM WAY

Every class is a 50-minute full-body experience. You'll alternate between the treadmill and the floor, combining high-intensity cardio intervals with focused strength training. START ON THE FLOOR OR THE TREADMILL

Choose your starting station — every class alternates between both.

The Floor

Dumbbells, resistance bands, and bodyweight movements target specific muscle groups each day. Our instructors guide every rep to ensure proper form and maximum results. Whether it's chest and back, arms and abs, or a full-body burn, the floor portion is where you build the strength that transforms your physique.

The Treadmill

From power walks to all-out sprints, the treadmill portion is designed to torch calories and build cardiovascular endurance. Every speed and incline is coach-led — all levels welcome. You control your pace, but our instructors push you past what you thought possible.
