THE REDROOM WAY

Hustle and heart set us apart.

Every class is a 50-minute full-body experience.

You'll alternate between the treadmill and the floor, combining high-intensity cardio intervals with focused strength training. Our method is designed to maximize calorie burn, build lean muscle, and keep your metabolism elevated for hours after class.

Start on the Floor or the Treadmill

Dumbbells, resistance bands, and bodyweight movements target specific muscle groups each day. Our instructors guide every rep to ensure proper form and maximum results.
