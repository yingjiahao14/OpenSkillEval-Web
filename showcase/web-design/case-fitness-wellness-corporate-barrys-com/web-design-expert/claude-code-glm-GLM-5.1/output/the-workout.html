Our method is designed to maximize calorie burn, build lean muscle, and keep your metabolism elevated for hours after class. Hustle and heart set us apart. Start on the Floor or the Treadmill

Floor Training

Dumbbells, resistance bands, and bodyweight movements target specific muscle groups each day. Our instructors guide every rep to ensure proper form and maximum results. Whether you're building strength or toning up, the floor portion is where the real work happens.

Treadmill Training

From power walks to all-out sprints, the treadmill portion is designed to torch calories and build cardiovascular endurance. Every speed and incline is coach-led — all levels welcome. Push your limits with intervals that keep your heart rate in the zone.
