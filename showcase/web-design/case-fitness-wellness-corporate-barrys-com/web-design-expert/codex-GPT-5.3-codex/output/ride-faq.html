Ride FAQ

Everything About RIDE x LIFT

Answers to the most common questions before your first class.

RIDE x LIFT is a hybrid class that combines rhythm-based cycling intervals with off-bike strength training in one session.

Wear breathable training clothes and supportive athletic shoes. Bring a towel and water bottle.

Cycling shoes are optional at most locations. Standard cross-training shoes are accepted for the full session.

The class runs approximately 45 minutes with brief transitions between cycling and floor blocks.
