THE REDROOM WAY

Every class is a 50-minute full-body experience. You'll alternate between the treadmill and the floor, combining high-intensity cardio intervals with focused strength training. Our method is designed to maximize calorie burn, build lean muscle, and keep your metabolism elevated for hours after class. Hustle and heart set us apart.

WORKOUT

Start on the Floor or the Treadmill

Choose your entry point — the workout flows both ways

The Floor Workout

Dumbbells, resistance bands, and bodyweight movements target specific muscle groups each day. Our instructors guide every rep to ensure proper form and maximum results.

The Treadmill Workout

From power walks to all-out sprints, the treadmill portion is designed to torch calories and build cardiovascular endurance. Every speed and incline is coach-led — all levels welcome.
