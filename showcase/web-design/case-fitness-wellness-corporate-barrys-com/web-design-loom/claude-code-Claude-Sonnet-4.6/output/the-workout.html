The Method

Our method is designed to maximize calorie burn, build lean muscle, and keep your metabolism elevated for hours after class. Hustle and heart set us apart.

The Two Stations

Start on the Floor or the Treadmill

🏋️

The Floor

Dumbbells, resistance bands, and bodyweight movements target specific muscle groups each day. Our instructors guide every rep to ensure proper form and maximum results.

  • Dumbbell strength work targeting specific muscle groups
  • Resistance band exercises for activation and endurance
  • Bodyweight movements for core and functional fitness
  • Instructor-guided form coaching every rep
  • Scalable to all fitness levels
🏃

The Treadmill

From power walks to all-out sprints, the treadmill portion is designed to torch calories and build cardiovascular endurance. Every speed and incline is coach-led — all levels welcome.

  • Coach-led speed and incline programming
  • Intervals from power walking to full sprints
  • Cardiovascular endurance and calorie torching
  • HIIT intervals proven to elevate metabolism
  • All levels and paces welcome
