Ride FAQ

Ride x Lift combines high-energy cycling intervals with off-bike strength training for a complete cardio and power session.

Wear breathable, fitted workout gear and supportive athletic shoes suitable for both cycling and floor training.

Optional. Most studios provide dual-sided pedals compatible with athletic sneakers and clip-in shoes.

Most Ride x Lift classes run approximately 50 minutes, including warm-up and guided recovery.
