Start on the Floor or the Treadmill

Floor

Dumbbells, resistance bands, and bodyweight movements target specific muscle groups each day. Our instructors guide every rep to ensure proper form and maximum results. Each floor block is designed to complement the day's muscle group focus, building strength and definition where it counts.

Treadmill

From power walks to all-out sprints, the treadmill portion is designed to torch calories and build cardiovascular endurance. Every speed and incline is coach-led — all levels welcome. Whether you're a first-timer or a seasoned runner, our instructors cue every interval so you can work at your own pace while staying challenged.
