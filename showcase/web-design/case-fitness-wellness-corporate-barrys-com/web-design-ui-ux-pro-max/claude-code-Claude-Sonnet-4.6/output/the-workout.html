Choose Your Station

Start on the Floor or the Treadmill

Each class splits time between both stations — here's what to expect.

Floor Training

Dumbbells, resistance bands, and bodyweight movements target specific muscle groups each day. Our instructors guide every rep to ensure proper form and maximum results.

  • Targeted muscle group work
  • Dumbbell & resistance band exercises
  • Bodyweight movement patterns
  • Instructor-guided form coaching
  • 6 unique muscle-group focuses per week

Treadmill

From power walks to all-out sprints, the treadmill portion is designed to torch calories and build cardiovascular endurance. Every speed and incline is coach-led — all levels welcome.

  • Power walks to full sprints
  • Variable incline intervals
  • Coach-directed speed cues
  • Maximum calorie burn protocol
  • All fitness levels accommodated
