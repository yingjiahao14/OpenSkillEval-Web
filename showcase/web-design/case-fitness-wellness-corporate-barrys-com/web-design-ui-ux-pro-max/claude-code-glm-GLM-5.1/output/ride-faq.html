RIDE x LIFT FAQ

Got questions about our signature cycling class? We've got answers.

RIDE x LIFT is RedRoom's signature cycling class that combines a high-energy spin session with strength training on the floor. It's 50 minutes of total-body work set to motivating playlists in our red-lit studio.
Wear moisture-wicking athletic clothing and supportive sneakers. Cycling shoes are available at the studio but not required.

Each RIDE x LIFT class is 50 minutes — approximately 25 minutes on the bike and 25 minutes of strength work on the floor.
