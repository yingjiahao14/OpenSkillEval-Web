Start on the Floor or the Treadmill

Choose where you begin — every class cycles through both.

Strength on the Floor

Dumbbells, resistance bands, and bodyweight movements target specific muscle groups each day. Our instructors guide every rep to ensure proper form and maximum results. Expect functional movements that translate to real-world strength — from weighted squats and deadlifts to core-blasting planks and rotational work.

Cardio on the Treadmill

From power walks to all-out sprints, the treadmill portion is designed to torch calories and build cardiovascular endurance. Every speed and incline is coach-led — all levels welcome. You control the pace, but our instructors push you to find your edge. Expect intervals, hills, and a playlist that demands your best.
