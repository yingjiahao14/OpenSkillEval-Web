## RIDE FAQ Content

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is RIDE x LIFT? | RIDE x LIFT is RedRoom's signature cycling class that combines a high-energy spin session with strength training on the floor. It's 50 minutes of total-body work set to motivating playlists in our red-lit studio. |
| What should I wear? | Wear moisture-wicking athletic clothing and supportive sneakers. Cycling shoes are available at the studio but not required. |
| Do I need cycling shoes? | No. We provide complimentary cycling shoes at every RIDE x LIFT studio. You're welcome to bring your own SPD-compatible shoes if you prefer. |
| How long is the class? | Each RIDE x LIFT class is 50 minutes — approximately 25 minutes on the bike and 25 minutes of strength work on the floor. |
| Is RIDE x LIFT good for beginners? | Absolutely. Our instructors coach every level. You control your resistance and speed, so the workout scales to your fitness level. |
| Where is RIDE x LIFT available? | RIDE x LIFT is currently available at our Chelsea, NYC studio with plans to expand to additional locations. |
