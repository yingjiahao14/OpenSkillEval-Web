Workout Format

Floor / Treadmill Breakdown

Floor Training

Expect dumbbells, bodyweight, and controlled tempo sets for strength and muscular endurance. Coaches layer regressions and progressions so every athlete works at the right intensity.

Treadmill Intervals

Alternating pushes, base pace, and all-outs build your aerobic engine quickly. Structured interval timing keeps effort high while maintaining safe, repeatable progress.
