The Workout

50 minutes. Full body. Every class designed to push you past your limits.

THE REDROOM WAY

Every class is a 50-minute full-body experience. You'll alternate between the treadmill and the floor, combining high-intensity cardio intervals with focused strength training. Our method is designed to maximize calorie burn, build lean muscle, and keep your metabolism elevated for hours after class. Hustle and heart set us apart.

Start on the Floor or the Treadmill

Floor Workouts

Dumbbells, resistance bands, and bodyweight movements target specific muscle groups each day. Our instructors guide every rep to ensure proper form and maximum results. Whether you're building strength or sculpting muscle, the floor is where the real work happens.

Treadmill Workouts

From power walks to all-out sprints, the treadmill portion is designed to torch calories and build cardiovascular endurance. Every speed and incline is coach-led — all levels welcome. Push your pace, beat your records, and feel the burn.
