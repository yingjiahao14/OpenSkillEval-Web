Floor Training

Dumbbells, resistance bands, and bodyweight movements target specific muscle groups each day. Our instructors guide every rep to ensure proper form and maximum results.

  • Targeted muscle group work every session
  • Dumbbells and resistance bands provided
  • Coach-led form guidance throughout
  • Scales to all fitness levels

Why It Works

Floor training builds lean muscle while improving functional strength. Our structured programming ensures progressive overload across the weekly schedule.

Treadmill Training

From power walks to all-out sprints, the treadmill portion is designed to torch calories and build cardiovascular endurance. Every speed and incline is coach-led — all levels welcome.

  • Coach-called speed and incline intervals
  • Power walks to all-out sprints
  • Builds cardiovascular endurance fast
  • All pace levels welcomed

Why It Works

High-intensity treadmill intervals maximize calorie burn during and after class, keeping your metabolism elevated for up to 36 hours post-workout.
