## Feature Tabs
Tab navigation options within the search feature section:
- **Screens** — Browse full-screen captures of real apps
- **UI Elements** — Explore individual components and patterns
- **Flows** — View connected sequences of screens
- **Text in Screenshot** — Search by visible on-screen copy

Clicking a tab switches the content area to show the corresponding search results view.

## Feature: Search
**Find design patterns in seconds.**
The search feature section combines the tab navigation (Screens, UI Elements, Flows, Text in Screenshot) with filter pills to let users quickly narrow down relevant design references from the library. The grid of screenshot results updates dynamically based on the active tab and selected filter category.

## Feature: Flows
**Explore entire user journeys with flows.**

- **Videos** — Experience flows the way they were meant to be experienced, complete with transitions, micro-interactions, and animations.
- **Prototype mode** — Walk through flows, step by step, by using the interactive hotspots at your own preferred pace.

A toggle switches between the Videos view and the Prototype mode view.
