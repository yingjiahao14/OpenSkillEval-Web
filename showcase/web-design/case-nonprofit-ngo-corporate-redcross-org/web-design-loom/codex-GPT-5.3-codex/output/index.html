When Disaster Strikes, We're There

From hurricanes and wildfires to global health emergencies, Global Aid Alliance delivers immediate relief and long-term recovery support to communities in crisis.

Our Mission

Global Aid Alliance prevents and alleviates human suffering in the face of emergencies by mobilizing the power of volunteers and the generosity of donors. Founded in 1947, we respond swiftly to disasters, strengthen vulnerable communities, and build long-term resilience through trusted local partnerships.

Programs & Services

Emergency shelter, food, water, and immediate support for communities impacted by hurricanes, floods, wildfires, and earthquakes.

Nationwide blood drives and partnerships with medical centers to keep lifesaving blood supplies available during routine and crisis periods.

Community workshops and school programs that help families prepare, respond, and recover from emergencies before disaster strikes.

Cross-border response operations supporting displaced populations with healthcare, sanitation, shelter, and long-term recovery tools.

Local services addressing public health access, chronic emergency needs, and resilient community care systems in high-risk regions.
