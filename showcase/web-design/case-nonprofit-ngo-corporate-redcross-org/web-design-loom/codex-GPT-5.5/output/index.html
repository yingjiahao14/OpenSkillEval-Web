Our Mission

Prevent and alleviate human suffering in the face of emergencies. Powered by volunteers, donors, and trusted partners worldwide.

Hope grounded in action

Global Aid Alliance prevents and alleviates human suffering in the face of emergencies by mobilizing the power of volunteers and the generosity of donors.

We are guided by the fundamental principles of humanity, impartiality, and neutrality. From the first hours of a disaster to the years of recovery that follow, we stand with affected communities every step of the way.
