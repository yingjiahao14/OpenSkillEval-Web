Our Mission

Humanity. Impartiality. Neutrality.

Global Aid Alliance prevents and alleviates human suffering in the face of emergencies by mobilizing the power of volunteers and the generosity of donors.

We are guided by the fundamental principles of humanity, impartiality, and neutrality. Every day, our teams work across borders and boundaries to ensure that help reaches those who need it most — regardless of race, religion, nationality, or political affiliation.

From the first hours of a disaster to the years of recovery that follow, we stand with affected communities every step of the way.

Programs & Services

How We Serve Communities

From rapid emergency response to long-term community health, our programs address every phase of disaster and recovery.

Rapid deployment of emergency shelter, food, water, and medical supplies to communities affected by natural disasters and conflict. Our response teams are on the ground within 24 hours of a disaster declaration.

Learn about disaster relief

Operating one of the largest blood collection networks, we supply approximately 40% of the nation's blood and blood products to hospitals and medical facilities across the country.

Find a blood drive near you

Training individuals, families, and organizations to prepare for and respond to emergencies through workshops, certification courses, and community drills that build lasting resilience.

Explore preparedness training

Partnering with a global network of humanitarian organizations to deliver relief and recovery programs in conflict zones and disaster-affected regions worldwide across 190 countries.

View international programs

Providing health and safety education, disease prevention programs, and support services to underserved communities, including mental health resources for disaster survivors.
