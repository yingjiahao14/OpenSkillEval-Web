Our Mission

Driven by Humanity, Guided by Principle

Global Aid Alliance prevents and alleviates human suffering in the face of emergencies by mobilizing the power of volunteers and the generosity of donors.

We are guided by the fundamental principles of humanity, impartiality, and neutrality. Every day, our teams work across borders and boundaries to ensure that help reaches those who need it most — regardless of race, religion, nationality, or political affiliation.

From the first hours of a disaster to the years of recovery that follow, we stand with affected communities every step of the way.

What We Do

Programs and Services

From immediate disaster response to long-term community health, our programs span the full cycle of emergency preparedness, relief, and recovery.

Rapid deployment of emergency shelter, food, water, and medical supplies to communities affected by natural disasters and conflict. Our response teams are on the ground within 24 hours. Learn more
Operating one of the largest blood collection networks, we supply approximately 40% of the nation's blood and blood products to hospitals and medical facilities. Learn more
Training individuals, families, and organizations to prepare for and respond to emergencies through workshops, certification courses, and community drills. Learn more
Partnering with a global network of humanitarian organizations to deliver relief and recovery programs in conflict zones and disaster-affected regions worldwide. Learn more
Providing health and safety education, disease prevention programs, and support services to underserved communities, including mental health resources for disaster survivors. Learn more
