Our Mission

Preventing & alleviating human suffering in the face of emergencies

Global Aid Alliance prevents and alleviates human suffering in the face of emergencies by mobilizing the power of volunteers and the generosity of donors.


We are guided by the fundamental principles of humanity, impartiality, and neutrality. Every day, our teams work across borders and boundaries to ensure that help reaches those who need it most — regardless of race, religion, nationality, or political affiliation. From the first hours of a disaster to the years of recovery that follow, we stand with affected communities every step of the way.

Programs & Services

How We Help

Rapid deployment of emergency shelter, food, water, and medical supplies to communities affected by natural disasters and conflict. Our response teams are on the ground within 24 hours. Learn about our disaster response
Operating one of the largest blood collection networks, we supply approximately 40% of the nation's blood and blood products to hospitals and medical facilities. Find a blood drive near you
Training individuals, families, and organizations to prepare for and respond to emergencies through workshops, certification courses, and community drills. Explore preparedness resources
Partnering with a global network of humanitarian organizations to deliver relief and recovery programs in conflict zones and disaster-affected regions worldwide. View international programs
Providing health and safety education, disease prevention programs, and support services to underserved communities, including mental health resources for disaster survivors. Learn about community health

Hurricane

Hurricane Response Update

Global Aid Alliance teams are providing shelter and supplies to over 15,000 families displaced by Hurricane Marlena along the Gulf Coast. Relief operations are ongoing across 12 counties.

Wildfire

Wildfire Relief Efforts

Emergency shelters have been opened in three western states as crews battle record-breaking wildfires. Volunteers are distributing meals, water, and comfort kits to evacuees.
