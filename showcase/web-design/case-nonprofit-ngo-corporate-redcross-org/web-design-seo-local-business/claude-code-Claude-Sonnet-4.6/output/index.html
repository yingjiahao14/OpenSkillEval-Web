Our Mission

Preventing and Alleviating Human Suffering

Global Aid Alliance prevents and alleviates human suffering in the face of emergencies by mobilizing the power of volunteers and the generosity of donors.

We are guided by the fundamental principles of humanity, impartiality, and neutrality. Every day, our teams work across borders and boundaries to ensure that help reaches those who need it most — regardless of race, religion, nationality, or political affiliation.

From the first hours of a disaster to the years of recovery that follow, we stand with affected communities every step of the way.

What We Do

Programs & Services

From rapid disaster response to long-term community health, our programs reach people in their most vulnerable moments.

Rapid deployment of emergency shelter, food, water, and medical supplies to communities affected by natural disasters and conflict. Our response teams are on the ground within 24 hours, providing critical support when communities need it most. Get involved →
Operating one of the largest blood collection networks in the nation, we supply approximately 40% of the country's blood and blood products to hospitals and medical facilities. Every two seconds, someone needs blood. Find a blood drive →
Training individuals, families, and organizations to prepare for and respond to emergencies through workshops, certification courses, and community drills. Knowledge saves lives before disaster strikes. Learn more →
Partnering with a global network of humanitarian organizations to deliver relief and recovery programs in conflict zones and disaster-affected regions worldwide. Aid reaches 190 countries with no political agenda. Partner with us →
Providing health and safety education, disease prevention programs, and support services to underserved communities — including mental health resources for disaster survivors who need long-term support. Get support →
