Our Mission

Guided by Humanity,
Driven by Action

Global Aid Alliance prevents and alleviates human suffering in the face of emergencies by mobilizing the power of volunteers and the generosity of donors.

We are guided by the fundamental principles of humanity, impartiality, and neutrality. Every day, our teams work across borders and boundaries to ensure that help reaches those who need it most — regardless of race, religion, nationality, or political affiliation.

From the first hours of a disaster to the years of recovery that follow, we stand with affected communities every step of the way.

Programs & Services

How We Help

Five core program areas that deliver critical aid when and where it's needed most.

Rapid deployment of emergency shelter, food, water, and medical supplies to communities affected by natural disasters and conflict. Our response teams are on the ground within 24 hours of a disaster declaration.

Learn more about Disaster Relief →

Operating one of the largest blood collection networks in the nation, we supply approximately 40% of the country's blood and blood products to hospitals and medical facilities nationwide.

Find a blood drive near you →

Training individuals, families, and organizations to prepare for and respond to emergencies through workshops, certification courses, and community drills. Be ready before disaster strikes.

View preparedness resources →

Partnering with a global network of humanitarian organizations to deliver relief and recovery programs in conflict zones and disaster-affected regions across 190 countries worldwide.

Explore international programs →

Providing health and safety education, disease prevention programs, and support services to underserved communities, including mental health resources for disaster survivors and their families.

Explore community health →
