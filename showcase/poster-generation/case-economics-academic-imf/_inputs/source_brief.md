# Global Economy in the Shadow of War: IMF World Economic Outlook Trends 2024–2026

## Overview

The IMF's World Economic Outlook (WEO) is the institution's flagship survey of global economic prospects and policies, published semi-annually with interim updates. Spanning eight editions from July 2024 through April 2026, the WEO paints a picture of an increasingly fragile global economy navigating geopolitical conflict, trade policy upheaval, and stubborn inflation. Growth projections have remained in a narrow but underwhelming band, while the balance of risks has been consistently tilted to the downside.

## Dominant Downside Risks

Across all eight editions, the IMF consistently flagged the balance of risks as tilted to the downside. Key recurring threats include:

- **Trade policy escalation**: Tariff increases and retaliatory measures, especially from 2025 onward, with front-loading of activity ahead of expected tariffs temporarily masking damage.
- **Geopolitical tensions**: Conflicts in the Middle East, broader security concerns, and the war referenced in the April 2026 title.
- **Technology re-evaluation**: Risk that elevated expectations around AI and tech investment could face a correction.
- **Persistent services inflation**: Particularly in the United States, where inflation is projected to return to target more gradually than in other advanced economies.

## Inflation and Monetary Policy Dynamics

Global inflation has been on a declining trajectory, enabling central banks to begin easing monetary policy—what the October 2024 WEO termed the "policy pivot." However, the path is uneven:

- Global headline inflation is expected to continue falling through 2026.
- US inflation is projected to remain above target longer than in peer economies.
- Services inflation has been particularly sticky, slowing the disinflation process.
- Renewed inflationary pressures emerged by April 2026, linked to defense spending and supply disruptions.

## Evolving Policy Recommendations

IMF policy guidance has shifted meaningfully over the period:

| Period | Primary Policy Focus |
|---|---|
| Mid-2024 | Manage disinflation; begin monetary easing carefully |
| Late 2024 | Pivot monetary policy; ensure fiscal sustainability |
| Early 2025 | Rebuild fiscal buffers; address demographic and gender gaps |
| Mid-2025 | Restore confidence and predictability amid tariff uncertainty |
| Late 2025 | Strengthen policy frameworks; cautious industrial policy |
| Early 2026 | Agile policy; manage defense-spending trade-offs; lay foundations for recovery |

## Special Analytical Themes

Each WEO edition features deep-dive analytical chapters. Notable themes across this period include the role of monetary policy in global disinflation, the social acceptability of structural reforms, the effectiveness and fiscal cost of industrial policy, and policies to address aging populations, gender disparities, and migrant skill alignment. These themes reflect the IMF's broadening analytical lens beyond traditional macroeconomic stabilization toward inclusive and sustainable growth strategies.
