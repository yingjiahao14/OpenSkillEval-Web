# Instruction: Samsung Galaxy Ecosystem 2025 Product Poster

## Overview

Create a bold, modern promotional poster showcasing Samsung's complete 2025 Galaxy ecosystem. This is a marketing campaign asset aimed at consumers and retail audiences — it should feel premium, tech-forward, and aspirational. The key message: Samsung's Galaxy lineup spans every product category, unified by AI innovation, with two exciting new flagship launches leading the charge.

## Key Priorities

- **The two flagship launches — Galaxy S26 Ultra and Galaxy Buds4 Pro — should be the visual heroes.** Give them dominant placement and visual weight so they immediately draw the eye.
- **Ecosystem breadth matters.** The poster must convey that Samsung covers 7+ product categories. The category highlights table should be scannable at a glance — treat it as a key information anchor, not an afterthought.
- **AI as the unifying thread.** Weave the "Galaxy AI" and "Bespoke AI" branding throughout so viewers understand AI isn't a feature — it's the foundation of the entire 2025 lineup.

## Visual & Style Guidance

- **Color palette:** Deep navy or black backgrounds with Samsung's signature blue accents. Use white and cool silver for text and highlights. Occasional warm accent (gold or copper) to denote premium flagship products.
- **Mood:** Sleek, confident, futuristic. Think flagship product launch event energy — clean lines, generous whitespace, and sharp typography.
- **Layout feel:** Portrait orientation with a clear visual hierarchy — hero product zone at top, ecosystem overview in the middle, and the quick-reference category table toward the bottom. Metrics (7+ categories, 14 products, etc.) should appear as bold callout badges or stat blocks that punctuate the design.
- **Typography:** Strong sans-serif headings, modern and geometric. Keep body text minimal — let numbers and product names do the talking.
