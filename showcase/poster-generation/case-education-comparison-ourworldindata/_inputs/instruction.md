# Instruction: The State of Global Education — Progress, Gaps, and Inequality

## Overview

Create an A1 portrait poster for public exhibition that tells a compelling data story about global education. The narrative arc is one of **dramatic progress followed by a sobering reality check**: humanity has gone from fewer than 1-in-5 adults being educated to more than 4-in-5 — yet 244 million children remain out of school, and in the poorest countries, children receive fewer than 3 years of quality schooling compared to 10+ in wealthy nations. The audience is the general public, so clarity and visual impact matter more than academic detail.

## Key Priorities

- **The "progress vs. gaps" contrast is the heart of the poster.** The historical transformation (1800 → today) should feel triumphant, but the learning crisis and out-of-school figures must land with equal weight — don't let one overshadow the other.
- **Make the 244 million children stat visually dominant.** This is the number viewers should remember after walking away.
- **The comparison between quality schooling in the poorest vs. richest countries (< 3 years vs. 10+ years)** should be immediately graspable — consider a bold side-by-side or bar contrast.

## Visual & Style Guidance

Adopt a **data-forward yet engaging** tone. Use a clean, modern layout with generous whitespace. Think deep navy or charcoal backgrounds with warm accent colors (amber, coral) to highlight key figures. Typography should be bold and hierarchical — large numerals for headline stats, concise labels beneath. Infographic elements (icon arrays, simple bar comparisons) are welcome; avoid clutter. The overall mood should feel serious and authoritative but accessible — not academic, not flashy.
