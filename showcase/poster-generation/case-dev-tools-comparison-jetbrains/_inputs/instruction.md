## Poster Brief: State of Developer Ecosystem 2024

You're creating a data-forward infographic poster (A1 portrait) that maps the shifting landscape of programming languages, databases, and cloud platforms heading into 2025. The source is a large-scale survey of **23,262 developers worldwide**, and the audience is technical — think conference hallway or tech-blog embed. They should walk away understanding one thing: **the hierarchy of programming languages is actively reshaping**, with Python closing in on JavaScript and TypeScript surging as the breakout story.

### Key Priorities

- **The language trajectory data is the centerpiece.** The 8-year arc from 2017–2024 — JavaScript's plateau at 61%, Python's climb to 57%, and TypeScript's explosive 12→37% growth — should be the most visually dominant element on the poster.
- **The "Promise Index" concept deserves a callout moment** — TypeScript at 199 signals where the ecosystem is heading, not just where it's been.
- Give the AI integration stat (18% of developers) a distinct but secondary spotlight; it's the forward-looking hook.

### Visual & Style Guidance

Lean into a **dark-mode, developer-aesthetic palette** — deep navy or charcoal backgrounds with vibrant accent colors (electric blue, amber, mint green) for data highlights. Typography should feel monospace-adjacent for headers but clean sans-serif for body text. Think IDE-inspired without being gimmicky. Charts and data visualizations should feel crisp and editorial — no 3D effects or gratuitous gradients. Use generous whitespace to let the density of data breathe.

The overall mood: **authoritative, clean, contemporary** — like a well-designed technical report, not a marketing flyer.
