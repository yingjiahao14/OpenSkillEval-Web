# Instruction: Global Internet Usage Comparison Poster

## Overview

Create a professional, data-forward poster comparing internet usage across the world's major digital economies. This is an A1 portrait-format piece aimed at a data-report audience — think conference handout, corporate briefing room, or research publication wall display. The key message: internet adoption varies dramatically across countries, and the scale of the world's digital population carries enormous economic implications.

## Key Priorities

- **Lead with the headline stat**: the ~5.56 billion global internet users figure should be visually dominant — it anchors the entire poster and immediately communicates scale.
- **The penetration rates comparison is the analytical centerpiece.** Make sure the cross-country penetration data is easy to scan and compare at a glance. A well-structured chart or ranked visual here is essential.
- **China and India's online populations** deserve special callout treatment — together they represent a massive share of the global digital population and should feel prominent.
- **Connect usage to economic impact**: the $8.0 trillion e-commerce projection should land as a powerful closing takeaway, tying connectivity to real-world outcomes.

## Visual & Style Guidance

Adopt a clean, modern aesthetic with a cool-toned palette — deep navy, teal, and white with selective accent color (e.g., bright cyan or electric blue) for emphasis. Typography should be sharp and hierarchical: large bold numerals for key metrics, clear section headers, and restrained body text. Use generous whitespace to let the data breathe. Avoid decorative clutter — every visual element should serve comprehension. Subtle globe or network-node motifs can reinforce the global connectivity theme without overwhelming the data.
