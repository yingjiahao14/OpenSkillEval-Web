# Instruction: Web Summit Lisbon 2026 — Event Promo Poster

## Overview

Create a bold, high-energy promotional poster for **Web Summit Lisbon 2026**, the world's premier technology conference. The poster targets tech professionals, startup founders, investors, and marketers — people who respond to scale, prestige, and urgency. The primary goal is to **drive ticket registrations** by making the event feel unmissable.

## Key Priorities

The **2-for-1 ticket offer (50% savings)** is the single most important conversion element — it must be visually dominant and impossible to miss. Pair it with a sense of urgency (24-hour window) so the viewer feels compelled to act immediately.

The **event title and dates** (November 9–12, 2026, Lisbon) should anchor the top of the poster — the viewer needs to know *what* and *when* within the first second.

The **scale of the event** (70,000+ attendees, world-class speakers) should reinforce credibility and FOMO. Use the key metrics to communicate this at a glance rather than through dense text.

## Visual & Style Guidance

Think dark, cinematic backgrounds — deep blacks or midnight blues — with vivid accent colors (electric cyan, hot magenta, or neon green) that evoke a tech-forward, futuristic feel. Typography should be oversized, sans-serif, and unapologetically bold. The layout should breathe confidence: generous whitespace, strong hierarchy, and a clean grid. Imagine the aesthetic of a premium tech brand launch, not a cluttered flyer. If imagery is used, think abstract light trails, geometric patterns, or a stylized Lisbon skyline silhouette — nothing stock-photo generic.

The overall mood: **ambitious, electric, and exclusive**.
