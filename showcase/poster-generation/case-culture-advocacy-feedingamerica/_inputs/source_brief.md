# Hunger Doesn't Wait — Together We Can End It

## The Hunger Crisis in America

Hunger is not a distant problem — it is a crisis unfolding in every community across the United States. Today, **48 million people** face hunger in the U.S., including **14 million children**. Families in every state, in urban and rural areas alike, struggle to put nutritious food on the table. This is not just a statistic — it represents neighbors, classmates, coworkers, and community members who need support right now.

## The Power of a Nationwide Network

Feeding America operates one of the nation's largest and most effective hunger-relief networks, moving food quickly, safely, and reliably to the communities that need it most. The results speak for themselves:

| Metric | Impact |
|---|---|
| Meals Distributed | 5.7 billion |
| Food Rescued | 4.3 billion pounds |
| Free Food Programs | 60,000+ |
| Food Banks in Network | 200+ |

This vast infrastructure ensures that rescued food — perfectly good food that might otherwise go to waste — reaches people facing hunger across all 50 states and Puerto Rico.

## How We Unite Communities

The approach rests on four interconnected pillars:

- **Research-Driven Insights**: Listening to neighbors facing hunger and sharing their stories and ideas. Their voices shape real solutions and guide the path toward an America without hunger.
- **Local Relationships, National Impact**: Food bank leaders are passionate community members. Local wisdom is turned into national action every day.
- **Nationwide Scale**: As one of the nation's most effective food networks, the system moves food quickly and reliably to where it is needed most.
- **Nutrition for Every Plate**: Making nutritious food more accessible so everyone can lead fuller, healthier lives. As one participant shared: *"I have high blood pressure and high cholesterol and am on a budget. They give you groceries and recipes. I've learned how to use whole foods and learned different options."* — Kaycee, Washington

## Every Action Counts

Ending hunger requires collective effort. There are many ways to contribute:

| Action | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **Donate** | Every $1 provides at least 10 meals to neighbors in need. Gifts are matched for a limited time to move fresh food to neighbors fast. |
| **Advocate** | Urge lawmakers to support community organizations and government programs that help neighbors facing hunger. |
| **Fundraise** | Inspire your community to come together and make a collective difference. |
| **Volunteer** | Sorting, packing, and distributing food takes many hands — including yours. |

## A Vision for the Future

Hunger touches every U.S. community, but together, we can meet this moment. Food rescue cannot wait. When communities unite — through donations, advocacy, volunteering, and shared purpose — an America without hunger is within reach. The resolve to end hunger is stronger than ever.
