# Every Child Deserves to Learn

Education is a fundamental right for every child. Yet around the world, millions of children and adolescents are denied this right due to conflict, poverty, discrimination, and systemic failures. UNICEF works across more than 190 countries and territories to ensure that every child — regardless of gender, ability, or circumstance — has access to inclusive, quality education.

## The Scale of the Crisis

Approximately **250 million children and adolescents** are out of school worldwide. Even among those who do attend, learning outcomes remain alarmingly low: an estimated **70% of children in low- and middle-income countries** cannot read a simple text by age 10, a condition often referred to as "learning poverty." These figures represent not just lost potential for individual children, but enormous setbacks for communities and economies.

## Key Barriers to Education

Several interconnected factors drive the education crisis:

- **Armed conflict and humanitarian emergencies**: Schools are destroyed, teachers displaced, and children traumatized. In crisis-affected regions, girls are 2.5 times more likely than boys to be out of school.
- **Poverty**: Families in the poorest communities often cannot afford school fees, uniforms, or supplies, and may rely on children for labour or income.
- **Gender inequality**: Deeply rooted social norms continue to limit girls' access to education, especially at the secondary level.
- **Disability**: Nearly half of all children with disabilities in low-income countries never set foot in a classroom.
- **Climate change and environmental shocks**: Floods, droughts, and extreme weather events increasingly disrupt schooling, disproportionately affecting the most vulnerable.

## UNICEF's Education Focus Areas

UNICEF's education strategy is built around several core pillars designed to tackle these barriers:

| Focus Area | Description |
|---|---|
| Early childhood development | Building strong foundations through pre-primary education and school readiness programmes |
| Inclusive education | Ensuring children with disabilities and marginalized groups can access and thrive in school |
| Gender equality in education | Removing barriers that prevent girls from enrolling, staying in, and completing school |
| Education in emergencies | Providing learning continuity for children affected by conflict, displacement, and natural disasters |
| Digital and innovative learning | Leveraging technology to expand access and improve learning quality |
| Adolescent skills development | Equipping young people with skills for employment, civic engagement, and lifelong learning |

## Aligned with Global Goals

UNICEF's education work directly supports **Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG 4)**: ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all. It also intersects with child rights, child protection, health, nutrition, and social policy — reflecting the understanding that education cannot be addressed in isolation.

## A Call to Action

Every child who misses out on education is a missed opportunity for a healthier, more equitable, and more prosperous world. Investing in education means investing in peace, innovation, and sustainable development. Together — governments, communities, organizations, and individuals — we can ensure that no child is left behind.
