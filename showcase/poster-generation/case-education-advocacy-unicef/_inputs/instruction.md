# Instruction: Every Child Deserves to Learn — Advocacy Poster

## Overview

Create a bold, large-format advocacy poster for UNICEF's global education campaign. This poster will be displayed in a **public exhibition** setting, so it needs to command attention from a distance while rewarding closer reading with compelling detail. The core message: **250 million children are out of school worldwide**, and urgent action is needed to change that.

## Purpose & Audience

This is an **advocacy piece** aimed at the general public. Viewers should walk away understanding the staggering scale of the education crisis and feeling motivated to support change. The tone is **bold and warm** — urgent without being hopeless, data-driven but deeply human.

## Key Priorities

- **Make "250 million" the visual anchor.** This headline statistic should be impossible to miss — it's the emotional hook that draws people in.
- The **barriers to education** section should feel immediate and tangible — conflict, poverty, gender inequality, disability, climate change. These aren't abstract; they're real obstacles affecting real children.
- End with a **clear, hopeful call to action** — the poster should leave viewers feeling empowered, not defeated.

## Visual & Style Guidance

Think UNICEF's signature cyan/blue as a foundation, paired with warm tones (amber, soft orange) to convey hope and humanity. Use **high-contrast typography** for key stats — bold sans-serif headlines that read from across a room. The overall layout should breathe; don't overcrowd. Let whitespace guide the eye from crisis → barriers → action. The mood should feel like a hand reaching out — strong, steady, compassionate.
