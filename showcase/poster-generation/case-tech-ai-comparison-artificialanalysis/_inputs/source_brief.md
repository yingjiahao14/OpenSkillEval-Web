# Frontier AI Models Compared: Intelligence, Speed & Cost

Based on independent analysis from Artificial Analysis (artificialanalysis.ai), this brief compares the leading frontier AI language models across three critical dimensions: intelligence, output speed, and pricing. All data reflects the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.0.

## Key Trade-Off Insights

- **Best overall intelligence**: Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview and GPT-5.4 (tied at 57) but at mid-to-high cost ($4.50–$5.60).
- **Best value**: gpt-oss-120B offers the fastest speed (206 tok/s) and lowest price ($0.30) but ranks last in intelligence (33).
- **Balanced pick**: Gemini 3 Flash scores 46 in intelligence, 149 tok/s in speed, and costs just $1.10 — a strong middle ground.
- **Premium tier**: Claude Opus 4.6 delivers 53 intelligence but is the slowest (40 tok/s) and most expensive ($10.00).

## Methodology

The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.0 incorporates 10 independent evaluations spanning coding, agentic tasks, scientific reasoning, knowledge accuracy, hallucination resistance, and instruction following. All measurements are conducted independently by Artificial Analysis using standardized conditions.
