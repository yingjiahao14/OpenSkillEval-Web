# The World's Most Valuable Companies in 2025

As of mid-2025, the global public equity landscape is tracked across 10,796 companies with a combined market capitalization of approximately **$142.9 trillion**. A remarkably small number of firms account for a disproportionate share of that total value.

## The Trillion-Dollar Club

Eleven companies have crossed the trillion-dollar threshold. NVIDIA leads the world at $4.82 trillion, followed closely by Alphabet (Google) at $4.03 trillion and Apple at $3.87 trillion. Together, the top five alone are worth over $18.5 trillion — roughly 13% of all tracked public company value.

## U.S. Dominance in the Top 20

Of the 20 largest companies by market capitalization, **15 are headquartered in the United States**. The remaining five come from four different countries: Taiwan (TSMC), Saudi Arabia (Saudi Aramco), South Korea (Samsung, SK Hynix is at rank 21), China (Tencent at rank 18), and the Netherlands (ASML at rank 20).

## Sector Concentration: Tech Reigns Supreme

Technology and semiconductor companies dominate the upper ranks. NVIDIA, Broadcom, TSMC, and Apple all derive significant revenue from chips or consumer electronics. Software and internet platforms — Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta — fill out the rest of the top tier. The only non-tech entries in the top 11 are Saudi Aramco (oil & gas), Tesla (automotive/energy), and Berkshire Hathaway (diversified conglomerate/investment).

## Scale in Context

The gap between the very top and the broader market is striking:

- **NVIDIA alone** is worth more than the combined market cap of the bottom 5,000+ tracked companies.
- The **top 10 companies** (~0.09% of all tracked firms) hold roughly **18% of total global market value**.
- Moving from rank 1 ($4.82 T) to rank 20 ($554 B) represents a nearly **9x drop** in valuation.

This concentration underscores how a handful of technology-driven firms have reshaped the global economic hierarchy, with AI-related companies like NVIDIA and Broadcom seeing explosive growth in recent years.
