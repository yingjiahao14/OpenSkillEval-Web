# Global Economic Prospects: Resilience Amid Uncertainty

## A Historic but Uneven Recovery

The global economy has demonstrated notable resilience in the face of heightened trade tensions and policy uncertainty. Last year's faster-than-expected growth capped a recovery from the 2020 recession that is unmatched in six decades. However, this headline resilience masks a widening gap: vulnerable emerging market and developing economies (EMDEs) continue to lag significantly behind advanced economies and more robust peers.

In the current year, global growth is projected to edge downward, driven in part by firms scaling back inventory accumulation and the intensifying effects of tariffs. Growth could falter further if trade tensions escalate or financial market sentiment deteriorates sharply.

## Key Risks to the Outlook

- **Trade tensions:** Escalation of tariffs and retaliatory measures could disrupt supply chains and dampen investment.
- **Financial market volatility:** A deterioration in sentiment could tighten financing conditions, especially for EMDEs.
- **Inventory correction:** Firms that front-loaded purchases are now drawing down stocks, reducing near-term demand.
- **Climate shocks:** Extreme weather events continue to threaten agricultural output and infrastructure in vulnerable regions.

## Policy Priorities for Sustained Growth

Global and domestic action is essential to sustain the recovery and foster inclusive job creation. The World Bank identifies the following reform areas:

| Reform Area | Global Action | Domestic Action |
|---|---|---|
| Trade | Improve the multilateral trade environment | Diversify trade partners and products |
| Finance | Ease external financing constraints for EMDEs | Strengthen macroeconomic policy frameworks |
| Climate | Scale up climate risk mitigation and adaptation | Invest in resilient infrastructure |
| Investment | Catalyze private capital flows | Remove structural bottlenecks to business entry |
| Jobs | Support cross-border labor mobility | Enhance skills development and labor market flexibility |

## World Bank 2030 Strategic Priorities

With 1.2 billion young people reaching working age in the next decade, job creation is central to the World Bank Group's mission. The Bank has set ambitious 2030 targets across five flagship initiatives:

| Initiative | Target |
|---|---|
| Health Services | Deliver quality, affordable health services to 1.5 billion people |
| Mission 300 | Provide 300 million people in Sub-Saharan Africa with electricity access |
| AgriConnect | Help 300 million farmers move up the agricultural value chain |
| Capital Support | Provide 80 million more women and women-led businesses with access to capital |
| Water Forward | Improve water security for over 1 billion people, with a 400 million World Bank Group commitment |
